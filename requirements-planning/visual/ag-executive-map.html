SA-1B: what it is, who needs it, and when to do it

What SA-1B contains (Content Pack Configurator, v1.2 · reviewed 22 Sep · 28 decisions)

The library, authored onceCanonical fields flagged lead / prospect / case, field groups, answer lists, reusable sections, master documents with substitutes and validity, the form list (auto-fill parked), rules and scoring, reference tables, government steps, regulator wording.
The configuratorOne row per full combination Pathway › Program › Category › Sub-category, holding the ordered list of sections, documents, forms and steps, each with who it is for and an optional condition.
Filings and routesA case holds filings: side by side for a family's separate permits, in sequence for two- or three-step provincial routes.
People and rolesA family is one case with per-person questionnaire, checklist, forms, fee and decision.
Prospect tiers and the PQROne prospect questionnaire per country; a capped lead form that pre-fills the next tier.
Assessment output, startable checks, firm questionsVerdict, reason and gap with "rules as at"; a program is offered only when its row is complete; firms add their own questions but never change a required one.

Who reads from it: AG-3 (assessment), AG-7 (cases and filings), AG-8 (questionnaire), AG-9 (checklist), and in small ways AG-2 (lead-form tiers) and AG-5/6 (fee wording). Still open: the three extensions AG-17 requires (steps before submission, action lines, clocks from reference tables) are not yet in the file; which forms ship; the Canada content scope session has no date or owner. Not the same thing as the Practice Pack (firm's regulator, trust, fee and retention rules, FD-22), which has no module file at all and which Charter V2 mislabels as "SA-1-B".

Three ways to time it

Before all AG

  • Nothing is reviewed twice
  • Clean dependency story
  • Delays AG-01, AG-02, AG-18, which do not need it and are in the October build
  • About a week of calendar lost for no gain on 12 modules
RECOMMENDED

Now, in parallel with Wave 1; closed before Wave 3

  • AG-3/7/8/9 are reviewed once, against a settled model
  • Keeps the Charter's October build and 28 Oct dates
  • Serves the 13 Nov "v0 packs on staging" and the 11 Dec core-journey gate
  • Removes the triage's "bigger risk than any feature"
  • Two tracks at once: Tanu and Sanjay on SA-1B content, the AG reviews beside it

After AG

  • None that hold: AG work does not make SA-1B easier
  • AG-3/7/8/9 written against a moving target, then rewritten
  • v0 packs miss 13 Nov; no firm can become ready without a published pack
  • Dev paused SA-01 "waiting on 1-B" since 18 Sep; QA on hold

Remaining SA-1B requirements work is small: one revision pass (apply the AG-17 extensions and AG-07 filings, close "which forms") plus one Canada content scope session. The build of the configurator is the long pole, and it starts from that revision.
