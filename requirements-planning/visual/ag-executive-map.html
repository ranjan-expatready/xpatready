1The scope rule

Release 1 = everything in the files, except AI

  • In: every feature written in the 18 files, including WhatsApp, corporate and LMIA, data import, reporting, client portal.
  • Out, a separate Release 2 stream: anything AI: helpers that read documents, sort inbound mail, draft text, cross-check facts; the automation engine with its recipes, approval inbox and safeguards (AG-11); the AI switches in settings.

What the rule changes in the files

  • The files currently say the opposite twice: AI helpers "in Release 1, switched off" (4–5 Oct), and earlier "Release 2" lists for non-AI features (corporate depth, extra dashboards, lead sequences, invoice-history import). Both get rewritten to the rule in wave 0.
Two leftovers for the founder: form auto-fill was parked on 23 Sep (SA1B-D28): keep parked or bring back? · plain date reminders at launch (biometrics due, permit expiry) sit in the automation module today: keep a simple reminder in AG-13, or by hand until the AI stream?

4SA-1B

What it is

The Canada rules and content every case runs on, held as data.

  • The questions a client is asked, and in which order
  • The documents required, their substitutes and expiry rules
  • The list of government forms
  • Eligibility rules and points
  • Reference tables: wages, fees, language scores
  • Government waves and deadlines
  • Program structure: who the client is › program › application › variant
  • The regulator's required wording
Open: three additions the LMIA module needs · which forms ship · the Canada content session has no owner or date. Not the Practice Pack (the firm's own regulator, trust, fee and retention rules), which has no file and which the Charter mislabels "SA-1-B".

Who depends on it

Cannot be finished without it · 5AG-03 assessment · AG-07 cases · AG-08 questionnaire · AG-09 documents · AG-17 LMIA
Takes one value from it · 6AG-01 regulator wording · AG-02 lead-form questions · AG-05 required clauses · AG-06 fee table · AG-13 deadline types · AG-18 program names
No dependency · 6AG-04 booking · AG-10 communication · AG-12 templates · AG-14 reporting · AG-15 portal management · AG-16 settings

When: wave 3

  • After the seven modules that do not need it: they tell SA-1B what they want (lead-form questions, clauses, fee table).
  • Before the five that run on it: they are then reviewed once, against a settled model.
  • Not first: would push the three modules being built this month (AG-01, 18, 02) back for nothing.
  • Not last: the five would be written twice, and no firm can open a case until the Canada rules are published.
  • The Canada content itself (which programs, forms, documents) is a separate job for the team's product owner and the immigration expert after wave 3.
