# Grilling: interview the founder, never just ask

**Why:** the founder asked to be interviewed with selectable options, never asked to type answers.

## When
Any time a choice is the founder's to make: a ratified decision would change, a scope or sequence changes, a trade-off has no obvious default, a Proposal needs the founder's yes. Not for routine reversible work: the Lead decides and reports.

## The one shape, every time
Use the interview tool (`AskUserQuestion`), **one decision per question**, and only ask the next after the founder answers.

1. **Header:** the decision in at most 12 characters (e.g. "Self-approve").
2. **Question:** one sentence, plain words, ending in a question mark. Name the thing decided, not the document.
3. **Scenario:** one concrete, fictional case in the question text, roles only ("a solo licensed consultant…", "Client A…"). The scenario shows what happens on a real day, not an abstract principle.
4. **Options:** two to four, mutually exclusive. Each label is the choice in 1–5 words; each description is **the consequence** of choosing it (what the firm sees, what changes in a requirement, what it costs or risks). The Lead's recommendation is the **first** option and its label ends "(Recommended)". The founder can always pick "Other" and type; never make typing the only way.
5. **Multi-select** only when the choices are genuinely not exclusive (e.g. which refinements to apply).

## After the answer
- Record it the same session in the product home's `DECISIONS.md`, next free P- number (a weighty one also gets a page in `decisions/`), and log the request in `REQUESTS.md`; **in the founder's words** (copy the chosen option label, plus anything typed), with the question, the scenario, the recommendation and the consequence applied.
- Apply the consequence to the files it touches, then ask the next question. Never bundle two decisions into one question to save time.
- A question dismissed without an answer is **not decided**; say so in the log and ask again when the founder returns.
- The independent checker does not record decisions; it reports them and the Lead records.

## Never
- A decision stated only in chat prose ("shall I…?"): it does not count as asked.
- Options without consequences, or a recommendation hidden in the description instead of marked on the option.
- Leading questions that remove a real option, or a scenario with a real person's name.
- Asking the founder to settle something a source or a ratified decision already settles — challenge instead, with the finding.
