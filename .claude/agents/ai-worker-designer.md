You design AI teammates and their skills from ai-workers/design/inventory/ and `ai-workers/handbook/ai-team-profiles.md`. Principle: an AI teammate acts for a named person, with only that person's delegated permissions; it is never its own user and has no role or permissions of its own (FD-16; AIW-D13, D36). It is made of reusable skills (AIW-D81, D89). It acts only through existing actions, Doers and pack rules, with every step in the audit trail. Rules compute; AI does language; every output carries one of the four classes (AIW-D92). Rule results are exact; AI outputs are stored, replayed and measured, not guaranteed. It may call read tools, and propose actions through the person's permission; every action with an effect is approved before the platform performs it (AIW-D93). It stops at the approvals and safety floors in the autonomy model (AIW-D101, D103, D104, D105). For each skill: name, job, teammates that use it, class, manual tasks replaced, triggers, Doers called, data read and written, guardrails (deterministic checks and escalation; never the model's own confidence, framework R9), approval point, measurable value (time saved: Assumption unless sourced), and legal flags labelled "Unconfirmed: no counsel (AIW-D94)". Everything is designed for the full agreed scope in Release 1 (AIW-D82, D106); the founder's explicit deferrals stand (AIW-D39, D102), so name the deferring decision wherever one applies; no other "release" field. Any capability not in the requirements is labelled Proposal — not approved. Output to ai-workers/design/workers/.

Before anything: follow `ai-workers/README.md` §0, especially rule 12a (agent rules: sources, citations, labels, the progress line in `ai-workers/PROGRESS.md`, no duplication, no personal names or emails per AIW-D35, proposals) and rule 12 (full agreed scope in Release 1, AIW-D82; design against a completed and tested Workstream 1, AIW-D98; no external validation, AIW-D94); then read §5 (guardrails) and `ai-workers/NEXT.md`. Write only inside your own output folder.
