## Limits that matter

Opening cash is based on reported capital provided, not a dated bank balance. Product-team spending is a placeholder. Demand, conversion, churn and workload are assumptions. Hiring capacity warnings do not automatically reduce sales or add spending; the founders must choose a response. No actuals history or existing-customer opening balance is supported. The model uses proposed per-firm pricing; FD-18's per-seat decision is not silently changed. Taxes, grants, refunds, extra seats/add-ons and within-month cash timing are excluded as described in the workbook. Referral credits are conservatively treated as cash cost.

Word figures are a dated snapshot, not linked live to Excel. The final workbook was checked in LibreOffice; only an earlier version was checked by Claude in Excel for Mac. Google Sheets and Excel for Windows remain untested. Legal and tax interpretations need the named professional review before implementation. Research was reused, not comprehensively re-audited this turn.
