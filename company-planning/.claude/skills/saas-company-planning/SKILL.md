# Expat Ready company planning

Resolve the planning root as three directories above this skill folder. Read its [AGENTS.md](../../../AGENTS.md), [source register](../../../SOURCE-REGISTER.md) and the latest commercial baseline before making recommendations. Preserve the four existing commercial artifacts; do not generate another competing plan set.

Choose the relevant mode from [WORKFLOW.md](../../../WORKFLOW.md):

- **Continue / begin:** reconcile current sources and unfinished work, then execute the next useful stage. Do not repeat answered interviews or completed research.
- **Research:** use primary sources, dates and evidence labels; investigate gaps affecting a decision. For specialist AI-tool questions, independently verify or challenge [the existing research](../../../research/ai-tools-2026-10-07.md).
- **Build:** create the relevant artifact in commercial/, with owners, triggers, economics and linked assumptions. Cover both product operations and the revenue/customer lifecycle.
- **Review:** independently inspect evidence and calculations against [COVERAGE.md](../../../COVERAGE.md); reviewers write findings only. Agreement between models is not evidence.
- **Update:** incorporate new facts once in the commercial baseline, recalculate dependent outputs and identify decisions that changed.

For every delivery, map relevant coverage IDs to exact output sections; mark unknowns and deliberate deferrals. An outline is not coverage. Never claim completeness without this mapping and a recorded review. No process can guarantee every future need is known.

Challenge the founder's and both models' assumptions using evidence and alternatives. In particular test network size versus reachable buying firms, buying versus referring, commission economics, one-person sales capacity, fundraising necessity, and website promises versus verified delivery. Criticism should produce a test or decision, not theatrical harshness.

Use [HANDOFF.md](../../../HANDOFF.md) when switching author/reviewer. Keep the user-facing result brief; put substantiation in linked appendices. End with what changed, what remains unverified and the next useful action.
