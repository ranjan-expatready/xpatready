# Expat Ready — start here

**The simplified package is ready for founder discussion (8 October 2026).** Claude wrote it in a fresh session; Codex challenged the recommendations and independently checked representative calculations. The earlier large package is superseded; its research remains available.

You need two working files:

| Open | Use it for |
|---|---|
| [Business plan — Word](../commercial/plan.docx) | Discuss the buyer, offer, first customers, referrals, people, funding and next actions. Six pages of planning plus a final contact/source page. |
| [Money model — Excel](../commercial/model.xlsx) | Change the yellow cells on **Inputs**, then read **Summary**. Customers, MRR, ARR, collections, costs, cash and funding need update together. No scripts needed. |

**First meeting:** agree who will sell and how much time they have; replace the product-team cost and opening cash with real figures; decide whether pricing is per firm or per seat. Then choose the first customer conversations and the funding route. The plan gives proposed owners and dates.

The workbook is a planning forecast, not an accounting system. It assumes no existing paying customers. Changing assumptions recalculates all months; save a dated copy to compare experiments. Its “observed” column is notes only. Amounts printed in Word are a dated snapshot and need updating after the workbook changes.

**What is still provisional:** available cash, product costs, customer demand and conversion, launch readiness and the proposed offer. Per-seat pricing needs a seat-count calculation if chosen. No spending, hiring, outreach or fundraising terms have been approved by this work.

For edits, tell Claude what changed and ask it to keep the [plan source](../commercial/plan.md) and Word copy consistent. There is no need to restart research or restore the old framework.
