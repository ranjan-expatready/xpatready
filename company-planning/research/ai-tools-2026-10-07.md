Buy no new planning software yet. Trial a specialist only against a task where our actual output is deficient. This is a recommendation, not a claim that general models are universally superior.

## What “specialized” can mean

| Mechanism | What it changes | What it does not establish |
|---|---|---|
| Fine-tuning | Model weights trained on examples for a task | Current market knowledge, factual accuracy or fit for this company |
| Retrieval/context | Supplies relevant current documents/evidence at use time | Source truth or completeness |
| Skill/template/workflow | Reusable instructions, artifact structures and checks | A retrained model or guaranteed judgment |
| Planning application | Guided inputs, linked financial calculations, exports and collaboration | Superior underlying language model |

OpenAI describes skills/plugins as reusable workflow resources, and its optimization guidance recommends evaluation and context/prompt improvements before considering fine-tuning for a demonstrated need. Our choice follows that sequence; training a custom model is disproportionate to this one-company planning request. [Skills and plugins](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/skills-and-plugins), [model optimization](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/model-optimization).

## Fair sample test before adopting another tool

**Not yet run.** Only run a paid/account-based comparison when authorized; public-document research can proceed now.

Give each candidate the same sanitized brief, source pack, unresolved conflicts and requested outputs. Include a known stale claim and an explicit unknown cash balance. Compare with Claude/Codex using identical task scope; record version, date, tools, prompts, cost, elapsed time and manual corrections. Do not supply live client/immigration data.

Ask for three bounded outputs:

1. A launch/hiring sequence that distinguishes prototype, pilot and public launch and challenges one-person sales assumptions.
2. A referral-program proposal with contribution economics, cancellation handling, professional-rule questions and an initial experiment.
3. A linked monthly cash scenario, milestone-based funding argument and short investor narrative without inventing traction.

Proposed scoring rubric (a procurement aid, not an industry standard):

| Dimension | Weight | What the reviewer checks |
|---|---:|---|
| Evidence and uncertainty | 25 | Claims trace to sources; stale/conflicting inputs caught; no fabricated facts |
| Required coverage | 20 | Relevant COVERAGE.md topics handled, including product/support feedback |
| Economics and calculations | 20 | Units/cash/referrals/cohorts correct; formulas inspectable; assumptions explicit |
| Actionability | 15 | Owners, triggers, capacity, cost, measures and useful trade-offs |
| Critical reasoning | 10 | Challenges unsupported funding/hiring/channel assumptions with alternatives |
| Portability and effort | 10 | Editable files, reproducibility and less total manual correction |

Score each dimension 0–5, multiply by weight/5. Assess outputs blind to vendor names where practical. Treat fabricated customer/financial facts, hidden formula failures or invented legal certainty as critical failures regardless of score. Adopt only if the relevant gain is material relative to cost and corrections; otherwise retain the existing workflow. Any numerical adoption threshold is a founder-approved procurement choice, not scientific proof.

Check before paying: current billing/renewal, editable DOCX/PPTX/XLSX availability, whether financial exports retain formulas, access after cancellation, data retention/deletion and output-use rights. A one-month purchase is not necessarily lifetime access. Recheck terms at purchase; no subscription is being recommended now.
