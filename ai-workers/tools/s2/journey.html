PHow the PQR, SQR, agent view and forms are rendered and pre-filled

  1. One renderer, four views. Each view is the package's definition (sections, fields, rules for this person and role) laid over the fact store. No view stores its own answers.
  2. PQR is small and eligibility-focused: block 0 plus the blocks for what the prospect wants; rules give an indicative result.
  3. SQR is larger: everything the combination needs from the client, minus what is known, supplied by documents, or staff-only.
  4. Agent working view is the full set, every tier, by library section, with each value's source and status; it is where facts are confirmed and conflicts decided.
  5. Form check view is each form, per applicant, in the form's own order, filled through the box addresses; read-only.
  6. Re-render on change. When a fact changes, every view and every form that reads it re-renders, and earlier approvals of those forms are withdrawn.
