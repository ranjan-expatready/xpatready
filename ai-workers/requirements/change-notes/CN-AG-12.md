**In plain words.** Firm templates send on their own once the firm has approved them, which is right for routine messages. Two safeguards are needed now that AI assistants also send templates. First, a mark for templates that talk about a client's status or eligibility. No automatic or AI-drafted send ever delivers a marked template to a lead, because the founder decided that eligibility reaches a lead only when a consultant shares the assessment. A person's own message is not blocked. To an existing client, an assistant's send of a marked template waits for a person by default. The check works only on templates that carry the mark: wording in an unmarked template is not checked by machine. Second, a letter type for case letters, with locked wording, a list of the facts used, wording the Writer may never touch, and a court-declaration flag; the letters themselves are country masters Expat Ready publishes. "Drafter" in this module is the template engine and stays.

**Terms used.** *Lead*: a prospect in AG-02 who is not yet a client. *Automatic or AI-drafted send*: a send made by any automation through the Mailer or Messenger (an AI assistant's recipe, a firm's own recipe, a seeded or ready-made recipe, or another product automation), or of a message the Writer drafted; a person's own message typed or sent by hand is not one. *Master*: a template Expat Ready publishes for every firm of a country, which a firm adopts as its own copy (SA-05).

## 1. A "contains status or eligibility content" mark; never sent to a lead by an automatic or AI-drafted send

1. **Where.** §7 `Template` (L396); AG-12-33 (L314); new requirement **AG-12-38**; new rule **BR-15**.
2. **Current words.**
   > L396: "**`Template`** (the container) — `id` (`templateCode`, e.g. `TPL-…`), **`firm_id`** (CLIENT scope; null/platform for SYSTEM), **`scope`** (`SYSTEM | CLIENT`), **`type`** (template type, §3.9), **`channel`**"
3. **Proposed words.**
   > **AG-12-38 (status or eligibility mark).** A template SHALL carry a mark **contains status or eligibility content**. The firm sets it when it publishes a template. For the templates of the seeded and ready-made recipes, Expat Ready presets the mark where the template carries such content (SA-5-47 as proposed in CN-SA-05), and the firm sees and may set it on the one "Review your standard messages" screen (AG-11-91; AG-16-48). (a) A marked template SHALL NOT be sent to a lead by any automatic or AI-drafted send, whatever its autonomy level and whoever approves it; the send step refuses it (AG-11-115 as proposed in CN-AG-11 change 13), with the reason that eligibility reaches a lead only through the consultant's audited assessment share (AG-3-38; AG-03 BR-19 as proposed in CN-AG-03). A person's own hand-written or hand-sent message is outside this rule. (b) To an existing client, when an AI assistant (AW-01) would send a marked template, the send SHALL default to "Ask me first" (AG-11-51); the firm MAY change that default per recipe, as for any template message (AG-11-52). (c) Free text an AI assistant adds to any template is AI-written text and stays "Ask me first" (the AG-11-53 floor). (d) The check rests on the mark: a template left unmarked, and free text, are not machine-checked for eligibility content (AG11-D1); the firm marks its own templates.
   >
   > **BR-15 (Eligibility never reaches a lead through an automatic send).** A template marked as containing status or eligibility content is never sent to a lead by an automatic or AI-drafted send; to an existing client an assistant's send of it waits for a person by default.

   `Template` gains the mark (proposed field `statusOrEligibilityContent`, boolean; name to assign).
4. **Why.** AIW-D41 (the founder: "No eligibility content (score, band, *likely*, levers) in any automatic or AI-drafted message to a lead; eligibility reaches a lead only through the consultant's audited share (AG-3-38)"); `00-step3-plan.md` "Eligibility to leads, exact reach" (every automatic or AI-drafted send, at the send step; a person's own hand-written or hand-sent message is not blocked; no fifth fixed safeguard against AG-11 BR-34); IMP-28(c) for the client-side default (marked templates default to "ask me first" when a worker sends them), as `00-step3-plan.md` "Status-content templates sent by an assistant" keeps it a default; IMP-28(b) (any worker free text is "ask me first"). Relied on by `case-assistant.md` §9 G-9, `billing-assistant.md` §9 G-28 and `firm-assistant.md` §9 G-26. Expat Ready presetting the mark on the seeded templates is a **Proposal** (review S3c-b n-10): the platform's system templates live in SA-05, and CN-SA-05 change 1 (SA-5-47) carries it (`00-step3-plan.md`, "SA-05 template mark"). This replaces the first fix round, which also blocked a person's hand send on no recorded basis (review S3c-b N-2).
5. **Knock-ons.** AG-11-115 (CN-AG-11 change 13: the one send-time check for every automatic or AI-drafted send) and AG-11-114 (g) (CN-AG-11 change 9: the client-side default); the composer's Writer drafts (CN-AG-10 change 4); AG-03 BR-19 says the same (CN-AG-03 change 2); AG-02 §3.10 nurture (CN-AG-02 change 1); AG-16-48 "Review your standard messages" shows the mark; SA-05 (CN-SA-05 changes 1 and 2); AW-1-46 and AW-1-90 cite this change. Counsel: whether an unlicensed-sent template with status content to an existing client is lawful under supervision (IMP-28(d)).

## 2. A letter type for case letters, within the document/letter family

1. **Where.** §3.9 (L252); AG-12-33 (L314); AG-12-24 (L299); new requirement **AG-12-39**.
2. **Current words.**
   > L252: "**Document / letter & checklist** → `DOCUMENT_CHECKLIST` exists (case + document-list variables). The rebuild treats a **document-request letter** and a **case checklist** as document-context types rendered by AG-7/AG-8."
3. **Proposed words.**
   > **AG-12-39 (case letter templates).** Within the document/letter family of AG-12-33, AG-12 SHALL support a **case letter** type (for example a letter of explanation, a submission cover letter or a reply to a government concern), beside `DOCUMENT_CHECKLIST`, with: (a) **locked blocks** the Writer SHALL NOT alter; (b) blocks marked **legal text**, which the Writer SHALL NOT draft, rewrite or translate (AG-12-24); (c) a **"facts used" footer** built by rule from the verified profile, listing each fact the letter states and its source document, with unverified facts listed as not yet verified; (d) a **litigation document** flag that inserts the court's declaration on AI-generated content, with wording counsel fixes, and routes the draft to the representative. The list of case letter masters lives in SA-05, as country masters the content author publishes with the immigration specialist (SA-5-48 as proposed in CN-SA-05); a firm adopts a master as its own copy like any other master. No new pack content area is added.
4. **Why.** AIW-D34 item 11 (the founder: W-06 letters are drafted inside the Case assistant and the representative approves them); `case-assistant.md` §9 G-14 (no letter-of-explanation template type exists; the "facts used" footer; a "legal text" marker the Writer refuses) and G-28 (the litigation flag); `proposals.md` rows of 9 Oct (translation helper, the "legal text" marker the AI refuses) and of 10 Oct (Lead, the Federal Court declaration flag); review S3c m-13 (place it inside the existing family); `00-step3-plan.md` "Case letters" (the list of case letter masters lives in SA-05, as country masters the content author publishes with the immigration specialist; no new pack content area). Counsel fixes the declaration wording (counsel pack question 11).
5. **Knock-ons.** AG-09 generate-from-template (AG-9-22) files the approved letter; AW-01 (the Case assistant's letter duty); SA-05 (CN-SA-05 change 2, SA-5-48, the case letter masters).

## 3. Templates used inside an AI job and as answers in the launch chat (12 Oct 2026)

1. **Where.** §3 (template use); after AG-12-39; new requirement **AG-12-40**.
2. **Current words.** None: AG-12 v1.2 does not say how a template may be used inside an AI teammate's job or as an answer in a chat.
3. **Proposed words.**
   > **AG-12-40 (templates used by AI teammates and in the lead chat).** (a) A published template sent by its defined automatic use counts as approved by the firm's own approval of that template, also inside an AI teammate's job (AG-11-114 (g) as amended, CN-AG-11 change 17). Its merge fields SHALL be filled only from verified facts or approved engine results (AG-11-97); any text an AI wrote or edited, including text put into a merge field, SHALL make the send an approval item. (b) Where a firm offers a chat to leads at launch, each answer SHALL be a published template version chosen by a rule (the guided intake flow, a menu or a keyword), never by a model; a template carrying the mark of AG-12-38 is never an answer to a lead; the chat surface is the team's to design. (c) No template SHALL be chosen by a model.
4. **Why.** AIW-D105 (the founder, 12 Oct 2026: "Templates must be rule-selected, with merge fields populated from verified facts or approved engine results … AI-written or edited content—including text inserted into merge fields—still requires approval before sending"); AIW-D102 ("Allow an interactive chat interface using that approved content"; mechanism M-1 waits for the founder's answer on the AIW-D102 amendment, `proposals.md` row of 12 Oct; any conversational AI is pending a founder decision with the team). Behaviour: AW-1-137, AW-1-138 (AW-01 v0.7).
5. **Knock-ons.** CN-AG-11 change 17; AG-02 lead capture (the chat surface, a team question, journeys G-1).
