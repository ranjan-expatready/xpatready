**In plain words.** Firm templates send on their own once the firm has approved them, which is right for routine messages. Two safeguards are needed now that assistants also send templates. First, a mark for templates that talk about a client's status or eligibility. No automatic or AI-drafted send ever delivers a marked template to a lead, because the founder decided that eligibility reaches a lead only when a consultant shares the assessment. A person's own message is not blocked. To an existing client, an assistant's send of a marked template waits for a person by default. The check works only on templates that carry the mark: wording in an unmarked template is not checked by machine. Second, a letter type for case letters, with locked wording, a list of the facts used, wording the Writer may never touch, and a court-declaration flag; the letters themselves are country masters Expat Ready publishes. "Drafter" in this module is the template engine and stays.

**Terms used.** *Lead*: a prospect in AG-02 who is not yet a client. *Automatic or AI-drafted send*: a send made by any automation through the Mailer or Messenger (an assistant's recipe, a firm's own recipe, a seeded or ready-made recipe, or another product automation), or of a message the Writer drafted; a person's own message typed or sent by hand is not one. *Master*: a template Expat Ready publishes for every firm of a country, which a firm adopts as its own copy (SA-05).

## 1. A "contains status or eligibility content" mark; never sent to a lead by an automatic or AI-drafted send

1. **Where** *(re-pointed 15 Oct 2026 to AG-12 v2.2, snapshot 1535)*. §7 `FirmTemplateVersion` (L516); AG-12-38 (L339, the three lists); AG-12-52 (L359, the optional "Review your automatic messages" page); new requirement **AG-12-112**; new rule **BR-27**. *History: drafted 11 Oct against v1.2 §7 `Template` (L396) and AG-12-33 (L314); v2.2 replaces the container's scope and type with kinds and firm versions (AG-12-1, AG-12-33 changed, L299, L331).*
2. **Current words.**
   > L516 (AG-12 v2.2): "**`FirmTemplateVersion`** — `id`, `firm_id`, `templateId` (ExpatReady template or firm template), `basedOnExpatReadyVersion?`, `versionNumber`, `state` (DRAFT / PUBLISHED / SUPERSEDED), `origin` (…), `bodies` (…), `mustFillFields[]`, `freeTextSlot?`, `conditionalSections[]`, `purpose`, `aiEdited` (bool), …"
   >
   > Nothing in AG-12 v2.2 marks a template as containing status or eligibility content.
3. **Proposed words.**
   > **AG-12-112 (status or eligibility mark).** A template SHALL carry a mark **contains status or eligibility content**. The firm sets it when it publishes a template version. For ExpatReady's own wording (kinds 2 and 3 used until the firm edits), Expat Ready presets the mark on the `SystemTemplateVersion` where the template carries such content (SA-5-110 as proposed in CN-SA-05), and the firm sees it on its three lists (AG-12-38) and on the optional "Review your automatic messages" page (AG-12-52), and may set it on its own version. (a) A marked template SHALL NOT be sent to a lead by any automatic or AI-drafted send, whatever its autonomy level and whoever approves it; the send step refuses it (AG-11-116 as proposed in CN-AG-11 change 13), with the reason that eligibility reaches a lead only through the consultant's audited assessment share (AG-3-38; AG-03 BR-19 as proposed in CN-AG-03). A person's own hand-written or hand-sent message is outside this rule. (b) To an existing client, when an assistant (AW-01) would send a marked template, the send SHALL default to "Ask me first" (AG-11-51); the firm MAY change that default per recipe, as for any template message (AG-11-52). (c) Free text an assistant adds to any template is AI-written text and stays "Ask me first" (the AG-11-53 floor). (d) The check rests on the mark: a template left unmarked, and free text, are not machine-checked for eligibility content (AG11-D1); the firm marks its own templates.
   >
   > **BR-27 (Eligibility never reaches a lead through an automatic send).** A template marked as containing status or eligibility content is never sent to a lead by an automatic or AI-drafted send; to an existing client an assistant's send of it waits for a person by default.

   `FirmTemplateVersion` gains the mark (proposed field `statusOrEligibilityContent`, boolean, beside `aiEdited`; name to assign); the send record carries the version used, so the check reads the mark of the version sent.
4. **Why.** AIW-D41 (the founder: "No eligibility content (score, band, *likely*, levers) in any automatic or AI-drafted message to a lead; eligibility reaches a lead only through the consultant's audited share (AG-3-38)"); `00-step3-plan.md` "Eligibility to leads, exact reach" (every automatic or AI-drafted send, at the send step; a person's own hand-written or hand-sent message is not blocked; no fifth fixed safeguard against AG-11 BR-34); IMP-28(c) for the client-side default (marked templates default to "ask me first" when a worker sends them), as `00-step3-plan.md` "Status-content templates sent by an assistant" keeps it a default; IMP-28(b) (any worker free text is "ask me first"). Relied on by AW-1-46 and AW-1-46. Expat Ready presetting the mark on the seeded templates is a **Proposal** (review S3c-b n-10): the platform's system templates live in SA-05, and CN-SA-05 change 1 (SA-5-110) carries it (`00-step3-plan.md`, "SA-05 template mark"). This replaces the first fix round, which also blocked a person's hand send on no recorded basis (review S3c-b N-2).
5. **Knock-ons.** AG-11-116 (CN-AG-11 change 13: the one send-time check for every automatic or AI-drafted send) and AG-11-115 (g) (CN-AG-11 change 9: the client-side default); the composer's Writer drafts (CN-AG-10 change 4); AG-03 BR-19 says the same (CN-AG-03 change 2); AG-02 §3.10 nurture (CN-AG-02 change 1); AG-12-52, the optional "Review your automatic messages" page, shows the mark (was AG-16-48's "Review your standard messages", history); SA-05 (CN-SA-05 changes 1 and 2); AW-1-46 and AW-1-90 cite this change. Whether an unlicensed-sent template with status content to an existing client is lawful under supervision: unconfirmed Lead guidance; no counsel (AIW-D94) (IMP-28(d)).

## 2. A letter type for case letters, within the document/letter family

1. **Where** *(re-pointed 15 Oct 2026 to AG-12 v2.2, snapshot 1535)*. §3 "Documents" (L201); AG-12-41 (L342, the Documents list); new requirement **AG-12-113**. *History: drafted 11 Oct against v1.2 §3.9 (L252), AG-12-33 (L314) and AG-12-24 (L299); v2.2 withdraws AG-12-24 (L322, "Rule 7: no mandated wording, no SME confirmation") and moves letters into the Documents list.*
2. **Current words.**
   > L342 (AG-12 v2.2): "**AG-12-41** *(changed v2.1 — cross-check D3)* The Documents list SHALL show the document templates of SA-05 Appendix A (proposal, retainers, pre-consultation agreement, assessment summary, corporate documents, letters, document checklist letter) with the AG-12-39 columns that apply; generation SHALL stay in the owning module."
3. **Proposed words.**
   > **AG-12-113 (case letter templates).** In the Documents list of AG-12-41, AG-12 SHALL support a **case letter** template (for example a letter of explanation, a submission cover letter or a reply to a government concern), beside the document checklist letter, with: (a) **locked blocks** the Writer SHALL NOT alter; (b) blocks marked **legal text**, which the Writer SHALL NOT draft, rewrite or translate (Workstream 2's own guard, AW-1-82 and AW-1-84, AIW-D79 and AIW-D149, now that AG-12-24 is withdrawn; it binds the Writer only, not the firm's own editing, so rule 7 stands); (c) a **"facts used" footer** built by rule from the verified profile, listing each fact the letter states and its source document, with unverified facts listed as not yet verified; (d) a **litigation document** flag that inserts the court's declaration on AI-generated content, with wording that is unconfirmed Lead guidance; no counsel (AIW-D94), and routes the draft to the representative. The list of case letter masters lives in SA-05, as country masters the content author publishes with the immigration specialist (SA-5-111 as proposed in CN-SA-05); a firm adopts a master as its own copy like any other master. No new pack content area is added.
4. **Why.** AIW-D34 item 11 (the founder: W-06 letters are drafted inside the Case Officer's AI and the representative approves them); AW-1-84 (no letter-of-explanation template type exists; the "facts used" footer; a "legal text" marker the Writer refuses) and G-28 (the litigation flag); `proposals.md` rows of 9 Oct (translation helper, the "legal text" marker the AI refuses) and of 10 Oct (Lead, the Federal Court declaration flag); review S3c m-13 (place it inside the existing family); `00-step3-plan.md` "Case letters" (the list of case letter masters lives in SA-05, as country masters the content author publishes with the immigration specialist; no new pack content area). The declaration wording is unconfirmed Lead guidance; no counsel (AIW-D94) (counsel pack question 11).
5. **Knock-ons.** AG-09 generate-from-template (AG-9-22) files the approved letter; AW-01 (the Case Officer's AI's letter duty); SA-05 (CN-SA-05 change 2, SA-5-111, the case letter masters).

## 3. Templates used inside an AI job and as answers in the launch chat (12 Oct 2026)

1. **Where.** §3 (template use); after AG-12-113; new requirement **AG-12-114**.
2. **Current words.** None: AG-12 v2.2 does not say how a template may be used inside an assistant's job or as an answer in a chat.
3. **Proposed words.**
   > **AG-12-114 (templates used by assistants and in the lead chat).** (a) A published template sent by its defined automatic use counts as approved by the firm's own approval of that template, also inside an assistant's job (AG-11-115 (g) as amended, CN-AG-11 change 17). Its fill-in fields SHALL be filled only from verified facts or approved engine results (AIW-D105; AG-11-97 covers only fields marked immigration-significant); any text an AI wrote or edited, including text put into a fill-in field, SHALL make the send an approval item. (b) Where a firm offers a chat to leads, each answer SHALL be a published template version chosen by a rule (the guided intake flow, a menu, a keyword, or the approved topic an AI Doer sorted the lead's question into, AIW-D107), never by a model; each template that answers a topic SHALL be mapped to that topic by the firm; a question with no topic, and any eligibility question, gets a published hand-off template and goes to a person; a template carrying the mark of AG-12-112 is never an answer to a lead; the chat surface is the team's to design. (c) No template SHALL be chosen by a model.
4. **Why.** AIW-D105 (the founder, 12 Oct 2026: "Templates must be rule-selected, with merge fields populated from verified facts or approved engine results … AI-written or edited content—including text inserted into merge fields—still requires approval before sending"); AIW-D102 ("Allow an interactive chat interface using that approved content"; *amended by AIW-D107, 12 Oct 2026:* the conversational AI for leads is in Release 1 as Option A: the AI sorts the question into an approved topic, a rule picks the published answer, booking goes through the Scheduler, everything else hands off to a person, and nothing AI-written reaches a lead unapproved). Behaviour: AW-1-137, AW-1-138 (AW-01 v0.7).
5. **Knock-ons.** CN-AG-11 change 17; AG-02 lead capture (the chat surface, a team question, journeys G-1).
