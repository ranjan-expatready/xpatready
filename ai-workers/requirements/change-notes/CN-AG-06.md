**In plain words.** Two of the founder's own decisions are older than this module and win over it (README §0 rule 11): trust money is handled "trust-aware from day one, light" (FD-17) and there are no credit notes in version 1 (FD-11). The founder kept both on 11 Oct, and on FD-17 he chose "Keep FD-17 in full" (AIW-D72): all four parts are in Release 1. This note brings AG-06 back to FD-17's words (change 1) and, as Proposals for the team, adds what makes the four parts buildable (change 6): the per-client trust ledger, fed by every path that records client money; the recorded trust-to-operating move, tied to a delivered fee milestone of the retainer or to an issued invoice, which also settles that invoice so no reminder chases money already taken; and the trust reconciliation report. One conflict goes to the founder: FD-17 counts refunds among the version 1 records, but AG-06 holds refunds to a later phase; until he answers, this note proposes the minimum, an offline refund that a person records. Until then, an invoice that has been paid cannot be cancelled in Release 1. The platform still never holds or moves money (FD-12). Two further changes give the Billing assistant a safe route for money: it may only propose; a person types the amount, picks the invoice and saves in AG-06's own screens; the bookkeeper sees the candidate invoices and that a payment notice is linked, never the message itself. No assistant creates even a draft invoice, posts a trust entry, sets a designation or computes a balance, and for trust money it keeps no list: it only reminds the person who reconciles.

**Terms used.** *AI assistant*: an assistant working for one named person under that person's delegation (AW-01; FD-16). *Proposed match / proposed invoice*: an approval item that, when approved, only opens AG-06's own screen (CN-AG-11 change 6). *Funds designation*: whether a client-money record is trust money or operating money, using the codes the firm's Practice Pack supplies. *Trust ledger*: the running record, per client, of money held in trust. *Recorded bank position*: the trust bank account balance at a date, as a person typed it. *Responsible practice unit*: the office whose regulator's rules govern a record (FD-22 A22-6.1). *Processor's payment notice*: the signed message the firm's card processor sends when an online payment succeeds (a webhook, AG-6-16); the *missed-webhook poll* asks the processor for notices that never arrived. *Fee milestone*: a step in the retainer's fee schedule at which a fee becomes due (for example a milestone draw). *Offline refund*: money the firm returns to a client by its own bank or cheque, recorded in the product; the product never sends it.
