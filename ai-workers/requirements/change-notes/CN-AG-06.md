**In plain words.** Two of the founder's own decisions are older than this module and win over it (README §0 rule 11): trust money is handled "Trust-aware from day one, LIGHT." (FD-17) and there are no credit notes in version 1 (FD-11). The founder kept both on 11 Oct, and on FD-17 he chose "Keep FD-17 in full" (AIW-D72): all four parts are in Release 1. This note brings AG-06 back to FD-17's own words (change 1). How the trust ledger works is the team's billing design, not Workstream 2's: this note only names the trust records the team's own vision document already names, adds the two keys that record a move and run the reconciliation (never lent to an AI teammate), states what no AI teammate may do with trust money, and lists every open accounting question for the team (change 6; legal points: unconfirmed Lead guidance; no counsel (AIW-D94)). No AI teammate creates even a draft invoice.

**Terms used.** *AI teammate*: an AI teammate working for one named person under that person's delegation (AW-01; FD-16). *Proposed match / proposed invoice*: an approval item that, when approved, only opens AG-06's own screen (CN-AG-11 change 6). *Funds designation*: whether a client-money record is trust money or operating money, using the codes the firm's Practice Pack supplies. *Trust ledger*: the running record, per client, of money held in trust. *Responsible practice unit*: the office whose regulator's rules govern a record (FD-22 A22-6.1).
