**In plain words.** The founder decided on 11 Oct that the Practice expert's first version answers four fixed questions, picked from a menu, built by rule from four sources, with at most one optional AI summary line; and that the rule-built answers belong to the product team (Workstream 1) as reports. This note adds those four reports to AG-14, with three lines every answer shows: whether the asker is licensed (for information only), whose files the answer covers, and that the answer is information for staff, not a message to a client. The answers are product features: no AI switch, assistant switch or stop turns them off; only the AI summary line has a switch. An answer that relies on a pack version whose test set has not passed says so, and gets no AI summary. Workstream 2 keeps only the optional summary line. The note also adds the firm's own AI measures, which the pilot needs, and records one open question the Firm assistant review raised about manager views.

**What the team changed on 10 Oct (v1.11).** Reports is a left-menu module on every plan; saved views, scheduled reports and export are the sellable *Advanced reports* extra (§3.12, L350 to L359). The four answers and the AI measures below are standard reports under that rule, not an extra; saving, scheduling or exporting them follows §3.12. Nothing proposed here was already made.

**Terms used.** *Holdout match rate*: when a person approves many AI values at once, a few values are hidden for the person to fill in themselves (AIW-D57); the match rate is the share of those the person filled the same as the AI had proposed. *Agreement alarm*: an alert when a person's rate of agreeing with AI proposals rises suddenly (AIW-D57). *Shadow mode*: the AI proposes in the background while a person does the work, so the two can be compared. *Test set (evaluation set)*: a fixed set of synthetic sample questions with known answers, run before a pack version or an AI change is released (AW-1-119; IMP-5).

## 1. The Practice expert menu: four fixed questions answered by rule

1. **Where.** §4, new heading after AG-14-34 (L422); new requirements **AG-14-35 to AG-14-38**; new rule **BR-17**. Nearest existing text: AG-14-17 (L394) and AG-14-22 (L401).
2. **Current words.**
   > L394: "**AG-14-17.** The Reporter SHALL provide **deadline adherence %**, the **upcoming deadline** horizon and list — with at-risk items only where the firm has switched at-risk marks on (AG-16-32) — **SLA-breach** trends, and **audit-event volume** — `firm_id`-scoped, drill-through to the case/record."
   >
   > L401: "Every metric/segment/row SHALL be **drill-through** to the underlying operational list/detail, carrying the exact filter; the target module SHALL **re-enforce its own permission + firm_id** (no escalation via drill)"
3. **Proposed words.**
   > **Practice expert menu — four fixed questions answered by rule** *(new — AIW-D39)*
   >
   > **AG-14-35 (the menu).** The Reporter SHALL offer firm staff a menu of four fixed questions, each answered by rule as a report: (1) *at risk this week, and why*; (2) *what is missing on this file*; (3) *which rule is behind this item*; (4) *what is waiting on me*. The asker SHALL pick the question from the menu and the file, client or item with the product's permission-filtered search; version 1 SHALL accept no typed question. The menu is part of the Reports module feature (§3.12).
   >
   > **AG-14-36 (sources and permissions).** Each answer SHALL be built only from four sources — Minder watches (AG-11 §3.4), deficiencies (AG-07 §3.6), Needs you items (AG-11 §3.13) and pack rule text with its section and version — each read under the asker's own permissions at the moment of reading, as a drill-through is (AG-14-22, BR-8). Each answer SHALL list its sources (record and field, or pack section and version). With no source, the answer SHALL say so and stop. Restricted fields SHALL appear as status only (present, expired, missing). *All files I may see* SHALL be offered only to holders of `agent/report:view.all`; others are answered over their own and case-team files (BR-2).
   >
   > **AG-14-37 (dates and pack version).** *This week* SHALL mean the next seven calendar days from the moment of asking, in the firm's time zone (AG-11-40; BR-6); unmet deadlines already passed SHALL show first, under *Overdue*. A question about a file SHALL be answered from the pack version pinned to that file (AG-07 BR-21); a general question from the current published version; where they differ, the answer SHALL name both. Where the firm has at-risk marks off, the answer SHALL use watch statuses and SHALL NOT say *at risk* of a person's file (AG-11-49).
   >
   > **AG-14-38 (no model in the answer).** The four answers SHALL be built without a model and SHALL show with AI switched off. They are product features: no AI switch (AG-16-35), assistant switch (AG-16-57), emergency stop or spend cap SHALL turn them off. An optional one-line AI summary under an answer belongs to the AI layer (AW-01): it needs the firm's AI switch (AG-16-35) and the summary-line switch (AG-16-57), is labelled AI-written, and is never an input to another report or another assistant.
   >
   > **BR-17 (Practice answers are built by rule).** The four answers quote the product's own records and pack text; they never compute eligibility, a score, a fee or a deadline themselves (those come from the Qualifier, the Biller and the Minder), never cross firms (BR-1) and never rank staff (AG-11-49; BR-15).
4. **Why.** AIW-D39 (the founder, 11 Oct: "Practice expert version 1: four fixed questions from a menu, answers built by rule from four sources, one optional AI summary line … The rule-built answers go to Workstream 1 as a change note to AG-14"); AIW-D54 ("the expert's answers work with AI off"; "the expert's line display-only"); AIW-D25 (answers only, sources only); `proposals.md` rows of 9 Oct (expert version 1, the four shapes and sources) and 10 Oct (Practice expert: the menu with record pickers and the seven-day window; answering from the pinned pack version); IMP-20 rules 1 to 4; IMP-21. Behaviour, not restated here: `practice-expert.md` §2.0 rules 13 to 20 and §2.1 to §2.4; §9 G-25 to G-27, G-36.
5. **Knock-ons.** AG-11 Needs you and the Minder are read, not changed; the question record and the summary line's AI run log row are AW-01's (CN-AG-11 change 7 adds the link field); AG-16 hosts the summary-line switch, the expert's only switch (CN-AG-16 change 4); AG-16-39 firm export carries the question records (CN-AG-16 change 5); the screen label is the team's (AIW-D31 keeps "Practice expert" an internal name, never shown to a client); the answer lines are change 4.

## 2. The firm's own AI measures

1. **Where.** AG-14-4 (L371, `MetricDefinition` as data); new requirement **AG-14-39**.
2. **Current words.**
   > L371: "Metrics SHALL be declared as **`MetricDefinition`** data (source rollup, aggregation, money/FX treatment, drill target); adding a metric/dashboard tile is configuration, not bespoke controller code."
3. **Proposed words.**
   > **AG-14-39 (AI measures).** The Reporter SHALL provide, as `MetricDefinition` rows read from the AI run log, approval items, audit entries (AG-11 §7; AG-13 `AuditLog`), the assistants' run records and the Practice expert's question records (AW-01 §7), the firm's own AI measures, per AI assistant, per AI Doer and per item kind: acceptance (approved unedited, edited, rejected, with the rejection reasons); agreement in shadow mode (a person's own value matched the proposal); turnaround (prepared to decided) and the age of AI items in Needs you; stale, paused and cancelled items (with the pause reason or cancel reason, AG-11-112); items per file; override rate on Reader values and Checker findings; document-type accuracy (AG-9-11; AIW-D71); catch rate (deadlines and findings the AI found that a person then acted on); reliability (provider errors, fallback to the manual path); safety (the holdout match rate and the number of agreement alarms, firm-wide only); spend against the firm's caps (the stricter of Expat Ready's platform cap, SA-12-33 as proposed in CN-SA-12, and the firm's own, AG-16-57 as proposed in CN-AG-16); time saved (labelled an assumption until measured against files done without AI); and, each month, how many AI items were approved by an Owner route, under either wording of AG-11-90 ("approved by Owner — no active holder of the approval permission"; "approved by Owner without a licensed user"; told apart by `ownerRouteWording`, AG-11-112). These measures SHALL be firm-wide figures under `agent/report:view.all` and SHALL NOT rank or name staff (AG-11-49; BR-15).
4. **Why.** IMP-29 (AI performance measures for AG-14; firm-side views follow AG-11-49: they show the firm its own AI, never rank its staff), relied on by `case-assistant.md` §9 G-19, `billing-assistant.md` §9 G-19, `firm-assistant.md` §9 G-16 and `intake-assistant.md` §9 GI-14; IMP-17(c) (the monthly Owner-route count), relied on by `case-assistant.md` §9 G-8; AIW-D71; AIW-D57 (holdout and alarm, provisional until counsel); AIW-D62 (spend-cap values are decided after the pilot); `00-step3-plan.md` "One home per rule" (a metric is data, so its home is AG-14; AW-1-121 cites this list), "Pilot safeguard values, one home" (platform caps live in SA-12-33) and "A firm's tightening, write path" (the firm's stricter caps live in AG-16). The list matches AW-1-121 and counts both Owner-route wordings (review S3c M-9, m-20).
5. **Knock-ons.** SA-09 platform analytics carries Expat Ready's own AI measures, aggregated and de-identified across firms (CN-SA-09, Super Admin half); this list stays the firm's view of its own AI; the per-person agreement-rate alarm itself is AW-01's (AW-1-33) and is not a report here.

## 4. Three lines on every Practice expert answer, and answers over an untested pack version

1. **Where.** After AG-14-38 (change 1); new requirement **AG-14-40**.
2. **Current words.** None: AG-14 v1.11 has no Practice expert answers before change 1 adds them. The nearest text is the drill-through rule (L401, quoted in change 1).
3. **Proposed words.**
   > **AG-14-40 (answer lines).** Every answer of AG-14-35 SHALL show three lines, built by rule, which show with AI switched off: (a) the licensed-or-not mark of the asker, *answered to a licensed user* or *answered to an unlicensed staff member*, read from the credential as AG-11-88 defines a licensed user, for information only and never a permission check, and stored in the question record (AW-01 §7); (b) a scope line, *your own files* or *all files you may see*, from the permission check of AG-14-36, never from the question; (c) a standing line saying it is information for staff, not a message to a client. Where an answer cites a pack version (AG-14-37) whose evaluation set has not passed (AW-1-119), the standing line SHALL say so, and no AI summary line SHALL be made over that answer (AG-14-38).
4. **Why.** `00-step3-plan.md` "The Practice expert's answer lines" (the licensed-or-not mark, the scope line, the standing line and the untested-pack rule are product safeguards on AG-14's rule-built answers, so they live in CN-AG-14; AW-01 points to them); the content follows AW-1-126 and AW-1-116, moved here by that rule; `practice-expert.md` §2.0 rules 14 and 17, and §1 precondition row 4 (the pack version the answer reads has a passed test set; IMP-5, Proposal); AIW-D39; AIW-D26 (approval is by whoever the firm's settings allow, licensed by default, so the mark informs and never gates); AIW-D70 (the test set gates the expert per pack version, synthetic samples only). **Proposal** (a Workstream 2 safeguard on a Workstream 1 report). Counsel: whether the mark and the standing line keep a staff answer on the right side of the advice boundary (counsel pack question 2).
5. **Knock-ons.** AW-1-126 and AW-1-116 point here and keep only the assistant's side (no summary line over such an answer); AG-14-36 supplies the scope; the question record keeps the mark (AW-01 §7); the evaluation result per pack version is recorded under AW-1-119.
