**In plain words.** AG-11 is the engine that runs automations and the product's small tools (Doers). The founder's decisions of 11 Oct and the Step 2 reviews found that the engine lacks a few verbs, events and approval-item kinds the assistants need, still calls the AI writing tool "Drafter" (now *Writer*), has no field that says which named person an assistant worked for, and has no way to tell that a seed already did what an assistant is about to do. This note adds exactly those. It also gives one rule for halted AI work: every halt that is not an end is *paused*, with its reason recorded. It keeps a person in front of every AI-drafted internal note and of every payment amount. AI unlocks only when Expat Ready has *published* a provider record, everywhere the module says so. And it adds one send-time check for all automatic sends: a template marked as carrying status or eligibility content is never sent to a lead by the system. A person's own message is not blocked.

**What the team changed on 10 Oct (v1.12), and what it means here.** The four AI Doers are now one sellable extra with one firm switch (AG-16-35; SA-03 v2.17 feature catalogue row 30, L345), and volumes are not plan limits (§0.2 SA-03 row, L57). This note adds no plan feature: each assistant is a firm setting under Advanced (CN-AG-16 change 4; `00-step3-plan.md`, "Assistants are firm switches, not plan features"). Nothing in this note was already made by the team.

**Terms used.** *assistant*: the AI working for a named person's role, under that person's *delegation* (a record of which assistant works for whom, with a subset of that person's permissions; AW-01 §7 `Delegation`); it has no identity and no permission of its own (FD-16; AIW-D13, D33, D36). *Writer*: the AI Doer that drafts text for a person to approve (AIW-D53); *Drafter* stays the template engine (AG-05, AG-09, AG-12). *Needs you*: the approval inbox (§3.13). *Item kind*: the value of `ApprovalItem.itemType`. *Paused*: the state of AI work halted for a reason that can be lifted; nothing sends while paused, and nothing is deleted. *Pause reason*: the value of `ApprovalItem.stopMarker`, which says why. *Published provider record*: the AI provider record Super Admin has entered, had approved by a second person and published, which it can be only with a processing region in Canada or the United States (change 19) and a passed test call (SA-8-66, CN-SA-08). *Lead*: a prospect in AG-02 who is not yet a client. *Seeded recipes*: the recipes switched on at firm provisioning; *ready-made recipes*: the five further Release 1 recipes of AG-11-106. *Idempotent*: doing the same thing twice has the effect of doing it once. *Standard Conductor envelope*: the fixed wrapper every trigger event uses (module, event type, record, firm, time). *Delimited data*: client content handed to a model fenced off as text to read, never as instructions. *Row-level security*: a database rule that lets each firm's queries see only rows carrying that firm's id.

## 20. A licensed approver for flagged AI-written client drafts (14 Oct 2026, AIW-D136)

1. **Where.** AG-11 v1.13 (snapshot `drive-snapshot-2026-10-13-1535`): a new requirement **AG-11-119** after AG-11-90 (L702); AG11-D11 (L42) keeps its definition of a licensed user.
2. **Current words.**
   > SA-06 v3.6 SA6-D14 (L39): "**No licence-based approvals; no supervised client messages.** … AI-written client text is approved by a human — any holder of the approval permission; the "who may approve AI text" setting and the licensed-approver requirement are withdrawn."
   >
   > AG-11-90 (L702): "When **no active user holds the relevant approval permission** … the item SHALL go to the **Owner**, who MAY approve it even without the permission"
3. **Proposed words.**
   - **AG-11-119 (new):** "An AI-written client draft that a rules check or the Checker flags as containing an eligibility statement, a legal opinion or a deadline promise SHALL be approved only by a holder of the sending permission for that channel who is a licensed user (AG11-D11) at the moment of approval; any other approval SHALL be refused and recorded. The item SHALL show each flagged sentence and its kind. An edit re-runs the check; when nothing is flagged, the ordinary rule applies. Where no active licensed user holds the sending permission, the flagged draft SHALL NOT be sent and SHALL NOT go to the Owner under AG-11-90; it waits, marked "Needs a licensed person", and the Owner's dashboard shows the count. Unflagged drafts follow SA6-D14 unchanged."
   - **`ApprovalItem`** (§7, as changed by changes 6, 15 and 16) adds `licensedApprovalFlags?` (a list of flag kind and sentence position) and `needsLicensedApprover` (yes or no, set by the check).
4. **Why.** AIW-D136 (the founder, 13 Oct 2026: "Yes, rules check (Recommended)"). The scenario: an unlicensed case officer holding the email key approves a draft telling a client they meet the Express Entry requirements and the firm will file before a deadline. Advice of that kind is the licensed person's job; the rule touches only AI-written text the check flags, so SA6-D14's "whoever holds the right acts alone" still governs everything else. Legal points: Unconfirmed: no counsel (AIW-D94).
5. **Knock-ons.** AW-01 AW-1-161 and AW-1-31 (pointer); tests TC-AW1-376 to 379. **Conflict stated for the team (README §0 rule 11):** SA-06 v3.6 SA6-D14 and SA6-X6 (AG11-D11, AG-11-89 follow SA6-D14); the team decides whether to keep this narrow licence rule for AI text, as it kept two other licence rules as business rules (SA6-D14: the retainer block and the lead-representative picker). The say and never-say phrase list is Expat Ready's (AW-02).
