**In plain words.** The firm's one AI switch stays locked until Expat Ready has published a provider record for a provider that processes in Canada, with no-training and no-retention terms and a test call that passed (the founder, 11 Oct); every place in this module that says "recorded" now says "published". Three settings are added for the founder's other safety decisions and the assistants: what the firm sees when AI is paused (nothing sends; on release each waiting item must be approved afresh); a pause of client-facing AI preparation while the firm has no licensed user (on by default, provisional until counsel); and, under Advanced, one switch per assistant, off until the Owner switches it on. These switches are firm settings, not plan features: the four AI Doers are one sellable extra, and each assistant is a setting inside it. The Practice expert has one switch, for its AI summary line only; its rule-built answers are product features that nothing switches off. The same screen shows, read-only, Expat Ready's values for the approve-all safeguards and the spend caps, and lets the Owner or an Admin set stricter values for the firm, held in the firm's own settings; the product always uses the stricter of the two. Spend caps can only be made stricter, at all times; they are a cost control, not a founder safeguard. One existing rule, that a setting change touches only items created after it, gets an exception: switching AI or an assistant off pauses the AI-prepared items already waiting. The firm's data export gains the AI records and the provenance view, and "Drafter" (the AI one) becomes "Writer".

**What the team changed on 10 Oct (v1.13).** Own-account rails are one sellable extra and the four AI Doers are one extra with one switch (AG-16-35; SA-03 v2.17 feature catalogue row 30, L345: "AI Doers (Reader, Sorter, Drafter, Checker — one switch, AG-16-35)"). So the per-assistant switches below are firm settings under Advanced inside that one extra, never separate plan features or catalogue rows. AG-16-35 itself still says "which Doers or volumes are plan-gated is still open (§13 Q7)" (L470); SA-03 v2.17 has settled that part (a team wording to align, not a Workstream 2 change). Nothing proposed here was already made.

**Terms used.** *Paused*: AI work halted for a reason that can be lifted; nothing sends while paused, nothing is deleted, and the reason is recorded (one of the four reasons of AG-11-112, CN-AG-11 change 6). *Published provider record*: the AI provider record Super Admin has entered, had approved by a second person and published, which it can be only with a processing region in Canada and a passed test call (SA-8-49, CN-SA-08). *Founder safeguards setting*: the platform setting, on from the first deployment, with two parts: the approve-all safeguards of AIW-D57, and AIW-D59's second Expat Ready person who confirms any account or plan change the Support assistant proposes. Each part ends only by a recorded founder decision naming that part, entered by the Platform Owner under SA-12's existing Owner-only settings pattern (SA-12-27; SA-12 L571) with the founder's decision reference recorded (SA-12-33, CN-SA-12). Only the approve-all part has values a firm sees; the second-person part governs Expat Ready's own Support assistant and has no firm value. *Spend cap*: a limit on what AI calls may cost (per firm per month, per Practice expert user per day, per assistant run; SA-12-33 (c)); a cost control, not a founder safeguard. *Stricter*: for an approve-all safeguard, a higher confidence level below which values open first, a larger share of values hidden for the approver, or a lower agreement-alarm level; for a spend cap, a lower cap. (Which direction of the alarm's time window counts as stricter is not settled: a question for the team and counsel.)
