# AI provider test: one-page script for the team's tech lead (AIW-D69)

*Written by the Lead, 11 Oct 2026; **re-cut 14 Oct 2026 for the founder's AIW-D147**: client data is stored in Canada; AI and OCR processing of client data may happen in **Canada or the United States** only, under written no-training and zero-retention terms, with client notice; never global routing (replacing AIW-D52's "only in Canada"). The product keeps the AI switch locked for every firm until a provider record is published with the provider, the processing region, the terms and a passed test call (SA-8-66, `CN-SA-08` change 2; AG-16-35; AG-11-93; AG11-D15 as changed by `CN-AG-11` change 19). This test produces the facts for that record. No test call runs before development (AIW-D139); this script is for when a key exists. Routes and facts below are from the providers' own pages read on 14 Oct 2026 (`technical-solution.md` §1); re-read them on the day, they change.*

**Rule for the test:** use made-up text only. No client data, no firm data, no real names.

## 2. For each route, record yes / no with the evidence

1. **Region.** Call the model through the route above, never a global one. Record the endpoint or profile used and the region the response or the console reports; for Bedrock, record the profile's destination regions (`GetInferenceProfile`) and confirm they are only Canadian and US regions.
2. **Capability.** Send three made-up tasks and keep the outputs: (a) read a made-up passport bio page image and return name, date of birth and expiry as a structured list; (b) draft a four-sentence plain-English email asking a client for two missing documents; (c) compare two made-up paragraphs and list the facts that differ. Note obvious failures; quality is judged later by the evaluation sets (AW-1-119, AW-1-155).
3. **No training.** Copy the provider's exact words that client data is not used to train models, the page address and the date.
4. **No retention.** Record: is request and response logging off; is prompt caching off where it stores data; is zero data retention in force. **Bedrock:** the account's retention mode is `none` in `ca-central-1` and in each US destination region (read it back from the account), Sonnet and Haiku 5.5 accept `none` (each model's `allowed_modes`, read on the bedrock-mantle endpoint of a US destination region; there is no console, and the account mode is set per region through the Bedrock Control Plane API), and AWS's written confirmation that `none` covers prompt caching and that the CSAM review (abuse-detection page; AWS Service Terms §50.12.2.1) is the only storage left; a missing listing is not a yes. **Anthropic API (named alternate):** the zero-retention agreement in force; record its written exception (flagged content "for up to 2 years", or where required by law) and confirm that Batch processing and the Files API are not used, since both sit outside zero retention. **Microsoft:** the Limited Access approval. **Google:** the abuse-monitoring exemption. Keep the provider's words and the page.
5. **Contract.** Is there a data processing agreement naming the processing regions (Canada and the US only)? Where is it?
6. **Price.** The price per million input and output tokens on the route used (regional and US-only routes usually add about 10 %; check).
7. **OCR route.** Amazon Textract in `ca-central-1` (or the route's own OCR): the region, the retention terms and the price per 1,000 pages. **By default Textract fails rules 2 and 3:** the AWS Service Terms §50.3 (last updated 13 Oct 2026) let AWS use and store Textract content "to develop and improve" the service and store it outside the region. Record that the AWS Organizations AI-services opt-out policy covers Textract before any page is sent, and AWS's written confirmation that no page image or extracted text is kept beyond the request and nothing is stored outside Canada or the US (`technical-solution.md` §1.5.2). Without it, the OCR route fails.

## 3. Pass, and what to send back

A route **passes** when items 1, 3 and 4 are all yes on the provider's own written terms and item 2 shows the three tasks work; its OCR passes when item 7 is yes on the same terms. Send back to the founder, one page per route: the endpoint or profile and regions; the three outputs; the copied terms with page addresses and dates; the price; pass or fail with the reason. The Lead turns a passing route into the provider record and adds it to the build specification as a replaceable provider; if none passes, the founder decides again (AIW-D52 as corrected by AIW-D147).
