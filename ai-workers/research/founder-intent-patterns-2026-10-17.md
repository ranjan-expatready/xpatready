## 3. The final stand for v5

The founder asked for one stand, implemented autonomously. This is it. It applies D203 and stays within recorded decisions.

1. **One page, same path, version 5.** Use v4's corrected facts as the base. Add back v2.1's depth and v1's catalogue clarity, and take v3's card style. Nothing useful is deleted. Anything removed is listed with its reason in the preservation ledger.
2. **Shape:** a cover, Part A, Part B, a shared part, then appendices grouped by part. The cover holds the two propositions, the link between them in one line, a reading guide by role, and the colour legend. Each part follows the seven beats in `pitch-plan.md` §2.
3. **Visible by default:** the seven beats of each part, the stage cards showing AI, rules and person, and the AI map (area → assistant responsibility → skills). No more than two levels of disclosure. Every appendix card carries a mini-index. The target is far fewer closed panels than v4's 46.
4. **Names:** apply `terminology-register.md` everywhere: one business name, the stable key, kind (AI or rules), function type and limits. Old names survive as aliases. The owner's co-pilot and "no client-side AI" are shown explicitly. Avatar and brand names stay parked.
5. **Catalogue:** restore the function type (Reader, Sorter, Writer, Checker, Filler or Rules) and split the old "exists" into definition status and build status. Restore Case Setup, Next Filing Planner and Document Expiry Monitor as rules entries.
6. **Shared part:** foundations, getting a firm live (migration), what the AI never does, money, the pilot scorecard, risks, decisions and open points, FAQ, glossary.
7. **Truth:** every number is labelled. No time-saved claim (D190). No invented field counts. XFA, portal terms, field inventory and data region stay Unknown.
8. **Proof before calling it done:** a content-by-content preservation ledger against v1, v2.1, v3 and v4, plus a visual browser check through the project's local preview server on desktop and phone widths, with back-links tested. Then a re-score against §2 and the separate checker.

## 4. Coaching flags for the founder

- **"Zero words" against "keep every detail".** Both are met by layers: a sharp main view, with depth one click away and indexed. That is a reading-order problem, not a reason to delete.
- **Competitor logins.** A side thread offered a competitor login to study its product. Using it may breach that product's terms. We keep to public pages only (`research/m0-v4-intent-audit-2026-10-17.md` §7). Unconfirmed: no counsel.
- **Migration is a sales promise.** The commercial plan says "client records only", but the team's spec covers far more. Settle the promise before the pitch (`pitch-plan.md` §5).
- **Speed.** Most lost time came from rewrite-then-restore cycles. The never-lose snapshot guard (`tools/snapshot.sh`) and the ledger stop that loop.
