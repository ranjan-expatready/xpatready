3. **Cost and maintainability:** use unchanged-document hashes/saved real responses; inspect provider caching and appropriately asynchronous batches with complete ledger accounting. A cheaper model or new parser is a controlled candidate, not a silent substitution for accuracy. Keep output-token spend visible.
4. **Reliability:** exercise interrupted work, retries and duplicate actions in the planned bounded comparison before adding a graph/harness. Application authorship/SME/release permissions and source state remain authoritative.

These are triggers for focused work inside the authorised next outcome, not a new mandatory platform build, additional sessions or a prerequisite to every visible demo. D275's configuration MVP remains first.

## Standing research/value decision procedure (D278)

The policy has its single home in root `AGENTS.md` under Technology decisions. Apply it at a consequential choice/new capability, a meaningful quality/cost/recovery gap or an affected handoff; do a short current-source check rather than a whole-market survey at every minor edit. Reuse this dated comparison until a relevant assumption changes. Treat “multifold value” as substantial evidenced net business benefit, not an invented universal multiplier or an unmeasured promise. A one-point accuracy change can matter when it removes a high-impact error; total correction effort and risk matter more than a marketing score.

For a candidate record: **problem and current baseline → current official source/date and maturity/licence → what code/work it removes or capability it adds → same-input comparison and success measures → integration/migration/maintenance/data/cost consequences → adopt / bounded benchmark / defer, with the reason and reconsideration trigger.** Clearly label inspected, executed, maker-reported and unverified evidence. No unsupported comparative score or performance multiplier.

Run only a necessary bounded experiment on the same permitted documents, configuration and independent expected answers. Prefer offline/replay for orchestration; preserve original outputs, vary one concern and record actual prompt/model/package/data versions. Measure exact critical facts, person/meaning/period, unsupported confident answers, retrieval recall/coverage, evidence linkage, missed/false conflicts, review/correction effort, latency and total operating/integration cost as applicable. A schema-valid response or two models agreeing is not verification. No new cloud/model/data route, installation, spend, hosted tracing or real-client input is authorised by research.

Keep the current solution if benefit is not established. A genuine capability/security/compatibility requirement can justify a targeted change without pretending it has a measured speed multiplier. Routine fixes stay autonomous; consequential changes to founder decisions and existing reserved approvals still go to the founder. Research does not create another reviewer or permit a contributor to approve its own changes. Legal: Unconfirmed: no counsel (AIW-D94).
