0. The idea in five lines

  1. Expat Ready is built for people: every action has a permission, every message has a template or an approval, every rule comes from a published Country Pack (the rulebook for a destination country; FD-22, Founder Decisions Log v1.5).
  2. An AI persona is an assistant to one of those people. It does the same work, through the same product, under that person's own permissions, and it stops where a named person approves (AIW-D13, AIW-D26).
  3. There is no AI user. FD-16 (Founder Decisions Log v1.5): no AI principal in the identity spine, no autonomous permissions, and Marketing may say 'AI-assisted'; it may not say the AI decides. The team's Product Vision says the same in four words: AI assists; humans decide (Product Vision v1.0 (team), section 00).
  4. Five personas serve the firm; two serve Expat Ready's own team. Together they carry all 28 workers in the catalogue.
  5. Nothing is built yet. Every "hours saved" figure is an Assumption until the pilot (AIW-A6); every price is an Assumption until the Step 5 conversations.

Four facts that hold for every persona

  • Works under one named person's delegation (IMP-1, Proposal; assumptions AIW-A2): the record says which worker, for which person, which subset of that person's permissions, on which files, from when until when. It takes no seat (AIW-A7). The person can end it at any time.
  • Stops at a named person's approval (AIW-D26): whoever the firm's own settings allow for that role, licensed by default (AG11-D11; AG-16 v1.12 §3.9). We never claim product-enforced licensed sign-off (README §5).
  • Is a recipe, not a model with hands (IMP-10, Proposal): the product's automation engine runs it as an ordered list of product actions; the AI inside proposes values, never chooses actions (AG-11-94).
  • Is an "assistant to the role", never a user (AIW-D27; FD-16).

2.1 Case assistant

assistant to the Case Officer · catalogue W-25 "AI Case Processor"
Job
Prepares every case officer's files from retainer signed to ready for review, reads what arrives, drafts what goes out, and hands the officer and the reviewer a file where every value shows its source.
What it does
  1. Builds the file from envelope.signed to a readiness report: chases documents, reads them, proposes questionnaire values, cross-checks facts, keeps the checklist current (W-01; AG-07 v2.7 §3.4, AG-08 v1.7 §3.8, AG-09 v1.8 §3.8–3.9; recipes AG-11 v1.11 §3.5).
  2. Checks each upload on arrival and drafts the accept or reject reason in the client's language (W-04; AG-09 v1.8 §3.8–3.9; Doc4 v1.3 CP-4 §3.5).
  3. Files every inbound email and WhatsApp on the right record and hands attachments to the Reader for typing (W-07; AG-10 v1.9 §3.2, §3.5; IMP-12).
  4. When a government letter arrives, proposes the milestone, the deadline, the task, a client notice and a response plan (W-03; AG-07 v2.7 §3.7).
  5. Drafts the firm's free-text client words in the client's language, and the French of non-legal template text (W-08; AG-10 v1.9 §3.3; AG-12 v1.2 §3.6; SA-05 v2.2 §3.5).
  6. Assembles the form set and fills every box from verified values only, flags what is unmapped or unverified, routes four-eyes (W-05; AG-07 v2.7 §3.5; SA-01B v1.2 §3.5). AI stays out of the fill path by rule (SA1B-D16; IMP-25).
  7. Drafts letters of explanation, submission letters and procedural-fairness responses with a "facts used" footer (W-06; AG-10-15; SA-01B v1.2 §3.9).
  8. On a corporate (LMIA) file: reads advertising proof, builds the candidate log from staff entries, proposes the duties comparison, drafts the recruitment report and prepares the inspection-pack list (W-13; AG-17 v0.5 §3.4–3.8). Never screens or ranks candidates (FD-7).
  9. Writes an internal pickup summary per file, a leaver's handover list and each person's ranked day (W-17; AG-13 v1.12 §3.4; AG-07 v2.7 §3.8).
Never does
The hard limits (IMP-27) and the blocked list (IMP-23), both Proposal for Ranjan to ratify (catalogue G16): never deletes, never makes a billing, payment, refund or trust entry, never exports, never changes users, roles, settings or switches, never signs, never holds a professional approval, never moves a case stage, never creates a lead from an unknown number, never sends free text to a client, never edits a delegation. Specific: never submits anything to a government authority (AG-11-54; G5); never verifies a value (AG-09 v1.8 §3.9; approving a Reader value is the verification and needs agent/sqr:verify, AG11-D10); never fills a form with AI (SA1B-D16); never types into a government portal until counsel answers IMP-40; worker-sent client messages use firm-confirmed templates only, and a template the firm marks as carrying status content defaults to "ask me first" (G17; IMP-28(c)).
Who approves
Readiness report and internal review: agent/case:review-approve (AG-07 v2.7 §3.6; Owner and Case Manager by default, SA6-D2). Values: agent/sqr:verify (AG11-D11(e)). Upload verdicts: agent/case:document-manage. Filed messages: agent/communication:resolve-unmatched. Forms: agent/case:form-approve (AG11-D8). Recorded decisions: agent/case:record-decision. Any client-facing text: the firm's "Who may approve AI-written client text" setting (AG-16 v1.12 §3.9; AG11-D11(a)); template chases at the recipe's autonomy setting (AG-16-36). Template French: agent/template:publish (AG-12 v1.2 §3.7). Internal summaries: the reader, no approval. Four-eyes: the officer the persona worked for counts as the preparer, so neither the persona nor that officer approves what it prepared (IMP-1, Proposal; AG-7-65). Per AIW-D26 the firm's role settings decide who the person is.
Where it sits
Beside the Case Officer (SA-06 v3.1 §3.3), one named officer per delegation (IMP-22 rule 6: several officers means several delegations, never a shared "AI case officer").
Bundles
W-01, W-03, W-04, W-05, W-06, W-07, W-08, W-13, W-17. The catalogue's W-25 bundle is W-01, W-03, W-04, W-07, W-08, W-17; this card adds W-05, W-06 and W-13 (Proposal) because the Product Vision's Case Officer persona includes "form-filling" and "corporate recruitment campaigns" (Product Vision v1.0 (team), section 06) and W-06's letters are prepared from the same case data for the representative who approves them. 2.2 Intake assistant

assistant to the intake role · catalogue W-26 "AI Intake Clerk"
Job
Answers every enquiry fast with a consented, eligibility-grounded message, asks only what is missing, books the consultation and hands the host a complete profile and a drafted proposal.
What it does
  1. Answers an enquiry in minutes with a consented message, books the consult, recovers no-shows and cold leads, tells the rep the next move (W-10; AG-02 v1.9 §3.6–3.7, §3.13; AG-04 v1.5 §3.8).
  2. Lists missing facts, drafts targeted questions, and proposes questionnaire answers from a client's free-text reply, for staff to approve (W-09; AG-03 v1.4 §3.1; AG-08 v1.7 §3.4, §3.11).
  3. With consent, transcribes or reads the consult and drafts the consult record and proposal scope for the host (W-11; AG-04 v1.5 §3.12; AG-05 v1.9 §3.1–3.3).
  4. Calls on the Case assistant's inbox filing (W-07) for lead messages; W-07 is counted once, under the Case assistant.
Never does
The hard limits and blocked list (IMP-27, IMP-23; G16). Specific: never converts a lead (AG-2-47); never creates a lead from an unknown number (AG-10-58; AG11-D18(b)); never overrides, reviews or shares an eligibility result (AG-03 v1.4 §10); never sends a marketing message without express consent checked at send time (AG-11 v1.11 §3.10; AG-02 BR-9; G8); never promises an outcome (AG-02 BR-10; G13); never records a consult without the consent gate (AG-02 v1.9 §3.12).
Who approves

Where it sits

Bundles
W-09, W-10, W-11 (W-07 shared, counted under the Case assistant).

Assumption. Unknown volume; no source gives enquiries per week for the beachhead firm (FD-19: solo and small, 1 to 5 people). 2.4 Firm assistant

assistant to the Owner and the Case Manager · not a catalogue twin
Identity
Proposal: a new grouping. It bundles the five firm-wide workers whose "Works for" line names the Owner, the Admin or the Case Manager, so that oversight, renewals, migration, privacy requests and the weekly numbers sit with one assistant to the people who run the firm.
Job
Watches every deadline on every file, finds each client's next immigration moment, brings an old practice's files across, keeps privacy requests on their legal clock and explains the week's numbers, all as drafts and lists for the Owner or Case Manager to act on.
What it does
  1. Watches every government, document and status deadline on every file; nudges the right person with a firm-confirmed template; escalates when the firm chose that; flags a slipping date (W-02 "Deadline Guardian", the hook; AG-11 v1.11 §3.4 Minder; AG-07 v2.7 §3.3; Doc4 v1.3 CP-4 §3.6; escalation and at-risk marks are Team safeguards parts, AG-16 v1.12 §3.8, §3.10).
  2. Computes each client's next immigration moment from pack rules (permit expiry, PR card, citizenship, residency obligation), drafts the consented re-engagement, and opens an opportunity only when a person clicks (W-14; AG-02 v1.9 §3.10–3.11; AG-07 v2.7 §3.7, §3.10).
  3. Proposes column, value and program mappings for a migrated practice, pulls key dates from legacy notes, files old documents and hands the Owner a dry-run (W-15; AG-18 v1.2 §3.2, §3.5, §3.6).
  4. Logs each access, correction, erasure or consent-withdrawal request on its clock, prepares an internal draft of the response and a checklist of what the law stops, reminds before the deadline (W-16; Doc4 v1.3 CP-8 §3.2, §3.5, §3.6; SA-11 v2.7 §3.8).

The hard limits and blocked list (IMP-27, IMP-23). Specific: never imports (a person confirms after the dry-run, AG-18 v1.2 BR-1; AG-18-49); never runs the privacy export or the deletion (Owner-only, SA-06 v3.1 §3.3; catalogue W-16); never exports a report (AG-14 BR-12; G16); never converts a re-engagement into an opportunity (AG-2-47); never sends a re-engagement without consent (CASL class open, proposals.md lifecycle row); never computes a deadline itself: dates come from the Minder and the pack (G10).

Each extracted deadline: the file owner (AG-9-51); template reminders: the recipe's autonomy setting (AG-16-36), status-content templates defaulting to "ask me first" (IMP-28(c)); escalation: Team safeguards (AG-16 v1.12 §3.10, Owner only to switch off); re-engagement text: the AI-text setting, and the Owner switches the recipe on (catalogue W-14); migration: the Owner's attestation and confirm (AG-18-49); privacy requests: the Owner (export is one of the five Owner-only actions, SA-06 v3.1 §3.3); the weekly note: the schedule owner (catalogue W-18).

Beside the Owner (and Admin) and the Case Manager, one named person per delegation; reminders reach the file owners, oversight stays with the managers.
Bundles
W-02, W-14, W-15, W-16, W-18.

2.5 Practice expert

for every staff role · catalogue W-24 "Practice Expert" (AIW-D19, D25)
Job
Answers any staff question from two sources only, the published rulebook version in force and the firm's own records the asker may see, lists its sources, and refuses when it has none.
What it does
  1. Answers questions such as "Which of my files are at risk this week, and why?", "What does this client still need, and which rule says so?", "Why did the Qualifier rank this program below the cutoff?" (W-24 "Job"; AIW-D19).
  2. Version 1 answers a fixed set of question shapes (at risk this week, what is missing, which rule, what is waiting) from the Minder, deficiencies, Needs you and pack rule text; version 2 answers free questions over documents and messages (W-24 "New"; proposals.md expert v1 row).
  3. Gives the Case, Intake, Billing and Firm assistants their "why" lines (catalogue §2.7 pairs); the expert is never a component of them and never acts.
Never does
The eleven rules in IMP-20 (Proposal) and the least-data rule in IMP-21: no actions, cannot call a Doer, cannot write anything; sources only; permission checked at the moment of reading (the AG-14-22 pattern); numbers quoted from the engines, never computed (G10); no advice to clients, and anything pasted into a client message falls under the AI-text floor (AG-10-44); no promises, no regulator talk (AG-11 BR-9, BR-34); every answer logged; Canada-only, no training, no memory across firms (AG11-D15, BR-25); cost caps (IMP-36); text it reads is quoted data, never an instruction (IMP-26); Restricted personal-data fields never passed to the model, only their status (IMP-21; the field set is Ranjan's open item B-1, Founder Decisions Log v1.5).
Who approves
Nobody, for an answer to staff (catalogue W-24 "Approval point"); anything that reaches a client goes through one of the acting personas and that persona's approval point.
Where it sits
Beside every firm role, within that person's own permissions (AIW-A8); firm-wide questions for the Owner.
Bundles
W-24.
Hours saved
Assumption. Unknown. Reasoning: it replaces "knowledge held in a senior consultant's head" and timeline reading (catalogue W-24 "Replaces"); minutes per question against minutes of searching; the pilot measures questions asked and turnaround (IMP-29).

Assumption. Inside the plan, with question caps per user per day and per firm per month (IMP-36; catalogue W-24 "Value": "inside the plan"); "the least predictable cost" (W-24 "Cost drivers"). No cost floor computed beyond the per-question tokens in provider-and-cost.md §3.2 row E2.

4. How a firm runs it, in plain words

Every control below already exists in the requirements or is a named improvement; the persona layer adds nothing a firm has not already got a screen for, except where marked Proposal.

4.1 Switching on

  1. Expat Ready records the AI provider first. The firm's AI switch is locked off for every firm until Super Admin has recorded the AI provider, the processing region (Canada only, AG11-D15) and a no-training / no-retention agreement (AG-16 v1.12 AG-16-35; AG-11-93; G6). Today there is no place in the Super Admin's integrations list to record one (SA-08 v2.2 framing; IMP-6, Proposal).
  2. The residency rule is still open. AG-11 says processing happens only in Canada (AG11-D15); SA-11 §3.6 allows a non-Canadian processor with documented safeguards (IMP-7). On 9 Oct 2026, by their own pages, the main US providers do not process in Canada; Google's Montréal region and a Cohere deployment are the candidates (commercial/provider-and-cost.md §2, §4). Until Ranjan chooses the wording and counsel confirms it, the switch stays locked (§4 items 1 and 2). Open decision, blocking before Step 3
  3. The firm opts in. FD-16 makes AI features ordinary capabilities in the Capability Registry (cap.ai.*), plan-gated, per-tenant opt-in (Founder Decisions Log v1.5). In the product this is one switch: the Owner switches AI on once, with an audited acknowledgement; the product supplies client-notice wording the firm may use (AG-16 v1.12 §3.9). The notice wording does not exist yet (IMP-11, for counsel).
  4. Each persona is its own item on the plan. Proposal: the four AI Doers as one product-map item, each persona as its own item, the expert as its own, each off on existing plans until added (IMP-9; SA-3-31, SA-3-38). SA-12 names no AI flag today; the per-persona switch and the platform emergency stop are IMP-8 (Proposal) on top of the generic kill switch in SA-12 v2.7 §3.4.
  5. A delegation is granted. The person the persona will work for accepts it; the Owner may set it up but it takes effect only when the person accepts it (IMP-1 rule 1, Proposal). One delegation per person per persona (rule 2).
  6. Rehearsal first. Every persona is tried in rehearsal mode (AG-11-29 dry-run: shows what it would do, does nothing) before it is enabled (IMP-10). Shadow mode shows proposals beside the person's own entry until the agreement rate earns trust (IMP-30, Proposal).

4.2 Setting how much each persona may do on its own, per action

  • Every action a persona takes carries an autonomy level the firm sees as "Send automatically / Ask me first / Off" (AG-11-51; Overview v1.11 "Autonomy levels and product floors").
  • Defaults: internal actions (create a task, file a message, assign) run automatically; template messages run automatically once the firm has confirmed "Review your standard messages"; every other client-facing message asks first (AG-11-52; AG11-D12).
  • Floors the firm cannot lower, shown as locked rows with a plain reason on the settings screen (AG-16-36): AI-written text sent to a client; a template whose body AI edited; any government form; any value written into the verified profile nobody has looked at (AG-11-53; G2). The engine re-checks the floor at run time (AG-11 v1.11 §3.10 "Autonomy floor").
  • For personas, two more floors are proposed: a template the firm marks as carrying status or eligibility content defaults to "ask me first" when a persona sends it (IMP-28(c)), and any persona free text is "ask me first" (IMP-28(b)).
  • Who approves AI-written client text is a firm setting under Advanced: By default the approver must be a licensed user … the Owner may allow other roles (AG-16 v1.12 §3.9; AG11-D11(a)). Per AIW-D26 this setting decides who approves a persona's client text.
  • Team safeguards (one switch, four parts: four-eyes on forms, Minder escalation, at-risk marks, routing of unlicensed staff's items to licensed users) turns on at 5 active staff; only the Owner switches it off, with a recorded reason (AG-16 v1.12 §3.10; Overview v1.11 "Easy for solo, safe for teams"). Supervised client messages is a separate setting, on as soon as the firm has an unlicensed user who can message clients (AG-16 v1.12 §3.11).

4.3 The approvals queue

  • Anything a persona prepares at "Ask me first" waits in Needs you, the staff home screen, followed by a Done for you feed of what the Doers did (AG-11 v1.11 §3.13; AG11-D7; AG-11-64). If the approver is the person acting, they approve inline and no item is created (AG-11-60).
  • Each item shows what will happen … why … the provenance of every value and the confidence of any AI output, its age and its due time (AG-11-56). The approver can approve, edit then approve, or reject with a reason (AG-11-57). An item whose record changed is flagged stale and the server refuses an approval of a rendering the approver did not see (AG-11-61).
  • Items needing a professional approval go to every active holder at once, licensed holders marked "preferred"; if nobody holds the permission, the Owner, recorded as such (AG-11-55; AG11-D11(d)). A backup approver catches items whose approver is away (AG-11 v1.11 §3.13 "Backup approver").
  • The approval record, after AIW-D26 (IMP-17(a), Proposal): approver name; whether the approver was linked to a regulator credential at that moment; the firm's setting in force; the Owner-route wording if used. A monthly count of Owner-route approvals (IMP-17(c)). One proposed exception for Ranjan: pause client-facing preparation while the firm has no active licensed user, on by default (IMP-17(b); decision 7).
  • One item per file, not per value for the Case assistant's readiness report, with approve-all, approve-some and reject-with-reason per line (IMP-34, Proposal), so a reviewer can clear a file in minutes.
  • Nothing executes because time passed: A pending item SHALL never execute because time passed (AG-11-59; G3). Overdue after 2 working days by default; due at least 2 working days before any government deadline (AG-11-59, AG-11-100).

4.4 The audit view

  • The firm's audit log is immutable, append-only, tamper-evident, readable firm-wide by holders of agent/audit:read (Owner and Admin by default) in the entity audit panel and the firm audit viewer (AG-13 v1.12 §3.5; SA6-D11). Expat Ready's Super Admin reads across firms with system/audit-log:list, with date-range queries and chain-verified exports (SA-11 v2.7 §3.3; SA-11-8, SA-11-9).
  • Every decision in Needs you is audited: who, when, the draft before and after any edit, and the rejection reason (AG-11-62). Every model call is logged with Doer, model, prompt version, firm, record, time and outcome (AG-11-72).
  • What the persona layer adds (Proposal): each audit entry says whether a person acted directly, a persona acted for them, or a plain automation ran, and which delegation (IMP-1); one AI provenance view per file lists every AI-prepared item, its run, its sources, the approver and the decision (IMP-2); the AI run log becomes the single authoritative record with a retention that matches the record it touched, not one year (IMP-4; values for counsel). The firm-data export already includes the Needs-you approval history (AG-16-39); IMP-2(d) adds the AI run log to it.
  • "Tamper-evident" holds while the hash chain stays in Release 1 (IMP-39; assumptions AIW-A9).

4.5 Spend caps

  • Today: a cap on how many AI jobs one firm runs at once and caps on automated messages (AG-11 v1.11 §11; AG-11-38; BR-11). No cap on model spend.
  • Proposal (IMP-36): per firm per month (a warning at 80 %, a hard stop at 100 %, then the manual path), per expert user per day, per persona run (a runaway run is stopped and parked for a manager). Caps are plan limit rows (IMP-9). The high-case cost per prepared file (CAD 14.38 mid-tier) is the number the caps are set from (provider-and-cost.md §4 item 6).

4.6 Switching off and the emergency stop

  • The firm switches off: The firm can switch AI off again (AG-11 v1.11 §3.10); with AI off every path still works by hand (AG-11-71; AG-16 BR-18). A setting change SHALL apply only to items created after the change; an item already waiting in Needs you SHALL NOT be sent or executed because of the change (AG-16-37).
  • A person ends a delegation at any time; it also ends by itself when the person is deactivated or suspended, when the firm's AI switch goes off, or when the platform emergency stop trips (IMP-1 rule 4, Proposal). A persona run stops when the client is paused, the retainer ends, the case closes or a person stops it (IMP-35, Proposal).
  • Expat Ready's emergency stop: the platform kill switch exists as a generic control with a mandatory reason that pauses in-flight feature work (SA-12 v2.7 SA-12-12, §3.4); a firm cannot switch on what the platform has switched off (AG-16 v1.12 §3.9). Proposal (IMP-8): one stop for all four AI Doers and one per persona; tripping one pauses queued AI work and ends running delegations at the next safe point, never in the middle of a send; items already in Needs you stay visible and approvable, marked "AI paused by Expat Ready on <date>: <reason>"; a separate audited "withdraw AI proposals" action exists for a safety recall; the Owner sees a banner.

8. Glossary (plain words; the product's own terms where they exist)

AI personaThis document's unit: an assistant to one human role, made of several catalogue workers (duties), working under that role's delegation. Not a user (FD-16).
AI WorkerThe workstream's name for one job-shaped bundle of Doers and recipes that does a whole job a person does today, on behalf of a named person, stopping at a human sign-off (ws2-improvements.md glossary). The catalogue has 28 (W-01 to W-28).
DoerOne of the product's own single-job tools: the Mailer sends, the Reader reads documents, the Minder watches dates, the Filler fills forms (AG-11 v1.11 §3.0, §3.3). Four are AI Doers: Reader, Sorter, Drafter, Checker (AG11-D4).
ConductorThe engine that runs Doers when something happens (AG-11 v1.11 §3.0).
RecipeA ready-made automation: when this happens, check these things, then do these actions in order (AG-11 v1.11 §3.5). Every persona is specified as a recipe (IMP-10).
Needs you / Done for youThe staff home screen: the queue of drafts waiting for a person, then the feed of what the Doers did (AG-11 v1.11 §3.13).
Autonomy level · FloorPer action, "Send automatically / Ask me first / Off" (AG-11-51). A floor is an action that can never be set to "Send automatically" (AG-11-53).
DelegationThe record that says which persona works for which named person, with which subset of that person's permissions, on which files, from when until when (IMP-1, Proposal).
Verified profileThe client's facts a person has checked against a document (AG-08). Approving a Reader value in its review view is the verification (AG11-D10).
Readiness reportThe Case assistant's one-item summary of a file: every value with its source and confidence, every open deficiency, every message sent (catalogue W-01 "New (a)"; IMP-34).
Prepared fileOne Case assistant run on one case whose readiness report a person approved (IMP-18, Proposal). The unit of price-card option (a).
Country Pack · Practice PackThe rulebook for the destination country (programs, forms, checklists, deadlines) and the rulebook for the firm's own regulator (who may advise, trust money, retention, privacy) (FD-22, Founder Decisions Log v1.5).
MinderThe Doer that registers and watches dates and runs deadline cascades (AG-11 v1.11 §3.4). "Specified, not built" today (AG-11-47).
Team safeguardsOne firm switch with four parts for firms of five or more staff (AG-16 v1.12 §3.10).
Emergency stop (kill switch)Expat Ready's platform-wide off switch with a mandatory reason (SA-12 v2.7 §3.4); the AI-specific stops are IMP-8 (Proposal).
