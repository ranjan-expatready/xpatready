0. The idea in five lines

  1. Expat Ready is built for people: every action has a permission, every automated message has a template or an approval (a person's own hand-written message is a normal send, AG-11 v1.11 §3.3), every rule comes from a published Country Pack (the rulebook for a destination country; FD-22, Founder Decisions Log v1.5).
  2. An AI persona is an assistant to one of those people. It does the same work, through the same product, under that person's own permissions, and it stops where a named person approves (AIW-D13, AIW-D26).
  3. There is no AI user. FD-16 (Founder Decisions Log v1.5): no AI principal in the identity spine, no autonomous permissions, and Marketing may say 'AI-assisted'; it may not say the AI decides. The team's own Product Vision says the same in four words: AI assists; humans decide (Product Vision v1.0 (team), section 00).
  4. Five personas serve the firm; two serve Expat Ready's own team. Together they carry all 28 workers in the catalogue. The free Deadline watch, the hook (the free duty that gets a firm to switch AI on; the wedge is the first paid one, the File Builder; AIW-D17), is shown on its own in the chart.
  5. Nothing is built yet. Every "hours saved" figure is an Assumption until the pilot (AIW-A6); every price is an Assumption until the Step 5 conversations.

1.1 How to read it

Each persona is drawn beside the human role it assists, never above it or in its place. Two charts follow: first a solo firm, the beachhead buyer (FD-19: Solo and small RCIC practices, 1–5 people, Founder Decisions Log v1.5); then a firm with a team. Three sentences hold for both:

  • No persona ever approves anything; the approver is always the person in the left column.
  • A named person is answerable for every step, and the record shows it (IMP-1: Regulator: a named person is answerable for every step, and the record shows it).

The human role names are the firm's own (SA-06 v3.1 §3.3: the six starter roles seeded in every firm; AG-01 v1.16 §2 and §3.3; Product Vision v1.0 (team), section 06 "Actors & Personas"). 1.5 Four facts that hold for every persona

  • Works under one named person's delegation (IMP-1, Proposal; assumptions AIW-A2): the record says which worker, for which person, which subset of that person's permissions, on which files, from when until when. It takes no seat (AIW-A7). The person can end it at any time. A named person is answerable for every step, and the record shows it (IMP-1). Where one persona serves two roles (the Firm assistant), the Owner and the Case Manager each grant their own delegation; nothing is shared between them (IMP-1 rule 2).
  • Stops at a named person's approval (AIW-D26): the approver is whoever the firm's own settings allow for that role, licensed by default (AG11-D11; AG-16 v1.12 §3.9). We never claim product-enforced licensed sign-off (README §5). No persona ever approves anything.
  • Is a recipe, not a model with hands (IMP-10, Proposal): the product's own automation engine runs it as an ordered list of product actions; the AI inside proposes values, and a model never selects which action runs (IMP-10, resting on AG-11 v1.11 AG-11-94: models have no tool access).
  • Is an "assistant to the role", never a user (AIW-D27; FD-16).

2.1 Case assistant

assistant to the Case Officer · catalogue W-25 "AI Case Processor", with three duties added (W-05, W-06, W-13; see "Bundles")

In plain wordsThe case officer's own assistant. From the moment the retainer is signed it chases the client for documents, reads what arrives, fills in the questionnaire with values that each show their source, drafts the letters and forms, and hands the officer a file marked "ready for review". The officer and then the reviewer decide; the assistant never files anything with the government.

Job
Prepares every case officer's files from retainer signed to ready for review, reads what arrives, drafts what goes out, and hands the officer and the reviewer a file where every value shows its source.
What it does
  1. Builds the file from the moment the retainer is signed (the product's event envelope.signed) to a readiness report: chases documents, reads them, proposes questionnaire values, cross-checks facts, keeps the checklist current (W-01; AG-07 v2.7 §3.4, AG-08 v1.7 §3.8, AG-09 v1.8 §3.8–3.9; recipes AG-11 v1.11 §3.5).
  2. Checks each upload on arrival and drafts the accept or reject reason in the client's language (W-04; AG-09 v1.8 §3.8–3.9; Doc4 v1.3 CP-4 §3.5).
  3. Files every inbound email and WhatsApp on the right record and hands attachments to the Reader for typing (W-07; AG-10 v1.9 §3.2, §3.5; IMP-12).
  4. When a government letter arrives, proposes the milestone, the deadline, the task, a client notice and a response plan (W-03; AG-07 v2.7 §3.7).
  5. Drafts the firm's free-text client words in the client's language, and the French of non-legal template text (W-08; AG-10 v1.9 §3.3; AG-12 v1.2 §3.6; SA-05 v2.2 §3.5).
  6. Assembles the form set and fills every box from verified values only, flags what is unmapped or unverified, routes four-eyes (a second person checks the form before it goes out) (W-05; AG-07 v2.7 §3.5; SA-01B v1.2 §3.5). AI stays out of the fill path by rule (SA1B-D16; IMP-25).
  7. Drafts letters of explanation, submission letters and procedural-fairness responses (the reply to a letter in which the government states its concerns before deciding) with a "facts used" footer (W-06; AG-10-15; SA-01B v1.2 §3.9).
  8. On a corporate file (an LMIA, the federal approval an employer needs before hiring a foreign worker): reads advertising proof, builds the candidate log from staff entries, proposes the duties comparison, drafts the recruitment report and prepares the inspection-pack list (W-13; AG-17 v0.5 §3.4–3.8). Never screens or ranks candidates (FD-7).
  9. Writes an internal pickup summary per file, a leaver's handover list and each person's ranked day (W-17; AG-13 v1.12 §3.4; AG-07 v2.7 §3.8).
Never does
The hard limits (IMP-27) and the blocked list (IMP-23), both Proposal for the founder to ratify (catalogue G16): never deletes, never makes a billing, payment, refund or trust entry, never exports, never changes users, roles, settings or switches, never signs, never holds a professional approval, never moves a case stage, never creates a lead from an unknown number, never sends free text to a client, never edits a delegation. Specific: never submits anything to a government authority (AG-11 v1.11 AG-11-54; G5); never verifies a value (AG-09 v1.8 §3.9; approving a Reader value is the verification and needs agent/sqr:verify, AG11-D10); never fills a form with AI (SA1B-D16); never types into a government portal until counsel answers IMP-40; worker-sent client messages use firm-confirmed templates only, and a template the firm marks as carrying status content defaults to "ask me first" (G17; IMP-28(c)).
Who approves
The file's reviewer approves the readiness report and the internal review: the Case Manager or the Owner, whichever the firm's settings allow (both hold that permission by default). Whoever may verify a value approves the values. Client-facing text goes to whoever the firm's "Who may approve AI-written client text" setting names, a licensed person by default. Forms go to the form approver. Template chases run at the level the firm set for that recipe (its recipe autonomy setting: "Send automatically / Ask me first / Off"). The internal summaries need no approval. Where four-eyes applies, the officer the persona works for counts as the preparer, so neither the persona nor that officer approves what it prepared.

4.6 Switching off and the emergency stop

  • The firm switches off: The firm can switch AI off again (AG-11 v1.11 §3.10); with AI off every path still works by hand (AG-11 v1.11 §3.10 for the words; AG-11-71 for the rule; AG-16 BR-18). A setting change SHALL apply only to items created after the change; an item already waiting in Needs you SHALL NOT be sent or executed because of the change (AG-16-37).
  • A person ends a delegation at any time; it also ends by itself when the person is deactivated or suspended, when the firm's AI switch goes off, or when the platform emergency stop trips (IMP-1 rule 4, Proposal). A persona run stops when the client is paused, the retainer ends, the case closes or a person stops it (IMP-35, Proposal).
  • Expat Ready's emergency stop: the platform kill switch exists as a generic control with a mandatory reason that pauses in-flight feature work (SA-12 v2.7 SA-12-12, §3.4); a firm cannot switch on what the platform has switched off (AG-16 v1.12 §3.9). AI personaThis document's unit: an assistant to one human role, made of several catalogue workers (duties), working under that role's delegation. Not a user (FD-16).

DoerOne of the product's own single-job tools: the Mailer sends, the Reader reads documents, the Minder watches dates, the Filler fills forms (AG-11 v1.11 §3.0, §3.3). Four are AI Doers: Reader, Sorter, Drafter, Checker (AG11-D4).
