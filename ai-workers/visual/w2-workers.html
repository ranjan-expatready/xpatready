How every worker is governed

The key design sentence: an AI Worker is a recipe the Conductor runs from a closed menu of actions; the AI inside never holds tools and never chooses actions. It keeps your founding rule, the automation rules and the reproducibility claim true at once (IMP-10).

On behalf of
Every worker works for one named person, with a subset of that person's permissions, recorded in a delegation record. No AI identity (FD-16 kept, AIW-D13). The person may end it at any time.
Approval
Output is approved by a named person, whoever the firm's settings allow for that role, licensed by default (AIW-D26). Floors the firm cannot lower: client text, government forms, verified-profile values. Nothing runs on timeout.
Hard limits
Never submits to a government authority. Never fabricates a value. Never promises an outcome. Never exports data or writes a money entry. Never approves anything. The expert never acts. (IMP-27, proposed for your ratification.)
Record
One record joins "AI prepared this" and "this person approved it" (IMP-2, proposed). Every AI model call is logged. Each result carries the rulebook version it was computed from.
Switches
Plan includes it → firm Owner switches it on → Expat Ready can stop it instantly. AI stays off until a Canada-only, no-training provider is recorded.
Safety
Protection against instructions hidden in client documents or messages (IMP-26). Spend caps. Shadow mode first: the worker runs silently beside the staff and shows what it would have done before it does anything (direction review).

Honest limits

Nothing is built

All 28 workers are requirements and strategy. The product they run on is itself partly unbuilt (case spine, Minder, pack content). By your rule we assume it will be built; investors will ask what is live today.

No customer has seen this

Every time-saved figure is an Assumption. The per-file price is untested. Five to ten conversations with licensed consultants come before any number is fixed.

"Unsolved" is mostly "partly"

VisaFlo has announced several of these. What no rival's evidence shows: answers from a versioned, two-person-approved rulebook under the user's permissions; a stateful deadline model; lifecycle re-engagement; delegation-scoped twins; a visible value-by-value trace.

Form filling is the gap firms will name first

Every rival fills IMM forms and the IRCC portal. Ours is parked, and the built engine refuses the format IRCC's barcode forms use. The Form Preparer is "hard but worth it" and needs a decision on the technical route.
