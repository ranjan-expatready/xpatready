How AI is governed: the layers that already exist, and where an AI Worker sits

Read from top to bottom: each layer can stop the one below it. The purple layer is the only new idea. Everything else is already in your requirements.

Founding rule
FD-16: "AI assists, humans decide." No model output is ever a decision. AI is a declared, switchable capability.
Switches
Plan includes it → firm Owner switches it on → Expat Ready can kill it instantly. AI stays locked off until a Canada-only, no-training provider is recorded (AG-11-93).
AI Worker (new)
A job-shaped bundle, such as "intake and document chase" or "form preparation", that runs on behalf of a named licensed person, with only that person's permissions (AIW-D13). It uses the Doers and recipes below and cannot do anything a person in that role could not.
Conductor (brain)
Recipes: "when X happens, do Y". Each action is Send automatically / Ask me first / Off. The floors can never be lowered. Loop guard, consent check at send time, rate caps. It runs once only, with no double effects.
Doers (hands)
Rule-based Doers: Qualifier, Biller, Signer, Scheduler, Binder, Minder, Mailer, Tasker, Filler (parked). AI Doers: Reader, Sorter, Drafter, Checker, which only propose.
Rules & packs
Country Pack and Practice Pack: versioned, approved by someone other than the author, with conformance tests before publish. The engines (eligibility, scoring, checklists) are deterministic: the same input always gives the same result.
Needs you
An approval inbox showing what will happen, why, and where every value came from. Approve, edit then approve, or reject. Nothing runs on timeout. Four-eyes check on government forms.
Audit spine
Append-only, written with every action; every AI model call is logged (AG-11-72). The tamper-evident hash chain is specified, but the 5 Oct triage proposes moving it to Release 2. Gap: no single record says "AI prepared this, this person approved it".
Hard limit
ExpatReady never submits anything to a government authority on its own.
