## Part 3: AI and automation already written into the requirements

- **No mention of AI, machine learning, OCR, content extraction, auto-drafting or next-best-action anywhere in Doc4 v1.3.** I searched as I read all nine modules; none of these appear. Facts only: the words do not occur in this file.
- **Explicitly excluded by the Charter:** Charter V2 slides 1 and 4: "NOT IN RELEASE 1   AI-assisted features · Automation engine (AG-11) and its Doers · Advanced engagement scoring". Slide 1 speaker notes give the reason only as scope: "Ordinary tasks, reminders and three plain notices (consultation reminder, unsigned document chase, missing evidence nudge) stay."
- **Automation that is written in (rule-based, not AI):**
  - Smart questionnaire: "ask-only-remaining — the runtime subtracts what the store already knows" (CP-3 §1); prefill from prior cases, "freshness-gated" (CP-3 §3.3).
  - Answer-driven document checklist: "a marriage certificate becomes required only once the client answered 'married.'" (CP-4 §3.2).
  - Automatic chase that stops on payment: "auto-exit the instant the balance clears" (CP-5 §3.5).
  - Expiry watch: Minder `deadline.approaching` (CP-4 §3.6).
  - Ranked to-do: "ranks by urgency (overdue first, then nearest due, then oldest)" (CP-2 §3.3). This is the closest thing to a "next-best-action" and it is a fixed sort rule.
  - Submit triggers the Conductor: "re-score AG-3, advance case, notify firm" (CP-3 §3.7).
- **Places an AI Worker could build on (Inferred, not in the document):** the officer's document verify/reject decision and written reason (CP-4 §3.5); reply drafting in the case thread (CP-7); review of conflicts and contradictions flagged by AG-8 (CP-3 BR-9, §3.4); correction-request review (CP-8 §3.2); triage of data-rights requests against the statutory clock (CP-8 §3.6); deciding what to expose to an employer behind the employee wall (CP-9 §3.3). Each of these is client-facing or affects what the client sees, so it needs firm review.
- **Explicit limits that touch any future AI use:**
  - "no on-the-fly translation of legal text" (CP-6 §3.2); CP-6-22: "the legal document text SHALL be the localized variant AG-5 rendered".
  - "CP-2 does **not** invent outcome language" (CP-2 §3.5).
  - "Release 1 does not file electronically with the government and does not auto-fill government forms (parked by the team, SA1B-D28)" (Charter V1 md and V2 slide 1 notes).
  - Charter V2 slide 1 notes: "'Human review' means a client can ask a named person to review an automated eligibility result (FD-20)." (Not in Doc4; recorded because it governs automated results the client sees.)

## Part 5: Conflicts, gaps and surprises

1. **Minder is used by an R1 module but excluded from R1.** CP-4 §3.6 depends on "the **Minder** Doer"; Charter V2 says "Automation engine (AG-11) and its Doers" are NOT IN RELEASE 1, and the slide 1 notes say: "Founder decision needed: case deadlines and document expiry need a watcher; either keep a simple deadline and expiry reminder in scope or track deadlines by hand at launch." Messenger/Mailer (CP-4 §3.6, CP-8 §3.4) and the Conductor (CP-3 §3.7, CP-8 §3.5) are also named in Doc4 v1.3. The Charter names Messenger as an excluded Doer. Doc4 does not say what replaces them if they are out.
2. **"Employer view (read-only)" vs Doc4.** The Charter V2 label for CP-9 is "Employer view (read-only)". Doc4 CP-9 specifies upload (`portal/corporate:document-upload`), pay (`:pay`), sign (`:sign`), seat management and messaging. Which one is the R1 scope is unclear.
3. **No AI anywhere in Doc4 v1.3.** It is written as fully rule-based. The AI Worker scope (AIW-D1) has no anchor in this document. Every AI touchpoint would be new requirements.
4. **AI authorship is not modelled.** Firm replies appear "attributed to the **firm's brand**" (CP-7 §3.1). Answers record provenance in `sourceJson` (CP-3 BR-8), and messages record `sentByUserId` / `sentByClientCustomerId` (CP-7 §7). There is no field for "drafted by an AI Worker". **Inferred:** the audit trail can show which staff member sent a message but not that an AI Worker drafted it.
5. **Who translates?** The document requires every client-facing string, rejection reason, firm note and reply in the client's language (CP-4 BR-8, CP-3-25, CP-7-19). It never says who writes the French version of free-text firm content (rejection reasons, request-changes notes, replies). Charter V1 md: "Immigration content may appear in English only at launch (SA1-D2 makes French optional for pack text); screens, client messages and legal documents are English and French." **Inferred:** firm-typed French text is manual work today and a natural AI Worker task, but it must not touch legal text ("no on-the-fly translation of legal text", CP-6 §3.2).
6. **Translation of client documents is not covered.** CP-4 open question 1 lists "which require translation/notarisation" as Country-Pack content. Doc4 has no flow for getting a document translated.
7. **Firm-side review queues are referenced, not specified here.** Officer verify/reject (AG-9), conflict review (AG-8), correction requests (SA-11 CORRECTION), data-rights handling (SA-11), seat requests (AG-15), bind mismatches (AG-2). Doc4 only shows the client side. Volumes and effort are **not stated** anywhere in Doc4.
8. **Decision disclosure is open.** "whether the portal shows a refusal at all before a human contact, or holds it for firm release" [FOUNDER-CONFIRM] (CP-2 §3.5). This matters for any automated client update.
9. **Launch date differs between sources.** The Charter V1 md text says "Release 1 launches on 12 Mar 2027 (production release 23 Feb)". The Charter V2 slide 1 notes say "Release 1 launches on 28 Feb 2027 (production release 23 Feb)". Charter V2 slide 3 shows Client portal in October as "Designs signed off | Not Scoped".
10. **Doc4 is older than the module files.** Doc4 v1.3 was last updated 2026-07-03. The Agent module files it depends on (AG-15 v1.6, AG-9 v1.8, AG-10 v1.9) are dated October 2026. I did not check them, so changes to `PortalVisibilityConfig`, `ClientWorkItem` or `seatRole` may have drifted.
11. **Open architecture choice for pre-account questionnaires.** Option A vs Option B (CP-3 §3.9), with "console-side default = Option B". Under B, the second database check does not apply before activation.
12. **Multi-org employer seats are a security question, not only UX.** CP-9 OQ-2: "if `resolve_subject()` admits a row for ANY active `PartyRelationship`, a multi-org person sees a UNION of both orgs".
13. **No realtime.** "A server-pushed realtime gateway (WebSocket/SSE) is **explicitly out of foundation scope**" (CP-7 §3.3). Clients see firm changes on the next load or poll (15 s to 60 s).
14. **Data-rights coverage of AI artefacts (Inferred).** The canonical export covers "the answer store, documents, comms, cases, financials-as-recorded" (CP-8 §3.6). Anything an AI Worker stores about a client (drafts, summaries) would need to be inside this export and the erasure flow. Doc4 does not mention it.
