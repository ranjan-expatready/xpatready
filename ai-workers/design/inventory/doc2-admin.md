**Plain-word notes.**
- *Country Pack* = one country's immigration rules held as data.
- *Entitlement* = what a firm's plan lets it use.
- *Flag* = an on/off switch the Expat Ready team controls.
- *Impersonation* = an audited, time-limited session in which Expat Ready staff see a firm's console.
- *RLS* = the database rule that keeps each firm's data separate.
- *[FOUNDER-CONFIRM]* = the document's own marker for values the founder (and, where stated, counsel or the immigration expert) must supply.

**Important framing from D2's header.** The document says it is "**Content-neutral** — specs the BUILDERS/machinery; the Canada Country-Pack content is the founder's deferred deliverable." In plain words, Doc 2 specifies the tools, not the immigration facts. Every AI Worker that relies on pack data depends on content that does not yet exist.

## Part 5: Conflicts, gaps and surprises

1. **No SA-01B in Doc 2.** A search for "SA-01B" in D2 returns zero matches. The Content Pack Configurator exists only in `SA-01B-content-pack-configurator_v1.2_2026-10-05.md`, which another reader covers. Pack data that AI Workers would rely on is therefore split between D2 SA-1 and the SA-01B file.

2. **Doc 2 is older than the module files and is contradicted in places.**
   - D2 is "version 1.4 · Last updated 2026-07-03". CH2 notes say "Super Admin Console v1.4 (SA-1 to SA-4 superseded by newer module files)".
   - Contradictions with PH v2.14 §16:
     - SA-6: D2 has per-plan role sets plus push-to-firms. PH says "the same six fixed starter roles for every firm ... no per-plan role sets" and "push-to-existing-firms withdrawn v2.13 — SA6-D3".
     - SA-7: D2 has "exactly one" role per person and 15-minute / 30-minute / 8-hour timeouts. PH says "several per person" and "(60 min / 12 h)".
     - SA-2: D2 allows several countries per firm. PH says "firm record with one locked country".
     - SA-3: D2 statuses are FINAL/ACTIVE/DEPRECATED. PH says "Draft → Active → Retired".
     - SA-4: D2 is multi-currency. PH says "Two CAD prices per plan" and puts multi-currency in Phase 3.
     - SA-12: D2 derives plan flags from SA-3. PH says "derived from PlanPermission".
   - Any AI Worker requirement must cite the newer module files, not D2, for these points.

3. **Release scope is narrower than Doc 2.**
   - CH2: SA-9 and SA-10 are "launch minimum".
   - CH2: "SMS and WhatsApp", "government form auto-fill" and "single sign-on" are out.
   - PH puts SLA tracking and escalation (SA-10), the breach register (SA-11), banners and rate limits (SA-12) in Phase 3.
   - **Inferred:** much of the machinery an AI Worker could use (for example the Filler, support SLA routing, the breach workflow) will not exist at the March 2027 launch.

4. **Launch date differs between Charter copies.** CH2 notes say "Release 1 launches on 28 Feb 2027". The V1 markdown copy (`Program Charter V1 - G1 expanded.md`, 2 Oct) says "12 Mar 2027". Both are facts in the sources.

5. **"Doers" deferral is unclear against Doc 2.**
   - CH2 defers "the AG-11 automation engine and its Doers (rule-based ... Minder, Tasker and Messenger)".
   - D2 has Release 1 modules using Doers by name: the Mailer sends the owner invite and dunning emails (SA-4 §3.2, §3.5), and the Signer is configured in SA-8 (PH Phase 1 "in-house Signer settings").
   - **Inferred:** the deferral covers automation-style Doers, not every Doer. The founder needs to confirm this.

6. **No place for an AI Worker identity.**
   - Platform users must be invited by email, use a second factor, and be "attributable to a named human" (SA-7).
   - Role families are only `PLATFORM_SUBROLE | FIRM | PORTAL` (SA-6 §7).
   - The audit actor type lists only "super_admin / firm_staff / client / system" (SA-11 §3.1).
   - Support message types have no machine-drafted marker (SA-10 §6).
   - **Inferred gap:** the specs do not say how an AI Worker gets its own role, permissions and audit identity, or whether it acts as `system` or "on behalf of" a named human. See the open question below.

7. **No capability kind for AI.** Capability kinds are only DOER, SUBFUNCTION, CONDUCTOR and INFRA (CT.2). This matches the founders' June model (`research/email-evidence.md` E4: the AI copilot is "an intelligence layer", not a Doer). Switching AI Workers on per firm or plan would need either a new kind or `SUBFUNCTION` rows. **Unknown** which.

8. **No AI model provider rail.**
   - The `railType` enum is OBJECT_STORAGE, EMAIL, PAYMENT, SMS, IDENTITY, DOMAIN (SA-8 §7).
   - BR-1 rejects third-party tools ("build it as a Doer").
   - BR-16 requires every external rail to be declared with residency.
   - SA-11 flags any data leaving Canada without a safeguard.
   - **Inferred:** an external AI model would be a new rail and sub-processor, needing a Canadian-residency answer and a change to Doc 1 §6.3 / §7.7. That is a founder and architecture decision.

9. **No AI usage meter or AI feature key.**
   - The SA-3 launch caps do not include AI usage.
   - D2 says "a new metered metric is configuration, not a migration".
   - New feature keys, however, "require a code release".
   - Tier values are all [FOUNDER-CONFIRM].
   - This bears on W3 pricing (CAD 199/seat, per-firm vs per-seat still open).

10. **Pause hooks.** Any capability with work in progress must declare `pauseBehavior: NONE | HOOK`, and a kill-switch-eligible capability without one "is rejected at catalog seed" (SA-12-29). **Inferred:** AI Workers holding queued work would need a pause hook.

11. **Law-25 automated decisions are an open legal item** (SA-11 §13 Q2). CH2 also says a client can ask "a named person to review an automated eligibility result (FD-20)". Both bear directly on AI Workers: *confirm with counsel / RCIC*.

12. **Content does not exist yet.**
    - Pack values, regulator wording, retainer clauses, retention floors, RROSH criteria and DSR deadlines are all founder or counsel deliverables (SA-1 §13 CONTENT-deferred; SA-5 §13 Q1; SA-11 §13).
    - CH2 dates: 13 Nov (pack content), 11 Dec (legal wording), 15 Jan (retention and breach rules).
    - AI Workers that "operate the product" depend on this content being authored and signed off. They cannot supply it.

13. **Small document defects that affect reading status words.**
    - "FINAL" is used for the *draft* state across D2 (for example "`FINAL` freely editable, not firm-visible"; plan "FINAL → ACTIVE"). **Inferred:** a global rename of DRAFT.
    - The header reads "Status: FINAL. FINAL (2026-06-22)".

    - SA-4 has the heading "## 2. Users & Jobs-to-be-Done" twice (lines 1620 and 1637).
