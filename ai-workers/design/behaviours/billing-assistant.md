## 0. Summary: the persona in five plain lines

1. The Billing assistant is the bookkeeper's assistant. The product's own Biller already does three things on its own: it cuts the deposit invoice when the retainer is signed, it marks invoices overdue and it runs the polite reminder sequence (AG-6-32, AG-6-11, AG-6-12). The assistant covers what those do not: it finds work that was done but never invoiced, matches money that arrived by e-transfer or cheque to the right invoice, assembles the invoice the person would otherwise key by hand, drafts the one personal reminder the sequence cannot write, and lists the trust-tagged payments for the person who exports to the accountant (AG-06 v1.7 §1: the three leaks a firm bleeds from are "work that was done but never invoiced", "invoices that were sent but never chased" and "payments that landed but were never reconciled to the right client").
2. What a billing assistant that cannot post an entry is for, in the card's words: it "drafts invoices from milestones, time and government fees, proposes which payment matches which invoice, lists work that has not been billed, and drafts reminders. A person issues, records, refunds and exports; the assistant never moves money" (`../ai-organisation.md` §2.3). Its value is that nothing earned is forgotten and nothing received sits unreconciled, while every number a client sees is written into the books by a person's own click.
3. Every step is a rule over the firm's own records (open invoices, time entries, expenses, government fees, the signed fee schedule) or a draft for a person; the only model calls are the Drafter's words for a reminder and for the weekly money note (IMP-10; IMP-26 rule 1). It never invents an amount (AG-06 v1.7 §9: "never fabricate an amount").
4. The person approves at the product's own money edges: the proposed match is recorded by the person who may record a payment (`agent/payment:record`); the proposed invoice is created and issued by the person who may create and issue one (`agent/invoice:create`, `agent/invoice:issue`); a drafted reminder is approved by whoever the firm allows to approve AI-written client text, a licensed user by default, who also holds the sending key; the export is run by its holder (`agent/billing:export`); the assistant holds none of those keys (AG-06 v1.7 BR-11; section 1).
5. It works under one named Billing user's delegation, with a read-mostly subset of that person's own permissions, and every step is in the firm's audit log with the delegation's reference number, so an auditor or a regulator can be shown who invoiced, who took the payment and who exported.

### 1.1 W-12 Money Matcher (four jobs; the catalogue's "Job" line)

- **Triggers.** (a) *Payment matches:* a hand-off from the Case assistant's Inbox Filer (W-07) when the Sorter has classified an inbound message as a payment notice (the classification value is firm or pack content, AG-10-37; the hand-off is the Case assistant's Proposal, `case-assistant.md` §2.3 step 5), carrying only the values the classification structured (amount, currency, date, reference, the party a person linked); or a manual run by the Billing user who has an e-transfer or cheque in hand (AG-11-7). A bank-statement import as a source is **Unknown** in the requirements (catalogue W-12 "New"; G-5). (b) *Unbilled work:* scheduled (AG-11-5), once a week at the firm's chosen time in the firm's timezone and business hours (AG-11-40); also on `case.milestone_reached` (AG-11-4) and `invoice.paid` (AG-11-4). (c) *Reminder drafts:* manual, from the collections queue's "send reminder" (AG-6-13; "staff SHALL also send a manual reminder", AG-6-12), or by rule when the product's dunning sequence has run its last step for a balance still open (reading the enrollment's state is **Proposal**, G-3). (d) *Trust-tagged list:* scheduled monthly on the first working day, or on demand before the accountant's export.
- **What the product already does on its own (referenced, mode AUTOMATION, not metered; the Lead's call of 10 Oct applied here as for the seeds).** On `envelope.signed` the Biller "SHALL **idempotently** cut the deposit/first-milestone invoice or **activate the seeded `PaymentPlan`**" (AG-6-32); in draw mode "each installment, when due, **generates an invoice**" (AG-06 v1.7 §3.2); "A **Conductor sweep** SHALL mark installments/invoices `OVERDUE` when the due date passes with a balance" and notify Billing / Finance (AG-6-11); "A **dunning sequence** (pre-built, editable, **bilingual**, **transactional** consent class) SHALL chase overdue balances via **Conductor + Mailer/Messenger**, send in the **client's language**, and **auto-exit the instant the balance is cleared**" (AG-6-12); every recorded payment issues "a **numbered receipt** to the client (PDF + portal)" (AG-6-22); an online payment is reconciled by the processor's signed, idempotent webhook (AG-6-16, AG-6-17); the Biller emails the issued invoice with its pay link (AG-06 v1.7 §3.1 "Data flow"). The persona's run record **references** these runs so the weekly note and the lists can account for every send and every fact; a **double-run guard** keyed on the action's idempotency key (AG-11-25) and on the installment's status (`INVOICED`, AG-6-9) stops the persona proposing an invoice the Biller already cut or a reminder the sequence already sent (IMP-35; G-13).
- **Recipes it runs.** Its own *proposed payment match*, *unbilled work*, *personal reminder* and *trust-tagged list* recipes, all Proposal (IMP-10). The matching, the scan and the list are rules; only the reminder calls the Drafter (`draft_message`, AG-11-2).
- **Autonomy.** The match proposal, the unbilled list and the trust list are internal (Send automatically, `AUTO`) and change nothing: every entry they lead to is a person's act. The reminder draft is AI-written client text: Ask me first (`APPROVE`), floor (AG-11-53; AG-10-44).
- **When AI is off or the provider fails.** "With AI off, gated, or the provider unavailable, every path SHALL still work by hand" (AG-11-71). Nothing in (a), (b) or (d) uses a model, so all three keep running; the Sorter's payment-notice classification stops, so a payment notice reaches the Unmatched inbox by the deterministic matcher only (AG-10-39) and the Billing user types the match by hand in the "Reconciliation view (unmatched offline payments → invoices)" (AG-06 v1.7 §3.4); for (c) the person writes the reminder by hand, a normal manual send (AG-11-79), and the product's template dunning keeps running because it is a template, not AI (AG-6-12).

### 2.1 W-12: a payment arrives outside the portal (the proposed match)

*In plain words.* Many clients pay by e-transfer, cheque or bank transfer rather than through the pay link. Today the bookkeeper opens the bank's notice, finds the client, finds the invoice and types the payment in. The Billing assistant does the finding: when a payment notice has been filed to a client (by the product's matcher or by a person), it compares the amount, date and reference with that client's open invoices and proposes which invoice the money belongs to, with the recording screen filled in. The bookkeeper looks and clicks record; that click is the bookkeeper's own entry of the payment, and the product then issues the receipt and stops any reminders. The assistant never records a payment and never proposes more than the balance.

## 10. Glossary additions

Only terms not already in `../ai-organisation.md` §8 or `case-assistant.md` §10.

- **Biller:** the product's own billing Doer, "the **in-house Biller Doer** that issues invoices, schedules installments, collects payments (online and offline), records every cent received against the right client, and reports what was billed, what was collected, and what is outstanding" (AG-06 v1.7 §1).
- **Invoice statuses:** `DRAFT → ISSUED → PARTIALLY_PAID → PAID`, with `OVERDUE`, `CANCELLED`, `VOID` and, Phase-2, `PARTIALLY_REFUNDED`/`REFUNDED`; "derived from facts … not hand-set" (AG-6-7). **Issue:** the act that assigns the number, freezes the lines and tax and sends the invoice (AG-6-5, AG-6-6).
- **Payment fact:** the "**append-only fact**" of money received, "never edited or deleted"; a mistake is reversed by a refund fact (AG-06 v1.7 §3.0). **Offline payment:** one the staff record (e-transfer, cheque, cash, bank transfer, card terminal, AG-6-19). **Recorded on the client's behalf:** the mark on a payment the client made outside the portal (`recordedOnBehalf`, AG-6-43).
- **Line kinds:** service fee, disbursement (a cost passed through), government fee (a statutory fee, non-taxable by default), milestone draw (a scheduled payment from the signed fee schedule), time-based, discount (AG-6-3, AG-06 v1.7 §3.1).
- **Payment plan, installment, draw mode:** the client-money schedule seeded from the retainer's milestones; in draw mode each due installment generates an invoice (AG-6-9, AG-6-10). Not the firm's own subscription plan (SA-04).
- **Dunning:** the product's automated reminder sequence for an overdue balance, bilingual, transactional, stopping the instant the balance clears (AG-6-12). **Collections queue:** the list of every overdue invoice with one-click remind or record (AG-6-13).
- **Aged receivables:** what is owed to the firm, grouped by how long it has been outstanding ("current / 1–30 / 31–60 / 61–90 / 90+", AG-14 v1.10 §3.3).
- **Reporting currency and FX snapshot:** the one currency the Owner sees totals in, and the stored exchange rate captured at the economic moment so totals stay stable (AG-6-25, AG-6-26; AG-16-4).
- **Tax profile:** the tax components for a region of the firm's country, from the Country Pack, frozen on each issued invoice (AG-6-4).
- **Bill to:** the party a case's invoices go to; the case's own client by default (AG-7-52, AG-6-41).
- **Funds tag (`fundsNature`) and funds designation (`fundsDesignation`):** the optional label AG-06 v1.7 puts on a payment ("retainer/trust-intended vs earned/general", AG-6-24) and the mandatory field FD-17 requires (TRUST | OPERATING); not the same thing (A22-6.5; G-8). **Trust account:** a separate account for clients' money held before it is earned (general knowledge; confirm with counsel).
- **Accounting export:** the file of invoices, payments and refunds for the firm's bookkeeper or trust accountant (AG-6-31), run under `agent/billing:export`.
- **Payment rail:** the firm's own account with a payment processor on which clients pay online; connected by the Owner alone (`agent/payment-rail:connect`, SA-06 v3.1 §10b). **Webhook:** the processor's signed notice that a payment succeeded, which marks the invoice paid (AG-6-16, AG-6-17; general knowledge for the word).
- **Timer / Clocker:** the Doer that records billable time (AG-13 v1.12 §3.6; FD-8 names it "Clocker").
- **Separation of duties:** issuing, taking money and refunding are distinct permissions (BR-11).
- **E-transfer:** a bank-to-bank transfer by email, common in Canada (general knowledge); one of the offline payment methods (`E_TRANSFER`, AG-6-19).
- **Proposed payment match, proposed invoice:** this document's names for the two approval item kinds the persona needs (Proposal, G-1, G-2).
- **Nothing outstanding:** this document's name for the run state when a billing party has no open invoice and no unbilled work (Proposal, G-13).
