## 0. Summary: the persona in five plain lines

1. The Billing assistant is the bookkeeper's assistant. The product's own Biller already does three things on its own: it cuts the deposit invoice when the retainer is signed, it marks invoices and installments overdue and it runs the polite reminder sequence (AG-6-32, AG-6-11, AG-6-12). The assistant covers what those do not: it finds work that was done but never invoiced, shows which open invoice a payment the person is recording belongs to, assembles the invoice the person would otherwise key by hand, drafts the one personal reminder the sequence cannot write, and lists the trust-tagged payments for the person who exports to the accountant (AG-06 v1.7 §1: the three leaks a firm bleeds from are "work that was done but never invoiced", "invoices that were sent but never chased" and "payments that landed but were never reconciled to the right client"; *reconciling* means tying each payment received to the invoice it pays).
2. What a billing assistant that cannot post an entry is for, in the card's words: it "drafts invoices from milestones, time and government fees, proposes which payment matches which invoice, lists work that has not been billed, and drafts reminders. A person issues, records, refunds and exports; the assistant never moves money" (`../ai-organisation.md` §2.3). Its value is that nothing earned is forgotten and nothing received sits unreconciled, while every number a client sees is written into the books by a person's own save in the product's own screen.
3. Every step is a rule over the firm's own records (open invoices and installments, time entries, expenses, government fees, the signed fee schedule) or a draft for a person; the only model calls are the Drafter's words for a personal reminder and for the weekly money note (IMP-10; IMP-26 rule 1). It never reads an amount out of a message and never invents one (AG-06 v1.7 §9: "never fabricate an amount").
4. The person acts at the product's own money edges: a payment is recorded by the person who may record one, in the Record payment modal opened from the persona's candidates (`agent/payment:record`); an invoice is saved and issued by the person who may create and issue one, in the builder the persona opens pre-filled (`agent/invoice:create`, `agent/invoice:issue`); a drafted reminder is approved by whoever the firm allows to approve AI-written client text, a licensed user by default, who also holds the sending key; the export is run by its holder (`agent/billing:export`); the assistant holds none of those keys (AG-06 v1.7 BR-11; section 1).
5. It works under one named Billing user's delegation, with a read-mostly subset of that person's own permissions, and every step is in the firm's audit log with the delegation's reference number, so an auditor or a regulator can be shown who invoiced, who took the payment and who exported. In the worked example (section 8) it makes seven proposals and eight model calls over 44 days; twenty messages reach the client (twenty-one if the founder keeps FD-21's signing passcode), nineteen by the product's own automations and one approved draft; four receipts are issued to the portal; the persona sends nothing and records nothing.

**What stops a run (IMP-35, Proposal, with the product rules it rests on).** Two run types (the Lead's call of 10 Oct: stop rules follow the run type). *Per billing party:* the match, the unbilled scan, the proposed invoice and the reminder draft are one persona's work on one billing party (a client, a case or a prospect, AG-6-2), not on one case (section 9, G-13). Such a run stops, and any item it has waiting stays visible but cannot send, when: the client is paused (waiting items are marked "client paused" and "SHALL NOT be sendable", AG-11-101; a quiet, "Not started" client never receives a client-facing action, AG-11-87); every invoice of the party is PAID, CANCELLED or VOID and no unbilled work remains (*nothing outstanding*; the run reopens on the next event, since starting again "continues the same run rather than opening a second one", IMP-35). *Firm-wide:* the weekly money note (2.2b) and the monthly trust-tagged list (2.1d) serve the firm, not a party; client pause and *nothing outstanding* do not apply to them, and a paused client's figures still appear in them because neither sends anything to a client. *Both types* stop when a person stops them (audited, reason optional, IMP-35); when the delegation ends because the Billing user ended it, was deactivated or suspended, the firm switched AI off, or the platform emergency stop tripped (IMP-1 rule 4; AG-11-45; IMP-8); and when a spend cap is reached (IMP-36, Proposal). A setting change never sends or executes an item already waiting (AG-16-37). **The retainer ending is Unknown as an event** (`case-assistant.md` §9 G-22): billing relies on the invoice states and on client pause.

### 1.1 W-12 Money Matcher (four jobs; the catalogue's "Job" line)

**What the product already does on its own (referenced, mode AUTOMATION, not metered; the Lead's call of 10 Oct applied here as for the seeds).** On `envelope.signed` the Biller "SHALL **idempotently** cut the deposit/first-milestone invoice or **activate the seeded `PaymentPlan`**" (AG-6-32); in draw mode "each installment, when due, **generates an invoice**" (AG-06 v1.7 §3.2); "A **Conductor sweep** SHALL mark installments/invoices `OVERDUE` when the due date passes with a balance" and notify Billing / Finance (AG-6-11); "A **dunning sequence** (pre-built, editable, **bilingual**, **transactional** consent class) SHALL chase overdue balances via **Conductor + Mailer/Messenger**, send in the **client's language**, and **auto-exit the instant the balance is cleared**" (AG-6-12), configured by the holder of `agent/payment-plan:manage` ("Create/manage payment plans + installments + dunning", SA-06 v3.1 §10b, held by Billing / Finance) in the "Dunning sequence config (toggle steps, edit copy, manage per-locale variants)" screen (AG-06 v1.7 §3.2); every recorded payment issues "a **numbered receipt** to the client (PDF + portal)" (AG-6-22); an online payment is reconciled by the processor's signed, idempotent webhook (AG-6-16, AG-6-17); the Biller emails the issued invoice with its pay link (AG-06 v1.7 §3.1 "Data flow"). The persona's run record **references** these runs so the weekly note and the lists can account for every send and every fact; a **double-run guard** keyed on the action's idempotency key (AG-11-25) and on the installment's status (`INVOICED`, AG-6-9) stops the persona proposing an invoice the Biller already cut or a reminder the sequence already sent (IMP-35; G-13, Proposal).

#### 1.1a Payment matches

- **Trigger: always a person's act (the Lead's calls of 10 Oct, after reviews S2e B-1 and S2e-b N-2).** Either (i) a person links a message the Sorter classified as a payment notice (the classification value is firm or pack content, AG-10-37; G-4) to the client in the Unmatched inbox, an act under `agent/communication:resolve-unmatched` (AG-10 v1.9 §3.2; held by Owner, Admin, Case Manager and Case Officer, not by Billing / Finance, SA-06 v3.1 §10b); or (ii) the Billing user, with a payment seen in the firm's bank, opens AG-06's own "*Reconciliation view* (unmatched offline payments → invoices)" (AG-06 v1.7 §3.4 "Key screens") for the client. AG-06 names the screen but specifies nothing more of it, and records a payment only "against an issued invoice" (AG-6-19), so how the view holds a payment before it is tied to an invoice is Unknown (G-2). How either act reaches the persona is **Proposal**: the link as an entity event of the existing vocabulary (`UPDATED`, AG-11-4 "today's `CREATED/UPDATED/EDITED/DELETED/STATUS_CHANGED/ASSIGNED/ARCHIVED/RESTORED`"), whose payload carrying the link is Unknown (G-4); the Reconciliation view showing the persona's candidates on the AG-11-78 inline pattern (G-2). **An invoice already in the collections queue needs no persona:** the queue's own one-click "record payment" (AG-6-13) starts from the invoice, and the persona adds nothing. **A payment notice the matcher filed automatically starts nothing** (G-4: the first link teaches the matcher the bank's address as that client's, AG-10-12). **Never from message text:** in version 1 no model reads an amount, currency, date or reference out of a message; that capability is a later Proposal (G-31). No hand-off from the Case assistant is used (none exists, `case-assistant.md` §2.3 step 5).
- **Recipe.** The persona's *proposed payment match* recipe, Proposal (IMP-10): a rule over the ledger lists the party's open invoices and installments as candidates, oldest due first, and once the person types the amount, marks the one whose balance equals it as *exact*.
- **Autonomy.** The proposal is internal and changes nothing. On either route the person sees the candidates, types the amount from the notice or the firm's bank, sees the *exact* mark appear where a balance equals it, and picks one; the product's Record payment modal then opens pre-filled with the party, the chosen invoice (and installment) and the amount, the person completes method, date and reference and saves under `agent/payment:record`. That save is the record (the Lead's one standard).
- **When AI is off or the provider fails.** "With AI off, gated, or the provider unavailable, every path SHALL still work by hand" (AG-11-71). The match is a rule and keeps running; only the Sorter's payment-notice label stops, so payment notices reach the Unmatched inbox by the deterministic matcher alone (AG-10-39) and a person links them as today.

A step with no existing action, Doer or pack rule behind it is marked **Proposal** and listed in section 9. *Doer or rule* uses the sources' names: Biller (AG-06 v1.7 §1: "the **in-house Biller Doer**"), Mailer (`send_mail`), Reminder (`create_reminder`), Notification (`send_notification`), Drafter (the AI Doer, AG11-D4), Sorter (AG11-D4), Timer (billable time, AG-13 v1.12 §3.6), Reporter (AG-14). **Two of these names are pending the founder:** "Timer" is AG-13's name and "Reminder" a separate Doer in AG-11-2, while FD-8 (Founder Decisions Log v1.5, ratified) says "**Clocker** (time-tracking; not "Timer")" and "**Reminder folds into Minder**"; the conflict is open for the founder under IMP-24, and this document uses the module names only to point at the actions as specified today. *Proposes / writes:* the persona proposes; it never writes an invoice, a payment, a refund, an expense, a time entry or a trust record. Every model call is a plain Doer call: "no tools, output checked against a fixed expected shape, treated as untrusted" (IMP-10; the product rule is AG-11-94: "models SHALL have no tool access"); model output "can propose *values* (a date, a type, a draft), never *actions*, recipients, addresses or permissions" (IMP-26 rule 1). *Autonomy* uses the screen words with the internal name in brackets (AG-11-51). *Recorded:* the audit entry (AG-11-43; AG-6-38 for money actions), the run (AG-11-34), the AI run log entry (AG-11-72) and, Proposal, the delegation reference and the audit mode *the person acted directly* (PERSON), *a persona acted for a person* (DELEGATED), *plain automation ran* (AUTOMATION) (IMP-1, IMP-2). Plain words come first and the product's internal name follows in brackets.

### 2.1a W-12: a payment arrives outside the portal (the proposed match)

*In plain words.* Many clients pay by e-transfer, cheque or bank transfer rather than through the pay link. Today the bookkeeper sees the money in the firm's bank, finds the client, finds the invoice and types the payment in. The Billing assistant does the finding, and only after a person has said which client the money is from: either someone links the bank's notice to the client, or the bookkeeper opens the product's own reconciliation screen for that client. The assistant then lists that client's open invoices and installments, oldest first, and, once the person types the amount, marks the invoice whose balance it equals. The person picks, checks the details in the product's record-payment screen and saves; that save is the person's own entry of the payment, and the product then issues the receipt and stops any reminders. When the invoice is already overdue and sitting in the product's collections queue, the person records from there in one click and the assistant has nothing to add. The assistant never reads the amount out of the message, never records a payment and never proposes more than the balance.

## 10. Glossary additions

Only terms not already in `../ai-organisation.md` §8 or `case-assistant.md` §10.

- **Biller:** the product's own billing Doer, "the **in-house Biller Doer** that issues invoices, schedules installments, collects payments (online and offline), records every cent received against the right client, and reports what was billed, what was collected, and what is outstanding" (AG-06 v1.7 §1).
- **Reconciliation / to reconcile:** tying each payment received to the invoice it pays, so the books and the bank agree (AG-06 v1.7 §1, §3.4 "Reconciliation view"; general knowledge for the word). **Reconciliation view:** AG-06's screen for "unmatched offline payments → invoices" (§3.4), named but not otherwise specified; the bookkeeper's entry to a payment match in this document.
- **Invoice statuses:** `DRAFT → ISSUED → PARTIALLY_PAID → PAID`, with `OVERDUE`, `CANCELLED`, `VOID` and, Phase-2, `PARTIALLY_REFUNDED`/`REFUNDED`; "derived from facts … not hand-set" (AG-6-7). **Issue:** the act that assigns the number, freezes the lines and tax and sends the invoice (AG-6-5, AG-6-6).
- **Payment fact:** the "**append-only fact**" of money received, "never edited or deleted"; a mistake is reversed by a refund fact (AG-06 v1.7 §3.0). **Offline payment:** one the staff record (e-transfer, cheque, cash, bank transfer, card terminal, AG-6-19). **Recorded on the client's behalf:** the mark on a payment the client made outside the portal (`recordedOnBehalf`, AG-6-43).
- **Record payment modal:** the product's own screen where a person records an offline payment against an invoice: "method, amount ≤ balance, currency, reference, date, payer, notes" (AG-06 v1.7 §3.4). It opens pre-filled with the party, the chosen invoice and the typed amount; the person's save is the record.
- **Line kinds:** service fee, disbursement (a cost passed through), government fee (a statutory fee, non-taxable by default), milestone draw (a scheduled payment from the signed fee schedule), time-based, discount (AG-6-3, AG-06 v1.7 §3.1). **Pass-through:** money the firm paid to the government on the client's behalf and bills back at cost, with no tax and no margin (AG-06 v1.7 §1 "an IRCC fee is a pass-through, not a taxable service").
- **Deposit invoice (retainer deposit):** the first invoice the Biller cuts from the signed fee schedule when the retainer is signed (AG-6-32).
- **Payment plan, installment, draw mode, single-invoice mode:** the client-money schedule seeded from the retainer's milestones; in draw mode each due installment generates an invoice; in single-invoice mode one invoice carries scheduled partial payments (AG-6-9, AG-6-10). Not the firm's own subscription plan (SA-04).
- **Dunning:** the product's automated reminder sequence for an overdue balance, bilingual, transactional, stopping the instant the balance clears, configured under `agent/payment-plan:manage` (AG-6-12; AG-06 v1.7 §3.2). **Dunning enrollment:** one overdue balance's place in the sequence (which step is next, when, and whether it is active, completed or exited); Doc 3 v1.4's model (`DunningEnrollment`, L2677), which AG-06 v1.7 does not carry. **Collections queue:** the list of every overdue invoice with one-click remind or record (AG-6-13).
- **Aged receivables:** what is owed to the firm, grouped by how long it has been outstanding ("current / 1–30 / 31–60 / 61–90 / 90+", AG-14 v1.10 §3.3).
- **Reporting currency and FX snapshot:** the one currency the Owner sees totals in, and the stored exchange rate captured at the economic moment so totals stay stable (AG-6-25, AG-6-26; AG-16-4).
- **Tax profile:** the tax components for a region of the firm's country, from the Country Pack, frozen on each issued invoice (AG-6-4). **HST:** the harmonized sales tax charged in some provinces, Ontario among them (general knowledge; the rate in §8 is fictional and the values are the SME's).
- **Bill to:** the party a case's invoices go to; the case's own client by default (AG-7-52, AG-6-41).
- **Funds tag (`fundsNature`) and funds designation (`fundsDesignation`):** the optional label AG-06 v1.7 puts on a payment ("retainer/trust-intended vs earned/general", AG-6-24) and the mandatory field FD-17 requires (TRUST | OPERATING); not the same thing (Founder Decisions Log v1.5 A22-6.5; G-8). **Trust account:** a separate account for clients' money held before it is earned (general knowledge; confirm with counsel).
- **Accounting export:** the file of invoices, payments and refunds for the firm's bookkeeper or trust accountant (AG-6-31), run under `agent/billing:export`.
- **Payment rail:** the firm's own account with a payment processor on which clients pay online; connected by the Owner alone (`agent/payment-rail:connect`, SA-06 v3.1 §10b). **Webhook:** the processor's signed notice that a payment succeeded, which marks the invoice paid (AG-6-16, AG-6-17; general knowledge for the word).
- **Executed copy and certificate:** the signed retainer as a file, and the certificate of completion that comes with it (the document's hashes, the signer table and the audit trail), emailed to each signer (AG-5-31; AG-05 v1.9 §3.9). **Evidence bundle:** the sealed record of a signature that FD-21 requires (who signed, how they were verified, when, from where, and which exact document version), exportable for a regulator or a court (Founder Decisions Log v1.5 FD-21).
- **Address↔Contact mapping:** what the matcher learns when a person links an unmatched message: the sender's address becomes that contact's for future matching (AG-10-12).
- **Timer:** the Doer that records billable time (AG-13 v1.12 §3.6); FD-8 calls it "Clocker"; the name is pending the founder (IMP-24).
- **Separation of duties:** issuing, taking money and refunding are distinct permissions (AG-06 BR-11).
- **E-transfer:** a bank-to-bank transfer by email, common in Canada (general knowledge); one of the offline payment methods (`E_TRANSFER`, AG-6-19).
- **Proposed payment match, proposed invoice:** this document's names for the two approval item kinds the persona needs (Proposal, G-1, G-2).
- **Nothing outstanding:** this document's name for the run state when a billing party has no open invoice and no unbilled work (Proposal, G-13).
