## 0. Summary: the persona in five plain lines

1. **The free Deadline watch is the product, not this persona** (pending the founder, section 11 decision 1). Every file owner's deadline reminders, the managers' alerts where the firm switched escalation on, and the at-risk marks are the product's own Minder, on every plan, with no AI. The managers' deadline list is a product report built by rule with no model call, there whether AI is on or off. The Firm assistant adds nothing to it.
2. With AI, the Firm assistant does four smaller things. It works out each client's next immigration moment from the pack's rules and the dates already on the file, and drafts a consented message for the Owner to approve; a person reopens the opportunity (W-14).
3. When a firm brings an old practice's files across, it proposes how the old columns and values map onto the firm's fields and lists what it could not map; the Owner confirms, attests and runs the import (W-15).
4. When a client asks to see, correct or erase their data, it opens the Owner's task and reminder, lists what the law stops, and drafts an internal outline and the reply; the Owner verifies identity, exports and answers (W-16). Each week it writes the Owner and the Case Manager a plain-language note from their dashboards, quoting only numbers the product computed (W-18).
5. **Said plainly: once the hook is reclassified, this persona has little daily AI work.** Its model calls are drafts at low-frequency, high-consequence moments (a renewal, a migration, a privacy request) and one weekly note per person; the Case Manager's delegation is left with the weekly note alone (section 11 decision 2). Everything it produces is a list, a proposed value or a draft; the Owner or the Case Manager acts; every step is in the firm's audit log with the delegation's reference number (IMP-1).

### 1.1 W-02 Deadline watch (Deadline Guardian; the hook): uses the Minder

- **What it is, under the reclassification (pending the founder).** The Minder "reminds the **file owner** (the person responsible for the case) as the date nears, with no automation to switch on and no plan upgrade needed" (AG-11 v1.11 §3.4); its cascade re-evaluates the live file before each step and disarms when the date is met (AG-11-18); where escalation is on, it alerts "the firm's users who can reassign cases (`agent/case:assign`) 2 days before a standard deadline and on the day" (AG-11-19); where at-risk marks are on, "on breach the Minder SHALL mark the case **at-risk** and record an **at-risk event**" (AG-11-19); the two Release 1 recipes "biometrics countdown" and "PR card / status expiry watch" send client-facing template reminders and are on by default (AG-11-106); an orphaned deadline creates an unassigned task and notifies the users who can reassign cases: "<file> — <deadline> — no active owner. Reassign?" (AG-13-66). All of this is mode AUTOMATION, needs no AI and no persona.
- **The managers' deadline list.** A product report built by rule from the Minder's Active watches view ("every armed Minder, its next step, its at-risk status — per case and firm-wide for the manager", AG-11 v1.11 §3.4 key screens) and the compliance view (AG-14-17), read by the Owner or the Case Manager under their own keys (the compliance view under `agent/report:view-compliance`, SA-06 v3.1 L941; the Active watches view names no key, G-18), with no model call, whether AI is on or off. It is the product's own two views, as the product shows them, with no Workstream 2 filter (the Lead, 11 Oct, after re-check S2g-b N-1; the views are specified, not built, G-18).
- **The hook's one AI step** is the Reader reading deadlines from government letters (IMP-41: "Reader letter deadlines (the Guardian's only AI step)"); it belongs to the Case assistant (W-03, `case-assistant.md` §2.4), not here.
- **Trigger.** None of its own: the Minder runs on its own schedule; the list is rendered when a person opens it.
- **Autonomy.** Not applicable: no persona action.
- **When AI is off or the provider fails.** Nothing changes: the Minder and the list need no AI (AG-11-48; AG-16 BR-18). If the founder keeps the list behind the AI switch instead (section 11 decision 1, the alternative), then with AI off the delegation has ended (IMP-1 rule 4), the list stops, and the Minder's reminders continue.

### 1.2 W-14 Lifecycle Keeper

- **Trigger.** A date-field-anchored trigger (AG-11-6) on a date already recorded on the client's file, offset by the pack's lead time, for example the Canada pack field `prCardExpiry` (AG-11 v1.11 §3.12); reminders on recorded dates keep working after a case closes (AG-7-34), so a recorded date on a closed file coming within the pack's lead time starts a run. A trigger on the decision itself (`case.decided`, which AG-07 v2.7 §3.9 lists among case events) is not in AG-11-4's vocabulary: **Proposal** (G-17).
- **Recipe.** The persona's own *next moment* recipe, Proposal (the catalogue's W-14 "New" line: an off-by-default, consent-checked re-engagement recipe). The product's "PR card / status expiry watch" recipe already sends "client-facing reminders ahead of a registered expiry date" (AG-11 v1.11 §3.5) and is referenced, not repeated. Which moments exist ("permit expiry, PR card, citizenship, residency obligation", `../ai-organisation.md` §2.4) is pack content.
- **Autonomy.** The re-engagement draft is AI-written client text: Ask me first (`APPROVE`), floor (AG-11-53), approver per the firm's AI-text setting (AG-16-46). It is prepared with no person present, so it waits in Needs you (AG-11-60(a): "Drafter drafts from recipes"). The recipe is off by default (catalogue W-14) and the Owner switches it on. At send time the consent gate applies per recipient (AG-11-37; BR-8) and reads the action's consent class (`consentClass`, transactional or marketing, AG-11 v1.11 §7); this document sets the re-engagement action to marketing until counsel classes it (**Proposal**, G-27; `../../proposals.md` lifecycle row of 9 Oct). Reopening an opportunity is a person's act: a reopen "**creates a new Prospect (a new opportunity)**" (AG-02 v1.9 §3.10), under `agent/prospect:lose` ("Mark lost / **reopen**", AG-02 v1.9 §10; SA-06 v3.1 §10b); no AG-11-2 action creates a prospect.
- **When AI is off or the provider fails.** "With AI off, gated, or the provider unavailable, every path SHALL still work by hand" (AG-11-71). With AI off the delegation ends (section 1, What stops a run), so this duty does not run at all, its rule-built parts included; the product's own Minder still reminds the file owner of every recorded date (AG-11-48), and the Owner writes any message by hand (a normal manual send, AG-11-79). *Corrected 11 Oct after re-check S2g-b N-2.*

### 1.3 W-15 Migration Mapper

- **Trigger.** The Owner creates an import batch and uploads a source file (AG-18 v1.2 §3.0, §3.1), then runs the persona's recipe on demand (AG-11-7). AG-11's on-demand run is "against a chosen entity", and an import batch is not among the entities the Conductor knows (AG-11 v1.11 §3.1: `PROSPECT, CASE, CLIENT, INVOICE, DOCUMENT_CHECKLIST`), so both the on-demand run against a batch and an event when a batch reaches mapping are **Proposals** (G-17). Only under the Owner's delegation (`agent/import:view-report`).
- **Recipe.** The persona's own *mapping proposals* recipe, Proposal (the catalogue's W-15 "New" line: "AI-proposed column and value mapping", key dates from notes, and a *could not map* report). The product already groups look-alike program names and "MAY **suggest** a program for a group (exact or near match)" (AG-18-10), classifies imported documents with the Reader (AG-18-33) and registers imported key dates with the Minder (AG-18-25): all referenced, mode AUTOMATION.

**What happens when a limit is hit.** (1) A floored action set too low: the engine "SHALL re-check at run time and treat a floored action as `APPROVE`, recording why" (AG-11-53). (2) A blocked action is refused on the server (IMP-27) and the run continues ("Actions are non-fatal to each other", AG-11 v1.11 §3.3). (3) A failure is retried with a growing wait between tries (backoff, AG-11-26), then "moved to a **dead-letter** store with full context and SHALL raise a Manager Notification" (AG-11-27). (4) A spend cap: "a runaway run is stopped and parked for a manager to look at, with a notification" (IMP-36, Proposal). (5) A stop condition (section 1). (6) Who is told: the delegating person through their queue; the users who can reassign cases and the Owner for overdue and unassigned items (AG-11-55); the Manager Notification for dead letters. (7) Always recorded: the audit entry with the delegation reference (IMP-1) and the run record state (IMP-35).

## 10. Glossary additions

Only terms not already in `../ai-organisation.md` §8 or `case-assistant.md` §10.

- **Standard deadline:** a date a module registers directly with the Minder, with the file owner to remind, "without any automation existing, with no firm set-up and no entitlement" (AG-11-47); never plan-gated (AG-11-48; SA-3-35).
- **Watch (Minder watch):** the Minder's record of one deadline it watches, with a status ARMED / ESCALATING / SATISFIED / BREACHED, a next step and an at-risk flag (`MinderWatch`, AG-11 v1.11 §7). **Satisfaction condition:** the fact that ends the watch (for example biometrics completed; AG-11-17, AG-11-18). **Cascade / T-minus steps:** the reminders and alerts a watch runs as the date nears (T-14, T-7, T-2, T-0; AG-11 v1.11 §3.4, an example; lead times are pack values).
- **Escalation:** alerting people above the file owner; part 2 of Team safeguards; when on, the users who can reassign cases are alerted 2 days before and on the day, with "no further settings" (AG-11-49). **At-risk mark / at-risk event:** part 3; a missed date marks the case at risk and records an event (AG-11-19).
- **Orphaned deadline:** a standard deadline that fires on a file whose owner was deactivated; the product creates the task unassigned and notifies the users who can reassign cases (AG-13-66).
- **Deadline list:** this document's name for the managers' list of approaching, overdue and orphaned deadlines; under the Lead's recommendation a product report built by rule (Proposal, 2.1). **Slipping (date):** its mark for a watched date whose satisfying work has not started inside the pack's lead time (Proposal).
- **Derived date:** a key date a pack rule computes from other verified values; proposed, never applied without a person (catalogue W-02; Content).
- **Biometrics:** fingerprints and a photo the government collects from an applicant by a set date (general knowledge; confirm with the Immigration SME). **Landing:** a new permanent resident's first entry to Canada as a permanent resident, with its own deadline (general knowledge; confirm with the Immigration SME). **ADR:** an additional-document request from the government, with a response date (AG-07 v2.7 §3.7; expansion general knowledge).
- **Next immigration moment:** the pack-defined date after a decision at which the client is likely to need the firm again (permit expiry, PR card renewal, citizenship eligibility, residency obligation); pack content (catalogue W-14; `../ai-organisation.md` §2.4). **PR card:** the card a permanent resident of Canada holds, with an expiry date the Canada pack watches (AG-11 v1.11 §3.12 `prCardExpiry`; general knowledge, confirm with the Immigration SME). **PGWP:** a post-graduation work permit (catalogue W-14 "Job"; general knowledge). **Residency obligation:** the time a permanent resident must spend in Canada to keep status (general knowledge; confirm with counsel / Immigration SME).
- **Reopen:** AG-02's action, under `agent/prospect:lose`, that creates a new opportunity on the same contact (AG-02 v1.9 §3.10; BR-15); a person's click.
- **Consent class:** the field on an automated action that says whether it is transactional or marketing; the consent gate checks marketing sends against the recipient's express consent at send time (`consentClass`, AG-11 v1.11 §7; AG-11-37).
- **Import batch:** one run of the import engine over one uploaded source; reported on and undone as one unit (AG-18 v1.2 §3.0). **Attestation:** the Owner's record that the firm has the right to transfer the data, required on every batch with personal data (AG-18-49). **Start / Pause client automations:** the acts that switch imported records from "Not started" to active and back, after a preview (AG-18-62, AG-18-71). **Closed summary record:** a closed case imported with only the minimum history (AG-18-28). **Could-not-map report:** the persona's list of columns and values it could not propose a mapping for (Proposal).
- **Data-subject request (DSR):** a client's request to access, correct, erase or port their data, tracked in SA-11's register on a statutory clock (SA-11-27; CP-8-15). **Statutory clock:** the legal response deadline, a value for counsel (SA-11 v2.7 §13 item 3). **Legal hold:** a freeze that blocks archival, disposal and DSR deletion (SA-11-18). **Retention floor:** the minimum keeping period per data category, enforced in code (SA-11-13, SA-11-14). **Consent record:** the register entry with timestamp, scope, locale and capturing form version (SA-11-29).
- **Per-individual data package:** the export of one client's personal data under `agent/data-export:request` (AG-16-21); distinct from the whole-firm export, one of the five Owner-only actions (SA-06 v3.1 §3.3).
- **Reporter / compliance view:** AG-14's read-and-aggregate layer and its compliance dashboard (deadline adherence, the upcoming-deadline horizon and list, at-risk items only where the firm switched that on; AG-14-17). **Schedule owner:** the person who created a scheduled report (AG-14-24).
- **Backup approver:** the one person the Owner may nominate to receive items whose approver is away or that are overdue (AG-11-83; AG-16-49).
- **Double-run guard:** the rule that a persona never repeats what a recipe, the Minder or a product Doer already did on the same record, keyed on the action's idempotency key (IMP-35; AG-11-25; `case-assistant.md` §1.1). **Dead letter, backoff:** as `case-assistant.md` §10.
- **Run type:** whether a persona run covers one case, one client, one import batch, one request or the whole firm; it decides the run's stop rules (Proposal, section 1).
- **Task link field (`sourceItemRef`):** the field on a product task that links it to the dated item that created it and completes both together (AG-13 v1.12 §7; AG-13-65); a persona task never carries it.
