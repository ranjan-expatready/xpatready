## 0. Summary: the persona in five plain lines

1. When a firm's ticket is assigned to a support agent who has switched the assistant on, it reads the ticket and the facts Expat Ready already holds about that one firm's account, suggests the category and priority (a hint the agent confirms), works out by rule what the facts say happened, and puts a first reply in the firm's language into the agent's reply box. The agent reads it, edits it and sends it; the assistant never posts anything (W-22).
2. It says which existing fix would clear the problem and which role may do it (look in the payment processor's notification log and have a failed notification re-run, send an update-your-card link, point the firm's Owner to Pay now, ask the account manager for a plan change, open a support session); it never does any of them, and it cannot even read the notification log.
3. When one firm is going into trouble (over a limit, trial ending, payment overdue, cancelling), it drafts that firm's account manager's outreach and, when the firm asks for a plan change, the note describing what would change and when under the product's own rule; a second Expat Ready person confirms that note during the pilot (AIW-D59); the account manager sends and acts (W-23).
4. It never reads a firm's clients, cases or documents, never opens an attachment, never reads a list of firms, never starts a support session, never changes a firm's plan, billing, flags, contact or status, never answers an immigration-content question, and never lets an internal note reach a firm.
5. It works under one named Super Admin user's delegation, with a read-only subset of that person's own keys, and every step it takes is in the platform audit log with that person as the actor, stamped with the one firm the run serves, the delegation's reference number and the marker that a persona acted for them.

## 2. What it does, step by step

**How to read the tables.** *Action* is a read of a platform record under a key the delegation holds, a rule the platform already evaluates (a routing rule SA-10-6; the plan-change timing rule SA-4-29; the blocked-state rule SA-4-69), or a model call. A step with no existing action, rule or record behind it is marked **Proposal** and listed in section 9. There is no Doer roster on the platform plane: AG-11-2's closed list (`send_mail`, `send_notification`, `create_task`, `draft_message` and the rest) belongs to the firm's Conductor, so the model call here is written *model call (Writer shape)*, after the **Writer**, the AI Doer the sources call the Drafter (AIW-D53): "no tools, output checked against a fixed expected shape, treated as untrusted" (IMP-10, whose words these are; the rule behind it is AG-11-94: "models SHALL have no tool access"). Model output "can propose *values* (a date, a type, a draft), never *actions*, recipients, addresses or permissions" (IMP-26 rule 1). *Autonomy* on this plane is one level: suggest-only (section 1). *Recorded:* the platform audit entry (SA-11-1; the fields of SA-11-2), the AI run log row (AG-11-72 shape; its platform-plane home is G-3), and, Proposal, the delegation reference and the audit mode (IMP-1): *the person acted directly* (PERSON), *a persona acted for a person* (DELEGATED), *a platform automation ran* (AUTOMATION, `actorType = system`, SA-11 BR-3). Every entry is stamped with the one firm the run serves (`tenantId`, section 5). **Outputs counted in §8** closes the *Proposes / writes* cell of every step: the outputs the step produces, in the six kinds of section 8 (a proposal, a model call, a send, an issue, an act by a person, an act by the product). In every cell the plain words come first and the product's internal name follows in brackets.

### 2.1 W-22 Support Triager

*In plain words.* A firm raises a ticket from its console. The product acknowledges it, tells the on-call agents and starts the clock in the firm's own business hours. When an agent with a delegation takes the ticket, the assistant reads the ticket and its thread, the firm's account facts Expat Ready already holds (plan, status, trial end, usage against limits, last login, recent tickets) and, for a money question, that firm's subscription record. The assistant never opens an attachment, never looks at the firm's clients or cases, never posts, never changes the ticket. If the question is about immigration content it says so and stops: that ticket goes to the Content Manager. After a ticket is resolved, the agent can ask it to draft a help article with the firm's details taken out.

## 4. Where the person approves

**The rule.** There is no Needs you queue on this plane. Every proposal is shown to the named person inside the screen where that person already acts (the ticket workspace, the firm detail, the subscription detail) and becomes real only through that person's own act under that person's own key. The persona never approves anything (IMP-1 rule 5). The record of each act carries the person, the role they held at that moment, the delegation reference and the proposal they acted on (IMP-17(a) adapted, Proposal).

**Decided 11 Oct 2026 (AIW-D59): a second person on a persona-proposed account change, for the pilot.** For a plan, billing-period, discount or status change on a firm's account that the persona proposed, a second Expat Ready person (a second Super Admin user) confirms the note before the person holding the key applies it, during the pilot; the founder reviews the rule after the pilot. ## 7. Confirm with counsel

One list; each line says why. The Code of Professional Conduct binds the firm's licensees, not Expat Ready; it appears here only where the firm's duty touches what we do with its data. Where a term is law, this document states general knowledge and asks counsel to confirm (README rule 5). No counsel is engaged yet (AIW-D64): every safety decision the founder has taken that appears here is provisional until counsel has read this section, and **no pilot runs on real client files until counsel has read the counsel pack** (AIW-D64).

1. **Reading a firm's account data to answer its ticket is a privacy act.** The subscription terms promise that "Expat Ready uses firm data only to provide and support the service, keeps it confidential, logs every support session (shown to the owner — SA-2-29), and deletes it under the 90-day rule (SA-2-21)" and are described as "the written service-provider contract Quebec's Law 25 (s. 18.3) requires, and the same protection under PIPEDA elsewhere" (SA-4-65). Whether a model call on ticket text and account facts is "to provide and support the service", whether the AI provider must appear on the list of *sub-processors* (the outside companies that handle firm data for us, SA-11-22) and in the residency declaration (SA-11-20), and whether firms must be told: counsel.
2. **Ticket text and attachments as client personal data.** A ticket "can carry a firm's — and by extension a *client's* — personal information" (SA-10 v2.5 §1); "Ticket and message media are treated as client PII" (SA-10 BR-13). The least-data rule (IMP-21: attachments never, text as delimited data, identifiers by reference) is the Lead's design; whether it is enough, and what the Restricted field set is (founder item B-1): counsel.
3. **Processing region for Expat Ready's own calls.** AG11-D15 "only in Canada" against SA-11 §3.6 "with documented safeguards" (IMP-7). **The founder chose *only in Canada*, for Expat Ready's own support tickets too (decided 11 Oct 2026, AIW-D52);** SA-11 §3.6's *with safeguards* wording gets a change note. AG11-D15's own words, "AI and OCR processing of client data", arguably already reach a ticket that carries a client's facts. Counsel confirms the wording; until then it is provisional (AIW-D64).
4. **Retention of the AI run log on the platform plane.** SA-11-14: "every audit entry of an Expat Ready staff (Super Admin) action SHALL be kept ≥ 7 years, whatever its category"; whether a persona's proposal made for a staff member is such an entry, and whether the model call's log follows the ticket's retention or the 7 years (IMP-4): counsel.
5. **The firm's confidentiality duty.** From the College of Immigration and Citizenship Consultants Code of Professional Conduct (SOR/2022-128, current to 2026-09-21), verified by the Lead on 10 Oct 2026 at laws-lois.justice.gc.ca; **the mapping is the Lead's reading; counsel has not read it.** s. 28(1): confidentiality kept "indefinitely". A firm's ticket can disclose a client's matter to us; our handling (BR-3 scoped access, no attachments to a model, Canada-only processing, the 90-day deletion) must not put the firm in breach. The other sections mapped in `case-assistant.md` §7 item 5 (19(2)(e), 22(2), 22(3), 38(1) to 38(3)) concern the licensee's own service and supervision and do not apply to Expat Ready's support desk.
6. **A cross-firm sweep, if one is ever added.** Version 1 has none (decided 11 Oct 2026, AIW-D54; section 11, decision 9). If a sweep over the over-limit list or the overdue list is added after the pilot, it is a read "spanning >1 tenant" (SA-11-12); whether a model's view of several firms' figures in one call needs any safeguard beyond that entry: counsel.
7. **Knowledge-base articles drafted from a firm's ticket.** De-identification before the call (section 2.1 step 8) and before publication to other firms; what counts as de-identified under PIPEDA and Law 25: counsel.
8. **Outreach messages.** Whether an account manager's outreach drafted by a model is a commercial message under *CASL* (Canada's anti-spam law; master §8) when it proposes a higher plan, and whether the existing billing notices' transactional status (SA-4-53) covers it: counsel.
9. **Two-person confirmation of persona-proposed account changes.** Adopted for the pilot (AIW-D59); a design choice, not a legal one; counsel to say whether the record (section 5) is enough evidence that no automated decision changed a firm's account (IMP-33).

## 10. Glossary additions

Only terms not already in `../ai-organisation.md` §8 or `case-assistant.md` §10. Paraphrase, no quotations; the source is in brackets.

- **Writer (and the Writer shape):** the AI Doer that drafts text for a person to approve (the `draft_message` action; the sources call it the Drafter, AG11-D4); renamed by the founder on 11 Oct 2026 (AIW-D53). The four AI Doers are Reader, Sorter, Writer and Checker; *Drafter* now means only the template engine (generate-from-template, `agent/document:generate`, SA-06 v3.1 §10b). On this plane there is no Doer roster, so a model call is written *model call (Writer shape)*: no tools, a fixed expected shape, output treated as untrusted (IMP-10). The AI run log's Doer value for the Writer is the source's `DRAFTER` (AG-11 v1.11 §7).
- **Super Admin / the platform plane (GLOBAL):** Expat Ready's own console and staff, a separate sign-in group from firm users and clients; a staff account belongs to no firm (SA-07 v2.10 §3.0); its keys are `system/*` (SA-06 v3.1 §10a). **LOCAL plane:** the firm's console, keys `agent/*`.
- **Ticket:** a firm's request for help, owned by the firm and served by Expat Ready; the same record is shown two ways, to the firm and to Expat Ready (SA-10 v2.5 §3.0). **Internal note:** a message on the ticket only Expat Ready staff can see (SA-10 BR-4). **First response:** the first agent reply, which stops the first clock (BR-9). **Status-change notice:** the message the firm receives each time its ticket changes status (SA-10-14).
- **Composer (reply box):** the box on the ticket screen where an agent writes a reply or an internal note (SA-10 v2.5 §3.4).
- **SLA:** the promised time to first response and to resolution, per priority, counted in the firm's business hours (SA-10-15; *service-level agreement*, general knowledge). **CSAT:** the one-tap satisfaction survey after resolution (SA-10-22; *customer satisfaction*, general knowledge). **Reopen window:** the days after resolution in which a firm reply reopens the ticket (SA-10-21).
- **Routing rule:** category to agent pool (SA-10-6). **Escalation:** passing a ticket to a senior by hand or on breach (SA-10-17). **Canned response:** a pre-written reply in each language, with slots filled from the record, that the agent inserts and edits (SA-10-10). **Knowledge base:** help articles for agents and, in part, for firms (SA-10-23).
- **Support session ("log in as", impersonation):** a staff member seeing a firm's console after a step-up, with a reason, a time limit and every action tagged in the firm's audit trail (SA-02 v2.19 §3.7); read-only unless the firm's Owner allowed changes (SA-2-28). **Step-up:** the extra identity confirmation, with the person's own passkey or code, before a sensitive act (SA-07 v2.10 §3.2; SA-7-10).
- **Account manager:** the one Super Admin user who looks after a firm's account (SA-2-26). **Shared team inbox:** where a firm's alerts go when it has no account manager (BR-17).
- **Over limit, warn and hard:** a plan limit a firm has passed; in warn mode the action succeeds and a 14-day window runs before the limit refuses (SA-03 v2.14 §3.2).
- **Overdue, payment hold:** 14 days of full access after a failed charge, then only the billing screen and the data download work (SA-04 v2.14 §3.5, §3.6). **Processor notification (webhook):** the payment processor's message to Expat Ready about a charge, failure or refund, recorded once and re-runnable if it failed (SA-04 v2.14 §3.10). **Proration:** charging only for the part of a billing period that is left; the processor works it out (SA-04 v2.14 §3.7; general knowledge for the word). **Sent-notices record:** the list of billing notices the product has sent a firm, by type, recipients and time, kept so none is sent twice (`BillingNotice`, SA-04 v2.14 §7).
- **Trial, Internal plan, Demo firm:** a plan's free days (SA-04 v2.14 §3.8); a $0 plan for Expat Ready's own or pilot firms (SA-4-46); a marked firm left out of the counts (SA-2-40).
- **Rollup:** a total added up ahead of time, so a dashboard reads one number instead of counting every record (SA-10 v2.5 §3.11).
- **Sub-processor:** an outside company that handles firm data on Expat Ready's behalf, listed for the firm's data-processing agreement (SA-11-22; general knowledge for the word).
- **Platform tenant rule:** this document's name for the Lead's design decision of 10 Oct: a run is stamped with the firm it serves, null only when it serves none, and never reads another firm's data (section 5; confirmed by the founder on 11 Oct 2026, AIW-D54; Step 3 writes it into AG-11 and SA-11).
- **Second-person confirmation:** a second Expat Ready person confirms any account or plan change this assistant proposes before it is applied, during the pilot (AIW-D59; section 4).
- **Cross-tenant access entry:** the audit entry any read across more than one firm writes about itself (SA-11-12); none in version 1. **Actor type:** who acted, on every audit entry: Expat Ready staff, firm staff, client or the system (SA-11-2).
- **Kill switch, firm override:** the platform-wide emergency off switch with a mandatory reason (SA-12-12) and a one-firm exception a person sets with a reason (SA-12-10).
- **De-identified:** stripped of anything that identifies a firm or a person before it is used elsewhere (SA-09 v2.2 §3.4 uses the word for usage events; general knowledge for the rest, confirm with counsel).
- **Counting unit (worked examples):** one row per step output, of six kinds: proposal, model call, send, issue (a document the product issues), act by a person (firm staff and Expat Ready staff counted apart), act by the product (section 8; the Lead's decision of 10 Oct).
