## 0. Summary: the persona in five plain lines

1. When an enquiry lands, the product's own speed-to-lead recipe thanks the lead and tasks the rep; the Intake assistant then runs the firm's eligibility engine (the Qualifier, a rule engine, never a model), works out by rule what is still missing, and drafts the questions that would unlock a firm answer (W-09, W-10).
2. When the lead answers, it proposes the facts it read in the message for a person to accept, re-runs the Qualifier, and drafts the reply that offers the consultation booking link; it never tells the lead an outcome, score or lever: eligibility reaches a lead only through the consultant's own audited share (W-10, W-09).
3. When the consult is held, it drafts the consult record for the host: what was discussed, the recommended pathway taken from the Qualifier, the services and fee the host quoted, the next step; the host accepts it, clicks "Create proposal from consult", and the firm's template engine renders the retainer by rule from the record (W-11).
4. Every decision is a person's: template messages go out at the level the firm set, any AI-written lead text is approved by whoever the firm's settings allow (a licensed person by default), proposed facts are accepted by a person, pipeline moves are the rep's, the retainer is approved for send and sent by the consultant, and a lead is converted only by a person's click.
5. It works under one named intake rep's delegation, with a subset of that rep's own permissions, and every step is in the audit log with the delegation's reference number.

### 2.1 W-10 Lead Responder

*In plain words.* The moment an enquiry lands, the product thanks the lead and tells the rep to call; the Intake assistant reads what the lead told the firm, runs the eligibility engine on it, and decides by a fixed rule what comes next: ask for the facts that are missing, or offer the consultation. It drafts the message, a firm template where one fits and the writer only where none does, and it never puts an eligibility result, a score or a lever into a lead's message. When the lead writes back with a question, it drafts a reply that says the consultant will answer it in the consultation. It proposes pipeline moves for the rep to approve and writes the rep a one-line next move with the reason. It never creates, converts, moves, loses or calls a lead itself.

### 2.2 W-09 Intake Assistant (gap asker and message-to-answer)

*In plain words.* The eligibility engine already says which facts would turn a maybe into a firm answer. The Intake assistant lists them, ranked by how many programs each fact unlocks, and asks the lead for them in the lead's language, through a firm template where one exists. When the lead replies, the assistant reads the message and proposes the facts it found, each marked with the message it came from; a person accepts or corrects each one. Accepting is not verifying: no document was seen, so the fact is recorded as declared by the lead and typed in by staff, unverified, and a later document can still verify it. Then the engine runs again.
