## 0. Summary: the persona in five plain lines

1. When an enquiry lands, the product's own speed-to-lead recipe thanks the lead and tasks the rep; the Intake assistant reads the enquiry and, by a fixed rule, asks the rep to run the firm's eligibility engine (the Qualifier, a rule engine, never a model); once a person has run it, the assistant works out by rule what is still missing and prepares the message that asks for it (W-10, W-09).
2. When the lead answers, it proposes the facts it read in the message for a person to accept (accepted facts stay *unverified*), asks the rep to re-run the engine, and prepares the reply that offers the consultation booking link; it never tells the lead an outcome, score or lever: eligibility reaches a lead only through the consultant's own audited share (W-10, W-09).
3. After the consultation, the host types the services, the fee and the next step by hand; the assistant may draft only internal notes of the meeting. The retainer draft takes its scope and fee only from what a person entered, so it carries no model-written text by rule (W-11).
4. Every decision is a person's: template messages go out at the level the firm set, any AI-written lead text is approved by whoever the firm's settings allow (a licensed person by default), proposed facts are accepted by a person, pipeline moves are the rep's, the retainer draft is approved for send and sent by the consultant, and a lead is converted only by a person's click.
5. It works under one named intake rep's delegation, with a subset of that rep's own permissions, and every step is in the audit log with the delegation's reference number.

**How to read the tables.** As in `case-assistant.md` §2: *Action* is from the Conductor's closed list (AG-11-2) or a rule the engine already evaluates; a step with no existing action, Doer or pack rule is marked **Proposal** and listed in section 9. *Doer or rule* adds the Qualifier (AG-03, "a **deterministic pure function**", AG-3-39), the Scheduler (AG-04), the Signer and the template-engine Drafter (AG-05; a different Drafter from the AI Doer, IMP-24) and the Dialer (AG-02 §3.12, a person's tool). ### 2.1 W-10 Lead Responder

*In plain words.* The moment an enquiry lands, the product thanks the lead and tells the rep to call. The Intake assistant reads the enquiry and, if nobody has assessed the lead yet, puts a task in front of the rep: run the eligibility check. Once a person has run it, the assistant applies a fixed, ordered rule to the result: if every program is out of reach, it hands the lead to the rep and sends nothing about eligibility; if any program looks reachable, it prepares the message that offers the consultation; otherwise it prepares the message that asks for the facts that are missing. It prepares that message from a firm template where one fits and asks the writer only where none does, and it never puts an eligibility result, a score or a lever into a lead's message. When the lead writes back with a question, it prepares a reply that says the consultant will answer it in the consultation. It proposes pipeline moves for the rep to approve. It never creates, converts, moves, loses or calls a lead itself.

## 7. Confirm with counsel / RCIC

1. **The CASL class of each lead message.** A direct reply to the lead's own enquiry is transactional ("a direct reply to the client's own message", AG-10 v1.9 §3.6); an unprompted nudge to book, or a going-cold or no-show follow-up, may be marketing and then needs express consent "evaluated **per recipient at send time**" (AG-10-22). Which of the persona's messages fall on which side, and whether a booking offer made within a reply is still transactional: counsel (catalogue W-10 "CASL class of each message: confirm with counsel / RCIC").
2. **Pre-retainer questions as advice.** Asking a lead for facts, and replying that the consultant will answer a question in the consultation, before any retainer: whether that is advice or representation under section 91 of the Immigration and Refugee Protection Act when done by an assistant to an unlicensed rep: counsel / RCIC (catalogue W-09, W-26).
3. **Eligibility content to a lead, and eligibility-driven routing.** The Qualifier's result reaches a lead only through the consultant's share (AG-3-38), under "the regulator-safe no-guarantee disclaimer" whose "Exact wording = SME/founder" (AG-3-32); whether review is mandatory before client-share is open ("**Mandatory review before client-share?**", AG-03 v1.4 §13 Q9). The product's own nurture design plans "your CRS would change if…" nudges (AG-02 v1.9 §3.10). Even with clean text, the persona's routing is eligibility-driven: a likely lead is offered a booking, an out-of-reach lead is handed to the rep and sent nothing about eligibility. Whether that routing, or any lever nudge (the firm's or ours), is advice: counsel / RCIC (section 11 decision 2).
4. **Recording consent and storage.** Call recording follows "the firm's jurisdiction rules (e.g. one-/two-party consent)" (AG-02 v1.9 §3.12); consult recording is unspecified; transcripts are personal data: counsel (catalogue W-11 "Recording consent and storage: confirm with counsel").
5. **Supervision, delegation and the Code of Professional Conduct (IMP-15, IMP-17).** The sections below are from the College of Immigration and Citizenship Consultants Code of Professional Conduct (SOR/2022-128, current to 2026-09-21), verified by the Lead on 10 Oct 2026 at laws-lois.justice.gc.ca; ## 10. Glossary additions

Only terms not already in `../ai-organisation.md` §8 or `case-assistant.md` §10.

- **Lead / Prospect / Contact / Party:** a lead (Prospect) is "a *single pursuit* of converting a Party into a retained client"; a Contact is "the CRM lens on a *Party*"; a Party is a person or an organisation (AG-02 v1.9 §3.0).
- **Pipeline stage and kind:** the firm's nine seeded stages (New, Contacted, Qualified, Consult, Proposal, Retainer Sent, Retainer Signed, Lost, Nurture), each with a kind `ACTIVE | WON | LOST | NURTURE`; WON and LOST are terminal (AG-02 v1.9 §3.4, §3.5). *The Proposal stage* is the pipeline stage "Services + fee proposed", not our **Proposal** label.
- **Speed to lead / first response / SLA clock:** the time from `prospect.created` to the first logged outbound touch, counted in business hours (AG-02 BR-12); the seeded recipe of the same name (AG-11 v1.11 §3.5). SLA (service-level) clock: the timer that measures it.
- **Next-Best-Action / work-queue:** the product's suggested next move on a lead and the rep's ranked list of what to work (AG-2-37, AG-2-38). **Going cold:** no activity for the firm's N business days, seeded 7 (AG-2-39).
- **Sequence:** a pre-built series of follow-up messages the firm switches on (AG-02 v1.9 §3.6); in Release 1 they run as the product's recipes.
- **Qualifier outcomes:** `LIKELY_ELIGIBLE`, `ELIGIBLE_BELOW_CUTOFF`, `POSSIBLY_ELIGIBLE` (missing data, with the inputs named), `NOT_ELIGIBLE` (with the failed gate named) (AG-3-10 to AG-3-13). **Gate vs score:** a gate decides eligibility, a score decides competitiveness (AG-3-11). **Band:** the Qualifier's competitiveness grade for draw-ranking programs (AG-3-15). **Lever:** a recommendation with an estimated point change, "indicative levers (never promises)" (AG-3-24). **Run type:** `AUTO`, `MANUAL` or `RERUN` on each Assessment (AG-03 v1.4 §7).
- **CRS, FSW-67, CLB:** the Canada pack's Express Entry ranking score, the federal skilled-worker 67-point grid, and the Canadian Language Benchmark that test scores convert to (AG-3-18, AG-3-19; general knowledge, confirm with the Immigration SME). **OINP, AAIP, SINP, BCPNP, MPNP:** the provincial streams the Canada pack covers (AG-3-19).
- **Consent class:** transactional (allowed to a reachable address) or marketing (needs express consent per channel and purpose at send time) (AG-10-22; `consentClass` on an action, AG-11 v1.11 §7). **Service-contact and marketing consent:** the Contact's two consent flags (AG-02 v1.9 §7).
- **Appointment type, booking, consult record:** the template, the instance and the outcome of a consultation (AG-04 v1.5 §3.0). **Booking page:** the firm's public page at `/{firmSlug}/{typeSlug}` (AG-4-16). **ICS:** the calendar-invite file attached to a confirmation (AG-4-32).
- **The retainer draft:** the agreement document the host generates with the product's "Create proposal from consult" button (AG-4-50; AG-05 v1.9 §3.13). **DRAFT / EXECUTED:** the agreement's first state and the state once every required party has signed (AG-05 v1.9 §3.4, §3.7). **Approve-for-send:** the consultant's recorded approval of the retainer draft before it is sent (AG-5-17). **Locked clause:** a pack-required clause the editor cannot remove or rewrite (AG-5-16).
- **Merge token:** a placeholder such as `{{fees.total}}` bound from the record at generation (AG-05 v1.9 §3.2).
- **Dialer, Scheduler, Signer, Biller:** the in-house tools for calls (AG-02 v1.9 §3.12), bookings (AG-04), e-signature (AG-05) and money (AG-06).
- **PQR:** the light pre-retainer questionnaire on a prospect, whose scope is open (AG-08 v1.7 §13 Q7).
- **Staff-recorded answer:** a fact typed in by staff on the client's behalf, `source = STAFF_ON_BEHALF`, unverified until checked against a document (AG-8-25, AG-8-26); the client remains the person the fact is about, the staff member only who typed it.
- **Assignment rule:** the firm's rule that gives a new lead an owner (explicit, round-robin, by program, by source) (AG-2-13a).
- **Why-line rule:** the Lead's rule that the Practice expert's plain-words line is shown to a person only when they open the item or notification that carries it, and never feeds another persona's draft, ranking or decision; the pack's own reason text for a failed gate may be fetched by rule.
- **Shared sender:** *an address or number that many clients' messages come from (a bank, a switchboard); once a person links it to one client, the matcher files every later message from it there* (AG-10-12; `../../proposals.md` row of 10 Oct, Billing assistant, shared senders).
