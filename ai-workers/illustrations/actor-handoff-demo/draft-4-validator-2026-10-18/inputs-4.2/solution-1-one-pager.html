Solution 1 · Pathway Setup to Form Filling

Actors, handoffs and rules, from configuring a combination to a filed form · v3 · one-pager draft 4.2: functional and desktop correction pass; draft 4.1 archived whole · 18 Oct 2026

The problem. Every immigration combination (Pathway › Program › Category › Sub-category) is set up by hand: documents, canonical fields, sections, form field maps, conditional rules, questionnaires. The work repeats at every form edition, and each repeat raises the risk of a wrong form.

The answer. No manual mapping. The engine reads the government's own forms, checklist and guide, and drafts the whole setup: it selects sections from the library it maintains, selects the fields for each section, creates a new section or field only when none fits, and writes every form field map as data, once per form edition. During initial validation people review every new or changed item beside its source; approved items that have not changed are reused without another review; the SME confirms rules and values; a second person approves and publishes. At case time every fact is asked once and rules fill every form and portal screen from verified facts.

Prepare, check and fill each form

Who acts

  1. Platformrenders by rules, later fills and reads back
  2. AIflags meaning doubts
  3. Agent Firmresolves flags

Key rules

  • No AI writes a value; every written value is read back

Repeats

Decision

Anything wrong?

  • Wrong fact→ back to stage 6; forms that read it are prepared again
  • Wrong mapping→ support ticket to Expat Ready (stage 10)
  • All clear→ to the representative
then, handing over: Checked forms
8

Approve, sign and submit

Who acts

  1. Lead representative Firmapproves the final file
  2. Client and family Client portalsign and declare
  3. Lead representative Firmsubmits; the client, or the firm for them, pays the fee

Key rules

  • A missing required document holds that filing; a recorded reason alone never clears it
  • Signing keeps the approval only if the signed file matches the approved answers

Per filing

each applicant's filing moves on its own unless the route requires joint submission (the SME confirms)

Decision

Approved and signed without a problem?

  • Yes→ submission
  • A form returned→ back to stage 7 with a reason
  • Signed file does not match→ back to the representative's check
  • Wrong value, or a re-run check blocks→ back to stage 6
then, handing over: Submitted application
9

Record responses and continue after the decision

Who acts

  1. Government Outsidesends letters
  2. Agent Firmrecords each on its filing; a decision needs the record-decision permission

Key rules

  • A request becomes a task: it may need a document, a corrected fact (stage 6) or a form completed by hand
  • A decision is not the end of the case: After decision follows, and a route with levels opens its next filing when the government approves a level

Waits for

the government's acknowledgement, requests and decision, for each filing

Decision

What follows a recorded decision?

  • Always→ the filing continues in After decision
  • Also, if the government approved a route level→ the next filing opens (stages 5 and 6)
  • Once every filing is closed→ the case closes
then, handing over: Decision recorded

Continues in After decision

Each decided filing continues in the case's existing workflow; a route level the government approved also opens the next filing (stages 5 and 6).

On change

When a form, guide or screen changes

Stages 10–12Expat Ready, then the firm

Reviewed and published like setup; open cases adopt the new version through the agent. Detailed in view 7.

10

Identify government changes and reported mapping issues

Who acts

  1. Platformre-reads sources on a schedule
  2. Content publisher Super Admintriages firms' support tickets

Key rules

  • A source that cannot be checked is 'not verified', never 'no change'
  • Support is the firms' one channel for forms and mappings (from stage 7)

Decision

Anything changed?

  • No→ nothing happens
  • Yes→ only changed items go forward
  • Could not be checked→ 'not verified', shown and checked again; never 'no change'
then, handing over: Changed items
11

Review and publish the change

Who acts

  1. AIre-drafts changed items
  2. Content publisher Super Adminreviews
  3. Immigration SME Super Adminsigns off changed rules
  4. Second-person approver Super Adminapproves and publishes

Key rules

  • The same checks and two-person approval and publishing as setup
  • Earlier versions stay unchanged; new cases start on the new one
then, handing over: New published version
12

Apply published changes to affected cases

Who acts

  1. Agent Firmsees 'what changes for this case' and adopts

Key rules

  • Only affected forms are prepared again, and only their approvals are withdrawn
  • A government cut-off makes adoption required; submitted forms never change

Waits for

the agent's adoption; the case keeps its version until then

Decision

Does it affect this open case?

  • Yes→ adopt now, or later (by the cut-off, if there is one)
  • No→ nothing changes
then, handing over: Affected forms

Ends: case on the new version

Affected forms go back to stage 7; only their approvals are withdrawn.

Controls that run through every view

Validation
Five groups, drafted in 3.8: form-native checks, official programme and document rules, cross-record consistency, readiness warnings, read-back. Document expiry warns at 90 and 30 days by default and never blocks; history gaps block submission until filled with a recorded history entry and checked again. Checks run at verification and render; time-sensitive ones again before signing and submission. In the team's words: block = a failed hard check (red, stops approval, never waived); warn = a Critical warning (amber, acknowledged with a reason before approval); advise = a Major or Minor warning (shown and listed; no reason needed, never blocks: only Critical warnings must be fixed or passed, AG-7-27). AI drafts checks only in the existing kinds; anything else is a named gap. Records: Library, Facts per person or organisation.
Review and approval
Initial validation: every new or changed item is reviewed beside its source, whatever the AI's confidence; approved unchanged items are reused. The SME confirms rules, gates and values; a second person approves and publishes; the representative approves each form; people sign, pay and submit. Records: Review and approval records.
When a check fails
A blocking failure holds the affected item and everything that depends on it for correction and re-testing. AI disagreements, near-duplicates and uncertain items go to the reviewer marked 'decide', never back to drafting until the models agree. Approved, unaffected work stays usable. A whole step is switched off only for a stated, concrete reason. Records: Review and approval records, Library.
Versions
Every item is versioned. A case pins the version it opened on and adopts a newer one only through 10.3. Outputs are stamped with form edition, map version and answer snapshot; approvals are bound to the versions they covered. A change of AI model or prompt for any AI step is a change: the step is re-run against the expected answers before use. Records: Published pack version, Prepared and submitted outputs.
AI processing
Every AI step (drafting, document reading, meaning checks) must process client data in Canada or the United States only, under written no-training and zero-data-retention terms, with client notice; client data must be stored in Canada (a requirement; legal: unconfirmed, no counsel). Records: Document evidence, Facts per person or organisation.
Change
A scheduled re-read finds changes; only changed items go through the same review and publishing; open cases adopt with 'what changes for this case'; a government cut-off makes adoption required. Records: Library, Published pack version.

Principles

Repeatable code, with named limits
Reading, counting, matching approved labels, checking, filling and read-back are code. AI drafts where judgement is needed: new labels, guide prose, placements, rules, gates. People decide.
Reuse first
A field, section or document type that exists is reused. A new one is created only when nothing fits, and a person approves it once.
Every item cites its source
File, version, page and field or passage, with the date read. A reviewer sees the snippet beside the proposal.
Tested before trusted
AI steps are tested against independently checked expected answers. Acceptance criteria come from business consequences; measured results are reported separately. Nothing is measured yet.
Versioned, pinned, reproducible
Every item has a version. A case pins the version it opened on. The same versions and facts give the same values; byte-identical re-rendering is to be proven on standard fillable PDFs (9.1 acceptance); nothing is measured yet.
No AI at fill time
Filling is rules over approved mappings and verified facts. The same facts always give the same form.
Expat Ready runs the engine
Only the content team configures and publishes. Firms consume published combinations; they never author pack content or run the engine.

  • Proposed here The selected value is the current verified one: verified by a person, with a known source, and not superseded by a later verified value
