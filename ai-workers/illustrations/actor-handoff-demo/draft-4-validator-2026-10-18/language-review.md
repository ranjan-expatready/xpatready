## Cross-cutting findings

- Replace diagram-oriented headings such as “Two ways in” with case-initiation language, and generic “Good to know” with the actual category (Key rules, Important conditions). Use adaptive labels, not mandatory empty fields.
- Keep titles short; put the named actor and concrete input/action/result in the first sentence. A full requirement sentence is not needed in every heading.
- Define “canonical field”, “form field mapping”, “published pack version” and “read-back” once at the right layer; retain exact technical identifiers in source detail.
- 5.3 still conflates “pathway version” with the country pack. Clarify using the existing source model.
- The static per-filing tooltip truncates to “Per-filing mark: each applicant” because its apostrophe is inserted into a single-quoted HTML attribute without escaping (runs_html in build_one_pager.py). Escape the attribute and check the rendered full tooltip. This is a concrete text-delivery defect, not an invitation to add icons.
- The generic “one of the outcomes” decision tooltip should not describe combined outcomes as mutually exclusive. Use the specific decision semantics, especially After decision plus next-level continuation.
- “Start mark: where a case begins” is imprecise on pre-case enquiry intake; distinguish intake entry from case creation.
- Any sentence saying “complete”, “ready”, “approved” or “held” needs the correct object and state boundary; use the C-M3 state table in current-review.md rather than guessing from the word.
- Preserve proposal/unbuilt/evidence labels. Do not relabel a proposed screen as an existing product screen, or assume a working HTML interaction proves the AI or PDF path.

This is a content review and edit brief; it is not evidence of a real reader successfully comprehending the artifact. No arbitrary readability grade, maximum word count or blanket synonym replacement was used.
