## Preservation assessment

Read the 116 merged/clarified dispositions and compared their relevant meaning with the extracted panels. The mapped changes mostly improve clarity and retain substantive content. The 80% content-word heuristic and exact-string count are useful loss alarms, **not a semantic equivalence test**. Stage 11's reordered story demonstrates why sequence needs its own check. D215 demonstrates a different problem: preserving the immediately previous page does not prove that an earlier agreed requirement was ever fully carried into that page.

No blanket content-loss claim is made. The remedy is the linked need/source matrix and targeted scenario review, not another automatic AI reviewer or a wholesale rewrite of the builder.
