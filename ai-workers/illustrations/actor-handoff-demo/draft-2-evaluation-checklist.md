# First usable one-pager: independent evaluation checklist

17 October 2026. Prepared at the founder's request for reviewing the forthcoming draft. Independent review aid; not an amendment to shared decisions or an implementation instruction. No verdict has been issued: draft 2 has not been evaluated here.

Use `evaluation-framework-2026-10-17.md` to organise the verdict into six lenses: solution quality, completeness/precision, readability, usability, honesty/transparency and implementation feasibility. The sections below remain the practical checks; they are not seven additional scoring categories. The framework adds a founder-need coverage matrix, role-based scenario tasks and an explicit feasibility judgment without requiring more content on the main one-pager.

## What the document must accomplish

Give a reader who did not attend the discussion one coherent visual explanation of the problem, proposed solution and end-to-end behaviour. Make clear who acts, in what order, with which information, under which rules, what comes out and who acts next. Keep the overview readable and put useful requirements and illustrative examples inside the relevant activity inspector. Support business alignment and subsequent detailed requirements; do not imply that a documentation artifact proves a working product or AI performance.

## Review method and evidence

- Evaluate the delivered HTML and its actual interactions, not the maker's summary alone. Record the exact version/hash and decisions used.
- Use D233–D236 and later applicable founder decisions; check the current register rather than assume these remain the latest. Use the current validation brief and alignment addendum for presentation requirements.
- Before asserting implementation or team-requirement facts, run the project freshness checks. Mark unavailable verification explicitly; do not invent evidence or refresh shared sources outside the reviewer's authorised role.
- Mark each criterion Pass, Partial, Fail or Not verified. Give an activity/section, exact wording or observed behaviour, consequence and smallest correction for each finding.
- Distinguish Material (wrong behaviour, missing necessary requirement, misleading claim, or inability to follow/use the journey) from Minor (local wording or presentation issue). Do not average away a material defect in an overall score.
- Assess three dimensions separately: solution quality, communication/readability, and requirements completeness. Any numeric assessment must have explicit reasons and is a review judgment, not measured product accuracy.

## A. Readable overview

- The opening states the problem and outcome in plain business language.
- A new reader can identify setup, case preparation/filling and change handling; actors and phase transitions are clear.
- The main surface shows meaningful sequence, handoffs and essential conditions without exposing all detailed prose.
- Overview and phase views preserve a coherent journey; wide content is not made illegible through fit-to-page scaling or hidden behind excessive panning.
- Actor labels, notation and colour meanings are explained. Agent means firm staff; AI capability is distinguished.
- Decision history, meeting quotations and implementation debate do not crowd the main solution path.
- PQR, SQR, canonical field, master section and form field mapping are understandable from concise definitions/examples.

## B. End-to-end process and authority

- Activities sit with the responsible actor; external participants and independent processes are represented consistently.
- Start/end points, decisions, waits, true parallel work, joins and rework have defined meanings. A visual line is not inferred from list order.
- Sequence, message and data-dependency connectors are used consistently with the chosen notation and participant boundaries.
- Optional portal/document routes have a bypass or completion condition and cannot leave a join waiting indefinitely.
- Handoffs identify the transferred record, receiving owner and readiness condition.
- Setup drafting, review, specialist confirmation where needed and independent release are distinct responsibilities.
- Case fact confirmation, prepared-form review, approval, signature, payment and submission have the correct owners and limits.
- Expat Ready owns mapping preparation/release; unsupported forms follow the recorded central route with the existing urgent manual alternative.
- Individual, family and corporate differences are covered or their shared behaviour is explained. Answerer, fact subject, form owner and signatory are not conflated.

## C. Useful detail at every activity

Check the substance of all ten groups, not just the presence of headings:

1. Trigger.
2. Prerequisites.
3. Inputs and record dependencies.
4. Processing.
5. Output and readiness state.
6. Actor, authority and approval limits.
7. Business rules and validation.
8. Failure, recovery and return path.
9. Testable happy/alternate-path acceptance examples.
10. Next owner, transferred output and receiving condition.

Also check the relevant screen/context is labelled existing or proposed. Requirements must describe observable behaviour rather than generic statements such as 'AI handles it'.

## D. Data, sections, views and mappings

- Shared canonical definitions remain separate from person/organisation values and case/form context.
- AI reuses library sections and fields, proposes missing definitions and generates combination-specific placements without a manual mapping exercise.
- PQR/SQR/staff views use applicable projections of shared definitions and values; government form review keeps the government's headings/order.
- Lifecycle tier, collection responsibility and visibility/editing remain separate, as D235 requires.
- Form-edition mappings are reused across combinations; applicability rules and genuine mapping variations are explicit.
- Mappings resolve target, subject/relationship, canonical field, purpose/time, format, conditions, repeated rows and overflow where applicable.
- A wrong value and a wrong mapping have different correction paths. Neither is disguised by editing a shared fact to fit one output.
- Source evidence, confirmed facts, configuration versions, approvals and generated outputs have identifiable relationships and ownership.

## E. Validation, quality and change

- AI-generated checks cover native form constraints, sourced programme/document conditions, cross-record consistency, advisory readiness and actual-output readback.
- Rules carry applicability, source/version, inputs, timing, severity and action; unsupported expressions are named gaps.
- Immigration values require specialist confirmation. Illustrations do not introduce universal six-month rules or new blocking policy.
- Structural coverage, semantic correctness and correct writing/readback are distinguished. AI self-confidence is not proof.
- New/changed proposals receive required review; approved unchanged content is reused. Proposed targets, measured results and release criteria are separate.
- Test examples include wrong person, omissions, repeats, conditional branches, formatting and changed instructions; independent expected answers are used.
- A failed check holds affected work and its dependent outputs; unaffected approved work remains usable. No unnecessary whole-pipeline shutdown.
- Review priority, grey/red/amber/green check status and human approval remain separate. Important green items can still need focused review; colour is not the sole explanation.
- AI checker value is evaluated for semantic errors, separately from ordinary equality checks. No repeated prompting until agreement or guarantee of error detection.
- Guide/rule changes can affect unchanged fields. Dependency impact, version adoption, revalidation and invalidated approvals/outputs are explained without overstating what becomes stale.
- AcroForm, XFA/barcode, validate-only and portal routes have honest capability limits; native validation/barcodes are not assumed interchangeable.
- Runtime AI remains a bounded challenger experiment; changing the selected route requires evidence and founder agreement.

## F. Inspector and example usability

- Every activity has an accessible eye/View control, operable by pointer, touch and keyboard.
- Desktop detail opens beside/over the process, preserves selection, document scroll and map position, and stays open until closed.
- Another selection updates the same inspector. Close/Escape restores the correct focus and location. No separate-page/back-navigation requirement.
- Phone detail is readable with appropriate focus handling and a clear Close action.
- The inspector starts with Input → Action → Output → Next owner, followed by short labelled groups and useful expandable detail.
- An Example control expands within that inspector without another navigation layer; any persistent expansion preference is understandable.
- Useful examples cover all concepts in alignment-addendum.md, using tables/matrices/comparisons/short flows where appropriate.
- The same illustrative family and values stay consistent. Examples show normal behaviour and a meaningful exception, plus reviewer actions when relevant.
- Missing examples have a defensible reason; a generic example or decorative card does not count as explanation.
- No invented government identifiers, achieved accuracy, turnaround or task duration. Accessibility labels, contrast, selection and focus remain understandable without colour alone.
- Browser checks cover the planned desktop, tablet and phone widths; record actual observations and any untested mode.

## G. Preservation and claim discipline

- Compare all original activity content, rules, principles, case variations and measurements with their dispositions and destinations. Counts alone are insufficient.
- Moving the configuration library from activity to data store retains its useful definitions and controls.
- Old HTML, builder and styles are preserved for a major revision by the Lead.
- Main-path claims are consistent with current decisions; superseded proposals are not accidentally presented as the chosen design.
- Existing requirements, current implementation, proposals, illustrative behaviour and unknowns are distinguishable.
- Pending real-form measurements remain pending. Interaction/structural checks do not establish AI accuracy, complete government support or readiness to deploy.
- Required legal labels and specialist-confirmation boundaries are retained where applicable.

## Walkthroughs to perform

1. Follow one combination from official sources through AI-generated sections, fields, rules and mappings to reviewed/released configuration.
2. Follow one family from PQR/SQR and document reading to confirmed facts, applicant/spouse/child field resolution, filled output and human actions.
3. Open mapping, section, validation and handoff examples; explain them using only the visible card and its context.
4. Try missing information, a wrong-person value, a wrong mapping, a duplicate-person candidate and an unsupported rare form; follow the return paths.
5. Trace a form-edition or guide change through dependencies and affected approvals/outputs.
6. Repeat critical disclosure/navigation actions on a narrow screen and with the keyboard.

## Review result and subsequent work

Return a brief usability verdict, material findings first, the strongest retained content and the smallest fixes. The founder reviews the artifact and its independent findings. After alignment, the next work follows NEXT.md: real-form evidence, then the walkthrough and detailed solution/requirements artifacts. Do not expand this milestone into screenshots, a product prototype or production build.
