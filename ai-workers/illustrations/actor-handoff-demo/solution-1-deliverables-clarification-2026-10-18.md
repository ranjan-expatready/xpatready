## What real-form validation means

AIW-D229 records "Real read + measured match": read actual official PDFs/fields/scripts; create independently checked expected field matches; evaluate AI matching against that key and record the results. AIW-D233 also schedules a bounded runtime-AI challenger comparison, with no automatic change to the selected mapping route. The provider-API execution remains the team's step under D229.

This is a limited technical validation/spike, not a production build or a full end-to-end product proof of concept. Reading and matching alone do not prove barcode writing/validation, final-file read-back, portal operation or production accuracy. Name the result accordingly; avoid an umbrella label that implies all filling routes were tested.

Requirements specify the desired behaviour and observable acceptance criteria. A behavioural prototype illustrates those requirements. Technical validation supplies evidence for uncertain implementation assumptions; it need not become a new product-building workstream or make every requirement wait for full implementation.
