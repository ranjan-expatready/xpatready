### N-B2. Day 7 runs on an assumption that forbids it (gate 3)

**Where:** doc L263 ("it gives the Account manager no ticket key, so the account manager's delegation runs no ticket recipe"); rows 35 (L322, the account manager assigns the ticket to themself), 40 (L328, sends a reply on the ticket), 45 (L333, replies), 46 (L334, sets `RESOLVED`); §2.2 step 6 L126 ("the account manager's reply sent on the ticket under the person's own ticket key"); table (b) L58; G-17 L406.

**What is wrong.** Assigning, replying and changing status are `system/support-ticket:edit` acts (SA-10 v2.5 §10 L541; SA-10-5, SA-10-14). If the tech lead's mapping gives the Account manager no ticket key, as L263 assumes, rows 35, 40, 45 and 46 cannot happen. If the mapping gives the person ticket keys (as rows 35–46 need), then the person holds at least `system/support-ticket:list`, and IMP-22 rule 1 allows the delegation to hold it too, so "no ticket key in the delegation" is no longer forced by the sources: it becomes a design choice the document has not stated or justified. G-17 mixes the two readings: "the Account manager's delegation holds no ticket key" is written as a consequence of an Unknown, not as a choice.

**Fix.** Pick one reading and say it plainly. Either (a) assume the mapping gives the Account manager ticket keys, state that the delegation nevertheless holds no ticket key **by the Lead's choice** until the mapping is known (with the reason), and keep Day 7 as written; or (b) keep "no ticket key" and route the Day 7 ticket to a support agent who works it, with the account manager acting only on the firm (manual run, plan change). Align L58, L263, G-17, §2.2 step 6 and decision 11.
