It fails on two majors:

1. **C-1. The trust reminder goes to one person, not to every holder.** The Lead's rule "Who gets the trust reminder" (plan L113, from S3d-b m-2(b)) and the brief both say every holder of `agent/trust:reconcile` receives it. Test cases built from §6 and from §9 would contradict each other. This is the same kind of defect S3d-b rated major (N-2).

## 4. Findings

### Major

#### C-1. The W-12d reminder names one recipient; the Lead's rule says every holder of the reconcile key

**Where:**
- AW1-D19 (doc L140): "only reminds the person who holds the reconciliation";
- §3.10 (doc L302): "to the person who holds the reconciliation";
- AW-1-100 (doc L442): "the assistant SHALL create one reminder for the person who holds the reconciliation (the holder of `agent/trust:reconcile`, AG-6-46)";
- US-77 (doc L871): "a person who holds `agent/trust:reconcile` … that person receives one reminder".

**Evidence.** Plan L113, "Who gets the trust reminder (the Lead, 11 Oct, S3d-b m-2(b))", says: "every holder of `agent/trust:reconcile` in the firm receives it; with no holder, the delegating Billing user does, as before." CN-SA-06 L293 also says "the Billing assistant reminds whoever holds `agent/trust:reconcile`". The brief for this check names the same rule.

AW-1-100 says "one reminder for the person". Where a firm has two holders (for example the Owner and an Admin who was given the key), a builder would have to guess which one gets it. Item D's test cases would test a single recipient. The version 0.3 row (doc L8, part (4)) lists W-12d edits for cadence and figures only, so this rule was never applied.

**Fix.**
- AW-1-100: "… SHALL create one reminder for each holder of `agent/trust:reconcile` in the firm (`00-step3-plan.md` 'Who gets the trust reminder') …".
- AW1-D19 and §3.10: change "the person who holds" to "every person who holds".
- US-77: add a Given with two holders, and a Then where each receives one reminder.
- Optional, labelled Proposal or sent to the team as a question: say that a firm with more than one Billing assistant delegation still sends one reminder per holder per period, for example under the double-run check on `actGuardKey` (AW-1-18).

#### C-2. Withdrawing a pending offer is allowed in §6 and forbidden in AW-1-6, the §6 diagram, §9 and §10

**Where:**
- **The new exit:** §6 table (doc L528): "PENDING_ACCEPTANCE | ENDED | the person who set it up withdraws the offer before it is accepted … | audited; reason WITHDRAWN *(restored by the Lead after review S3d-b m-9)*"; §7 `endReason` adds WITHDRAWN (doc L570); `proposals.md` L172 records the decision ("the person who set it up may withdraw it while pending").
- **Text that contradicts it:**
  - AW-1-6 (doc L330): "only the named person MAY decline or end it, with no key; no key and no act SHALL let anyone else stop or end it";
  - §6 diagram (doc L520): "No transition is open to anyone but the person, except deactivation or suspension and the four pause reasons";
  - §9 (doc L997): "The Owner sets up a delegation for a person who never accepts → it stays PENDING until the person accepts or declines; nothing runs; the Owner may switch the assistant off meanwhile";
  - §10 acts table (doc L1114): "Accept, decline, end one's own delegation | the named person only, no key; no key lets anyone else stop or end it". No row lets the person who set it up withdraw the offer.
- **Gaps:** §7 `endedBy?` (doc L570) lists only "the person, or the actor of the deactivation or suspension". No user story covers WITHDRAWN.

**Evidence.** When the Owner sets up the offer, a withdrawal is an act by someone other than the named person that moves the record to ENDED. AW-1-6 forbids that act, and the §6 row allows it.

A builder of the permission check working from AW-1-6 would refuse the Owner's withdrawal. A tester generating from §9 L997 would assert that the offer stays PENDING until the person answers. A tester generating from §6 L528 would assert that it can be withdrawn. This is the kind of contradiction S3d-b rated major (N-2).

The §6 row's reason, "it never took effect, so no one ends another person's delegation", is sound. It is simply not carried into the requirement, the diagram, the edge case or the acts table.

**Fix.**
- AW-1-6: add "the person who set up an offer MAY withdraw it while it is PENDING_ACCEPTANCE (it never took effect), audited, reason WITHDRAWN".
- §6 diagram L520: add "and the withdrawal of an unaccepted offer by the person who set it up".
- §9 L997: "… until the person accepts or declines, or the person who set it up withdraws it".
- §10: add an acts row "Withdraw an unaccepted offer | the person who set it up, no key".
- §7 `endedBy?`: add the withdrawer.
- Add one story, for example a US-1 bullet: the Owner withdraws a pending offer; the state is ENDED (WITHDRAWN); the act is audited; a new offer needs a new acceptance.
- Move the process note "*(restored by the Lead after review S3d-b m-9)*" out of the §6 table and into the version row.
