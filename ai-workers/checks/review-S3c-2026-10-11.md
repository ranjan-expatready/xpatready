## Findings

### Blocking

**B-1. A path for eligibility content to reach a lead without the consultant**
- **Where.** `CN-AG-12.md` L22 (AG-12-38, last two sentences) and L24 (BR-15); against `CN-AG-03.md` L31 (BR-19) and `AW-01` L391 (AW-1-90).
- **What is wrong.** AG-12-38 says a marked template "SHALL NOT be sent automatically to a lead" and BR-15 says it "is sent by an AI assistant only after a person approves the send, and never automatically to a lead". Read together, an assistant MAY send a template with eligibility content to a lead once any person with the sending key approves it. That approver need not be the consultant and need not be licensed (the AI-text licensed-approver default does not apply to an unedited template, AG-11-79). AG-12-38 also says "A person sending a marked template by hand is unaffected." The founder decided the opposite: AIW-D41 "No eligibility content (score, band, *likely*, levers) in any automatic or AI-drafted message to a lead; eligibility reaches a lead only through the consultant's audited share (AG-3-38)". The maker's own BR-19 says "Eligibility SHALL reach a lead only through the consultant's audited share of the client-facing artifact (AG-3-38)", and AW-1-90 says "No message an assistant prepares for a lead SHALL contain an eligibility outcome, score, band, *likely*, lever or promise". Three of our files say "never"; this one says "with approval".
- **Fix.** AG-12-38 and BR-15: "A template marked as containing eligibility content SHALL NOT be sent to a lead by an automation or an AI assistant, approved or not; eligibility reaches a lead only through the consultant's audited share (AG-3-38; AG-03 BR-19)." Then the Lead decides whether a person hand-sending such a template to a lead is allowed (BR-19's second sentence says no) and words AG-12-38 and BR-19 the same way.
