## 5. Findings

### Major

#### N-1. FD-17 is quoted in part, not "in full, in FD-17's own words"

**Where:** AW1-D19 (doc L139).

**Evidence.** AW1-D19 writes part (a) in its own words: "a mandatory funds designation on every client-money record". FD-17 (Founder Decisions Log v1.5 L53) reads:

> "(a) a mandatory `fundsDesignation` (TRUST \| OPERATING) on every client-money record — quote, retainer, deposit, invoice line, payment, refund"

The paraphrase drops the two values and the six record names. Those names are what CN-AG-06 item 1 maps record by record (CN-AG-06 L36–L45).

AW1-D19 also paraphrases FD-17's Practice Pack sentence as "The rules come from the firm's Practice Pack (FD-22)". It leaves out FD-17's out-of-scope list: "the platform holding or moving funds (FD-12 stands — the platform never intermediates funds), bank-feed integration, three-way automated reconciliation".

The brief and AIW-D72 ("as the Founder Decisions Log words them") ask for FD-17 in full, in its own words. The meaning AW-01 gives is no longer wrong. But a reader comparing it with the log would find part (a) shortened, and part (a) is where the six record names live.

**Fix.** In AW1-D19, replace the part (a) paraphrase with FD-17's words. Add the pack sentence and the out-of-scope sentence in quotation marks, word for word from L53, or quote the whole of the "In scope for v1 … three-way automated reconciliation." passage once.

#### N-2. Two rules for the per-run spend cap

**Where:**
- AW-1-53 (doc L386): "When AI is on but an AI Doer is gated, a model call fails or a spend cap is reached, the assistant SHALL skip only the model step, record why, … and continue its rule steps";
- AW1-BR-16 (doc L496): "a failed or capped call skips only the model step";
- §3.6 (doc L285): "A failed or capped model call skips only the model step and the rest of the run carries on by rule".

Against:
- AW-1-54 (doc L387): "per assistant run, where a runaway run is stopped and parked for a person";
- US-41 (doc L745): "the run is stopped and parked";
- SA-12-33(c) (CN-SA-12 L144): "per assistant run (a runaway run is stopped and parked for a person, from day one)";
- CN-AG-11's halt table (L145): "parks a runaway run".

**Evidence.** For the per-run cap, three places say the run carries on by rule and two say the run stops. The change note wins on spend caps (CN-SA-12 L26 is their one home).

Step 3 item D builds test cases from acceptance criteria. A tester working from AW-1-53 and BR-16 would expect the readiness report to be built without its paragraph after a per-run cap. A tester working from US-41 would expect the run to be parked.
