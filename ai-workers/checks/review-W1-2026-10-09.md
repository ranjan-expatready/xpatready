**Not ready to go to the founder as "evidence complete", and not ready to start W2.** The reading work itself is strong: 0 word-for-word quote mismatches in 141 quotes checked, and the "read in full" claims held up in all 9 spot checks. But there are **3 blocking findings**, all about conclusions, not quotes:

1. The competitor comparison (field-scan §4 and §7) was built on the 3 Oct snapshot. That snapshot does not contain the four AI Doers, so the "we start behind" story is out of date (B-1).
2. The documents disagree about whether AI is in Release 1, and nobody has said which document wins (B-2).
3. The product requirements do **not** enforce *licensed* sign-off, but the workstream rules and AIW-D11 assume they do (B-3).

All three must be fixed or ruled on by the Lead before the W2 brief is written.
