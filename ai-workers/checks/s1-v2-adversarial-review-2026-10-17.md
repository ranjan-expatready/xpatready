## (d) What in v2 survives scrutiny and should be kept

- **The box address** (copy owner × whose fact × master field × row × format × overflow). It is the one idea that makes family boxes, per-person copies and history overflow correct, it is absent from today's code (verified in the research note), and it is explained with a concrete IMM 5707 example. Keep it; enumerate its vocabulary.
- **Reuse-first matching with learned labels, context-keyed** ("Family name" under the spouse heading never matches the applicant's family name). Right, simple, and it makes the AI's share shrink with every form.
- **Code reads and checks, AI judges, people decide; nothing live until two people have handled it; AI never sets a value at fill time.** The principle set in §1 is sound and matches the team's SA1B-D16 without copying it.
- **Scored before trusted**: precision and recall per AI step on a hand-verified answer key, with a step switched off below its level. Keep; add the numbers.
- **One shared section library with each form's own layout kept as a display layer** (§5). It resolves "same section, different names and fields per form" cleanly.
