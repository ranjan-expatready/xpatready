## Blocking (gate breach)

### B-1. A chart cell reads as the AI persona approving the AI's work (gate 8, FD-16)
- **Where:** MD L36, column "AI persona beside it": "**Firm assistant** (shared with the Owner, §2.4); approves what the Case assistant prepares". HTML L136, inside the purple "AI persona beside it" box: "**Firm assistant** (shared with the Owner) Approves what the Case assistant prepares".
- **What is wrong:** the grammatical subject of "approves" is the Firm assistant. On the page it sits in the AI cell. A buyer or regulator reads: an AI persona approves what another AI persona prepared. That is exactly the sentence FD-16 forbids: "no model output is ever a decision … every output is human-approved before it has effect" (FDL v1.5 L52); "Nothing AI writes takes effect without a human approval" (Product Vision v1.0 L1489); AIW-D26 (decisions.md L30).
- **Fix:** move the clause to the human column ("Case Manager … approves what the Case assistant prepares, by default: SA6-D2") and leave the AI cell as "Firm assistant (shared with the Owner)". Add one line under §1.1: "No persona ever approves anything; the approver is always the person named in the left column."

### B-2. Business-words gate not met: terms used without explanation, glossary incomplete (gate 6)
- **Where (MD; same places in HTML):** "four-eyes" L79, L84, L255, L262, L327 (never explained, not in §8); "LMIA" L81, L90, L214 (not in §8); "IRCC" L89, L175, L177, L349; "ESDC" L90; "procedural-fairness responses" L80; "box maps" L168, L177; "diffs editions" L168; "Finder" L160; "Qualifier" L151, L291 (the Doer glossary line L339 names Mailer, Reader, Minder, Filler only); "hash chain" L271; "Restricted personal-data fields" L154; "dry-run" first used L134 and only explained at L246; "permission key" and the fourteen `agent/…:…` strings in "Who approves" (L84, L102, L119, L138, L268) with no sentence saying what a permission key is or why a business document names one.
- **Why it is a breach:** README §0 rule 4b ("explain any unavoidable term where it appears"); PROGRESS "Per step" row 1 gate "business words only"; the Lead's own read (PROGRESS log, 10 Oct 13:05: "too register-like to sell with"). A licensed consultant knows LMIA, IRCC and ESDC; an investor does not; neither knows "four-eyes" or `agent/case:review-approve`.
- **Fix:** add glossary entries for four-eyes, LMIA, IRCC, ESDC, procedural fairness, box map, Finder, Qualifier, hash chain, Restricted field, dry-run, permission key. In the §2 preamble (L67) add one plain sentence: "A permission key is the product's own name for 'this role may do this'; we name it so the approval can be checked against the firm's settings, never as a new rule." Consider moving the key strings to a footnote per card so the card body stays in business words (the proposals rule, L23, still satisfied).
