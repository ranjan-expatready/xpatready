### Major

**M-1. A trust move's settling payment does not fit the usual trust flow, and the fee-milestone link is undefined.**
- **File and change.** `CN-AG-06.md` change 6:
  - AG-6-47 (L161);
  - the move row of the path table (L156: "`TrustMovementEvent`, and the settling `Payment`");
  - the §8 criteria (L171).
  - Also change 3, AG-6-44 (L99, "No automation or AI assistant SHALL record, edit or refund a payment"), and the Lead's plan rule L97.
- **Evidence.**
  1. **The deposit flow is blocked.** In the usual trust flow, the client pays a deposit into trust and the firm takes it as the fee is earned. On signature the Biller "cuts the **deposit/first-milestone invoice**" (AG-06 L328; AG-6-32). The online payment takes that line's designation, trust (L152), and the invoice becomes PAID.
     - When the fee is earned, the firm records a move. Linked to that invoice, the move is refused, because "the move SHALL be refused for more than that invoice's outstanding balance" (L161), which is now zero.
     - Linked to the fee milestone instead, "the settling payment SHALL be written against that milestone's invoice when one is issued … in the same way" (L161). That is the same paid invoice, so the payment would overpay, which AG-6-21 refuses ("a payment **SHALL NOT exceed the outstanding balance**", L375).
     - The note does not say whether the move is then refused or recorded with no payment. The most common trust movement therefore cannot be recorded as written.
  2. **"When one is issued" reads two ways.** Neither reading works.
     - *"If one is already issued."* A move against a milestone not yet invoiced writes no payment. The draw-mode invoice the Biller issues later (AG-6-10) is then swept OVERDUE and dunned. That is the S3c-d M-4 defect again.
     - *"At the later moment it is issued."* A payment is written automatically, outside the move's "same atomic act". AG-6-44 says no automation records a payment (L99), and the path table names no such path.
  3. **Single-invoice mode is never settled.** In this mode "one invoice is issued with a schedule of expected payments" (AG-06 L211), so a milestone has no invoice of its own. A move linked to the milestone settles nothing, the installment goes OVERDUE (AG-6-11), and dunning chases the client.
  4. **The tests miss it.** The §8 criteria (L171) test only the issued-invoice link.
- **Fix.** Give one rule, with words for both modes. For example, in AG-6-47: "A move settles only money still owed:
  - (1) Linked to an issued invoice with an outstanding balance: the settling payment is written in the same act, up to that balance.
  - (2) Linked to an invoice, or to a fee milestone whose invoice, is already paid from trust-designated money: no settling payment is written; the link is the move's reason.
  - (3) Linked to a fee milestone not yet invoiced: no payment is written at the move. When the Biller issues that milestone's invoice (draw mode), it SHALL apply the moved amount in the issuing act, as a payment from trust. In single-invoice mode the settling payment is written in the move's own act against the plan's one invoice, up to the installment's amount."

  Then:
  - add path (3) to the path table;
  - change AG-6-44's last sentence to "No recipe or AI assistant …", or name the product-written paths;
  - add acceptance criteria for (2) and (3);
  - the Lead aligns plan L97.

**M-2. The note does not limit which invoice a move may settle: designation, billing party and currency are unruled, and L51 says the opposite of AG-6-47.**
- **File and change.** `CN-AG-06.md` change 1, L51, and change 6, AG-6-47 (L161) and the path rows (L151, L152, L156).
- **Evidence.**
  - **Two designations for one payment.**
    - L51: "A payment takes the designation of the invoice line it pays, whichever path records it (change 6)."
    - L161: the settling payment has "designation `OPERATING`".
    - The two rules disagree whenever the linked invoice's lines are trust-designated. §13 item 10 (L62) itself suggests that automatically raised lines take the retainer fee's designation, which is often trust.
    - Under L51 the settling payment would post IN (L151, L152: "IN, when the payment's designation is trust") and cancel the move's OUT, so the ledger would show no movement.
  - **No party or currency check.**
    - AG-6-47 records "client, amount, currency" and "a required link". It does not require the linked invoice or milestone to belong to the billing party whose trust ledger is drawn on, or to be in the same currency.
    - Paying one party's invoice with another client's trust money is the mixing of funds that FD-17 exists to stop. FD-17's reason (FDL L53) is "retainers landing undesignated in an operating account a live CICC breach risk".
    - A currency mismatch would write a `Payment` in a currency the invoice does not carry (AG-6-2, one currency per invoice; BR-8).
- **Fix.**
  - At L51 add "… except the settling payment of a trust-to-operating move, which is `OPERATING` (AG-6-47)".
  - In AG-6-47 add: "The move SHALL be refused unless the linked invoice or fee milestone belongs to the same billing party, practice unit and currency as the trust ledger it draws on; it settles only invoice lines designated `OPERATING` (an invoice with lines of both designations follows the answer to §13 item 11 (c))."
  - Add two §9 edge cases: a move linked to another party's invoice is refused; a move linked to an invoice in another currency is refused.

**M-3. Once a move writes a second `Payment`, the client's statement and the firm's collected revenue count the same money twice.**
- **File and change.** `CN-AG-06.md` change 6, AG-6-46 (L147, L152) and AG-6-47 (L161).
- **Evidence.**
  - AG-06 treats every `Payment` as money received:
    - AG-6-28 shows "billed / collected / outstanding";
    - AG-6-33 sends `invoice.paid` to AG-2's collected revenue and to AG-14;
    - BR-15 says these "can never disagree or double-count".
  - After round 4 the same money becomes a `Payment` twice:
    - once when the client pays the deposit into trust (L152, IN);
    - again when the firm moves it to pay a fee invoice (L161, a new `Payment` "so the invoice status … see[s] it as paid").
  - The per-client statement then shows the client paying twice the cash they actually paid. Collected revenue counts the unearned trust deposit as well as the earned fee. FD-17 exists to keep those two kinds of money apart.
  - Neither change 6 nor §13 says how the reports treat a trust-designated payment or a settling payment. This is not one of the open items 11 (a) to (g).
- **Fix.** Add to AG-6-46, or as §13 item 11 (h) with this default stated:
  - "Collected revenue (AG-6-29, AG-6-33, AG-2, AG-14) counts `OPERATING` payments only, the settling payment included.
  - A trust-designated payment shows on the per-client statement as money received into trust, and on the trust ledger, never as collected revenue.
  - A settling payment shows on the statement as 'applied from trust', never as new money received."

  The team confirms the layout with counsel (question 9). Knock-ons: AG-14, AG-2 attribution, and the AG-6-31 export.
