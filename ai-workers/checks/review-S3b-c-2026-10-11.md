- *Evidence:*

Reword US-7's second scenario to test only the rule steps, or mark it "pending Q33".

**N-12. The *ticket resolved* pause is put on waiting proposals, against the Lead's call (c) and AW-02's own glossary.**
- *Where:*
  - §6 L488 (side effect "waiting proposals PAUSED with the reason" for the RESOLVED row);
  - L489;
  - the proposal-item line L494;
  - AW-2-19 L344 ("A proposal SHALL be PAUSED while its run is paused");
  - §7 `PersonaProposal.pauseReason?` L510;
  - US-46 L679 ("its waiting proposals re-render stale").
- *Evidence:*
  - Call (c) (`00-step3-plan.md` L73) says the pause at RESOLVED "is a run-level reason (`TICKET_RESOLVED`), not a waiting-item reason; its home is AW-01 §7 `PersonaRun` pause reasons".
  - AW-02's glossary (L27) says "the reasons for waiting items are the four of CN-AG-11 change 6", and CN-AG-11 L127 allows "exactly four values".
  - As written, a reply draft waiting in the agent's reply box is frozen when the agent resolves the ticket. Under the one stop rule it then comes back stale, a pattern built for a safety stop, not for a normal ticket state.
- *Fix:* The Lead states what happens to waiting proposals at RESOLVED. Either they stay OPEN and usable, or they close as *not used*, as at CLOSED. AW-02 then aligns L488–489, L494, AW-2-19 ("paused for one of the four reasons"), `PersonaProposal.pauseReason` (four values only) and US-46.

**N-13. The help-article duty cannot run under the new support-run rule.**
- *Where:* AW-2-16 L341; AW-2-68 L405; US-23 L619; §3.6 L302; §6 L479 and L487–489.
- *Evidence:*
  - AW-2-68: "A help-article draft SHALL be made only on the delegating agent's manual run on a resolved ticket".
  - AW-2-16: "at `RESOLVED` it SHALL be paused", and "Starting the persona again on the same subject while a run is open SHALL continue the same run".
  - A PAUSED run is still open (§6 L491, "any open"). Its only way out is "a firm reopen within the window" (L489).
  - So the agent's request for an article continues a run that is paused and cannot act. US-23 expects a Writer call. After the reopen window the ticket is CLOSED, not "resolved", so the duty has no ticket state in which it can run.
  - SU treats the article as its own recipe ("its *article draft* recipe", SU L82) and runs it by hand "on the `RESOLVED` ticket" (SU L299).
- *Fix:* Make the article draft its own run (own recipe; `startedBy` DELEGATING_PERSON) that may open on a RESOLVED or CLOSED ticket and is not affected by the ticket-resolved pause. Or exempt the article recipe from AW-2-16's "same run continues". AW-2-68 then says which ticket states allow it. This is a Lead's call.

- *Evidence:*
