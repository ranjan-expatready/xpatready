## Blocking

### B-1. The Money Matcher has no trigger that exists on the sources for a firm with a bookkeeper (gates 1, 4, 11)

**Where.** §1.1 trigger (a), L66; §2.1 step 1, L98; §8 7.3, L325 and Day 44, L376; §9 G-4, L431; §11 decision 8, L494.

**What is wrong.**
1. The document says the match starts from "a hand-off from the Case assistant's Inbox Filer (W-07) when the Sorter has classified an inbound message as a payment notice ... (the hand-off is the Case assistant's Proposal, `case-assistant.md` §2.3 step 5)". The template's §2.3 step 5 (`case-assistant.md` L177) reads, in full: "a government letter → W-03 (2.4); a client question → W-08 (2.5); a lead message → the Intake assistant". There is no payment-notice hand-off and no hand-off to the Billing assistant anywhere in that document. The citation is wrong, and the Proposal the Billing assistant leans on has not been made by anyone.
2. The hand-off is said to carry "only the values the classification structured (amount, currency, date, reference, the party a person linked)". The Sorter "SHALL (a) propose the file ..., (b) classify the message, (c) file each attachment ..., and (d) emit `message.received` carrying the classification" (AG-10-37, L419). Classification is a label from a firm or pack list; nothing in AG-10 or AG-11 extracts an amount, a date or a reference from a message body. The Reader extracts from documents (AG-11-66), not from messages. The structured values that §2.1 step 3 matches on come from no specified capability; the manual path ("the Billing user types the match by hand", L70) is the only sourced one.
3. The alternative trigger, "a manual run by the Billing user who has an e-transfer or cheque in hand (AG-11-7)" (L66), needs `agent/automation:run` ("Manually run an automation on an entity": Owner, Admin, Case Manager only, SA-06 L879). The Billing / Finance role does not hold it, and the document's own "never holds" row says so ("every ... automation key | the Billing user does not hold them", L59). The same gap hits trigger (c) "manual, from the collections queue's 'send reminder'" (L66; §2.3 step 1 "run as a persona request (AG-11-7)", L125) and trigger (d) "on demand before the accountant's export" (L66; §2.4 step 1, L137).

So in the solo firm of §8 everything works because the consultant is the Owner; in the team firm of §8.1 the bookkeeper can neither receive the hand-off (it does not exist) nor run the persona by hand (no key). §8.1 L409–L410 does not notice this: it says the case officer links the notice and the bookkeeper records the match, with the hand-off assumed in between.

**Fix.** (a) State the trigger on the sources: the person's link of the notice in the Unmatched inbox (`agent/communication:resolve-unmatched`, AG-10 L859) produces `message.received` with the classification (AG-10-47); the persona's rule starts from that event and from the invoice facts only; the amount, date and reference are typed by the person in the product's own "Record payment modal" (AG-06 §3.4 Key screens, L266), which the proposal pre-fills with the invoice and party only. If the maker wants values read out of the message, label it as a new Reader-type capability (Proposal) and add it to the proposals row of L57, not to the Case assistant's credit. (b) Either drop "manual run by the Billing user" or add a proposals row asking the team whether `agent/automation:run` belongs on the Billing / Finance starter role (the founder accepted the SA-06 defaults on 7 Oct, SA-06 L667, so this is a founder item like decision 6). (c) Rewrite §8.1 Days 7 and 44 to show who, in a team firm, triggers the match. (d) Correct G-4 and decision 8 to say the hand-off must be added to the Case assistant document at integration (gate 11), not that it is already there.

## Major

### M-1. The two hard-limit readings are held to different standards, and the stricter one is applied to the less consequential record (gate 2)

**Where.** §1.2 L75; §2.1 step 5 L102; §2.5 step 6 L155; §4 L214–L215, L226; §11 decisions 2 and 3, L488–L489; proposals L57.

**What is wrong.** Reading (a): the persona "never creates even a DRAFT invoice; its approval opens the product's own builder pre-filled and the person saves and issues" (L488). Reading (b): "the approving click on a proposed payment match is that person's own act under `agent/payment:record`" and the approval "runs the record-payment flow with the values pre-filled" (L489), so the immutable Payment fact (AG-6-20) is written by the engine the moment the person clicks approve. A DRAFT invoice is editable, carries no number ("Drafts never consume numbers", BR-3, L419) and is not among AG-6-38's "Sensitive money actions" (L402); a recorded payment is immutable ("a Payment/Refund SHALL never be edited or hard-deleted", AG-6-1), fires `invoice.paid` to AG-2 and AG-14 (AG-6-33), issues a numbered receipt to the client (AG-6-22) and auto-exits dunning (AG-6-12). The document forbids the persona the harmless record and lets a one-click approval write the consequential one. The sources support reading (a) only as a design choice (nothing in AG-06 or IMP-27 defines "billing entry"); they support reading (b) only if a `record_payment` verb exists, and the document itself says none does (G-2, L429) and that AG-11-57 executes "the action", so the verb would have to be invented for the engine to run. Reading (b) is, in plain words, a persona recipe action that writes a Payment fact on a person's click; whether that counts as "the person records" is exactly what the maker's own automation-bias row warns about ("a wrong match recorded in one click is the realistic failure of this persona", L498). The sources offer the exact parallel of the invoice builder: the "Record payment modal (method, amount ≤ balance, currency, reference, date, payer, notes)" (AG-06 §3.4 Key screens, L266).

**Fix.** Apply one standard. Recommended: the approval of a proposed match opens the product's own Record payment modal pre-filled (party, invoice, amount, method, date, reference, funds tag), and the person's save in that modal is the record under `agent/payment:record`. Then G-2 needs no new verb, decision 3 falls away, the automation-bias safeguard (open the candidates) becomes the modal itself, and reading (a) and (b) say the same thing. If the Lead prefers reading (b), say in §2.1 step 5 and §11 decision 3 that it requires a new Conductor verb (record payment) run by the engine on approval, and put that verb in the L57 proposals row beside the two item kinds.

### M-3. The worked example's draw invoice ignores the installment sweep (gate 4)

**Where.** §8 Day 30, L355 ("Assumption: the draw invoice carries the firm's default terms, due Wednesday 9 December, Day 37"); Day 33, L359.

**What is wrong.** The signed schedule says installment 2 is "due 2 December 2026" (L303). AG-6-11: "A **Conductor sweep** SHALL mark installments/invoices `OVERDUE` when the due date passes with a balance ... and SHALL raise a **Notification** to Billing / Finance"; the §6 table (L465) adds "start/continue dunning" on the OVERDUE transition, and the installment lifecycle is "`SCHEDULED → DUE → INVOICED (draw mode) → PAID`, or `OVERDUE` (sweep)" (L472). With the assumption as written, the installment is overdue from 3 December while its invoice is not due until 9 December: the sweep marks the installment OVERDUE on Day 31, notifies the consultant and starts dunning, none of which appears in the example; the payment on Day 33 then clears an overdue installment, not a current one. The simpler reading of draw mode ("each installment, when due, generates an invoice", §3.2 L210; "the plan tracks which installments are invoiced/paid") is that the draw invoice is due on the installment's date.

**Fix.** Either make the draw invoice due 2 December (Day 30) and show the courtesy step on Day 27 (Sunday 29 November, so deferred or fired per the firm's business-hours rule, labelled) and the due-day step on Day 30, with the Saturday payment on Day 33 clearing an OVERDUE invoice and installment after the Day 31 sweep; or keep net-7 and add the installment's OVERDUE on Day 31 with its notification. Either way, label which reading of "when due" the example takes (AG-06 §13 Q3 territory, G-10).

### M-5. One unbilled-work rule would propose a second invoice for money already on a single-invoice plan (gates 1, 4)

**Where.** §2.2 step 3, L115 ("a milestone reached with no draw line, where the plan is not in draw mode").

**What is wrong.** AG-6-10: a plan runs in "draw mode (each due installment generates a milestone-draw invoice) or single-invoice mode (one invoice, scheduled partial payments)". In single-invoice mode the milestone's money is already on the one issued invoice; a milestone "with no draw line" is the normal state, not unbilled work. A plan always exists once the retainer is signed with a fee schedule (AG-6-32 "activate the seeded `PaymentPlan`"); a signed retainer with no fee schedule is the "bill manually" case the document already handles (L116). As written, the rule proposes a MILESTONE_DRAW line against money the client has already been invoiced for, which is the double-bill the guard in §1.1 exists to prevent.

**Fix.** Limit the milestone rule to draw mode where the installment is DUE and the Conductor has not generated its invoice (a product failure case, AG-11-27 dead letter), or drop it and rely on the plan; in single-invoice mode the persona reports the milestone only in the money note.
