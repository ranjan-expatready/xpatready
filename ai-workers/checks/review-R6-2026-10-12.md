## Verdict: **FAIL**

There is 1 blocking finding (B-1), 13 major findings (M-1 to M-13) and 19 minor findings (m-1 to m-19).

The direction is right, and most of it can be kept. Grouping approvals into one checkpoint, keeping every team floor, and refusing an AI approver are sound. It is close to what the founder asked for. Most findings need a row added to part (e) or (d), or a sentence corrected. One needs a design change: B-1, deadline dates.

**What is good.** Read these before the findings:
- It keeps every team floor and says so plainly. The team's words "Lowering an AI-text floor is impossible" are quoted exactly. Proposal D3 keeps FD-16.
- No AI ever approves anything (f, item 2). Nothing executes because time passed (f, item 1, AG-11-59).
- The rubber-stamp risk is taken seriously: the bundle screen reuses AW-1-33 (a), (b) and (c).
- Every team citation the brief listed exists, and the quotes match (§3). Labels [V]/[S]/[VC]/[I] are used throughout.
- It builds on AG11-D1/D2: AUTO / APPROVE / OFF are kept, and "when to ask" is added as a property of APPROVE.
- It states nine of its conflicts in part (e) instead of hiding them.
- No email addresses. No founder, team or customer names (but see m-5 on author names).

## 4. Does it give the founder maximum autonomy within the floors?

- **Where it meets his ask.** He gets one approval at the end where the client is not waiting, and stops only at fixed points. No approval is ever done by a second AI. There are switches, a live panel and a daily summary. This matches AIW-D95's "end-to-end job and approve it at the end" and "not blind autonomy".
- **Where it is more cautious than he asked, without asking him:** M-4 (hard limits presented as permanent), M-6 (tool choice), M-3 (assessment run not placed), M-12 (earned autonomy limited to labels and notes without saying so).
- **Where it goes past a floor or a team rule without saying so:** B-1 (deadline due times), M-1 (AW-1-71), M-2 (AG-11-60 and AW-1-15), M-5 (D71/D80 widened).
- **FD-16:** kept (D3). The reading "approving at the end is still before the effect" (line 77) is sound, provided B-1 is fixed.
- **Over-engineering and non-AI mechanics:** M-10. The core idea needs one property and one screen. The rest is optional.
