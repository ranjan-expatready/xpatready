## Findings

### Blocking

**B-1. Who the audit trail names as the actor of a persona run: AW-02 contradicts the SA-11 change note and does not cover every trigger.**
- *Where:* AW-02 L124 (AW2-D3), L296 (AW-2-17), L494–496 (US-6); against `change-notes/CN-SA-11.md` change 4, L121–123.
- *What is wrong.* AW-2-17 says a run the persona's own schedule starts is recorded with "`actorType` system with mode DELEGATED and the delegation reference", and US-6 tests exactly that. The SA-11 change note written the same day for the same audit model says: "For a DELEGATED entry the `actor` and `actorType` are the named person the AI assistant works for (`firm_staff` or `super_admin`); no new actor type is added", and adds to BR-3: "Actions an AI assistant took for a named person carry that person as actor, with `mode` DELEGATED and the `delegationId` (SA-11-2); never `system`, never a separate AI actor." IMP-1 (which AW-02 cites for `mode`) says the same: "The actor on the entry stays the named person". A developer given both files gets two incompatible rules, and US-6 fails against one of them. Second problem: AW-2-17 covers only three kinds of start (a person's act, the persona's own schedule, the product's own jobs). It does not say who the actor is when (a) someone other than the delegating person starts the run, for example a support lead assigns a ticket to an agent who has a delegation (AW-2-62; SU §2.1 step 2 says the actor is "the agent"), (b) the product's own automatic alert starts it (AW-2-69, SA-2-14a; SU §2.2 step 2 says "the account manager as actor"), or (c) a watcher item starts the Pack Drafter (AW-2-44 trigger (b)). As written, (a) would record the support lead as the actor of work done under the agent's delegation, which defeats the point of AIW-D33 ("the named person the persona worked for counts as the preparer").
- *Fix.* The Lead picks one rule for DELEGATED entries (the named person always, as CN-SA-11 and IMP-1 say, or `system` for scheduled runs, as the passed PA §2 and SU §2 say) and both AW-02 and CN-SA-11 carry the same words. Then rewrite AW-2-17 as: *the actor of every DELEGATED entry is [the chosen rule], whatever started the run; the starter (a person, the persona's schedule, a product event) is recorded separately on the run record (`startedBy`)*. Update US-6 and add a story for a run started by another person's act. If the Lead keeps `system` for scheduled runs, say why it is consistent with FD-16 and IMP-1; that reading may need the founder.
