## Blocking

### B-1. FD-17 is misstated as "a list, never a ledger"

**Where:**
- AW1-D19 (doc L129)
- AW-1-100 (doc L403)
- Risk R-B5 (doc L1055)
- The same reading in `counsel-pack.md` L25 (question 9: "no trust ledger in version 1")

**What is wrong.** AW1-D19 says: "**FD-17 and FD-11 stand**: the trust list is a list, never a ledger". FD-17 as ratified reads:

> "**Trust-aware from day one, LIGHT.** In scope for v1: (a) a mandatory `fundsDesignation` (TRUST | OPERATING) on every client-money record … (c) a per-client **trust ledger** with running balance; (d) a **trust reconciliation report**"

(Founder Decisions Log v1.5, L53.) CN-AG-06 item 1 (L28) applies exactly that to AG-6-24.

AW-01 therefore presents a ratified founder decision as forbidding what it requires. The W-12d duty is also cut for a world FD-17 has replaced: "payments carrying a funds tag" (AW-1-100). Under FD-17, every payment carries a mandatory designation and the product itself keeps the ledger and the reconciliation report.

The assistant computing no balance is fine and should stay. Calling that FD-17 is not.

**Fix:**
1. Restate AW1-D19 as: *FD-17 stands as ratified (designation, no automatic sweep, per-client trust ledger and reconciliation report, all the product's; CN-AG-06 item 1). No assistant sets a designation, moves money or computes a balance; the product's ledger and report are read, never written.*
2. Re-cut W-12d (AW-1-100) as one of these:
   - list TRUST-designated receipts not yet moved and hand the product's reconciliation report to the export holder; or
   - drop the duty if the product's report covers it.
3. Reword R-B5.
4. Tell the Lead that counsel pack question 9 carries the same misreading.
