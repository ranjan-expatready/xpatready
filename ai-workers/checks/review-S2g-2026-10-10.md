## Verdict: Fail, fixable in one round

## Blocking

### B-1. What the free Deadline watch is: the document does not say, and contradicts itself about AI being off

**Where:** doc L3, L17, L31, L80, L84, L88; master `ai-organisation.md` L61, L78, L184, L440; `decisions.md` AIW-D34 item 8.
**What is wrong.**
- The founder sells the Deadline watch as the free hook "for every file owner" with "every deadline on every file watched, with a record of every reminder" (master L61; AIW-D34 (8): "shown on its own beside every file owner, counted under the Firm assistant"); the master's glossary says the hook is "the free duty that gets a firm to switch AI on" (master L440).
- In this document everything a file owner receives is the product's Minder, mode AUTOMATION, "the persona never repeats it" (doc L84). Those reminders already work "on **every plan**" and "SHALL **never stop** because a firm disables automations" (AG-11-48, L583) and are "always on and cannot be switched off by any plan or flag" (SA-3-35, SA-03 L394). They need no AI and no persona. Offering them "free" as an AI feature would present the base product as the AI hook.
- The persona's own part is a list for the Owner and the Case Manager (doc L17). Its only model call is the *why* words (doc L88, L277), and under the Lead's call of 20:15 IST (PROGRESS, call 4: "the pack's reason text is fetched by rule as a record ... the expert's own plain-words line is display-only at render time") even that model call leaves the list, which then has no AI in it at all. The hook's one real AI step, reading deadlines from government letters (IMP-41: "Reader letter deadlines (the Guardian's only AI step)"), sits in the Case assistant (W-03; doc L277 says so).
- The document then contradicts itself on AI being off. Every duty requires the firm's AI switch on (doc L31, "Preconditions that hold for every duty"), and the delegation ends when "the firm switched AI off" (doc L80; IMP-1 rule 4). Yet doc L88 says that with AI off "the list is built by rule and delivered without them". Both cannot hold. Today the switch is locked for every firm until a Canada-region provider is recorded (AG-11-93, L701; AG-18-36, L382 "which is every firm's state"), so as written the hook's persona part runs for no firm at launch.
**Why it blocks.** This is the hook in the founder's sales story (master §1.2, §1.3, price card page 1). A buyer or investor who reads AG-11-48 next to this document will see that the free part is the base product and the AI part is locked. The document must say which it is before the founder sees it.
**Fix.** State the split in §0, §1.1 and §2.1, and raise it as a founder decision in §11: (a) the file owner's part is the product's Minder: Workstream 1, on every plan, *Specified, not built*, never sold as AI; (b) the Firm assistant's part is the managers' deadline list, which needs a delegation and therefore the AI switch (IMP-1 rule 4). Rewrite doc L88 so it matches L31 and L80: with AI off the delegation has ended, the list stops, and the Minder's reminders continue. Offer the founder the alternative honestly: since the list is built by rule and, after the Lead's call of 20:15, carries no model text of its own, it could be a product view outside the AI switch (the Active watches view and the compliance view already exist in the specification, AG-11 L359, AG-14-17 L374), with the persona adding nothing but the delegation record. The Lead then reconciles master L61, L78, L184 and L440 and the price card wording. Proposals row needed (listed below).
