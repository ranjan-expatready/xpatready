## Major

### N-1. The escalation filter was decided for a daily push; the list is now an on-demand report, and the filter makes two product views disagree

**Where:** doc L142, L149 (2.1 step 4), L329 (§7 item 6), L567 (§11 decision 5), G-6; proposals L76.
**What is wrong.** S2g M-3 objected to "a daily notification to the Owner and the Case Manager". AG-11-49 defines escalation as "alerting people above the file owner" (L584), and a notification pushed each morning fits those words. After the reclassification the list is "rendered when a person opens it" (doc L102), with no persona and no push. The product already gives managers the same information on demand, with no escalation filter:
- the Active watches view: "every armed Minder, its next step, its at-risk status — per case and firm-wide for the manager" (AG-11 §3.4, L359);
- the compliance view's "**upcoming deadline** horizon and list" (AG-14-17, L374), filtered only for at-risk items.

Under the filter, an Owner in a firm with escalation off would see other owners' approaching deadlines in the Active watches view and the compliance view, but not on this list. The filter no longer protects anyone, because the same rows are one click away. It also creates two product views that disagree. Both the document and the proposals row L76 still describe the concern as "a daily list ... sent to managers", which is no longer the design.
A second point: the list's composition rules (the filter, the *slipping* rule, one line per file) are now a Workstream 1 product feature written inside a Workstream 2 document (README rule 1). They need an owner on the team side.
**Fix (the Lead's call).** Either (a) drop the filter: an on-demand view is not "alerting", so the list mirrors AG-11 §3.4 and AG-14-17, and decision 5 and G-6 narrow to any *pushed* digest; or (b) keep the filter and say in 2.1 step 4 why the list should be stricter than the product's own manager views. Either way, reword proposals L76 and §7 item 6 to match. Mark 2.1 steps 4 and 5 as a change note to AG-14 or AG-11 for the team (Step 3), not a Workstream 2 behaviour.

### N-2. The "AI off" lines for W-14 and W-16 contradict the stop rules, the same fault S2g B-1 found for W-02

**Where:** doc L111 (W-14: "the next-moment list is still built by rule"), L125 (W-16: the checklist "is still produced if the firm keeps the recipe on with AI off (the task, reminder and checklist need no Doer)"), against L31 to L35 and L85.
**What is wrong.** The preconditions for every persona duty, W-14 and W-16 included, begin with "The firm's one AI switch is on" (doc L35). The stop rules say "Every persona run stops ... when the delegation ends because ... the firm switched AI off" (doc L85; IMP-1 rule 4). The two AI-off lines then keep the persona's rule-built parts running with AI off. Both cannot hold. This also bears on test 5 (the honesty claim): the persona keeps rule-only work that needs no model (the next-moment list, the task, the reminder, the checklist, the could-not-map report). The reclassification moved that kind of work to the product for W-02 but not here.
**Fix.** Rewrite L111 and L125 to match L85: with AI off the delegation has ended and the persona's run stops, rule parts included; the manual path is the Owner's own work (the register's clock still runs, SA-11-27). Add one sentence to §0 line 5 or to §11 decision 2: the persona also holds rule-only steps (list, task, reminder, checklist) that run only while AI is on, because they sit in the persona's recipe; whether they should move to the product, as W-02 did, is part of the founder's decision 1. S2g did not flag the W-14 and W-16 lines; this is a miss of that review, found now.

### N-3. The stop table reads the privacy register's HELD as a pause; SA-11-28 makes it an outcome that needs a reply

**Where:** doc L92 (W-16 row: "a `HELD` request keeps the task and reminder but adds no draft until released").
**What is wrong.** SA-11-28 (L386): "where law prevents full deletion, the request SHALL be marked **HELD/partial** and the subject SHALL be informed what was deleted and what is retained and why — never falsely "COMPLETED."" HELD is an outcome, and it is exactly when the honest reply that the persona's 2.4 step 5 drafts (CP-8-15, "never a false "fully deleted"") is required. Nothing in the sources speaks of a request being "released". The row would stop the persona drafting the very reply the law requires.
**Fix.** In the W-16 row, HELD becomes: the run continues; the reply draft states what was deleted, what is retained and why (SA-11-28); the run stops when the Owner records the outcome (`COMPLETED`, or `HELD` with the reply sent). Drop "until released".
