**m-1. The AG-11 "published" sweep still misses four lines, including the call-time check, although the note says its search found every line.**

SA-8-50 (`CN-SA-08.md` L88) says withdrawing the record "stops at once the model calls the record governs", so this check must read *published*. Read as written, it would let a call through on a record that is entered but not published, or withdrawn.

    - **L34, first clause of the AG11-D4 row:** "AI off until the processing terms are recorded, then one firm switch the Owner turns on". The note changes only the row's later clause, and says "nothing else changed in the line".

- **Fix.**
  - Add new words for L479 ("Each AI Doer call: a published AI provider record exists (SA-8-49; not withdrawn, SA-8-50) and the firm AI switch is on?"), L520, L34's first clause and L48's two phrases, each with "published" in place of "recorded".
