*Plain words used here.* A **change note** is a list of edits we propose to one of the team's requirement documents; the founder places it on Drive. ---

## Verdict: **Fail, fixable**

The twelve Super Admin and decisions-log notes are careful work and mostly hold: every one of the 93 "current words" quotes matches the 9 Oct snapshot word for word, every change rests on a recorded basis, and no new ID collides inside its own file. The set fails on **the client portal**. That note was written against Doc 4 (July), but the newer team files in the snapshot already moved the signing step out of the portal and cut the Release 1 employer portal down to one contact with no seats and no recruitment counts. As a result:
- the email passcode is written into a portal signing room the newer files have removed;
- the claim that the employer portal "needs no change" for the founder's AIW-D67 is false;
- a conflict between a team decision and the founder's FD-7 was missed.

Five major findings also need fixing before the founder sees the set. Two of them are about the founder's own decisions: the wording of AIW-D52 is widened, and FD-3 is missed.

## Findings

### Blocking

**B-1. The portal note targets a superseded document: the passcode goes into a signing room the newer team files have removed.**
- **Where:** `CN-CP-portal.md` L5–L6 (the document changed), change 1 L21–L46 (the step 4a passcode and CP-6-25), change 2 L52–L73.
- **What is wrong:**
  - The note edits Doc 4 v1.3, "last updated 2026-07-03" (Doc 4 L3), CP-6 §3.2 "The ceremony" (L1954–L1962). The newer AG-05 v1.9 (7 Oct), which is in the snapshot, records team decision CP1-D4 (AG-05 L46): "Signing from the client portal hands off to the AG-05 signing page (consent, fields, certificate) and returns the client to the portal; the portal never renders its own signing step." AG-5-53 (L453) says the same: "The portal SHALL NOT render its own signing step: consent, fields and the certificate of completion stay on the AG-5 signing page, and the same engine serves portal and emailed links."
  - Under rule 11 the module file wins over Doc 4. So step "4a" and CP-6-25 amend a ceremony that no longer exists in the team's specification. The passcode belongs on the AG-05 signing page only.
  - CP1-D4 comes from **CP-01 v0.1 (5 Oct)**, the team's current client-portal module. AG-15 v1.6 L7 calls it "the new client-portal document CP-01 v0.1 (5 Oct)", and SA-06 v3.1 L287 cites "`FINAL-ClientPortal-FR/CP-01-client-portal-app_v0.1_2026-10-05.md`". **That file is not in the 9 Oct snapshot**: the manifest covers only the Super Admin, Agent, techno-functional and program folders. CP-01 numbers its requirements CP-1-n (CP-1-53 and CP-1-93 are cited in AG-05 L253 and AG-15 L7), so "CP-6-25" would not even fit the current portal file.
  - This also leaves brief-coverage asks B2 ("always the most up-to-date data") and B15 ("Read all requirements") unmet for the portal.
- **Fix:** The Lead takes a new dated snapshot that includes the client-portal folder (README §4 step 1). Remove step 4a and CP-6-25 from Doc 4. Carry the email passcode only on the AG-05 signing page (check CN-AG-05 by name) and, once the file is in the snapshot, in CP-01. Re-target change 2 to CP-01's open questions, or drop it.

**B-2. "The employer portal needs no change for AIW-D67" is false: newer team files limit the Release 1 employer portal, and the founder's decision is left unapplied.**
- **Where:** `CN-CP-portal.md` L15 ("No portal requirement says the employer portal is read-only, so AIW-D67 needs no change here"); `CN-FDL.md` L17.
- **What is wrong:**
  - The Doc 4 part of the check holds: CP-9-6 upload (L3062), CP-9-7 pay (L3063), CP-9-9 sign (L3065) and CP-9-10 seat management (L3066) are all specified, and the recruitment tile (L3043) is the only read-only *view* there. (CP-9-16 offline read-only, L3072, is a technical mode, not a limit on the employer.)
  - But rule 11 makes the newer module files current, and they cut the Release 1 employer portal down:
    - AG-15 v1.6 (8 Oct) AG-15-12 (L346): "**Release 1: only the employer's primary contact has portal access; additional seats come in a later release.**" (AG17-D38.) So there is no seat management in Release 1.
    - AG-15-51 (L407): "In Release 1 the employer portal SHALL show nothing about recruitment candidates (no list, no counts, no notes)" (CP1-D7, CP1-D10).
    - AG-15 §3.2 (L209): employer items appear "**only when the file's portal switch is on**".
  - AIW-D67 (the founder, 11 Oct) reads: "the employer actions the client portal document specifies (upload, pay, sign, seat management) in Release 1". AIW-D67 is the founder's decision; AG17-D38 is a team decision. Under rule 11 the founder's decision stands, so AG-15 and AG-17 (and CP-01) need a change note.
  - AG-15 is in neither maker's list in `00-step3-plan.md` row C, so no note will carry the founder's decision.
- **Fix:** Withdraw the "no change" paragraph. The Lead assigns a change note to AG-15 (and AG-17 AG17-D38, and CP-01 once snapshotted) applying AIW-D67: seats and seat management in Release 1. Then reword `charter-edits.md` §3 if it relies on the false claim.
