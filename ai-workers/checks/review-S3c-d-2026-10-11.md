  - FD-17 (b) ties the move to "a delivered milestone or issued invoice", so this case is central, not an edge.
- **Fix.** Add to AG-6-47: "Where the move is linked to an issued invoice, recording it SHALL, in the same atomic act (AG-6-20), write a `Payment` fact against that invoice for the moved amount (method: transfer from trust; designation `OPERATING`), so the invoice status and dunning reflect it; the move SHALL be refused for more than the invoice's outstanding balance." Or, if the team prefers, put this as §13 item 11 (f) for the team and counsel. Then say in AG-6-12 that dunning stops when such a payment is written, as it does for any payment.
