Six quotes are wrong or misattributed (one invents source words), and the business-words gate is not met (the Lead's L1 already holds this). Everything else is major or minor polish.

## Blocking

### B-1. The worked example does not run end to end on paper (gate 3)

**Where:** doc L373–L376 (Day 0), L404–L406 (Day 6), L408 and L427 (chases), L435 and L440 (Day 30 and the audit list).

**What is wrong.**
- **Day 0 is out of order.** Step 0.2 has the "E-sign → onboarding" seed send the "first document request" and set "checklist items to `Requested` with `requestedDate` = Day 0" *before* step 0.3 creates the case and builds the Binder "at `Required` then `Requested`". A document request against a checklist that does not exist yet cannot run. The sources also disagree on which event starts onboarding: AG-11 §3.5 puts the first document request on `envelope.signed` (L371); AG-07 §3.2 says "Creating a case fires the `case.created` Conductor event (onboarding playbook: portal invite, SQR link, welcome — idempotent)" (L391). The document should order 0.3 before the request and record the AG-11 / AG-07 disagreement as a gap (it is not in §9).
- **Step 6.7 breaks a rule the document cites.** It sends the acknowledgement at `AUTO` for two email attachments because "the filing here was deterministic (reply token)". AG-11-96 (L708) and AG-10-51 (L439) say the opposite: an acknowledgement "SHALL send at `AUTO` only for a client upload made against a request" (a portal upload against a checklist item), and "A document that the Sorter filed from an inbound message SHALL be acknowledged only after a human has approved the filing". The reply token matched the *message*; the *attachments* were filed by the Sorter with Reader-proposed types that step 6.3 says "go to review". The acknowledgement must wait for Day 7.
- **The counts do not reconcile.** Day 26 says four chases were sent ("Repeat 3 of at most 4"); step 30.3 says "5 messages sent (3 chases, 2 acknowledgements)"; the audit list at L440 says "chase 1, 2, 3, 4 sent" and then "nothing was sent to the client except four template messages, one acknowledgement and one approved draft", which drops the three onboarding templates (0.2), the second acknowledgement (6.7), the re-request (14.3) and the acknowledgement of the Day 16 re-upload (14.4 "steps 2.2 to 2.6 repeat"). A regulator reading this list would count the sends and find the document wrong about itself.
- **Photographs never arrive.** Step 0.5 lists nine Binder items including Photographs; no later step receives them; step 30.2 nevertheless finds "no required document below `Received`" and 30.3 reports "9 documents with status and verifier". Either Photographs arrive on some day or the item is marked not applicable with a reason (AG-9-28) before Day 30.

**Fix.** Re-walk the example after the fix: order Day 0 as case created → Binder built → onboarding messages; move the Day 6 acknowledgement to Day 7 after the filing is approved (or make the attachments portal uploads); one consistent send count across 30.3 and L440 (list every client-facing send by day); give Photographs a day or a not-applicable step; add the AG-11 / AG-07 onboarding-trigger disagreement to §9.

### B-2. The persona's relationship to the four seeded recipes is undefined (gates 1, 4)

### M-5. A path for AI-chosen client text to send without approval

**Where:** doc L130 (§2.2 step 4, the italic sentence): *a reason sent unchanged from a PUBLISHED template is a template message at the recipe's setting*.
**What is wrong.** Once the firm has confirmed a reason library as standard messages, this sentence lets a reason go out at the recipe's setting (Send automatically by default). It does not say who picks which template. If the Drafter (a model) picks it, a model has chosen an action and a recipient-facing message, which IMP-26 rule 1 forbids ("model output can propose values … never actions") and which turns the AI-text floor (AG-11-53, AG-10-44) into a template-selection loophole. If a rule over the finding type picks it (wrong type → template R1), it is a template message and the sentence holds; the document must say so. IMP-28(c) also applies: a rejection reason that says what a document must show is status-adjacent content and defaults to "ask me first" for persona sends.
**Fix.** State: the template is chosen by a rule from the finding type, never by the model; the Drafter is called only when no template fits; a reason template the firm marks as status content is "ask me first" (IMP-28(c)).
