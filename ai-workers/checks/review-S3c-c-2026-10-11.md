### Major

**K-1. Pausing waiting items contradicts AG-16-37, which CN-AG-16 leaves "unchanged".**
- **Where.** `CN-AG-16.md` L44 (knock-on: "AG-16-37 unchanged (no setting change sends a waiting item)"), L40 (AG-16-55), L64 (AG-16-57); `CN-AG-11.md` L140 and L141. Source: AG-16 v1.13 L472 (AG-16-37) and L714.
- **Evidence.**
  - AG-16-37 (L472): "A change to AI Doers, autonomy, Team safeguards, supervised client messages or the standard-messages review SHALL apply **only to items created after the change**; an item already waiting in Needs you SHALL NOT be sent or executed because of the change".
  - AG-16-55 (CN-AG-16 L40): when the firm switches AI off, "Items already waiting in Needs you that the paused AI prepared SHALL be `PAUSED` … when the last reason clears, each SHALL be re-rendered stale and need a fresh approval".
  - AG-16-57 (L64) does the same for one assistant's switch.
  - So switching AI off now applies to items created *before* the change. That is the opposite of AG-16-37's first clause. The second clause (nothing is sent) still holds.
  - A developer reading AG-16 after the change sees two rules for the same items: by AG-16-37 they are untouched and approvable, by AG-16-55 they are paused and not approvable. AW-01's AW-1-20 (f) cites AG-16-37 as well.
- **Fix.** CN-AG-16 adds a change to AG-16-37: "… SHALL apply only to items created after the change, **except that switching AI off (AG-16-35) or an assistant off (AG-16-57) pauses the AI-prepared items already waiting (AG-16-55; AG-11-112 (e))**; no setting change SHALL ever send or execute a waiting item." Then list L472 under item 2's "Where" and drop "unchanged" from L44.

**K-2. Approving a `PAYMENT_MATCH` opens the Record payment modal "pre-filled", which as worded skips the typed-amount safeguard.**
- **Where.** `CN-AG-11.md` L149, AG-11-112 (b): "Approving an `INVOICE_PROPOSAL` or a `PAYMENT_MATCH` SHALL only open AG-06's own invoice builder or Record payment modal pre-filled for the approver". Against `CN-AG-06.md` L93 (AG-6-44). Source: `design/behaviours/billing-assistant.md` L524 (G-27) and L576.
- **Evidence.**
  - AG-6-44 sets the route. Candidates are shown read-only, "The person SHALL type the amount received; the view SHALL then mark the candidate …; the person SHALL pick an invoice; the Record payment modal (§3.4) SHALL open pre-filled with party, invoice and amount".
  - billing-assistant G-27 (L524) records this route as the payment safeguard: "the person types the amount; *exact* appears after; then the person picks", marked "Adopted by the founder (AIW-D57); provisional until counsel (AIW-D64)".
  - CN-AG-11 makes `PAYMENT_MATCH` an `ApprovalItem.itemType` (L123). An approval item sits in Needs you with an approve action (AG-11-57), and AG-11-112 (b) says approving it opens the modal pre-filled.
  - A developer building Needs you from CN-AG-11 would give the item an Approve button that opens the modal with the AI's candidate and amount. The person would never type the amount first.
  - AW-01's AW-1-97 reads it the safe way ("picking opens the Record payment modal pre-filled"), so the AG and AW files now say different things.
- **Fix.** Split (b). "Approving an `INVOICE_PROPOSAL` SHALL only open AG-06's invoice builder pre-filled … Opening a `PAYMENT_MATCH` SHALL only open AG-06's Reconciliation view for that billing party (AG-6-44), where the person types the amount before any candidate is marked; the item never pre-fills an amount; the Record payment modal opens only from the person's pick, and nothing is saved until the person saves it."

**K-3. The trust ledger is posted only by "a person recording a payment", so trust money paid online never reaches it.**
- **Where.** `CN-AG-06.md` L124, AG-6-46: "An entry SHALL be posted only by the act that records its source (a person recording a payment, a refund or a move); no one types a balance." Source: AG-06 v1.7 L368 (AG-6-16), L651 and L702.
- **Evidence.**
  - Online payments are recorded by the processor's verified notice, not by a person. AG-6-16 (L368) says every processor webhook "SHALL be processed idempotently". L651 says "invoice marked paid by the **webhook fact**, not the redirect". L702 calls for "signed + idempotent webhooks writing the immutable Payment fact".
  - A retainer deposit paid by card through the portal, designated trust, is therefore recorded with no person.
  - As worded, AG-6-46 either leaves that payment out of the ledger, so the running balance and the reconciliation report (AG-6-48) are wrong, or forbids it. FD-17 (c) needs "a per-client trust ledger with running balance" (Founder Decisions Log L53) covering every trust receipt.

**K-4. The trust-to-operating move is tied to AG-07's `CaseMilestone` (a government step), not to the fee-earning milestone FD-17 means.**
- **Where.** `CN-AG-06.md` L126, AG-6-47: "a required link to a **delivered milestone** (AG-07 `CaseMilestone`) or an **issued invoice**". Source: AG-07 v2.7 L820; AG-06 v1.7 L194, L209 and L224; Founder Decisions Log L53.
- **Evidence.**
  - AG-07 L820 defines `CaseMilestone` as "`type` (pack-defined: AOR/biometrics/medical/ADR/PPR/COPR/PAL or country equivalents)": these are government steps.
