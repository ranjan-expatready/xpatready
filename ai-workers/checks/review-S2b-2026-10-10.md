**Does it change what the meter counts, and is that stated?** It does not change the unit (one File Builder run whose readiness report the officer accepted, IMP-18) and the document says so (L348: "the Reader, Sorter and Checker calls are the product's, not the persona's; their cost still belongs to the file and stays in the scenario"; L352: F2 "is now an upper bound"). It does change what a "persona model call" is: the persona's own calls are the Drafter (replies, notices, response plans, letters, the summary paragraph, briefs) and, under N-2, possibly the LMIA Checker. **Is it in §11?** Yes, decision 3 (L631), with the alternative and its cost consequence named.
