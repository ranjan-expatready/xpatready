## 1. How to read the tables

- **Part A** rows apply a founder decision; the origin column names it. **Part B** rows are ours: a Lead ruling ("Adopted by the Lead; the founder may overrule"), a Workstream 2 proposal (IMP rows, `proposals.md`), or a team need we inferred from a gap. The founder may drop any B row.
- **Priority.** *P1 Blocks Release 1 AI layer*: the AI layer cannot be built or switched on as decided without it. *P2 Needed before pilot*: the AI can run, but this must exist before pilot firm one works on real files (safety, records, client money). *P3 Improvement*: consistency, naming, housekeeping, or Release 1 scope that is not AI.
- **Type.** *change*: a proposed edit to a team document. *question*: the team must answer before we can finish. *need*: the team must build or do something its documents do not yet describe. *(conflict)* marks a question where a team rule and a founder decision disagree.
- **Our detailed wording** points to the change note and change number (folder `ai-workers/requirements/change-notes/`), or to our file and section. Paths are relative to `ai-workers/`.
- **Status** is "Not yet sent" for every row.
