**Plain words used below**
- **Run record:** the product's log of one AI job: what started it, each step, each AI call, the outcome.
- **AI run log (`AiRunLog`):** the team's log of every single model call (AG-11 v1.12 §7).
- **Approval item:** one thing waiting for a person's yes in "Needs you", the approval list on the home screen (AG-11 v1.12 §7 `ApprovalItem`).
- **Audit entry:** the permanent record of who did what and when (AG-13).
- **Shadow:** a skill prepares work on real files that nobody sees; the product compares it afterwards with what the person actually did (AIW-D83).
- **Prepared file:** one Case Officer's AI job on one case whose readiness report the officer accepted (AW-1-75).
- **Product AI:** the AI calls the product makes for every firm whether or not any teammate is on: the Reader on uploads, the Sorter on incoming messages, the Checker inside a review (AW-1-15).

## (a) Summary for the founder (10 lines)

1. **No customer has yet told us the problem, the saving or the price.** The problems below come from the team's own requirement documents, and the business plan says plainly: "No customer evidence supplied" (`plan.md` v1.1 §2). Every saving and every price is still an Assumption.
2. **What is evidenced is the machine, not the market:** what each AI teammate does, who approves it, and that every step is logged (AW-01 v0.6; AG-11 v1.12; AG-13 via CN-AG-13).
3. **Only one teammate has a price unit:** the Case Officer's AI, per prepared file (AW-1-75; AIW-D60). The other three firm teammates ride inside the plan; the two Expat Ready teammates are never sold (AIW-D61).
4. **New finding on cost:** most of the AI bill for a file is product AI (the Reader reading uploads), which the specification says is "never metered or counted to an assistant" (AW-1-15). On a typical file that is about CAD 1.54 of CAD 1.72; the Case Officer's AI's own calls are about CAD 0.18 [I]. So the AI price per file pays for value, not cost, and the subscription must carry the reading cost.
5. **The real cost is the reviewer's time, and the product does not record it today.** The approval item stores when it was created and decided, not when someone opened it (AG-11 v1.12 §7). We need one small addition: the time an approval item was first and last opened.
6. **The product also cannot cost an AI call as specified.** The AI run log has no token or cost field (AG-11 v1.12 §7 `AiRunLog`), yet AW-1-55 requires cost "from the AI run log". Second small addition: tokens, pages and price on each log row.
7. **Everything else can be measured from records already specified:** preparation time and person-acts per file, acceptance and edits, accuracy in shadow, response times and spend (AG-14-39 via CN-AG-14 change 2 already lists most of these).
8. **Break-even, as arithmetic not a claim:** at CAD 30 a file, the firm needs about 52 minutes of net assistant time saved per file (or about 34 minutes of consultant time) for the price to pay for itself [I]. That is the number the pilot must measure.
9. **Your headline needs proof before it is public:** "AI that does the legwork" is only honest if the run records show the AI, not the rules, does a real share of the legwork (AIW-D92); "measured" needs a passed driving test (AIW-D83, D99).
10. **Cheapest way to learn, with no outside gates:** our own two teammates on our own work, the driving tests, the founder-consultant's own practice as pilot firm one, founding-firm order choices, and an honest website test.

## (c) Measurement plan, using the product's own records

**Principles (cited, not new).** Measures are firm-wide figures and "SHALL NOT rank or name staff" (AG-14-39 via CN-AG-14 change 2; AG-11-49; AW1-BR-20 "Assist, don't monitor"). Expat Ready's view across firms is aggregated and de-identified (CN-AG-14 change 2 knock-ons: "SA-09 platform analytics carries Expat Ready's own AI measures, aggregated and de-identified across firms (CN-SA-09)"). AG-14-39 already names most measures, including "time saved (labelled an assumption until measured against files done without AI)". This plan says which records feed each measure, how to compare, and where the records fall short. It is also the content of the "pilot measurement sheet" that `PROGRESS.md` Step 4 asks us to supply.

**The comparison the product already allows (no experiment needed).** Three kinds of file exist side by side in the same firm, the same month and on the same pack version:
1. **Files with no teammate**: no `delegationId` on their audit entries (AG-13-68);
2. **Files where a skill is in shadow**: the person does the work by hand and the hidden proposal is compared afterwards (AIW-D83; AW-1-120);
3. **Files with a live teammate**: DELEGATED audit entries and a `personaRunId`.

Group 2 is the cleanest baseline: the same file, done by hand, with the AI's answer recorded. Files must still be compared like for like: by program, number of documents and pages read (the Reader's call count, `AiRunLog`), and whether the file was imported (AG-18) [I].

### c.6 The two places where existing records cannot meet a requirement

Both are new fields on records the team already specifies, not new subsystems.

**G1. Tokens and cost on each AI call.**
- *Problem.* AW-1-55 requires cost "from the AI run log", and AG-14-39 requires "spend against the firm's caps". The team's `AiRunLog` holds "`id`, `firm_id`, `doer` (READER/SORTER/DRAFTER/CHECKER), `model`, `promptVersion`, `entityRef`, `runId?`, `actionId?`, `inputRef` …, `outputRef`, `confidence?`, `outcome` (OK/UNREADABLE/PROVIDER_ERROR/DISABLED), `latencyMs`, `createdAt`" (AG-11 v1.12 §7). It has no tokens, no pages read and no cost, so neither requirement can be met and spend caps cannot be enforced. None of the 13 changes in CN-AG-11 adds them (its change list, our read).
- *Existing capability.* The log row itself (AG-11-72, `AiRunLog`); provider responses report token use; the architecture file already recommends "tokens in/out, cached tokens, cost, latency" on each step (§4.5 item 1).
- *Proposal.* Add to `AiRunLog`: input tokens, output tokens, cached tokens, pages sent for OCR, the route and region, and the cost computed from a dated price table. The price table is configuration, as SA-03 already treats metering ("a new metered metric is configuration, not a migration", SA-03 v2.14 §11, quoted in `provider-and-cost.md` §1). Because AI logging is Workstream 2's subject, the Lead may write this as a further CN-AG-11 change (README rule 11).
- *Verification.* A test call writes one row with all fields; the sum of rows for a test file equals the provider's billing export for that call within rounding; a firm whose sum passes its cap gets the named failure `budget_exceeded` (architecture §6.4 item 1).

**G2. When an approval was opened.**
- *Problem.* The strongest objection to every price option is review time: "Show me the number before you show me a price" (`price-card.md` page 3), and the pilot was to measure "Review minutes per prepared file" (`provider-and-cost.md` §5, U8). The approval item records creation and decision only (AG-11 v1.12 §7), so prepared-to-decided time mixes waiting with reviewing. AG-14-39's "time saved" cannot be measured without it.
- *Existing capability.* AG-11-61 already re-renders an item when it is opened after a change, and "An approve request SHALL carry the rendering version the approver saw". The product knows the item is open; it does not keep the time.
- *Proposal.* Record the first and the last time an item (and, once built, the end-of-job bundle, autonomy §c.4) is opened for a decision, and report open-to-decision minutes **firm-wide only**, never per person (AG-11-49; AW1-BR-20). Because Needs you is the team's mechanics, this is a stated need for the team, not our design (11 Oct scope lesson).
- *Verification.* Opening an item writes the time once (first) and updates it (last); the firm report shows median minutes per item kind and never a name; the agreement alarm stays the approver's own (AW-1-33(c)).

**One honesty check that needs no new field.** The share of each file's steps that are AI calls, rule steps and person acts can already be counted (AW-1-28; AG-13-68 modes; `AiRunLog` rows). Section (d) needs it.

### c.7 The break-even the pilot must test (arithmetic, not a claim) [I]

Value per file = (person minutes saved in preparation − extra minutes spent reviewing) × the loaded wage. With the Job Bank medians and the 25% employer loading that `provider-and-cost.md` §3.6 labels Assumption (assistant CAD 27.47 → about CAD 34.34 an hour; consultant CAD 42.56 → about CAD 53.20 an hour), a per-file price pays for itself when the **net** saving per file is at least:

| Price per prepared file | Net assistant minutes needed | Net consultant minutes needed |
|---|---|---|
| CAD 25 | about 44 | about 28 |
| CAD 30 | about 52 | about 34 |
| CAD 40 | about 70 | about 45 |

These are thresholds to measure against, not savings. If the pilot measures less, option (a) loses on value, whatever it costs us.
