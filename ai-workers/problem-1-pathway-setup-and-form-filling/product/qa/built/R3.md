R3 · a minor's travel answers (P-230 (3), closes FQ-4; #493 superseded by the runtime lane's PR): on the Visitor visa (acceptance setup S6, S6a), every applicant under 18, the principal applicant included, is asked "If under 18: who is this person travelling to Canada with?" (both parents, one parent, another adult, alone) and "If under 18: what is the custody arrangement for this person?" (not applicable, shared, sole, adopted, guardian); both facts arrive through C3.6 with their answer values (P-158). Under 18 only (worked out on the application date), the checklist adds: alone or another adult → letter of authorization (both parents, naming the adult responsible in Canada) and a copy of the birth certificate; one parent → letter (from the absent parent, or the parent with sole custody) and a copy of the absent parent's signed passport or ID (not with sole custody); sole or shared custody → custody documents; adopted or guardian → adoption or guardianship papers. Each line's reason is short; its source sits beside it as the line's quote: canada.ca minor-children page (2025-01-23, read 2026-11-02; "The letter does not need to be certified." on the letter only), IMM 5881 E (05-2026) page 2 for the absent parent's ID, IMM 5484 (05-2026) page 1 for custody. An adult applicant gets none of the lines and, once the application date is in, is not asked the two questions. Answers stay on the case (QA-149), so they can be changed back.
