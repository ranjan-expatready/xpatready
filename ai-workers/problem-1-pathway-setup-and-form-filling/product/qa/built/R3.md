R3 · a minor's travel answers (P-230 (3), closes FQ-4; #493 superseded by the runtime lane's PR): on the Visitor visa (acceptance setup S6, S6a), every applicant under 18, the principal applicant included, is asked "If under 18: who is this person travelling to Canada with?" (both parents, one parent, another adult, alone) and "If under 18: what is the custody arrangement for this person?" (not applicable, shared, sole, adopted, guardian); both facts arrive through C3.6 with their answer values (P-158). Under 18 only (worked out on the application date), the checklist adds, following the official words: alone → letter of authorization (both parents) and a copy of the birth certificate; another adult → letter (naming the adult responsible in Canada) and the parents' signed passport or ID copies; one parent → letter (from the absent parent, or the parent with sole custody), a copy of the absent parent's signed passport or ID (kept with sole custody: which parent holds it is not recorded) and a copy of the birth certificate; sole or shared custody → custody documents; adopted or guardian → adoption or guardianship papers. Each line's reason is short; its source sits beside it as the line's quote, the official instruction that calls for the document word for word: IMM 5881 E (05-2026) page 2 for the letter and the absent parent's ID, IMM 5484 (05-2026) page 1 for custody, and the canada.ca minor-children page (dated 2025-01-23, read 2026-11-02) for the birth certificate, the adoption or guardianship papers and "The letter does not need to be certified." (on the letter only). An adult applicant gets none of the lines and, once the application date is in, is not asked the two questions. Answers stay on the case (QA-149), so they can be changed back.
