C8.1 · signed PDFs, Visitor visa part (M3-F; runtime lane; migration `20261102170000_c81_signed_scan`): the prepare view names who signs each form item ("person", "parent" for a child's form signed by a parent, "principal" for the family, "representative"), worked out by SH3's one rule (R7). `GET /api/runtime/items/:id` shows a form's signing steps; the case officer does them in order with `POST /api/runtime/items/:id/sign-steps/:key`: Printed (naming a generated copy of that form for that person), Signed by hand, then `POST …/signed-scan` (one PDF, JPEG or PNG judged by its bytes, stored with its sha256, never changed in place) marks "Signed scan received" and the item Ready; "Uploaded to IRCC" marks it Done and is refused until the filing is Ready to file. A step out of order, a print without a copy, a scan that is not a PDF or image, or a client doing any of it is refused in words; there is no "sign for the client" action.
