On Shared library › Fields, › Document types and › Sections a configuration author sees where an item is used first. Their own never-released, unused draft is deleted at once, with Undo. Anyone else's draft is deleted only after a second person approves. A released item that nothing current uses is retired through a second person and restored the same way. Retired items appear only under "Retired"; every picker and write route refuses them. On a combination, the author sets the filing order of its forms (POST /combinations/:id/forms/reorder), and the draft keeps it. Checks retire with their combination's Checks tab (question to the tech lead in the PR).
