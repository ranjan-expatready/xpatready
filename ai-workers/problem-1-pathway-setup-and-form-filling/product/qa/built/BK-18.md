BK-18 (QA-448, from QA-447 W5; C7.6, C8.2, C8.3) — PR #1691: a filing's professional record now says who really acted. "Prepared by · Reviewed by" on Ready to file, the self-review gate and the recorded filing all read the case's own work record — who typed or confirmed its facts, uploaded a document, entered a section, opened or resolved a check, set a checklist line, passed a warning with a reason, or anything else the case's audit lines hold — never who is named on the case team. A case officer assigned to a case who did nothing is no longer recorded as its preparer, and a representative who did the work and approved it alone is asked to confirm "I prepared and reviewed this filing myself" and recorded "Self-reviewed" with their name, licence number and time (P-154, P-510 (4), P-155 (1)). Opening a case and sending a filing for review are not preparing it: they name the person who stands behind the work only when the case holds nobody else's. The record says what was true when the review was made, so later work never rewrites it, and Submission to IRCC names the same preparer as Ready to file.
