BK-18 (QA-448, from QA-447 W5; C7.6, C8.2, C8.3) — PR #1691: a filing's professional record now says who really acted. "Prepared by · Reviewed by" on Ready to file, the self-review gate and the recorded filing all read the case's own work record — who typed, confirmed or checked a fact, ticked "none apply", added a row, uploaded or read a document, made a form copy or read a validated copy back, added or removed a form, signed or scanned, entered an online section, opened or resolved a check, set a checklist line, uploaded a fee receipt, passed a warning with a reason, or anything else a person did that the case's own audit lines hold, including the lines kept against one of its filings, items or submission groups — never who is named on the case team. A case officer assigned to a case who did nothing is no longer recorded as its preparer, and a representative who did the work and approved it alone is asked to confirm "I prepared and reviewed this filing myself" and recorded "Self-reviewed" with their name, licence number and time (P-154, P-510 (4), P-155 (1)). Opening a case, sending a filing for review, marking a filing Ready to file, adding or deciding a review finding, and changing the case team are not preparing it: they never name a preparer, and a case holding nobody's work says so rather than guess. The record says what was true when the review was made, so later work never rewrites it, and Submission to IRCC names the same preparer as Ready to file.
