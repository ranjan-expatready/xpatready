C6.4 storage slice (M3-C; PR for C6.4 storage, migration `20261102140000_c64_document_storage`): each line a person needs is a `checklist_line` row (its id is stable; it keeps its state when the answers take it away and bring it back). The case officer requests a line (`POST /api/runtime/checklist-lines/:id/request`: Requested; again is the same request) or marks it Not applicable with a reason (`…/not-applicable {reason}`: 400 on the field without one; a Not applicable line can be requested again). The officer or the case's own client uploads one PDF, JPEG or PNG of up to 20 MB (`…/documents`, multipart field `file`, judged by its bytes): Received; the file is the person's (`case_document.subject_id`), and a new upload keeps the old one as history (one current). A line that was requested, has a file or is Not applicable stays on the list, marked kept and not required, when the answers no longer call for it; a line nobody acted on goes, and cannot be acted on (409). Another firm's officer: 404; a client: 403 for request and Not applicable. Being checked, Approved and Rejected are states in the data with no route yet (C6.5, C8.1). QA-149 (same PR): a question that decides a line, form, rule or another question stays on the case once answered, so a child's "travelling alone" can be changed back to no.
