PF-14 — PR #1757 (runtime, map row 9; AG-10-1, AG-11-17..20): the app has one background job runner, pg-boss in its own schema `platform_jobs` of our PostgreSQL (created by pg-boss at start; Prisma never sees it). Rows code to PF-0p's `JobPort` (`ports/jobs.ts`) and inject `PlatformJobs`. A job queued twice with one key is queued once, and a used key (run, cancelled or failed) never queues again. A job can be scheduled for a time, cancelled by its key, or set on a cron read in a time zone: 09:00 Toronto runs at 13:00 UTC on 31 Oct 2026 and at 14:00 UTC on 1 Nov 2026. A failing job is retried with growing waits (Sample: 3 retries, 30 s doubling, at most 1 h), then lands on the dead-letter list with its name, key, firm and error. A job dropped by a crashed process reruns once its claim expires (Sample 15 min). Every job carries its firm, and its handler runs only after `firmScope` accepts that firm; a recurring job is platform-wide and fans out per firm. The worker starts with the app and stops cleanly on SIGTERM or app close. Nothing to see on a screen. Check: `tests/check_pf14_jobs.ts` (no server; real pg-boss on its TestClock, so no real waiting).
