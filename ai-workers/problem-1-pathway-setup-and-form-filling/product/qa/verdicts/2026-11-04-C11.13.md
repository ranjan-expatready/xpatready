## What works
* **QA-192 fixed.** The near duplicate carries only the producer's own words. Accepting it without a person's reason is refused with a 400 that names the close field.
  * With the decider's reason it is accepted, and the reason is kept as a person's.
  * The second person sees that line on the batch and on Review and release.
  * In the browser, Accept stays off until the reason has 8 characters (pack and browser check, twice each).
* **Exact duplicates** are refused, saying where the existing item is used.
* **A form entering the library** (the six's replayed drafts) gives one "new" item per name; the other editions reuse it.
* **A twin added after the proposal** is caught at acceptance with a 409.
* **P-266:** renaming a released field needs a second person.
