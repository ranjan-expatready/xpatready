## 4. Test gaps: why every suite stayed green

Would have caught it, and did not: `check_forms_s4_finder.ts`, `check_w1_start_setup*.ts`, `check_s5_people_ai.ts`, `check_ai_drafting.ts`, `check_p2_guard`. Why: (1) the Studio suites use the fake launcher, which never starts p2; (2) the stub pass returns `p2/stubs/<kind>.json` and never reads `output_schema`; (3) the launcher validates answers with Ajv, which accepts `maxItems` — a wider validator than the provider; (4) the replayed answers were recorded before the schema changed. Nothing in the chain is the provider's own rulebook.

Smallest permanent guards, cheapest first (P-153: each names its guard test):
1. **Schema lint + stub presence** (offline, under a second): parts A and B of `check_qa_ai_paths.ts`, copied into the suites as the guard of "the provider accepts every schema we send". Catches QA-83, QA-84, QA-85.
2. **Runner error replay** (offline): part C, the guard of "a refused call is US$0 and says what happened". Catches QA-86, QA-87, QA-88.
3. **Live schema preflight** before the first live call of a new schema hash, the way the stub pass is required today: a one-line request with the real schema, about US$0.01, founder-approved once per schema change. The only guard that tests the provider itself.
4. **One ledger** outside the repository (QA-90). No test can protect a cap that each copy counts alone.
