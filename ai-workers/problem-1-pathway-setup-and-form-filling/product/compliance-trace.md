Each IRCC business rule the product relies on, with the official page, a short quote and the date it was read (P-170), the acceptance test that proves it and the capability it belongs to. **Status:** **Sourced** (quoted from canada.ca or ircc.canada.ca on the date shown) · **Sample** (no official source found, or our product choice where IRCC is silent; stays Sample until the immigration expert confirms) · **Unconfirmed: no counsel** (a legal point) · **FQ-n** (needs a founder decision, listed in the pack's PR).
