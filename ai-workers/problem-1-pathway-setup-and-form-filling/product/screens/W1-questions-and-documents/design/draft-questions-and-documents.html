1 · A document · 74 factsThe client uploads a passport, a marriage certificate…; AI reads it into proposed values; the agent confirms. Fallback: unreadable or missing → asked in the SQR.

3 · The agent · 24 factsThe representative's, office's and case's own facts: the representative's details, the application number, the fee.
