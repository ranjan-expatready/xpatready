Rules every screen follows

  • Levels only through LevelMark / LevelBadge / Trail; never colour a level by hand.
  • Status only through StatusChip (shape + icon + word); status colours are never used for levels, and level colours never for status.
  • Selection = primary tint + 3 px primary left bar; hover = text/3 on rows, text/5 on items; focus = 3 px ring at 50 %.
  • One next step per card or row: filled when it counts in "need you", outline otherwise, link for Open; one filled page action (New combination) in the title row.
  • Surfaces: page #F7F7F7, cards and tables white with a 1 px border, radius 12 (cards) / 10 (tables) / 8 (controls); raised shadow only on cards with work in progress.
  • Type: 24 page title · 18 section header · 16 card title and Pathway rows · 14 UI · 12 meta; Proxima Nova for headings; no 13 / 15 px [F P-48].
  • Spacing: 4 px grid; card padding 12–14; row 40 (tree) / 44 (list); gaps 8 / 12.
  • Motion: 150 ms colour/shadow only; AI stripes stop under reduced motion.
  • Widths: 1440 three card columns · 1024 two · 390 one; list collapses to stacked rows at 390; 200 % zoom = the 720 px layout.
  • Tooltips on every level badge ("Program: a group of related applications, such as Work permits"), status, icon-only button and count.
