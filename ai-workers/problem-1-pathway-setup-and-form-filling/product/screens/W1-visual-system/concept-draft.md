## 4. Layout — the five points (recommended first; board §0–§5)
1. **Structure tree: A guide lines + level marks + aligned columns** (Name · Status · Cases may be opened from · Combinations, slim header); Pathway rows on a slate band at 16 px; "+ Add …" rows with a dashed mark. *Why: the team's own tree, stays 40 px a row at 50+, lines show the parent four levels deep.* Rejected: B nested cards (≈2× taller, cramped at 1024), C shaded bands (four near-whites fail as a cue).
2. **Programs cards: A identity first** — context trail on its own line (never beside the chip), Category title navy with the status chip right, sub-category under it, bar + "Step n of 4" on one line, facts, a footer with the one next step; Not started cards flat. *Why: same height, and filled buttons = the need-you count, so work stands out among 50.* Rejected: B header band (taller, 50 teal bands), C compact tile (cuts trail and facts: [CONFLICT] BR-7/BR-14).
3. **List Pathway rows: A slate band** (4 px slate left bar, ■, 16 px semibold, count + need you), sticky under the header. *Why: visible while scrolling, same colour as the Pathway everywhere.* Rejected: B bold rule (unlinked to the rest), C Program sub-rows (+15 rows at 50, drops the full trail).
4. **Trail: A level colours, identity kept together**, wrapping whole. *Why: the eye reads down the navy parts; one scale with the tree.* Rejected: B grey context (Program loses its teal), C pills (250 pills on a 50-row list).
5. **App-wide: A level tokens + shared components** (LevelMark, LevelBadge, Trail, StatusChip, NeedYouChip, SetupBar, NextStep, PathwaySection, SetupCard). S3 "You are opening" = Trail 16 px; S4 "Used in" = Trail 12 px; S5 header = context Trail + 24 px identity + chip + one next step + SetupBar stepper; S6 "Where used" = Trail grouped by PathwaySection; S7 summary = one Trail per line. Rejected: B written rules only (drift), C team hues verbatim (clash; raw hues need the team's lint allow-list).
6. **Page name (Lead ask; board §7): "Program structure"** in the menu, title, the Immigration programs button and three copy lines; URL stays `/structure`. *Why: says what it is and pairs with "Immigration programs".* Rejected: "Structure" (structure of what?), "Program combinations" (blurs with Immigration programs, which lists every combination; the page also holds Pathways and Programs: [CONFLICT] PRODUCT §4), "Pathways and programs" (one more; "programs" twice in the menu, leaves out categories). Team: page "Programs & Streams", tree "Program hierarchy". [GAP] amends P-115 menu names.
7. **Pathway cards as the way in (Lead asks; board §8): C both views with a switch** like Immigration programs (Cards / Tree, remembered per person). Cards: large Pathway cards (counts by status, need you, accent stripe) open into Program cards that list their categories and sub-categories with small chips and "+ Add" links. Tree: thin connector lines in the parent level's colour (slate › teal › navy, 1.5 px), small chips, 30 px rows, sticky Pathway rows; drawn at 1440 and 390 px with 5 (P-112) and 50 Sample combinations. *Why: the overview the founder asked for plus a tree that stays 2–3 screens at 50, with find across every Pathway; same pattern as Immigration programs.* Cost: two views to build and test (shared parts). Compared against it: A cards column beside one Pathway's tree (one view, cheaper, Find shows match counts on cards; drawn at 1440 and 390) and B card grid with the tree in a side panel (details would stack: [CONFLICT] team "no stacked overlays"). Every control stays in all three (tabs, Find, Expand / Collapse all, counts line, All pathways, + Add rows, details panel).
Further (founder point 5): section headers and Pathway filter cards take the Pathway mark; card rows line up; search takes the free width; panel "Used by" / "Where used" become Trail rows with chips; sticky bands; level-badge tooltips. Nothing on today's screens is cut (board §6 maps every element).
