C6.17 · A multi-stage case (draft board, B139)

Intent, not pixels (P-44). Sample names (Client A principal; Client C, a child, in the states). Sample route "Study, then work": Study permit (outside Canada) → Employer-specific work permit with an LMIA (inside Canada). It shows the mechanism; not a recommended route (TAXONOMY case 3 has the post-graduation work permit after the study permit); the immigration expert names real routes. Revised after the fresh challenge (challenge.md). Dates, key details and the deadline rule are Sample (canada.ca not read from this session). Form and document counts are read from the releases at run time, never fixed (P-319).

1 · Open a case at a later stage (SA-1B-212)

Dialog pattern, ≤3 steps. Step 2 appears only when the chosen combination is a later stage of a route. Key details are optional and are kept on the stage record (the team's StageRecord). Only a fact the same at every stage (UCI) is also written as Client A's answer, by the case officer.
