**Lessons to copy**
- Put the default on the template, not the case: the document type holds ordered roles (Frappe HR's Role on the template item, Odoo Sign's signer roles, CODEOWNERS), and each case turns them into its own people. The officer types nothing when the defaults fit.
- One shared item, several providers, one state: the line is asked once and is satisfied once (OpenSPP's "Group/Household" target; GitHub's "any of the owners is sufficient"). Do not split it into one request per provider.
- Filter inside the resolver and the picker: Frappe HR drops Administrator from role matches and GitLab only allows members, so C6.14 drops under-18s and anyone not on the case in both the defaults and the "Change providers" dialog, and says why ("under 18").
- Store the officer's change on the item, and keep it when defaults change: as GitLab does, a later edit to the type's roles reaches new or untouched lines only, never a line the officer changed. "Use the defaults" is the way back.
- Ask for a reason on every override and record it with who and when (GitHub push protection and alert dismissal; OpenSPP "Reason for Change" plus "Remarks", tracked). A short list plus free text is the proven shape; C6.14's free text of 3-500 characters is the minimum.
- Error copy in GOV.UK form: empty gives "Enter a reason for changing providers"; too long gives "Reason must be 500 characters or less".
