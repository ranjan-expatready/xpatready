Who can be on a case

Change in Questionnaires
These are the people a case for this combination may include. For each person:
  • How many — the fewest and most a case may have; the questionnaire repeats for each one.
  • Is an applicant — gets their own copy of every form set to "Each applicant" (for example their own IMM 5669, Sample) and their own questionnaire sections. A representative is never an applicant.
  • Answered by — who types the answers: the person on their own link, or the principal for a child under 18.

Changing it changes the questionnaires, which forms each person gets, and the Preview.
