## Verdict
**Build after these changes.** The rules for the parts kept are mostly right and the words, keys and role names match the requirement. But two things stop the build as scoped: no page or row owns how an invited person joins and signs in through the web, and the API rows give these screens nothing to read (staff list, licences list, the two-step setting). The Cognito story for two-step sign-in also needs fixing: it only holds if two-step is on for every firm. **Count: 2 Blockers · 9 Majors · 10 Minors.**

## Objections

### Blockers

**B1 — Joining and signing in have no page and no owner, so the row's own acceptance journey cannot run.** *Needed for the proof.*
* **What breaks:** PF-6's Done-when is "invite → accept in a second browser → … → the person's next request refused" (rows l.39). The concept sends the invitee to "Cognito's own set-password and two-step pages, not ours" (concept l.6) and calls the local sign-in "scaffolding … never shown" (l.44). No row builds a web page for any of these: the public accept route `POST /api/public/invitations/:token/accept` (contracts §1.2 l.35), the sign-in callback, sign-out, or AG-1-55's fixed "You signed in on another device" / "Sign in again here" screen. PF-4 is API only. PF-1's seam leaves each page to "check who is signed in inside the page" (`modules/README.md`). The existing `/sign-in` page only chooses a simulated person.
* **Example:** the CI browser check runs with `PLATFORM_IDENTITY=local`, so no Cognito page exists. The second browser has no page to open the invitation link on and no way to hold a bearer token.
* **Evidence (Fact, high):** Cognito cannot read our `platform_invitation` token. Its own invite flow is different: `AdminCreateUser` emails a temporary password, and its pages then force a password change. That email is Cognito's, not our `MailPort`'s. AG-1-70 needs staff emails sent through the Mailer's single send path. AG-1 §9 lists the invitee-side cases: "expired token rejected (offer re-invite); already-consumed token rejected".
* **Fix:** choose one invite path and write it into the concept. Recommended: our token by `MailPort`, with the email's wording in §5. Then a public **Accept invitation** page (firm name, roles; states: valid, expired, used, revoked, person since deactivated) leads into password set-up. Name the row that builds the web session: either the local stand-in's sign-in, the Cognito callback, sign-out and the "signed in on another device" page here in PF-6, or a small web row after PF-4. Label the local stand-in as scaffolding for CI (P-33), not as missing.

**B2 — The screens have nothing to read: there are no list or settings routes.** *Needed for the proof.*
* **What breaks:** concept §6 (l.41) lists PF-4's routes: me, invitations, roles, deactivate/reactivate, `PUT` credential, plus "the settings route … (PF-3's typed settings)". None of them returns the Team table (name, email, roles, status, two-step, last sign-in), the "6 of 10 seats used" line, a person's panel, the Licences list, or the count of staff with two-step on. PF-3's `settings.ts` is a helper, not an HTTP route (rows l.13). Nothing stores whether a person has two-step on (PF-3 has no such column; Cognito holds it). PF-6 may touch only `web/src/modules/platform/` (rows l.39).
* **Example:** Team screen 1 renders with no `GET` to call. With Security on, nothing can read "2 of 4 active people have it on".
* **Fix:** add the read side to PF-4, or to a PF-4b: `GET /api/platform/staff` (cursor; status, roles, last sign-in, two-step state, plus `seats: {used, limit}`), `GET /api/platform/staff/:id`, `GET /api/platform/credentials`, `GET|PUT /api/platform/settings/:key` (key-checked by the definition's "who may change it"). Also state the two-step source: read from Cognito and cached on `platform_staff`, local stand-in `false`. Each route needs its cross-firm 404 test. PF-6 waits on that row.

### Majors

**M1 — Handing two-step sign-in to Cognito's pages fits only "on for every firm", not a per-firm switch.** *Needed for the proof.* AG-1-40 says two-step is optional and off by default. Anyone may turn it on for themselves with a passkey or an authenticator app, "with **recovery codes**". The firm switch is enforced at sign-in.
* **Facts (medium-high, check in PF-2):** Cognito sets MFA once per user pool (off, optional or required), not per firm. Its pages walk a person through set-up only when MFA is required. It has no page where a person turns on optional two-step, and no recovery codes. Passkeys are a sign-in choice in Cognito's own pages; whether they count as a second step is **Unverified**. PF-2's gate covers only `aws-jwt-verify` (gate l.16, l.40-50), not these features, so "PF-2's pick" (concept l.9) is unsupported.
* **What breaks:** with a shared pool, "Require two-step … for all staff" has nothing in Cognito to switch per firm (BR-9 l.20, "enforced by Cognito and our guard"). The "off" case and "anyone can turn it on for themselves" need our own set-up screens, which the concept says are not ours. A person who loses their phone has no recovery codes, and no path for an Owner to reset them (AG-1-67).
* **Fix:** founder question 1 below. If on for every firm (pool MFA required): Cognito's pages do the set-up, and the Security screen shows the switch on and locked, with that reason. Record the gaps as a stated deviation under P-553 (2): no self opt-in, no recovery codes (an Owner resets a lost device), passkeys only as Cognito offers them. If per-firm: add our own set-up screens to the row. Either way, add a Cognito entry to PF-2's gate.

**M2 — The identity check for the Owner change has no mechanism, no local version and no screen.** *Needed for the proof.* BR-5 (l.16) and the board ("confirms it's you (code or password)") need a check within 5 minutes (AG-1-42: code or passkey if enrolled, else password). The concept does not say whether it is a trip back through Cognito's sign-in (re-sign-in, then `auth_time` ≤ 5 min; **Unverified**) or a prompt in our panel. The first loses the panel's state, so it needs a return path. The second is a second overlay, and TEAM-PATTERNS says "no stacked overlays". The local stand-in has no step-up, so BR-5's pass test cannot run in CI. **Fix:** name the mechanism. Draw it as a step inside the Change roles panel, not a second overlay. Give PF-4 a local step-up stub (fresh-confirmation time on the local session) and its 5-minute fail test.

**M3 — The seat check at reactivation is missing.** *Needed for the proof.* AG-1-13 checks seats "at invite and reactivation"; AG-1-15 says "reactivation needs a free seat"; AG-1 §9 says it is "blocked until a seat frees"; contracts §7 (l.149) says "seat check at invite and reactivation". Revoke an invitation, deactivate someone or upgrade.") and a pass and fail test.
