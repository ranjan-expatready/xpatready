Row (BACKLOG, P-486, P-240, P-170): on the Study permit, the spouse's work permit filing and the non-studying child's visitor filing carry IMM 1294. The release should get IMM 1295 and IMM 5257 with a `who` per filing, and IMM 1294 should go only to people who study (data). Done when: each family filing lists only its own document's forms, each form cited or labelled Sample; the packs are green on the checkpoint; and there is **no engine code change** (P-449, P-452).

Read on `origin/main` 24b0b92a6. No build has started, and nothing in `engine/`, `api/src/` or `web/src/` has changed.

## Why it stops here

### 1. IMM 1294 "only for people who study" needs code
Today's data cannot say it. The people who study are the student (always) and a child who answered Yes to "6 months or more". The spouse does not study.

Who gets a form is set by the form line's `who`, and only roles can be named there:
- the list: `api/src/studio.ts:79-82` (`WHO`, `FORM_WHO`);
- what each value reaches: `engine/resolver.ts:29-52` (`subjectsFor`).

No `who` reaches "the principal and some children, never the spouse". There is also only one line per form on a combination: `combination/100-form-lines.ts:23-26` saves each rule by its form. So IMM 1294 cannot have one line for the student and a second for a child.

A condition does not close the gap either. It reads answers only (`engine/resolver.ts:132` `factsOf`), and no answer tells the student apart from a spouse:
- There is no role fact.
- `exists` is false on a missing answer (`prototype/contracts/rules.ts:121`), so "not answered" is the same for the student and the spouse.
- Asking every applicant "Will this person study in Canada for 6 months or more?" would ask the student a question with no point. It would also leave the student's IMM 1294 "Cannot tell yet" until answered, which breaks the packs. And a spouse answering Yes would get IMM 1294 while `fees_by_role` charges a work permit. Forms and fees would then disagree.

### 2. BK-15 (#1591) is not merged
BK-16's data builds on it:
- the spouse line in `samples/wave1.json`;
- `fees_by_role`, which names each filing "Work permit" or "Visitor visa".

WAY-OF-WORKING says never to build on an unmerged PR. #1591 is waiting for a re-run of the failed shard-1 part (`gh run rerun 43133255795 --failed`), then the label.

## What data alone can already do, once #1591 merges
- **IMM 1295**, `who: spouse_or_partner`, on every case.
- **IMM 5257**, `who: each_dependent_child`, condition `DEPENDENT person.studies_six_months_or_more = false`. The DEPENDENT condition is decided on the child's own answer (`engine/resolver.ts:61-68`). Unanswered, it reads "Cannot tell yet", like BK-15's "Application to be decided".
- Both forms are already in the library with a fill trial (`samples/fill-trials.json`, IMM 1295 (09-2023); IMM 5257 (09-2023) from Visitor visa).

That leaves IMM 1294 on the spouse's filing, so the row's "Done when" is not met.

## Options (the tech lead decides; P-452 is the founder's exit rule)
- **A (recommended): a small, generic engine change, ruled as an exception to P-452 for this row.**
  - Add one new `who`: "the principal applicant always, and each dependent child when the line's condition holds".
  - It copies how QA-221 already handles `principal_partner_adult_children`, where the minimum age applies to children only (`engine/render.ts:57-60`).
  - It touches three files: `contracts/resolver.ts` (`WHO_HOLDERS`), `engine/resolver.ts` and `engine/render.ts` (the condition is skipped for the principal), plus `api/src/studio.ts` `FORM_WHO`. No combination, form or migration is named in code, and B164's guard still holds.
  - The data is then: IMM 1294 with that `who` and the condition `studies_six_months_or_more = true`; IMM 1295; IMM 5257 as above.
  - Every other combination is unchanged.
- **B: the general version, "a form follows its filing".** A form line names the filing documents it belongs to, read from BK-15's filing per person. It is cleaner in principle, because fees and forms then come from one source. But the forms on every page would depend on the filings being worked out first. That is a much larger change (render, prepare, fill and readiness all read forms), so it is not for the Bake.
- **C: hold BK-16 and record it as a known gap for exit (b).** Exit (b) is reached with a student filing alone, which the packs already walk. A family's forms list stays wrong for the spouse and for a child who does not study (the fees are right), with a sentence on the case saying so. BK-16 then runs after the Bake, under option A or B.

Recommendation: **A** if a family walk is part of exit (b); otherwise **C**, which keeps P-452 unbroken.
