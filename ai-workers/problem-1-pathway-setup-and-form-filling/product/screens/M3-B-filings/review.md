## Findings

**M1 · Major · Per-filing fee lines contradict BR-18, and the money does not add up.** `engine/runtime/filings.ts:82` puts the full per-person CAD 100 + CAD 85 on every filing. The family cap exists only on the group. Live on :4436, 3 people: the filing lines add up to CAD 555, but the group total is CAD 470. With 6 people: CAD 1,110 against CAD 670. BR-18 asks for "fee lines per role and mode … on whose filing, who pays, fee-exempt marks (the APR 'Family Rate' words)". Its agreed test is "5 visitors in one group → cap on the principal's filing, others fee-exempt". There is no payer and no exempt mark, and the filing-level amounts overstate what IRCC charges. Any reader that totals per filing (receipts per filing at BR-11, AG-06 billing) gets the wrong figure. The test at `tests/runtime/check_vv_filings.ts:55` asserts the inconsistent shape. **Fix:** when a cap applies, put the capped line on the principal's filing and mark the other filings' lines exempt with the Family Rate words (amount 0, payer the principal), so the filing lines always add up to the group total. Add BR-18's pass and fail tests as written, including "sum of filing lines = group total" in the property test.

**M2 · Major · The API shape departs from the r5c contract silently, and is not in the contracts package.** The r5c contract (§"New", line 68) has `filings[{id, combination, applicants[{subject, role, mode}], group, waitsFor?, status, number?}]`, `groups[{id, kind, filings, splittable}]`, a top-level `fees[{filing, payer, line, amount, exempt}]` and `version`. The commit returns `key` for `id`, a flat `subjectId/role/person` for `applicants[]`, `applicationNumber` for `number`, no `status`, `combination` or `splittable`, fees nested under groups and filings with no `payer` or `exempt`, and no `version` (`filings.ts:40-47`, `api/src/case-filings.ts:19`). It also mixes snake_case (`case_id`, `release_version`) with camelCase (`notFiling`, `totalCents`). A separate `/filings` route for one slice is reasonable. Renaming the contract fields without a note in `challenge.md` or the PR goes against "never deviate silently" and STANDARDS §"Contract first" [M]: the response types live in the engine, while sibling routes type theirs in `contracts/runtime.ts`. **Fix:** use the contract's field names for the parts this slice derives (the rest may be omitted until later slices). Move the response type to `contracts/runtime.ts`, or record the deviation and its reason in `screens/M3-B-filings/challenge.md` for the Lead.

**m1 · Minor · Filing and group keys are positional.** In `filings.ts:79-81`, `filing-${i+1}` and `group-1` change meaning when a person stops travelling. On :4436, after the spouse is marked not travelling, `filing-2` is Child 1. Application numbers and receipts will later be recorded per filing (BR-11), so the identity should be stable. **Fix:** derive the key from the combination and the person (for example `vv.single:<subjectId>`).

**m2 · Minor · The quote is partly a paraphrase, and biometrics exemptions are not disclosed.** `filings.ts:19`: only the inner quoted part is IRCC's words. The rest, including the biometrics family wording, is the row-19 summary, but the field is presented as "IRCC's words" (test name `check_vv_filings.ts`, P-170 "short quote"). Separately, every applicant is charged biometrics with no note that exemptions are not evaluated. That is correct not to guess, since row 19 has no exemption rule, but the response does not say it. **Fix:** keep `quote` verbatim (the paraphrase can go in another field), and add "biometrics exemptions not applied" to `basis` (`filings.ts:92`). Re-read the fee list when canada.ca is reachable (it was not reachable this session, so the values rest on the Lead's check of 29 Oct).

**m3 · Minor · The `familyFrom` threshold is never exercised.** For both fees, the per-person total at `familyFrom` equals the maximum. So `people >= familyFrom` in `filings.ts:56` can be removed or shifted (5→6 for biometrics: 3) without any test failing. **Fix:** add one `familyFee` unit case with a synthetic rule whose maximum is below `familyFrom × perPerson`, so the threshold is the deciding condition.

**m4 · Minor · An internal code is in user-facing text.** `filings.ts:69`: "(C6.12, later slices)" in `why` is project jargon a case officer would read (EXPERIENCE plain words, D10). **Fix:** for example, "Filings and fees for this application are not worked out yet; nothing is guessed."
