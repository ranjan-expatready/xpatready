## Approved-source question (P-80, D147): may a previous edition now go to AI?
Yes, newly. Before this PR, `kind` was "Official file": `read()` refused it (sources.ts:199) and the plan marked it `not_eligible` (ai.ts:125).
Now it is a Form with status RECOGNISED, and both `read()` and the plan let it through to a field-map draft. That includes LIVE in a
program-wide batch, where it counts as a paid target without anyone picking that file. The guard admits it (p2/guard.ts:42-45: on the
manifest with a matching checksum), so P-80 is not breached: it is an official-library file, not an off-list one. However, its
manifest approval is "P-32: test input for the new-edition flow … AI reports the impact", not field-map drafting of a superseded
edition, and nothing checks IRCC's accepted-until date (impact-ai.ts:159) before a paid draft. Severity: Minor, a scope question
for the tech lead's ruling (a), not a code fault.

## What held
- Printed manifest label always wins: on a current form, a known `IRM 0002 (01-1999)` or `IMM 5476 (11-2025)` never overrides it.
- `""`, `"IMM 5476 (11-2021) "` (trailing space), `"…\n"`, lowercase `imm`, and `11/2021` are all rejected. The converter keeps the descriptive label and the run stays contract-invalid; nothing is invented.
- Inventory naming a file not in the manifest with no known edition: throws `no edition … in the official manifest`. In `launch.ts`, `admitRun` refuses such an inventory before conversion unless it is approved in the product, and then `known` must equal the approved edition (launch.ts:73).
- Reconvert with descriptive labels on the entries, `entries: []`, or no `entries` key: falls back to the descriptive label, `valid=false`. It refuses rather than guesses.
- The studio route passes the intake's printed reading (library row by checksum, sources.ts:285, 369), so with honest records the stamp is the file's own printed edition.

Not attacked (instruction or out of scope): live launch, browser walks, concurrency, roles, uploads. A pre-existing gap outside this
PR: `convertFieldMap` never compares the inventory's checksum with the manifest (a 2021 inventory relabelled `forms/imm5476e.pdf`
is stamped `IMM 5476 (11-2025)`); launch's `admitRun` covers it, reconvert does not.
