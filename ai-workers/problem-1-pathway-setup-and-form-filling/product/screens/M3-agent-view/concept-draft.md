## 1. Job and journey
**Job.** The case officer opens a case on a released combination with its people. The engine derives the case's filings from each person's filing mode. The officer collects every fact once, documents first, confirms the facts section by section, then produces, signs and records each item, and sends each submission group. A changed fact never silently rewrites anything finished.
**Persona.** The **case officer** (unlicensed staff who prepare the case) and the **representative** (licensed, named on IMM 5476, reviews before filing; P-154, P-155, §11). **Journey.** Activities 7.1–9.5; WAVE-1 W4; DEMO rows 11, 14, 15, 18. **Capabilities.** C6.1, C6.3–C6.6, C6.9, C6.10, C7.3, C7.5, C7.6, C7.7, C8.1, C8.2, C9.2.

**1b · What depends on what (frame 0a).** Combination (pinned release; portal fixed, P-137) → **filing mode per person role** (P-135) → the engine derives **filings** (how many, whose), **forms per person**, **fees on whose filing**, **submission groups** and **waits** → documents per person → facts (document › client › case officer; proposed → confirmed by section) → items of three kinds (form · document · IRCC step; signing inside form items) → "Ready to file" (P-71 + P-136) → record each submission group → after filing (minimal). **Back-edges**, why a wizard fails: a changed fact marks every finished item out of date; an upload adds proposals; a new person adds documents, facts and items; an employer-case change reaches the worker; a trigger (approval, AOR) lets a waiting filing start. The screen answers: *what stands between each filing and Submit, and who owes it?* **Use cases (frame 0b):** 20, each tied to a frame, among them a visitor family as one submission of three filings with the family fee cap, Express Entry as one filing with four people (one declared only), a spousal sponsorship co-filed with the PR application, a nomination before the eAPR, an in-Canada spouse's open work permit after the PR receipt, citizenship on paper by a representative, an employer-linked work permit, and one application mixing online, PDF upload, pen-signed forms, client e-sign and an IRCC interview.

## 3. Business rules
* **BR-1 Filing** (P-135). A filing is one application that IRCC numbers and decides. Each person role in the combination's "Who can be in this application" has a **filing mode**: **Files own application** (temporary residence, citizenship: the person gets their own filing, on the linked combination the officer picks for that role, e.g. a student's spouse: open work permit); **On the principal's filing, applying** (PR family: forms, documents, checks and fees repeat per person on the one filing); **On the principal's filing, declared only** (not accompanying: only the forms and checks marked for them, e.g. family information and medical; no fee; chosen per person on the case, not a new role). The engine derives the case's filings; the officer never creates one by hand. A sponsorship is a **co-filed** application (the sponsor's, its own forms) that cannot be sent alone. The employer's LMIA or offer is a linked employer case (BR-13), not a filing. Facts belong to the person; every filing reads them.
* **BR-2 Open a case.** Only on a released, not retired combination (built); people only within "Who can be in this application" (role, maximum, mode). "Will be created as" names the case, each derived filing with its people, the submission groups, any waiting filing and the release.
* **BR-3 Checklist per person.** Release requirements × role × mode × conditions on that person's facts; recomputed when answers change; reason on hover. Team words: Not requested · Requested · Received · Being checked · Approved · Rejected (reason) · Not applicable (file kept); "Uploaded to IRCC" is a tick. The document belongs to the person; a new passport keeps the old one as history.
* **BR-4 Fill this from documents** (P-134 D4). Per section, never on upload. M3's passport reader reuses `p5/reader/mrz.ts` on synthetic PDFs with a text layer and proposes only coded-line facts; place of birth is typed. One reader per kind (P-133). Values are proposals (`ReadValue`) apart from answers; typing always works.
* **BR-5 One row per fact.** Label · value · **Comes from** (Document · type / Client / Case officer; "Employer case" for linked facts) · **state** (Missing · Proposed · Confirmed · To check). Page, reader, who, when and history in the drawer and ⋯.
* **BR-6 Confirm section** (P-72). One action on the built route (409 kept). Blocked by a To check fact. Background and declarations are the client's (family link C6.2, or the officer entering with `onBehalfOf`): shown, never confirmed for them (P-97). Only staff mark a value as checked against the document (VERIFIED, with that evidence); the client's confirmation is their own declaration (P-142 D3). Names, date of birth, passport number and expiry, and anything from handwritten or foreign certificates, are always reviewed field by field (P-142 D5).
* **BR-7 Typed versus document.** A difference opens one check per person and fact; it blocks (P-71). The officer keeps the document's value, the client's or another, with a reason; the other stays on record. Differences the passport standard explains raise nothing; equal values add evidence; two documents that disagree follow the same rule. The client sees only "please check" on that answer (P-142 D6, client screens after M3).
* **BR-8 Items and channels** (P-122, P-143, P-145). **Form** (form line × filing person × channel), **document** (an IRCC upload), **IRCC step** (biometrics, medical, interview; a visa issued with a permit is a step, not a filing). Forms have one of **four channels**, inside what IRCC accepts **for that edition on that IRCC account**: **Online** (typed into the portal's screens: answer sheet in screen order with Copy and source → "Entered" per screen, freezing its answers), **PDF upload** (fill from confirmed facts → our check, never "Validated", P-46 → download → Validate in Adobe → person steps such as typed names → upload the validated copy → read back → "Uploaded to IRCC"; never printed; "Answer sheet only" before a fill trial passes, P-65), **Paper** (the same to read back → print with the barcode page → signed in pen → barcode page on top → mailed), **Not needed** (the portal asks it itself: visible, greyed, with the reason). Each line also has a **signature** value, derived from edition × channel: typed name · signed by hand and scanned · signed in pen · none ("signed by hand and scanned" adds print → signed → scan received before the upload). Online lines offer ⋯ **Download PDF copy**: IRCC's PDF filled from the same confirmed facts where IRCC publishes one, else our format (the answer sheet in portal order), marked "Copy, not for filing" and never counted (P-145). A filing that mixes Online and PDF upload lines is the normal portal case. Changed on the row, refused with the reason. Row words: *Cannot start yet* (says what it waits for) · To do · In progress · Ready · Out of date · Done · *Not needed*; tags *needed before signing · filing · after filing*.
* **BR-9 Ready to file** (P-71 + P-136 + P-144, C7.6). Per filing; blockers grouped by who owes them, each saying what it unblocks, never a percentage. **Blocks:** an error in our check; a missing, unconfirmed or To check fact on that form; **a signed, uploaded or entered item that is out of date** because a fact changed after it; **a missing required signature**; **a barcode form (e.g. IMM 5257, 0008, 1344) on PDF upload or Paper with no validated copy read back** (IRCC returns it unprocessed). **Warns, with a reason recorded:** AI review findings, missing documents, a stale PDF copy, and everything else.
* **BR-10 Signing**, inside each form item: the line's signature value (BR-8) plus the account's own step. APR Portal: e.g. IMM 5476 signed by hand and scanned, IMM 5707 typed name; the representative's own declaration at Transmit. PR Portal: IMM 1344 and 5532 typed names; the digital forms "Client e-signed in the client portal", ticked from what the portal shows. Paper: pen per form and signer; a child's signer from the line. **Citizenship:** a representative cannot submit online, so the combination's portal is paper; the client signs in pen. No "sign for the client" action; no client sign-in stored.
* **BR-11 Record the submission** (AV-6, BR-31). One action per **submission group**; per filing: our reference (typed as the APR "case ID"), submitted on (not after today), the application number (required), the fee receipt; UCI per person. A PR filing with four people has one number. Recording warns, never blocks, and makes the filings read-only; "Correct a number" keeps the history.
* **BR-12 Out of date** (P-65, C9.2). A changed fact recomputes drafts; signed, uploaded, entered or filed items get "Out of date", never changed, with the fact, old and new values and date, and Undo. After filing: "Filed with an earlier value" and a task to re-sign or tell IRCC (P-142 D7; whether IRCC must be told: **Unconfirmed: no counsel**).
* **BR-13 Employer case link** (P-106). Offered when a filing's forms need employer facts; the officer picks a Corporate case; facts show read-only as "Employer case"; a change there marks the worker's finished items out of date; unlinking asks for a reason. The employer section carries a **"Stand-in"** chip (`data-stand-in="SI-10"`), tooltip: "Today: one Sample employer case, linked read-only. Later: the team's AG-17 Corporate cases and LMIA."
* **BR-14 Eligibility page: consumes AG-03, never rebuilds it** (P-164 (1), P-141, P-76, P-77; AG-03 §3.3, §3.7, §3.9, §7, §12; stand-ins **SI-1**, **SI-2**). A case tab, staff only; the client and prospect never see a verdict. The page shows **AG-03's result shape exactly**, per filing person and program (`AssessmentProgramResult`, §7): **outcome**, one of four: **Likely eligible** · **Eligible, below the cut-off** (with the gap to pass) · **Possibly eligible — needs <facts>** · **Not eligible** (the failed gate in plain words); **score** of **max** with the pass mark or draw band; the **breakdown** per factor; **failed gates**; **missing fields**; and on the run the **rules version** (AG-03's pack id and version, `rulesAsOf` per program), the disclaimer text, who and when; the **override** beside the result (`AssessmentOverride`: from-outcome, the firm's label, reason required) and **Mark reviewed** (reviewer, time, note). Ranked as AG-03 §3.3 says: likely (by score) → below cut-off (smallest gap) → possibly (fewest missing) → not eligible; the case's combination is shown first, then the other released combinations of the pathway type. **Today an adapter fills that shape from our own gates** (rules as data on the release, run by `evalCondition`): it can give only **three** outcomes (Likely · Possibly · Not), **no score, no breakdown, no recommendations**; "rules version" is our release pin. Wherever a result carries no score the place reads "No score yet (team's AG-03)" rather than hiding, so the page does not change when the team's Qualifier plugs in (SI-1). Score grids, cut-off bands and what-if advice are the team's AG-03 (SI-2), never built here. The page carries a **"Stand-in"** chip (`data-stand-in="SI-1"`), tooltip: "Today: our own checks on this combination's rules, three outcomes and no score. Later: the team's AG-03 Qualifier fills this page and adds scores, cut-offs and advice." The drawer lists each gate: Met · Not met · Not answered, with the fact and its source; missing facts link to the case view, "needed by N programs". Each run is a frozen snapshot (AG-3-1, -25); a disclaimer that cannot be dismissed (AG-3-32); out of date when a fact or release changes → "Run again" with a reason (AG-3-28). Rules only, no AI; identical with AI off.
* **BR-15 After filing, minimal.** Read-only; a second recording is refused; milestones, letters, returned incomplete and decisions are the team's AG-07, not built here.
* **BR-19 Review page** (P-141, P-71, P-121, P-136; AG-11-69, -70, -71; AG-7-25, -26, -28). A case tab listing **findings** per filing and person, in the team's words: severity **CRITICAL · MAJOR · MINOR · INFO**, source **Rules · AI · Added by hand**, states **Open → Acknowledged (reason) → Resolved**, **Open → Waived** (CRITICAL only, a permission and a reason; reopens when its fact changes). **Rules cross-checks** (US$0) **on every form of every combination**: name, date of birth, document numbers and dates across documents, confirmed facts and filled form fields (read-back); a rule finding resolves itself when the fact is fixed. **AI review** of filled forms on demand, **live on the Visitor visa forms only (IMM 5257, IMM 5707, IMM 5476; one trial about US$1)**; on every other form the saved results are replayed at US$0, marked "Replayed" (P-164 (2), **SI-6**); other form families come later, one at a time, each with its own prompt and tests. The AI review button and the replayed findings carry a **"Stand-in"** chip (`data-stand-in="SI-6"`), tooltip: "Today: rules checks on every form; the AI review runs live on the Visitor visa forms only and is replayed elsewhere. Later: other form families one at a time (team AG-11 AI Doers)." Cost shown first, steps, Stop, saved runs replay at US$0 (P-110); two rounds per form, a draft and an independent checker (P-121): High when both and the rules agree, **Low with both reasons** when they disagree; every finding quotes its source and field (AG-11-70); nothing invented. Findings are advisory: only the P-71 and P-136 blockers stop "Ready to file". A Dismiss does not return for the same source. Out of date when a fact or form changes ("Run again"). With AI off, rules findings and manual findings still work (AG-11-71).
* **BR-16 Submission groups** (P-135). The engine proposes which filings go together: a family's temporary-residence filings in one submission (the officer may split one out, with the warning "the family fee cap is lost" and a reason); a sponsorship and its PR application always together (no split; the sponsorship alone cannot be sent); a citizenship group (warning: one incomplete application returns the whole envelope).
* **BR-17 Waits and sequence** (P-135, AG-7-45 rule 2). A filing that waits shows *Cannot start yet* with what it waits for ("Waits for the AOR on Client A's PR filing"; "Waits for the provincial nomination"); when the trigger is recorded (approval, AOR or invitation as a document on the earlier filing), "Start next stage" is offered; nothing starts by itself.
* **BR-18 Fees per filing** (P-135). Fee lines per role and mode from the release: on whose filing, who pays, fee-exempt marks (the APR "Family Rate" words), the family cap only within one submission group, the bundled sponsorship fee on the sponsored person's filing, none for "declared only". Amounts Sample; paying is not built here.

## 4. Layout and states

**States drawn (frame 11):** empty, loading, could not load, someone else saved, read-only after filing, not on the newest release, client's own answers, long family lists, reading, unreadable, no document yet, a filing waiting.
