## 1. Job and journey
**Job.** The case officer opens a case on a released combination with its people. The engine derives the case's filings from each person's filing mode. The officer collects every fact once, documents first, confirms the facts section by section, then produces, signs and records each item, and sends each submission group. A changed fact never silently rewrites anything finished.
**Persona.** Case officer or agent, acting as the authorized representative. **Journey.** Activities 7.1–9.5; WAVE-1 W4; DEMO rows 11, 14, 15, 18. **Capabilities.** C6.1, C6.3–C6.6, C6.9, C6.10, C7.3, C7.5, C7.6, C7.7, C8.1, C8.2, C9.2.

**1b · What depends on what (frame 0a).** Combination (pinned release; portal fixed, P-137) → **filing mode per person role** (P-135) → the engine derives **filings** (how many, whose), **forms per person**, **fees on whose filing**, **submission groups** and **waits** → documents per person → facts (document › client › agent; proposed → confirmed by section) → items of three kinds (form · document · IRCC step; signing inside form items) → "Ready to file" (P-71 + P-136) → record each submission group → after filing (minimal). **Back-edges**, why a wizard fails: a changed fact marks every finished item out of date; an upload adds proposals; a new person adds documents, facts and items; an employer-case change reaches the worker; a trigger (approval, AOR) lets a waiting filing start. The screen answers: *what stands between each filing and Submit, and who owes it?* **Use cases (frame 0b):** 20, each tied to a frame, among them a visitor family as one submission of three filings with the family fee cap, Express Entry as one filing with four people (one declared only), a spousal sponsorship co-filed with the PR application, a nomination before the eAPR, an in-Canada spouse's open work permit after the PR receipt, citizenship on paper by a representative, an employer-linked work permit, and one application mixing online, PDF upload, pen-signed forms, client e-sign and an IRCC interview.

**1c · Earlier designs compared (frame 0c).** v1: keep the Workspace pattern and right panels. v2 (agreed base): keep the deliverable row and the PDF copy; its counts disagree and its stages are headings only. v3 Split view: strongest (each stage has its own progress, blocker and next action; "Needs attention" with owner), but mixed units and three navigation devices. Team mock: keep the blocker → fix link, binder per applicant, waive with a reason; drop the readiness average, ten tabs for one job, channel-less form status. **Chosen:** v3 Split view with units fixed, one switch, the attention queue grouped by who owes it, one row vocabulary, "needed before" tags, a person view, the portal read-only, filings derived from filing modes, a minimal after-filing state.

## 3. Business rules
* **BR-1 Filing** (P-135). A filing is one application that IRCC numbers and decides. Each person role in the combination's "Who can be in this application" has a **filing mode**: **Files own application** (temporary residence, citizenship: the person gets their own filing, on the linked combination the officer picks for that role, e.g. a student's spouse: open work permit); **On the principal's filing, applying** (PR family: forms, documents, checks and fees repeat per person on the one filing); **On the principal's filing, declared only** (not accompanying: only the forms and checks marked for them, e.g. family information and medical; no fee; chosen per person on the case, not a new role). The engine derives the case's filings; the officer never creates one by hand. A sponsorship is a **co-filed** application (the sponsor's, its own forms) that cannot be sent alone. The employer's LMIA or offer is a linked employer case (BR-13), not a filing. Facts belong to the person; every filing reads them.
* **BR-2 Open a case.** Only on a released, not retired combination (built); people only within "Who can be in this application" (role, maximum, mode). "Will be created as" names the case, each derived filing with its people, the submission groups, any waiting filing and the release.
* **BR-3 Checklist per person.** Release requirements × role × mode × conditions on that person's facts; recomputed when answers change; reason on hover. Team words: Not requested · Requested · Received · Being checked · Approved · Rejected (reason) · Not applicable (file kept); "Uploaded to IRCC" is a tick. The document belongs to the person; a new passport keeps the old one as history.
* **BR-4 Fill this from documents** (P-134 D4). Per section, never on upload. M3's passport reader reuses `p5/reader/mrz.ts` on synthetic PDFs with a text layer and proposes only coded-line facts; place of birth is typed. One reader per kind (P-133). Values are proposals (`ReadValue`) apart from answers; typing always works.
* **BR-5 One row per fact.** Label · value · **Comes from** (Document · type / Client / Agent; "Employer case" for linked facts) · **state** (Missing · Proposed · Confirmed · To check). Page, reader, who, when and history in the drawer and ⋯.
* **BR-6 Confirm section** (P-72). One action on the built route (409 kept). Blocked by a To check fact. Background and declarations are the client's (family link C6.2, or the officer entering with `onBehalfOf`): shown, never confirmed for them (P-97). Only staff mark a value as checked against the document (VERIFIED, with that evidence); the client's confirmation is their own declaration (P-142 D3). Names, date of birth, passport number and expiry, and anything from handwritten or foreign certificates, are always reviewed field by field (P-142 D5).
* **BR-7 Typed versus document.** A difference opens one check per person and fact; it blocks (P-71). The officer keeps the document's value, the client's or another, with a reason; the other stays on record. Differences the passport standard explains raise nothing; equal values add evidence; two documents that disagree follow the same rule. The client sees only "please check" on that answer (P-142 D6, client screens after M3).
* **BR-8 Items** (P-122). **Form** (form line × filing person × channel), **document** (an IRCC upload), **IRCC step** (biometrics, medical, interview; a visa issued with a permit is a step, not a filing). Channels only on forms: **Online** (answer sheet, "Entered" per screen), **PDF upload** (fill → our check → download → Adobe Validate → upload validated copy → read back → uploaded; never "Validated" for our check, P-46; "Answer sheet only" before a fill trial passes, P-65), **Paper** (print with barcode page → pen → mailed). Changed on the row within `acceptedChannels`, refused with the reason. A form may also carry a "PDF copy, not for filing". Row words: *Cannot start yet* (says what it waits for) · To do · In progress · Ready · Out of date · Done · *Not needed through this portal*; tags *needed before signing · filing · after filing*.
* **BR-9 Ready to file** (P-71 + P-136, C7.6). Per filing; blockers grouped by who owes them, each saying what it unblocks, never a percentage. **Blocks:** an error in our check; a missing, unconfirmed or To check fact on that form; **a signed, uploaded or entered item that is out of date** because a fact changed after it; **a missing required signature**. **Warns, with a reason recorded:** AI review findings, missing documents, and everything else.
* **BR-10 Signing follows the portal**, inside each form item. APR Portal: IMM 5476 signed and uploaded; the representative's own declaration at Transmit. PR Portal: "Client e-signed in the client portal", ticked from what the portal shows. Paper: pen per form and signer; a child's signer from the line. **Citizenship:** a representative cannot submit online, so the combination's portal is paper; the client signs in pen. No "sign for the client" action; no client sign-in stored.
* **BR-11 Record the submission** (AV-6, BR-31). One action per **submission group**; per filing: our reference (typed as the APR "case ID"), submitted on (not after today), the application number (required), the fee receipt; UCI per person. A PR filing with four people has one number. Recording warns, never blocks, and makes the filings read-only; "Correct a number" keeps the history.
* **BR-12 Out of date** (P-65, C9.2). A changed fact recomputes drafts; signed, uploaded, entered or filed items get "Out of date", never changed, with the fact, old and new values and date, and Undo. After filing: "Filed with an earlier value" and a task to re-sign or tell IRCC (P-142 D7; whether IRCC must be told: **Unconfirmed: no counsel**).
* **BR-13 Employer case link** (P-106). Offered when a filing's forms need employer facts; the officer picks a Corporate case; facts show read-only as "Employer case"; a change there marks the worker's finished items out of date; unlinking asks for a reason.
* **BR-14 Eligibility page** (P-141, P-76, P-77; AG-03). A case tab, staff only; the client and prospect never see a verdict. Per filing person, the case's combination first, then every other released combination of the pathway type, ranked: **Likely eligible** · **Possibly eligible — needs <facts>** · **Not eligible** (with the failed gate in plain words) (AG-3-10, -12, -13, -16). The drawer lists each gate (rules as data on the release, run by `evalCondition`): Met · Not met · Not answered, with the fact and its source; missing facts link to the agent view, "needed by N programs". Each run is a frozen snapshot with the rules version, "rules as at", who and when (AG-3-1, -25); a disclaimer that cannot be dismissed (AG-3-32); Override with a reason, kept beside the result (AG-3-33); "Mark reviewed" (AG-3-34); out of date when a fact or release changes → "Run again" with a reason (AG-3-28). Rules only, no AI; identical with AI off.
* **BR-15 After filing, minimal.** Read-only; a second recording is refused; milestones, letters, returned incomplete and decisions are the team's AG-07, not built here.
* **BR-19 Review page** (P-141, P-71, P-121, P-136; AG-11-69, -70, -71; AG-7-25, -26, -28). A case tab listing **findings** per filing and person, in the team's words: severity **CRITICAL · MAJOR · MINOR · INFO**, source **Rules · AI · Added by hand**, states **Open → Acknowledged (reason) → Resolved**, **Open → Waived** (CRITICAL only, a permission and a reason; reopens when its fact changes). **Rules cross-checks** (US$0): name, date of birth, document numbers and dates across documents, confirmed facts and filled form fields (read-back); a rule finding resolves itself when the fact is fixed. **AI review** of filled forms on demand: cost shown first, steps, Stop, saved runs replay at US$0 (P-110); two rounds per form, a draft and an independent checker (P-121): High when both and the rules agree, **Low with both reasons** when they disagree; every finding quotes its source and field (AG-11-70); nothing invented. Findings are advisory: only the P-71 and P-136 blockers stop "Ready to file". A Dismiss does not return for the same source. Out of date when a fact or form changes ("Run again"). With AI off, rules findings and manual findings still work (AG-11-71).
* **BR-16 Submission groups** (P-135). The engine proposes which filings go together: a family's temporary-residence filings in one submission (the officer may split one out, with the warning "the family fee cap is lost" and a reason); a sponsorship and its PR application always together (no split; the sponsorship alone cannot be sent); a citizenship group (warning: one incomplete application returns the whole envelope).
* **BR-17 Waits and sequence** (P-135, AG-7-45 rule 2). A filing that waits shows *Cannot start yet* with what it waits for ("Waits for the AOR on Client A's PR filing"; "Waits for the provincial nomination"); when the trigger is recorded (approval, AOR or invitation as a document on the earlier filing), "Start next stage" is offered; nothing starts by itself.
* **BR-18 Fees per filing** (P-135). Fee lines per role and mode from the release: on whose filing, who pays, fee-exempt marks (the APR "Family Rate" words), the family cap only within one submission group, the bundled sponsorship fee on the sponsored person's filing, none for "declared only". Amounts Sample; paying is not built here.

## 4. Layout and states
**Pattern.** Workspace (EXPERIENCE §3), v3 Split view fixed. Tabs: Overview · **Prepare application** · History. Header: trail; the **filings chip counts filings, not people** ("Visitor visa · 3 filings" · "Express Entry · 1 filing · 4 people"; a sponsorship shows inside its group; a waiting filing says what it waits for); "Filed through APR Portal" read-only (P-137); ⋯ (person view, submission groups, history); three cards as the only switch: **1 Get the information** (sections) · **2 Prepare the filing** (items) · **Everything**.
* **One left list (340 px).** Default "Needs you", grouped by who owes it (You · Client · Employer · IRCC), then Done; chips All, Everyone ▾ and a filing filter. Rows: name · one pill · one short line; actions and reasons in ⋯ and tooltips. Counts add up.
* **Right side.** A section card, a document line or an item's steps. **Drawers, never modals:** fill from documents, the check, fact history, acknowledge, split a submission, record the submission, employer link.

**States drawn (frame 11):** empty, loading, could not load, someone else saved, read-only after filing, not on the newest release, client's own answers, long family lists, reading, unreadable, no document yet, a filing waiting.
