## 1. Job and journey
**Job.** The case officer opens a case on a released combination with its people. They collect every fact once, documents first. They confirm the facts section by section. They then produce, sign and record each deliverable of the filing. A changed fact never silently rewrites anything finished.
**Persona.** Case officer or agent, acting as the authorized representative.
**Journey.** Activities 7.1–9.5 on the solution page; WAVE-1 W4; DEMO rows 11, 14, 15, 18.
**Capabilities.** C6.1, C6.3–C6.6, C6.9 (inputs only), C6.10, C7.3, C7.5 (findings only), C7.6, C7.7, C8.1, C8.2, C9.2.

**1b · What depends on what (board frame 0a).** Combination (pinned release) → portal (from the combination, read-only, P-122) → channel per form · filings and people (one filing per person who files their own application, Q1) → documents per person → facts (document › client › agent, proposed → confirmed by section) → items of three kinds (form · document · IRCC step; signing inside form items) → "Ready to file" (P-71; Q2) → record the filings together → after filing (minimal; milestones are the team's AG-07). **Back-edges**, the reason a wizard fails: a changed fact marks every finished deliverable out of date; an upload adds proposals; a new person adds documents, facts and deliverables; an employer-case change reaches the worker. The screen answers one question: *what stands between this filing and Submit, and who owes it?* **Use cases (frame 0b):** 16, each tied to the frame that carries it; among them a family visitor visa with numbers per person, a spousal PR Portal filing with client e-sign, the same on paper, one application mixing online, PDF upload, signed PDF, paper and a video step, an employer-linked work permit, returned incomplete, a newborn after filing, two officers at once, three filings in one case. Marked *not built here*: filings in sequence, returned incomplete, newborn after filing, fast keyboard entry of histories.

**1c · Earlier designs compared (frame 0c).** v1: keep the Workspace pattern and right panels; no route or stages. v2 (agreed base): keep the deliverable row and the PDF copy; its counts disagree (14 vs 22), its stages are headings only. v3 Split view: strongest (each stage has its own progress, blocker and next action; "Needs attention" with owner); mixed units, three navigation devices. Team mock: keep the blocker → fix link, binder per applicant, waive with a reason; drop the readiness average (hides blockers), ten tabs for one job, channel-less form status. This draft's r1: too many left devices, no video or phone step, no route, nothing after filing. **Chosen:** v3 Split view with units fixed (stage 1 counts sections, stage 2 deliverables), one switch, the attention queue grouped by who owes it as default, one row vocabulary, "needed before" tags, a person × deliverable view, the combination's portal read-only (P-122), a minimal after-filing state; r3 adds one filing per person who files their own application and items of three kinds.

## 3. Business rules
* **BR-1 Filing.** A filing is one application that IRCC gives its own number and decision (team AG-7-45, BR-31; SA-1B-74). **Each person marked "Files own application" (P-119) gets their own filing;** people who do not (e.g. an accompanying child on a PR application) are applicants on the principal's filing. A case holds all of a family's filings; they show together on one screen (person filter) and are recorded together in one action. Each person has their own pin on the filing's release. Facts belong to the person, so every filing reads them and copies nothing. **→ Q1**
* **BR-2 Open a case.** A case opens only on a combination that is released and not retired (built). People come only within "Who can be in this application" (role and maximum, P-119). "Will be created as" names the case, its filings, its people and the release.
* **BR-3 Checklist per person.** The checklist comes from the release's document requirements × the person's role × the conditions on that person's facts. It is recomputed when answers change. Each line gives its reason ("Asked because marital status is Married"). Line states use the team's words: Not requested · Requested · Received · Being checked · Approved · Rejected (with a reason) · Not applicable (file kept). "Uploaded to IRCC" is a separate tick. The document belongs to the person and can be reused across cases. A new passport keeps the old one as history ("evidence replaced").
* **BR-4 Fill this from documents** (P-134 D4). This is offered per section and never runs on upload. In M3 the passport reader reuses the POC `p5/reader/mrz.ts` on synthetic PDFs that carry a text layer, and proposes only what the coded lines hold (names, number, nationality, date of birth, sex, expiry); place of birth is typed and stamps are never read (challenge M4). One reader per kind (P-133): passport coded lines: rules, no AI; printed documents and tables: Textract in Canada (in production); letters and foreign records: AI vision on synthetic documents only.

  Values are stored as proposals (`ReadValue`), apart from the answers, and fill nothing. Typing always works.
* **BR-5 One row per fact.** Each row shows label · value · **Comes from** (Document · type / Client / Agent; "Employer case" for linked facts) · **state** (Missing · Proposed · Confirmed · To check). Everything else goes in the drawer and the ⋯ menu: page, reader, who, when, and the history of values.
* **BR-6 Confirm section** (P-72). One action, on the built route; the 409 is kept when the client changed something meanwhile. A section with a **To check** fact cannot be confirmed. Names read from a document need a one-click "Matches page" each first (P-134 D2); the board also draws it for date of birth, passport number and expiry (report D5 default, confirmed at the click-through). Background and declaration answers are the client's, given on the built family link (C6.2) or entered by the officer for the client with `onBehalfOf`: shown, never confirmed for the client (P-97). Confirming a document's value makes it VERIFIED, with that document as evidence (research D3: staff only).
* **BR-7 Typed versus document.** A difference opens one check per person and fact, and it blocks (P-71: a conflicting fact). The officer picks the document's value, the client's, or another, with a reason; the other value stays on record. Name differences the passport standard explains (capitals, apostrophes, a shortened coded name) raise nothing. Equal values only add evidence. Two documents that disagree follow the same rule. It arises only when a value was typed before the upload or by the officer (P-130). This is our deterministic rule check (P-71); the team's AG-11 Checker cross-checks the same facts as warnings (§9).
* **BR-8 Items** (P-122; challenge M3). Three kinds: **form** (form line × filing person × channel), **document** (an IRCC upload of a checklist line), **IRCC step** (interview, biometrics). A channel exists only on forms: **Online** (answer sheet; "Entered" per screen freezes its answers, challenge M6), **PDF upload** (fill from confirmed facts → our check → download → Validate in Adobe → upload the validated copy → read back by position → uploaded; never "Validated" for our check, P-46; "Answer sheet only" when the fill trial has not passed, P-65) or **Paper** (print with the barcode page → pen → mailed). It is changed on the row (⋯) within the library's `acceptedChannels`, and a refused option says why; the portal comes from the combination (`portalKey`, read-only, P-122). Signing is part of a form item, from the line's signer and the portal (BR-10). A form may also carry a "PDF copy, not for filing". The firm's own calls with the client are tasks (team AG-13), not items. Every row uses one vocabulary: *Cannot start yet* (says what it waits for) · To do · In progress · Ready · Out of date · Done · *Not needed through this portal* (shown, not hidden), and a tag *needed before signing · filing · after filing*.
* **BR-9 Ready to file** (P-71, C7.6). The top shows blockers grouped by who owes them (officer, client, employer, IRCC), each saying what it unblocks, never a percentage. **Blocks:** an error in our check; a missing, unconfirmed or To check fact on that form. **Warns:** AI review findings, missing documents, and, until the founder answers **Q2**, out-of-date finished items and missing signing steps (challenge M1).
* **BR-10 Signing steps follow the portal** (AV-3), inside each form item. APR Portal: IMM 5476 signed and uploaded; the representative's own declaration at Transmit. PR Portal: "Client e-signed in the client portal", ticked by the officer from what the portal shows. Paper: pen signatures per form and signer. A child's signer is the line's `signedBy` or the person picked for the child. The product has no "sign for the client" action and never asks for or stores a client's portal sign-in.
* **BR-11 Filing record** (AV-6). Per filing: our reference (typed as the APR "case ID"); submitted on (not after today); the application number (required) and the person's UCI; the fee receipt as a document. The portal comes from the combination. Recording warns, never blocks, and makes the filing read-only. "Correct a number" keeps the history. Several filings recorded together is one action (team BR-31).
* **BR-12 Out of date** (P-65, C9.2). When a fact changes, drafts recompute; copies are never regenerated by themselves (built). Items that were signed, uploaded, entered or filed get "Out of date". They are never changed. The message names the fact, the old and new values, and the date, with Undo. After filing the item shows "Filed with an earlier value", and a task asks the officer to re-sign or tell IRCC (**Unconfirmed: no counsel**, research D7).
* **BR-13 Employer case link** (P-106, AV-4). Offered only when the filing's forms need employer facts; the officer picks a case under Corporate. The employer facts show read-only as "Employer case" with a link to it. A change there marks this filing's finished items out of date. Unlinking asks for a reason, and the facts then show Missing.
* **BR-15 After filing, minimal** (challenge M6). A recorded filing is read-only; a second recording is refused ("Correct a number" instead); a later change gives "Filed with an earlier value" and a task (BR-12). Milestones, letters, returned incomplete, newborns and decisions are the team's AG-07 (`CaseMilestone`, `Decision` per filing): not built here.
* **BR-14 Eligibility and AI review** (P-129 vs the brief's lean cut, challenge M5). P-129 puts the eligibility page (C6.9) and AI review of a filled form (C7.5) in M3; the brief cut them to the team's AG-03 inputs and AG-11 findings. Not settled here: the Lead asks the founder (§9.4). Either way findings are advisory and acknowledged with a reason ("Other" needs a note, P-71).

## 4. Layout and states
**Pattern.** Workspace (EXPERIENCE §3), v3 Split view fixed. Case tabs, one row: Overview · **Prepare application** · History (the filing record and after-filing rows live inside Prepare application). The header holds the trail, the filings chip ("Visitor visa · 3 filings", Q1), "Filed through APR Portal" (read-only, P-122), ⋯ (add a filing, person × deliverable view, history) and three cards that are the only switch: **1 Get the information** (sections) · **2 Prepare the filing** (deliverables) · **Everything**.
* **One left list (340 px).** Default filter "Needs you", grouped by who owes it (You · Client · Employer · IRCC), then Done; chips All and Everyone ▾. Rows: name · one pill · one short line; actions such as Nudge and reasons such as "Asked because …" sit in ⋯ and tooltips (challenge m8). Counts on cards, chips and lists add up.
* **Right side.** The open item: a section card (frame 2), a document line, or a deliverable's steps (frame 6).
* **Side drawers, never modals:** fill from documents (source page left, proposals right), the check, fact history, acknowledge, record the filing, and the employer link.
* **Little on each row.** Detail goes in the drawer, the ⋯ menu and tooltips (founder rule).

**States drawn (frame 11):** empty (start with the documents), loading, could not load, someone else saved (Reload), read-only after filing, not on the newest release, client's own answers, long family lists, reading, unreadable, no document yet.

## 5. Copy (key strings; the rest is on the board)
* "Fill this from documents" — tooltip: "Reads Client A's uploaded passport and proposes the values it supplies for this section. Nothing changes until you confirm. Typing always works."
* Check: "The client typed a different value from the passport. Choose with a reason; both stay on record."
* Out of date: "The signed copy uploaded 27 Oct is out of date. Date of marriage changed on 29 Oct (12 Jun 2015 → 21 Jun 2015). The signed copy was kept as it was."
* After filing: "Filed with an earlier value. Nothing filed was changed."
* Reference: "Type this as the APR Portal's 'case ID'. IRCC does not use it; it is how you find the filing."
* Channel refused: "IRCC accepts this form as a PDF only."

## 10. Questions for the founder (one at a time, recommendation first)
1. **What is a "Filing"?** In plain words: a filing is one application that IRCC gives its own application number and its own decision. *Recommend:* each person who files their own application gets their own filing. A visiting family of three is three filings, prepared on one screen and sent together in one action; a PR application with an accompanying spouse and child is one filing with three people on it; one case holds all the family's filings. This is the team's AG-7-45 and BR-31 and gives each person their own number and decision (SA-1B-74). *Alternative:* one filing per family submission, with numbers and decisions per person inside it; it breaks the team's filing status, withdrawing one filing alone, and "After decision".
2. **Does an out-of-date signed, uploaded or entered item, or a missing required signature, block "Ready to file"?** *Recommend yes,* as a narrow amendment to P-71: sending IRCC a signed form that disagrees with the record, or an unsigned one, gets the application returned. AI findings and missing documents still only warn. *Alternative:* keep P-71 as written and show these as warnings (what the board draws until you answer).
3. **Bring back the route choice?** The 26 Oct research (AV-1) proposed choosing the portal first; P-122 (27 Oct) removed it. *Recommend keeping P-122:* the combination already holds its portal (`portalKey`), shown read-only as "Filed through APR Portal"; each form's channel is still set on its row within what IRCC accepts, and forms the portal does not take show as "Not needed". *Alternative:* a route chip the officer can change per filing, which needs a rule for re-deriving every form's channel.

*Lead default (not a founder question):* a fact corrected after filing creates a task to re-sign or tell IRCC; whether IRCC must be told is left to counsel (research D7, **Unconfirmed: no counsel**).
