Rules Capability
  • Rows come from the release's "after filing" lines; IRCC letters are uploaded as documents and add rows (a document request, a deadline).
  • Returned incomplete: the filing reopens on a new round; the first round's copies and numbers stay in History.
  • Newborn added after filing: a new person on the filing; new documents and facts; deliverables only through IRCC's web form (a step).
  • No double filing: "Record the filing" is refused for a filing already recorded; "Correct a number" instead.
