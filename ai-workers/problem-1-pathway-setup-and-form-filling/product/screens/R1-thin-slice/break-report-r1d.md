## What held
* **Roles:** the client, the other client, user-A/B/C → 403 on list, copy, file, lock and generate. No header, an empty header or `USER-O` → 401. Bad copy ids (`abc`, the zero UUID, `%00`, encoded `../`, 5,000 characters) → 404, never a database error. Uppercase UUIDs work. Another case's subject, the case's own "case" subject or a subject on another case → 404.
* **Path traversal:** form names `../../../etc/passwd`, `IMM 5257/../..`, NBSP, NUL, `__proto__`, `constructor` and padded spaces → 404 (the name must match the release exactly). File names are built from the release's form number and UUIDs only.
* **UPDATE trigger:** on a locked copy, every column (including no-op and workspace updates) is refused. On a generated copy, path, sha256, result and status are refused, and so is a lock without a kind (CHECK). Moving status backwards is refused. The snapshot UPDATE is refused. `runtime_case.id` cannot change (pin trigger).
* **Lock (P-71, RT-16):** while QUEUED → 409. Ten simultaneous locks → 1 × 201 and 9 × 409, one kind stored. A second lock → 409. Kinds `__proto__`, arrays, `Filed`, `"filed "`, null, missing or extra fields → 400. Out of date → 409. Not ready → 409 with the count. Toggling the spouse to "not travelling" did not get a not-ready copy locked (it became out of date).
* **Idempotency and concurrency:** 20 simultaneous presses with one key → 1 copy. One key used for two forms at once → 202 or 409, never two copies. Keys that are short, 129 characters, contain NUL or bidi characters, or are numbers → plain 400. Generate racing an answer change and its confirmation (8 trials) → each copy froze one consistent value and was correctly out of date afterwards.
* **Staleness (RT-15):** an unchanged re-save is out of date until re-confirmed, then current again. A case-level fact (stay end) makes both the principal's and the spouse's copies out of date. Adding confirmed rows, and removing a row, flag exactly the six education facts. Nothing is regenerated on its own.
* **P-72:** the spouse's facts typed by the principal stay unwritten (`unconfirmed ≥ 1`). An over-long confirmed value is "by hand", never truncated. A single file change on disk → 409. A symlink in place of the file → 409.
* **Audit and logs:** 186 form-copy events with no answer value or typed name (searched for my sample values). Same in `api.log`.
* **Payloads and UI:** 6 people with 80 copies: forms list 76 KB in 170 ms. No sideways scroll at 1440 or 1024 with a 60-character principal name. Download works by keyboard (`IMM5257-copy-6c5ffbef.pdf`).
