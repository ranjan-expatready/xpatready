## What held
* Roles: author 403; the other client 404; no actor 401; client confirm 403. The client is limited to groups holding a client field (`groupFor`).
* Hierarchy: the case group sent for a person is 422 and a person group sent for the application is 422. A non-group section, an unknown group, a unicode group and a 6000-character group are 404. A bad or foreign subject is 404, and a malformed case id is 404.
* Body: a string boolean, a missing or empty `expectedId`, an array body, broken JSON and an empty body all give 400 in plain words. A random `expectedId` gives 409.
* Races: 10 simultaneous "none apply" with `expectedId:null` gave exactly one 200 and nine 409s, with one current row. "None apply" racing 5 adds gave 1 winner and the rest refused. 2 adds racing "none apply" gave 2 entries and "none apply" refused ("has 2 entries"). The database never held a group answer beside live entries (checked by SQL).
* Removing every entry, then "none apply", works. Adding while "none apply" stands is refused with a clear message.
* Confirmation rule (P-72): the principal's own answer counts; the principal answering for an adult spouse waits; the officer's answer waits for Confirm section. A child with no birth date waits; a child aged 8 counts. Confirm section refuses unseen, forged and ABA-replaced ids (409), confirms when seen (VERIFIED version with origin kept), and refuses a replay with the old id.
* Readiness names each blocking group per person and per form, with "not answered" or "not confirmed". The spouse's group blocks the principal's IMM 5707 only while that form reads it, and clears when the spouse is marked not travelling. A form with all groups resolved shows `ready:true`. The lock is refused while blocked. `GET /forms` takes about 25 ms.
* UI: the client and agent views offer "Add an entry" and "None apply" on empty groups. A stale officer press shows the server's message ("has 1 entry", "someone answered"). A double click sent one request. No horizontal overflow at 1024 px or at 512 px (200 % zoom). Non-English names (配偶者) render.
