## What held
* **Roles (every endpoint × no header, unknown, user-A/B/C, P, Q, O):** 401 for none or unknown. 403 for configuration people everywhere. 403 for a client on list, create, addPerson, updatePerson, confirm and the agent view. Client Q gets 404 on every A endpoint (get, SQR, put, rows, delete row). Reset gives 403 to the officer and the client.
* **IDOR, 17 swaps:** case B's subject in case A's URL (put, row add/delete, confirm, PATCH person); case B's row with A's own subject; the principal's row on the spouse; another group's row; PATCH of the principal. All return 404. Malformed UUIDs give 404, not 500.
* **Answers under concurrency:** 12 parallel first answers (mixed client and officer) → 1 × 200, 11 × 409, 1 row. 12 parallel edits on one `expectedId` → 1 × 200. Confirm racing an edit and a second confirm → 201/409/409, one VERIFIED current. Stale, null-when-exists and other-field `expectedId` → 409. Missing `expectedId` → 400. The DB refuses a second current answer (`uq_answer_current`) and a bad status (`ck_answer_status`).
* **People limits:** 6 parallel spouses → 1 kept. 10 parallel children → 1 kept. Over 5 cases, maximum 1 spouse and 1 child per burst. No orphan parties. This holds only by the accident of the `(case_id, order)` unique index; the cost is 409 "already exists" for legitimate parallel adds (part of B5). Sequential limits: a second spouse and a fifth child → 422.
* **Row removal:** 6 parallel deletes → 1 × 200, 5 × 409, 2 versions. An answer into a removed row → 404.
* **Field and level checks:** key not in the release → 404. Case fact on a person, and person fact on the case → 422. A row fact without a row, or with a row on a single fact → 422. Client on `verified_document` or `staff_working_view` → 403.
* **Re-release mid-case** (v2 inserted without `person.native_language`): the old case keeps v1 (same sha, the question is still asked, PUT 200). A new case pins v2, and the same PUT → 404.
* **No leaks:** no value, name or marker (Zed…, R999999, Hindi text, 4 MB strings, NUL payloads) appears in `audit_event` or `var/isolated-runtime_brk/api.log`, including the 500s.
* **Hostile input** is stored and flagged, never executed: `__proto__` keys, RTL override, Hindi, arrays, numbers and objects in place of a type. Views stay 200.
* **Budgets:** principal, spouse and 4 children with 542 answers and 120 rows → agent view 203–312 ms. PUT p50 182 ms, p95 503 ms (each PUT recomputes the whole view, which is the 1.18 s maximum). Confirm about 200 ms. The 115-case list takes about 10 ms.
