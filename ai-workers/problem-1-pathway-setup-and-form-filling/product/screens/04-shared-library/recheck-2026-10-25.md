## 3. UX/UI review of the board, as a demanding content publisher

### Blocker
* **UB1. A returned change is a dead end, and the board hides it.** The maker state promises "You can still delete it while it waits", but nothing can withdraw or correct a change. A returned new field freezes its combination for good ([s] `library-changes.ts:56-59`, breaker B1). *Fix:* "Withdraw" and "Correct and send again" in the drawer and on To review (retrofit 1). Draw a Returned state with the reviewer's reason.
* **UB2. The library's own "Add" bypasses the review.** Today's Fields page still posts typed keys (`main.ts:46`, `app/web/src/pages/library.tsx`). A field added that way is released with no second person (breaker M3). *Fix:* refuse the route (405, with a test) before slice 1, and route every add through Describe.

### Major
* **UM1. Two approval surfaces.** *Fix:* keep `/review/change/:id` as the only approval page. The drawer shows "Waiting for a second person · Open the review" and drops its own Approve. K2 changes accordingly.
* **UM2. Statuses are missing.** *Fix:* one calculated order in BR-2, each drawn with its one next action: Draft · Waiting for a second person · Returned to author · Approved, goes out with the next release · Retire waiting · Released · Retired · Merged into … (M4) · Name to check · A change is waiting.
