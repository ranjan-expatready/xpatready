# FO-4 — The firm's sales screens, thin: fresh challenge (13 Nov 2026)

Challenger: an independent agent that did not draft the concept (skill screen-concept §B). Read: `concept.md` (draft 1), `design/board.html`, contracts draft 3 §0, §2.1, §2.6 (`../../design/platform-and-front-office-contracts-2026-11-14.md`), rows rev 2 FO-1, FO-2, FO-4 (`../../design/lead-b-rows-2026-11-14.md`), `PRODUCT.md`, `STANDARDS.md`, `EXPERIENCE.md`, `QUALITY.md`, `design/TEAM-PATTERNS.md`, AG-02 v2.6 cards AG-2-4, 5, 13c, 18, 26, 37, 37a, 38, 44, 47, 75, 80, 83, 84, 86, 94 and BR-22, and the app (`web/src/App.tsx`, `web/src/components/frame.tsx`, `components/ui/controls.tsx`, `styles/tokens.css`, `web/package.json`, `api/src/core.ts` guard). `ai-workers/tools/freshness.sh` could not run in this copy (it needs `code/README-copies.md`, absent here); AG-02 v2.6 in `domain/requirements-2026-11-02/` is the newest copy present and names itself "the only build source". Line numbers: `concept.md` = C:n, `board.html` = B:n.

## Verdict
**Build after these changes.** The model is right (neutral lead, Engage makes the prospect, Won only by a person or a signature, the case opened by a person) and the thin cut is mostly well chosen. Two gaps stop screen 1 working at all. Seven Majors follow, where the concept or board contradicts an AG-02 rule it keeps, or leaves out what AG-02 requires for a kept action.

## Objections

### Blockers

**B1. The public form page cannot be built from FO-2's routes.** *Needed for the proof.* Fact, high confidence.
* What breaks: the hosted page and iframe need the firm's name, the consent wording and its version, the "Interested in" options, the thank-you text, on/off, and a fresh `submissionId` issued when the page is served. FO-2 offers only the POST. C:41 says FO-4 "Reads and writes only FO-2's routes".
* Example: the page cannot draw the consent tick's wording, and the "same submission id twice → one lead" protection (FO-2 Done when) has no id to send.
* Evidence: contracts:52 lists `POST /api/public/forms/:token/submissions` as the only public form route. AG-2-5 rule 2: "For the iframe form the server issues them when it serves the page." The `ConfigReadGuard` in `api/src/core.ts` refuses a GET with no person unless the route is marked `OpenRead`. The contract does not say how the iframe is limited to the firm's allowed sites. A POST from inside the iframe carries our own origin, so an Origin check cannot do it. A `frame-ancestors` policy on the served page can.
* Fix: add one public read to FO-2, `GET /api/public/forms/:token`, marked `OpenRead`, rate limited, and returning only form data. It issues `submissionId` plus the load stamp, and the served page sets `frame-ancestors` from the allowed sites. FO-4 lists the route in §6.

**B2. No firm can switch its form on: the web-form settings are on the board's menu but not designed.** *Needed for the proof.* Fact, high confidence.
* What breaks: FO-1 seeds "one web form (off)" (rows:17). The concept names the owner persona "(form, reasons)" (C:6) and the board's menu shows "Settings › Web form · Reasons" (B:48, links to `#`). No screen sets on/off, allowed sites, consent wording or thank-you text, or gives the link and snippet. The empty state's "[Copy your form link]" (B:56) has nowhere to read the link from.
* Example: a new firm opens Leads, and every visitor sees "This form is not available". The Done-when journey would pass only if the test switched the form on through the API. That is scaffolding standing in for the capability (P-33; C:43 "demo steps use the capability's own path").
* Evidence: AG-2-5 rule 1 (the form's settings), rule 13 ("Building and publishing a form needs `agent/prospect:configure-form`"). Contracts §2.1 already has `GET|PUT /api/front-office/web-form`.
* Fix: draw one right panel, "Web form", with: on/off, the hosted link and the iframe snippet with Copy, allowed sites, the consent wording (shown with its version), and the thank-you text. Fields stay fixed in month one. Add one BR for it ("only Owner/Admin; publishing refused without email or phone" is already met by the fixed fields). The Reasons editor is not needed in month one, because AG-02's lists are seeded (m9). Remove that menu link or mark it as a later row.

### Majors

**M1. Add a lead is in scope but not drawn, and the board's Sample contradicts AG-02's rules for it.** *Needed for the proof.* Fact, high confidence.
* What breaks: the board puts L-00033 "Added by hand" in Unassigned leads with "Call now" (B:54). AG-02 makes the person who adds a lead its owner, and gives such a lead no "Call now" task. C:28 lists "Add a lead" as a right panel, but the board has no panel. The duplicate check before saving, with its two choices, is not designed.
* Evidence: AG-2-4 rule 8: "The person who adds the lead owns it". Rule 9: no "Call now" task. AG-2-37 rule 2: "A lead with no 'Call now' task never shows it". AG-2-84 rule 12. AG-2-4 rules 4-6 cover the check before saving: name, label, what matched, owner, and a nameless line for a record the user cannot see.
* Fix: draw the Add a lead panel. It asks for name, email or phone, and source (picked), with "more" on request. Show the live match line. In month one, offer only **Add as new**, because Link is later (contracts §2.1 "Later"), and say so in the copy. Add BR-11: "A lead added by hand is owned by its maker and gets no Call now". Correct the Sample row.

**M2. "Book a consult": the board shows the primary button that the concept says must not exist, and the next step gets stuck.** *Needed for the proof.* Fact (board vs concept) and logic (next step), high confidence.
* What breaks: (a) B:78 draws "Book a consult" as the primary action, but C:44 says no button until booking is live. The builder gets two answers. (b) Showing the suggestion with no action breaks AG-2-37 rule 8 ("A suggestion opens the matching action when clicked"). Without booking, "no consult is booked" is always true. AG-2-37 rule 11 shows only the first true rule, so every prospect, even one in Fees discussed or Retainer sent, shows "Book a consult", and "Follow up" (rule 4) never appears. (c) The Log a call panel offers "a callback task" button (B:83), but tasks are not built (C:9 "Not built … follow-ups"). AG-2-94 rule 13: "A panel or button whose module is not built is not shown."
* Evidence: as above. AG-02's own precedent: AG-2-38 rule 7 says "Until AG-04 builds it, that reason does not appear". AG-2-37 rule 10 applies "only if AG-08 ships" it.
* Fix: while booking is not live, rule 2 does not apply: the next step skips to the next true rule, or shows none. Write this as a BR with pass and fail tests. Remove the "Book a consult" button from the board, and remove the callback-task offer until tasks exist. The right rail's primary is then the first next step that has a live action, or none.

**M3. Kept actions are missing the reverse that AG-02 requires.** *Needed for the proof.* Fact, high confidence.
* What breaks: Close lead is kept, but nothing reopens a lead. A "Closed" chip lists closed leads (B:50), and FO-2 already has `reopen` (contracts §2.1 API). Convert to client is kept without Undo convert. Both are one click plus a reason. Without the reverse, a wrong click has no way back in the screens.
* Evidence: BR-22, which concept BR-1 cites: "A CLOSED lead can be reopened as the same lead." AG-2-80 outcome: "They can reopen it." AG-2-94 states table: "Lead CLOSED | Reopen lead". AG-2-47 outcome: "A manual Convert can be undone while the client is empty"; rule 18 requires a reason. EXPERIENCE §2.8: "undo where possible".
* Fix: add **Reopen lead** on a closed lead. The route exists, and it needs one pass and one fail test (a lead closed by Combine is out of scope). For Undo convert, see founder question 1. If it is deferred, record the deviation, and say on the Convert confirm that it cannot be undone in month one.

**M4. A lost prospect cannot be found again.** *Needed for the proof.* Fact, high confidence.
* What breaks: Lost is not a column (C:26), the board and list filter only by owner (B:61), and Leads lists leads, not prospects. After Mark lost, the prospect is reachable only through its person's record, and only if you remember the person.
* Evidence: AG-2-44 rule 8: "listed in the Lost filter and reopenable". AG-2-18 rule 7: "Drag to Lost opens the loss flow". AG-2-18 rule 1: one column per stage.
* Fix: keep the action-not-column choice, which is sound for an open-work board, but add a stage-kind filter "Lost" to the List view. Add one sentence recording the AG-2-18 rule 1 deviation.

**M5. Drag and keyboard lifting need a library the row's fence forbids.** *Needed for the proof.* Fact on dependencies, judgment on effort, high confidence.
* What breaks: B:59 specifies drag plus "Space to lift, arrows, Space to drop". `web/package.json` has no drag-and-drop library (only Radix dialog, menu, select and so on). FO-4's Touch list is "new files under `web/src/modules/front-office/`". A new library needs PF-2's technology gate and a `package.json` hook (contracts §5.4; STANDARDS §1).
* Fix: keep the drag-and-drop library out of month one. Use a **"Move to…" menu** on each card and on the record's stage control, with the existing Radix dropdown. That gives the keyboard path (AG-2-94 rule 12; EXPERIENCE §2.12). Mouse drag uses native HTML drag events. If the Space-lift pattern is wanted, take it through the gate first and name it in the row.

**M6. Who sees what departs from AG-02 in two places.** *Needed for the proof.* Fact on the rules. Judgment that the risk is latent while the firm's "everyone sees all" is on (Sample default), medium confidence.
* What breaks: (a) contracts:54 lets "others" see Unassigned leads, and the concept inherits it. AG-02 shows that list only to holders of `view.all` or `assign`. (b) The match line always names the other person (C:38, B:52, B:75). AG-02 gives a nameless line to a viewer who cannot open the matched record, so a case officer limited to their own leads would learn a name they may not see.
* Evidence: AG-2-13c rule 6 and its test: "Given a user with `view.own` only and without `assign`, then Unassigned leads is not shown." AG-2-94 §12 edge, AG-2-4 rule 6: "Possible match with a record you cannot open — ask a manager" (D8.79). AG-2-64 example.
* Fix: in the concept (and in FO-2's test line), show the Unassigned leads chip only to `view.all` or `assign` holders. When the viewer cannot see the matched person, use the nameless match line. Test both with "everyone sees all" off.

**M7. BR-8 and BR-10 cannot be tested as written.** *Needed for the proof.* Fact, high confidence.
* What breaks: BR-10's "Buttons show only to people allowed" has no table of who may do what, so no fail test can be written. The personas (C:6) are not mapped to AG-02's keys (`edit`, `assign`, `lose`, `convert`, `configure-form`). BR-8 names "Signed offline" as a way to Won, but FO-2 has no route for it (contracts §2.1: `move|lose|convert`) and no screen offers it. STANDARDS §3.10 requires a pass and a fail test for every rule.
* Fix: add a five-line table: role → Take it, Assign, Engage, Close or Reopen, Mark lost, Convert, web-form settings. Take it from AG-02's §8 Access lines (for example, Convert needs `agent/prospect:convert`) and PF-4's roles, marked Sample where PF-4 has not settled them. In BR-8, keep the drag refusal and Convert, and say Signed offline arrives with FO-5/6.

### Minors
* **m1. Engage panel copy and BR-7.** *Needed.* B:82 says "You become its owner", which is wrong when the lead already has an owner: the prospect goes to the lead's owner (AG-2-75 §12). BR-7's "makes one prospect" could be read as forbidding the second Engage that AG-2-75 rule 9 allows. Fix: make the copy conditional ("Client C stays its owner"). Restate BR-7 as one prospect per request id, and either list existing prospects on a second Engage (rule 9) or mark Engage again as later.
* **m2. Log a call.** *Needed.* The panel has no start time (AG-2-37a rule 2: "default: the click, or now… editable"). A call logged an hour late would set the wrong first-response time (BR-6). The panel also does not handle an unreadable phone: AG-2-94 test, "disabled with 'Fix number'". Fix: add a "When" field defaulting to now, and the disabled Call button state.
* **m3. Board cards and columns.** *Needed.* Cards show the P-number and "Interested in". AG-2-18 rule 3 asks for owner, value, days in stage and the next-step indicator. Rule 5 puts the count and value total per currency in each column header. Rule 6 pages each column with "Load more". The record's timeline also loads one page at a time (AG-2-94 rule 15). "Won – client · 1 this month" (B:68) has no rule saying which won prospects show. Fix: match the card fields, add the column totals and "Load more", and state the Won column's window as a rule.
* **m4. One-user firm (X-4) is only partly covered.** *Needed.* BR-10 hides Take it, Assign and Unassigned. AG-02 also hides the owner line on the record (AG-2-94 rule 3d), the owner on cards (AG-2-18 rule 3) and, by implication, the "Owner: anyone" filter (B:61). Fix: extend BR-10 and its test.
* **m5. Consent tick wording.** *Needed.* B:42, "Email me updates about my enquiry", describes service messages. AG-2-83's purposes are marketing (`MARKETING_EMAIL`), and a reply to an enquiry needs no tick. Sample wording sets the pattern firms copy. Fix: Sample wording that names marketing (for example "Send me news and offers from <firm> by email. I can stop at any time."). Unconfirmed: no counsel.
* **m6. The BIC-19 claim does not hold.** *Needed.* C:24 says "five inputs and one tick". The board has six inputs (B:36-41: given names, family name, email, phone, interested in, message). Fix: count them honestly, and either drop one (the message, for example) or record why six.
* **m7. Retrofit words differ from AG-02 with no mapping.** *Needed.* Refused drop: AG-2-26 rule 3 has its own text ("This stage is set when the prospect becomes a client…"). Take-it conflict: AG-2-86 rule 3, "Client F already has this lead". Deactivated owner: "(Inactive) Name" (AG-2-13c rule 9). Contracts say `won_via CONVERTED` and `prospect.stage_moved`, while AG-02 says `MANUAL` and `prospect.stage.changed`. Fix: use AG-02's words, or add a one-line mapping.
* **m8. States missing or wrong.** *Needed.* A drag of a Won card has no refusal (AG-2-26 rule 2). A stale move, made after someone else moved the card, has no conflict message (AG-2-26 §12; STANDARDS §4, 409). A record the user may not see should show "not found" (AG-2-94 rule 16). A deactivated owner's lead is drawn as read-only (C:29), but AG-02 puts it back in Unassigned leads with Take it (AG-2-13c rule 6, AG-2-86 rule 1). Fix: add these four to §4's states and tests.
* **m9. Reason lists labelled Sample.** *Needed.* AG-02 names the seeded lists. Lead close: Not interested, Junk or spam, Cannot reach, Outside what we do, plus the system reason "Added to a prospect", never offered by the Close panel (AG-2-80 rule 2). Prospect lost: seven reasons (AG-2-44 rule 2). These are requirement values, not immigration values. Fix: seed and show AG-02's lists. This needs a matching edit to FO-1 and the contracts.
* **m10. Frame and menu.** *Needed.* C:9 says "the shell … reused". But PF-1's routes render ahead of the Studio's catch-all (rows:11), and the Studio `Shell` turns case people away (`App.tsx:113`), so the sales pages need their own `Frame` (with an area and a menu). `frame.tsx` has one. The Board pattern deviation (C:26) is justified (see What is sound), but it is recorded only in the concept, so a reviewer checking EXPERIENCE §3 ("do not invent") would fail it. * **m11. Board Sample shows month-two states.** *Not built.* "Client signed, waiting for the firm" and "Signed Thu" (B:67-68) are FO-5/6 states, and "Consult Tue 10:00" (B:65) is an FO-3 state. Month one cannot produce them, and a builder may build badges for them. Fix: replace them with month-one Sample, or label them "appears when FO-3/5/6 are live".

The sidebar badge "Leads 3" (B:48) does not say what it counts (EXPERIENCE §2.7). Fix: add a name, email or phone search box, or note why not, and label the badge ("3 unassigned").

## What is sound
* **The AG-02 model is kept faithfully.** A lead has no stage, type or value (BR-1 = BR-22). Engage is a person's click (AG-2-75). No drop onto Won, and Won comes once by Convert or a signature (AG-2-26 rule 3, AG-2-47 rule 1). Front office never opens a case: "Open a case?" belongs to the case side (contracts §2.6; AG-2-47 rule 9). Calls never move a stage, and the first call is the first response, once (AG-2-37a rules 5, 7). Every repeat makes a lead, and a match is flagged, never linked (AG-2-84 rules 7, 11). The visitor is never told whether they are known (AG-2-5 rule 10).
* **The thin cut is right for month one.** It leaves out Corporate, Link/Merge, round robin, follow-ups, saved views, bulk actions, reports, Park and Nurture. It adds no AI. Lost and Nurture are actions, not columns. I found no gold-plating beyond m11 and the callback offer in M2.
* **The Board pattern deviation is justified.** AG-2-18 (the team's requirement, first in the source order) requires a board and list "in sync". A board is the proven pattern for staged sales work (EXPERIENCE §0 "proven patterns"). The List toggle gives the accessible table route. It still needs recording (m10).
* **The record screen follows AG-2-94's anatomy.** Header label, context chips, left rail person with consent and source, centre timeline with composer, right rail with next step and actions. It has one primary per panel. Panels are right-hand, and Convert is a yes/no confirm (TEAM-PATTERNS Overlays). The Convert copy matches AG-2-47 rule 16 word for word. The "9 open" count correctly leaves out Won (AG-2-26 rule 12).
* **The app can host it.** `Panel`, `Dialog`, `Table`, `Pill`, `Notice`, `EmptyState`, `Skeleton` and `Tooltip` exist, as do the `--color-*` tokens and axe-core and Playwright. `Frame` takes a `menu`.
* **The best-in-class section (4b)** describes the right patterns: a three-column record, days in stage, speed to lead, enquiry → consult → retainer → matter. **Not checked:** I did no web research and found no primary sources cited in the section. It stays **Unverified**. "Nothing typed twice to the case" depends on FO-8 (contracts §2.6), not on FO-4.
