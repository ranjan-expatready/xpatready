2 · Remove filing: what changes is named before anything happens

Same derivation as Prepare application, run on the case as it would be. Every other filing that changes is named with the change. Reason required (as the C6.12 split). The button says the action.

Remove Sample Client B's filing?

Sample Client B stays on the case, not travelling: asked only what the others' forms need about them. Their answers and uploads are kept.
