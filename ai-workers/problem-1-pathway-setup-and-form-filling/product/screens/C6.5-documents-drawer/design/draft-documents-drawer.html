What the officer sees

  • A small secondary button in every section header, staff mode only. The client's questionnaire never shows it.
  • "1 to resolve" appears when this person has an open check. It comes from one GET …/checks?state=open per case view, shared by all sections.
  • Confirm section stays the section's one primary button. While a check is open it answers 409 open_checks, and the toast shows the server's sentence.
  • The line menu item appears only on a line with a current file that a reader reads. It opens the drawer on the person's first section that asks a fact the file gives. It needs reads on the checklist line (Q2); until then it is Not built.

Never

  • The button is never a primary button.

  • The read is stored once. Opening the drawer again shows the stored read (GET …/proposals).

Frame 6 · Resolve a check in place, reason missing (BR-10; C6.5-14, -15)POST /runtime/checks/:id/resolve {keep, reason, answerId}
  • The reason form from the findings panel, opened inside the row. No second overlay.
  • Two choices, as the API takes them: the document's value, or the typed answer. To use a third value, keep the typed answer and edit it in the Agent view (keep: other is Not built).
  • The server's words appear on the field. What was typed is kept.
  • Sends answerId (the answer shown). If someone changed it since: "The answer changed after you opened this check: read it again, then resolve." The drawer reloads.
  • After a correction (frame 3), "Keep the typed answer" is chosen already.
