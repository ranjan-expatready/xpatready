What the officer sees

  • A small secondary button in every section header, staff mode only. The client's questionnaire never shows it.
  • "1 to resolve" appears when this person has an open check. It comes from one GET …/checks?state=open per case view, under one shared query key, never per section.

  • The button is never a primary button.

Flow

  • On open the drawer calls …/fill-from-documents/plan (US$0). With a passport only, it calls POST …/fill-from-documents at once.
  • Skeletons while it reads. The words say what is happening and that it costs nothing.
  • The read is stored once. Opening the drawer again calls the same POST, which returns the stored read with its not_read list and reads nothing again.
  • A letter beside a passport is listed in waiting_for_cost: its own cost card shows below the passport's block (frame 8).

Frame 2 · Review before Confirm & use (BR-3 to BR-8)differences first · ticks only on names and values that will be written · one primary

Order (BR-7)

  • Differs: the server's differs, beside the current answer (current) and who gave it (the Agent view's channel and by). No tick: its check is the look.
  • Left for you: the server's left_why, shown before the click and on reopen. No tick. "Answer it" opens the question's own field in the row (frame 3).
  • Check each: only expanded values that will be written and agree. Here given names and sex. A tick or "Correct it".
  • Collapsed: one disclosure for the three values backed by check digits.
  • Not on this document: place of birth, answered in the row.

Confirm & use

  • Held only until each name and agreeing value is ticked or corrected. The tooltip says how many are left.
  • Never held by a difference. The footer says what Confirm & use will do with it.
  • Sends {subjectId, checked: [ticked read-value ids]}. The server ignores checked today (Q2).

Each value shows

  • Label, its section (from the Agent view), value, page, reader. For rules values: "check digit passed" or "no check digit of its own". No confidence words without set thresholds.

Frame 5 · After Confirm & use, with a check open (BR-9, BR-10, BR-12)confirm-and-use → {confirmed, used, agreed, checks, left, closed, message}

Summary

  • The server's message (labels, plain words), with who and when from confirmed {by, at} in IST. No button any more: one Confirm & use per document.
  • The server's left still lists a left value the officer already answered in the drawer, so its sentence may say "left for you" for it: the drawer shows the Left group with each answer, and the challenger or builder decides whether the server's message drops answered ones.
  • Any closed[] check (it was opened on an earlier file of this document) is listed with its reason in words.
  • Each check is resolved here, in place (frame 6). The section's "1 to resolve" badge counts it.

Mark checked against the passport (BR-12)

  • Offered only where it adds evidence: a left value once answered, place of birth once typed, an answer kept by a resolved check. Not on used or agreed values, which already carry the document.
  • Refused while a check is open (409 open_checks), on a replaced file (409 not_current) or a kind that does not give the fact (422 not_evidence): the server's words on the row.

Frame 6 · Resolve a check in place, reason missing (BR-10; C6.5-14, -15)POST /runtime/checks/:id/resolve {keep, reason, answerId}
  • A reason form inside the row, built from the app's Field and Textarea. Not the findings dialog: no second overlay.
  • The words name who gave the current answer, from the Agent view's channel and by: "Client A typed…", or "You entered…" after a correction.
  • Two choices, as the API takes them. To use a third value, keep the current answer, then correct it in place (keep: other is Not built).
  • The server's words appear on the field. What was typed is kept.
  • Sends answerId. 409 changed (the answer changed since) or 409 not_current (a newer file was uploaded: keep the answer, or read the new file) shows the server's sentence and reloads.
