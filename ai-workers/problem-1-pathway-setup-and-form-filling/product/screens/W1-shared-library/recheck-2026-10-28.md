## 2. New since 27 Oct, ranked
### Major
* **R1 · When does live drafting start: when a form enters, or on a click?**
  * *Fact.* P-120 (confirmed 28 Oct): AI drafts fields, sections, document types and checks *when a form or checklist enters the library*. P-125 amended 28 Oct (founder, S3 M1): Start setup loads a saved finder result at US$0; otherwise it *runs live only on the click* of "Find forms with AI (about …)". S3 is built that way [s].
  * *Judgment.* Under P-120 as written, a form found by a free replay or uploaded by hand would start paid drafting with no click. That is the opposite of the founder's 28 Oct call, and it brings back M7's budget risk.
  * *Recommend (founder Q1):* the same rule as S3. A saved draft for that form edition replays at once at US$0. Otherwise the form's row in the queue (and the library form) offers "Draft fields with AI (about US$x · at most US$y)", with one `costWords` line, and runs live only on that click. A Load sample recording pass is the single logged exception (M7).
