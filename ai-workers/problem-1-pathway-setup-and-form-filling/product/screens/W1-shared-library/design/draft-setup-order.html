How it fits together Capability[F] b2 explainer (Q2)

One path for every piece: a source goes in, AI proposes, a person decides, a second person approves new items, and a release publishes them.

1 SourceOfficial PDF form, checklist or guide, from canada.ca or uploaded
2 AI proposesReuse · new · ambiguous · conflict, beside the source page
3 Duplicate checkExact name: blocked, no AI. Same meaning: AI verdict
4 A person decidesAccept, edit or reject; or describes a missing item
5 Second personApproves every new, retired or restored item
6 ReleasePublished; every combination that uses it gets it
