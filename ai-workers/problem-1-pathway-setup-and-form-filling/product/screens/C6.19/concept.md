# C6.19 — Facts from an earlier case: offered, re-confirmed, never copied silently (concept r1)

Row (P-419, founder 8 Nov; brief from Lead 37, 9 Nov): a returning client's confirmed facts are offered on their next case and re-confirmed before use, never copied silently (P-65: copies, not live links, as BR-3); expiring facts (passport, police certificate) are checked against their dates, with an official-source check for each expiry rule. C6.3 reuses facts only within one case; DEMO.md row 18 is the promise. Area: runtime. No board (P-223 (4)): the tech lead approves after the fresh challenge.

## 1. Job and journey
**Job:** when a client the firm has served before opens another application, the case officer brings in what that person already confirmed, sees each value with where and when it was confirmed, says "still true" fact by fact, and never re-types it; an expired passport is caught before it reaches a form.
Persona: the case officer (firm staff). Journey: Open a case (C6.1) → **Facts from an earlier case (this)** → Answers and Confirm section (C6.3, C6.6) → fill (C7.1). Demo row 18: case B (With an LMIA) reuses case A's family facts (Visitor visa).

## 2. Layers (STANDARDS §2)
* **Exists:** append-only answers (`ClientCanonicalAnswer`, DECLARED/VERIFIED, `source_json`), `confirmation()` (`engine/runtime/case.ts`), Confirm section, firm isolation (`reach`, C6.11), the employer-case copy (C1.3, `api/src/employer-link.ts`: copy once, seen-ids stale check, row lock, nothing overwritten silently), the audit log.
* **Team:** a client profile reused across matters (team `Party`, AG-07); our runtime makes a new party per case, so a shared party would turn every relative lookup into a live link (`runtime.ts` finds relatives by party). **We do not share parties.**
* **New:** `engine/runtime/earlier-facts.ts` (pure: which facts are offered, their state, expiry flags), `samples/fact-expiry-rules.json` (rules as data with their source, P-425), `api/src/earlier-facts.ts` (GET offer, POST re-confirm), a card on the case page (`web/src/runtime/earlier-facts.tsx`). **No migration:** copies are ordinary answer rows; the link is an audit event, as C1.3 does.

## 3. Business rules
* **BR-1 Who is offered (firm, same person):** earlier cases of the same firm whose principal has the same name (trimmed, case-insensitive), not this case; another firm's case is never listed or reachable (404). The officer picks the earlier case explicitly; nothing is chosen for them. Source: P-419, C6.11 BR-20.
* **BR-2 What is offered:** for each person on this case matched to a person on the earlier case (same role and same name), that person's **confirmed** single facts there (`confirmation().confirmed` on the earlier case), that **this** case's pinned release asks for that person (a placement, person scope). Application facts ("The application": dates, purpose) are never offered: they belong to that application. Source: P-419 "confirmed facts", BIC-01 scope rule (P-316 (2)).
* **BR-3 Copies, not links:** a re-confirmed fact is written as a new answer on this case's person (`source_json.channel = "earlier_case"`, with the earlier case, the earlier answer id and its confirm date); the earlier case is never written; a later change on either case does not reach the other. Source: P-65, SA-1B-283 r4.
* **BR-4 Nothing used until re-confirmed:** an offered value is shown only; it reaches this case (and so a form) only when the officer ticks it and presses "Re-confirm and use"; it is then confirmed by that officer (VERIFIED, `verified_by`), since that press is the confirmation. Unticked facts never move. Source: P-419 "re-confirmed before use, never copied silently".
* **BR-5 Nothing overwritten silently:** a fact this case already has reads Same (not offered) or Different (shown with both values; replaced only when ticked). The officer's view is checked fresh by the answer ids seen, under the case row lock; a change in between is refused "Changed since you looked", nothing written. Source: C1.3 BR-4, BR-6; BIC-07.
* **BR-6 Expiry checked against dates, each rule cited:** rules are data (`samples/fact-expiry-rules.json`: fact, kind, months, the facts it covers, source with page, quote, date or "Sample"). An **expired** value (and every fact the rule covers, e.g. the whole passport) is flagged with its rule and cannot be re-confirmed: the officer enters the new document. An **expiring soon** value is flagged with its rule and may be re-confirmed. No today date means no check passed (fail closed). Source: P-170, below.
  * Passport expired: canada.ca, "Apply for a visitor visa" (recorded 27 Oct 2026, `official-sources/ircc/canada/pages/visit-canada-apply-visitor-visa.html`): "All applicants need to include a digital copy of your valid (not expired) passport or travel document".
  * Passport expiring within 6 months: same page: "Your passport or travel document should be valid for at least 6 months from the date you plan to travel." Checked against today (the travel date may not be known yet); a warning, never a block.
  * Police certificate older than 6 months: **Sample** (no canada.ca page with this rule is committed; the Express Entry police certificates page is cited only from a search summary in `research_notes/…/ircc_document_fact_map.md`). Its fact (`person.police_certificate_issue_date`) is not in the library yet, so the rule shows on no demo case; engine tests prove it. ASSUMPTIONS row added.
* **BR-7 Read-only and roles:** staff of the case's firm only (client and configuration people 403); a case recorded as submitted (C8.2) copies nothing. Answer values never go into audit text (STANDARDS §5).

## 4. Layout (EXPERIENCE §3: a card on the case page, the C1.3 employer card's pattern)
Card "Facts from an earlier case" under the people list, shown when BR-1 finds a case. Empty: no card. Header: "Client A has an earlier case: VV-0001, Visitor visa, opened 2 Nov". One select when several. Per matched person, a table: Fact · Earlier value · Confirmed there (date, by) · This case · Status chip (New / Different / Expired / Expires soon) · tick. Expired rows: tick disabled, the rule's words and source in a Details line. Unmatched people: one line "Child C is not on this case: add them to bring their facts". One primary button "Re-confirm and use (n)"; result line "Re-confirmed 7 facts from VV-0001. They are on this case now; the earlier case did not change." Design: `design/earlier-facts.html` follows with the build.

## 4b. Best in class (no outside network in this container on 9 Nov: from the committed study `reports/verify-2026-11-03/V4-best-in-class.md` (F1, F6) and published practice known to the drafter; to be re-fetched on the Mac)
* **Tax interviews carrying last year forward** (TurboTax, H&R Block): prior-year facts come in pre-filled and the person is asked "is this still correct?" section by section. Best: nothing typed twice. We copy that, but per fact with the source date, because an immigration value must be confirmed by a person who saw it (P-411).
* **Immigration case tools with client profiles** (CampLegal, Docketwise, INSZoom per V4 F1): one profile auto-populates every new case's forms. Best: zero re-entry. Weak: a live profile silently changes earlier filings' data and shows no "confirmed when". We beat it: copies, dated, per case (P-65).
* **Bank KYC refresh** (FATF risk-based periodic review): identity documents are re-verified when they expire, not reused. We copy that as BR-6, with the rule cited on screen.
* **GOV.UK "Check answers" pattern:** show what will be used, with a change link, before use. Our table is that check before the copy.

## 5. Copy
Chip tooltips: "New: this case has no value yet." · "Different: this case has its own value; tick to replace it." · "Expired on 1 Jan 2025: the passport's facts cannot be reused. Enter the new passport on this case." · "Expires within 6 months: canada.ca says a passport should be valid for at least 6 months from the date you plan to travel." Errors: "Changed since you looked: <facts>. Reload; nothing was copied." · "<fact> cannot be re-confirmed: the passport expired on <date> (<rule source>)."
