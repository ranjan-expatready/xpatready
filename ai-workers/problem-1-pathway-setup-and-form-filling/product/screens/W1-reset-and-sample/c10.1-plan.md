## Built (30 Oct, fourth runtime session; US$0)
* **Cross-lane (Lead choice (a); an agreed narrow touch in `api/src/sources.ts` and `api/src/proposals.ts`):** the review queue's transactions get room for their work: `QUEUE_TX = { timeout: 60 s, maxWait: 10 s }` (proposals.ts). It applies to the field-map draft's insert (sources.ts ingest, the `:581` transaction), the queue's decide and its undo. Decide and undo have the same pattern: a library query per item, up to 200 items. Past the room the transaction rolls back and the step says so in words ("Putting IMM 5707 (01-2023)'s 9 proposals on the review queue took longer than 60 s, so none was added…"), never Prisma's text with a file path. A test-only switch, `STUDIO_SLOW_TX="<file>:<ms>"` (isolated server only, like `STUDIO_FAULT_SOURCE`), slows one step inside its transaction. `check_queue_tx` 8/8.
