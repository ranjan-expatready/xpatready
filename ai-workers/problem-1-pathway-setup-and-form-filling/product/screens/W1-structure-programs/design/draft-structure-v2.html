Why A+ over v1 A (panel for every add)

  • Easier setup: no overlay to open per name; type, see the checks, press Enter; the next row is ready.
  • Cleaner: one outline, one detail panel only when a name is selected; actions appear on their own rows, not as links on every line.
  • Trust: the new name is typed exactly where it will live, right under the siblings it is checked against.
  • Team fit: the team's tree view + detail panel; WAI-ARIA tree pattern for the keyboard.
  • Columns (B) and diagram (C) stay rejected: see draft-structure.html.
