## What held
* **Concurrent switching:** four on-principal children switched travelling 6 times each, at once, mixed with 24 prepare reads (48 calls). All answered 200. The end state matched the last write. `filing_applicant` had 6 rows, 6 distinct, with no duplicates. Each person kept their 3 items, the same filing ids and one family group. 10 parallel first reads gave one version. A second run with 2 declared-only children and the sponsor switch gave 24×200 and 8×422 and the right final modes.
* **BR-6 by the API:** an on-principal child is applying on the principal's filing, then not travelling gives `declared_only` with the same filing id, no fee line and no forms, then travelling again gets the same item ids back. A C11.6 ONLINE choice on that child's item survived the round trip. A Declared-only child starts not travelling; `applies:true` on add is honoured.
* **Fees per person (BR-10):** 6 applying people across 2 filings gave a visa fee capped at CAD 500, paid once by the principal. Age edges on the principal's filing: 13 exempt, 14 pays, 79 pays, 80 exempt (DOB today and tomorrow). Biometrics for 3 payers was capped at CAD 170. After splitting the spouse out, the family group was re-priced (500/170) and the separate group was 100/85.
* **Co-filing (BR-4):** the sponsorship is always in the principal's group, including when the principal was split out first (it followed into the SEPARATE group). Split of the sponsorship: 409. Switching the sponsor's travelling: 422 with the concept's words. The sponsorship has no applicant row (`co_filed|0`).
* **Pinned release:** after release 3 changed the child to Declared only, the release 2 case still showed both children applying on the principal's filing.
* **Roles:** client, author, approver and reviewer get 403 on prepare, the travelling switch and split. No actor gets 401.
* **Checks:** the database refuses other modes. `check_c1112_modes.ts` 39/39, `check_vv_filings.ts` 46/46, `check_vv_prepare.ts` 32/32, `check_c1112_prepare_api.ts` 16/16.
* **Speed:** prepare on a 6-person case averages 49 ms.
* **Text:** a 120-character Cyrillic child name is accepted and becomes declared only by default.
