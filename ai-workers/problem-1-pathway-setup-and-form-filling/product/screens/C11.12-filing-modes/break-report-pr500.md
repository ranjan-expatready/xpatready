## What held
* **The shipped money path, through the API:** 6 people applying across 2 filings: visa CAD 500 (family maximum), biometrics CAD 170 for 4 payers, the under-14 child exempt with IRCC's words. A declared-only child has no line on any basis. Split the spouse out: family group CAD 500 + 170 (5 × 100, no cap), separate group CAD 100 + 85. Re-priced per group, so no cap crosses groups. Switch a child to not travelling: CAD 570, the same filing id, and the **version changed**. `…/filings` and `…/prepare` agree on totals and group ids.
* **No double charge:** a per-application line is charged once on the principal's filing and covers everyone on it (R2 checks 49/49). Release modes win over table modes: release 3's child Declared only and Sponsor co-files were honoured. A table putting the principal on another's application is refused (BR-2).
* **Split and stored view:** a split made with a version read before another session switched a child gets **409 stale**. A split and a switch sent at the same time gave 200, 200, 409 and 200, leaving one FAMILY and one SEPARATE group, with no duplicates. Split body checks: a reason over 400 characters, spaces only, or a right-to-left override mark gets 400 in words; no version gets 400. The sponsorship and the principal get 409 (from the check run).
* **Roles:** client, author, approver and reviewer get 403 on prepare, filings and split; no actor gets 401.
* **Screen:** the Filing and Fees columns and "What a case gets" are correct on `vv.single`, `fs.outside` ("2 filings sent together … cannot be split", fees not worked out, never CAD 0) and `wp.family`. Submit is refused with "Filing for Child (travelling): Filing not set…". The sample loader keeps `wp.family`'s child line consistent (`applies: true` with Files own). No sideways scroll at 1024 px or at 720 px (200 % zoom), reading or editing. No page errors.
* **Budgets:** `deriveFilings` takes 4 ms for 400 people with their own filings and 10 ms for 400 on the principal's. `…/prepare` on a 7-person case takes 70–95 ms when warm.
