The card sits where the run was launched (inside the finder panel, a section of a combination, a library drawer). The header chip follows you while it runs. History › AI work shows the same card. No overlay on top of a panel; the person never loses the run.

One look everywhere. When launched from a panel (the finder is one) it stacks a panel on a panel, against "no stacked overlays" (TEAM-PATTERNS); minimised, it is a chip like c-A anyway.
