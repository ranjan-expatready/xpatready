**For the Lead to edit and decide** (screen-designer, 26 Oct). Boards: `design/draft-library.html` (library, finder, download, approve, upload; 11 views) and `design/draft-ai-run.html` (the shared run component and every job state; 9 views); "Show labels" on each board marks Capability/Scaffolding, Needed/Not built and decision marks. ## 1. Job and journey
A content publisher picks a combination, sees which forms it has and which are missing, adds the missing ones (proposed from the official IRCC guide, downloaded from canada.ca, or uploaded) and every background run (AI or not) shows its steps, progress, cost and a working Stop. Personas: content publisher (author), a second person where Q2 says so. Journey 2.1–2.2 (sources), 3.x (AI). Capabilities **C11.2** (one door), **C11.6** library part (what IRCC accepts), **C11.8** (runs), feeds C2.12 and W1-08/W1-10/W1-11. Decisions: P-103, P-102, P-107, P-108, P-110, P-105 (pattern), P-51, P-41, P-32, P-57/P-80, P-52, P-83.

## 2. The honest split — what AI does, what the system does, what a person does (founder asked to be corrected)
| Step | Who | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Pick the combination and its official guide or checklist page (canada.ca only; remembered as the combination's guide source) | Person | The page decides everything after it; a person vouches for it |
| Read the page and list every form number it mentions, with the sentence around it (US$0) | System, no AI | Form numbers are printed and linked on IRCC pages; finding them is a pattern match, never a guess |
| Add the forms the known list for this application type expects (US$0) | System, rules | A released combination of the same category, or the expert's list, already knows; Sample in Wave 1 |
| Read the page for **conditions and exceptions**: "if it applies to you", "IMM 5707 or IMM 5645", "a separate application" (IMM 5782 on guide 5256 is PR renunciation, not part of a visitor visa), for whom, and what IRCC says about applying online | **AI** (optional, about US$0.10, Sample) | Prose with exceptions is where rules fail and AI reads well; AI only labels what is on the page |
| Check every AI proposal: the quoted line must be on the page; a number not on canada.ca's form list is flagged, never downloaded | System | AI never becomes the source of a form's identity (STANDARDS §3.1, §3.8) |
| Select the forms to add | Person | Nothing applies until accepted (EXPERIENCE §2.6) |
| Form number → canada.ca's list of forms ("Form number · Form title · Date modified", 230 rows) → the form's page → its PDF link → download → read number, title, printed edition, published date, checksum → duplicate check → held | System, no AI | Fully deterministic; checked 26 Oct from the Mac: IMM 1295 page → link `…/imm1295/01-09-2023/imm1295e.pdf` → checksum equals the manifest's |
| Approve the held file | Person (Q2) | P-103, P-80 |
**Recommended:** Rules then AI (option b-C). Without AI it still works: the free scan + known list give the list and the person ticks; AI only adds conditions and catches exceptions. The AI condition becomes a *proposal* for the combination's "Only if" (W1-08/W1-10), never applied here.

## 3. Options (real ones, with consequences)
**(a) Library page.** **A · Recommended — library list with a combination lens:** Shared library › Forms shows all forms with IRCC's columns; "For combination [picker]" turns it into three groups: *On this combination* · *In the library, not on this combination* (Add to combination) · *Missing* (from the finder: Download from canada.ca). The combination's Forms tab has one link, "Find missing forms", that opens this page with the lens and the finder. One code path, exactly the founder's "pick → present / missing → add"; cost: the author leaves the combination page (lens header: "Back to Visitor visa"). **B — one finder panel opened from both places:** stays in context; two entry pages to test; present/missing only on the combination; the library stays a plain list. **C — "Forms by combination" matrix page:** all five at a glance; a new menu item (needs a decision), unusable past ~8 combinations, horizontal scroll at 1024 px.
**(b) Form finder.** **A — AI only:** simplest to explain; pays every time; misses nothing it reads but is the only source (if AI is down, nothing). **B — Rules only:** free and repeatable; keeps "IMM 5782" as a candidate a person must recognise; no conditions; needs the known list kept. **C · Recommended — Rules then AI** (the P-105 pattern): free scan + known list first, AI for conditions/exceptions on request, cost shown first; Rules only stays as the switch-off.
**(c) AI run panel.** **A · Recommended — inline run card + header chip:** the card appears where the run was launched (inside the finder panel, a section, a drawer); a chip in the Studio header ("Finding forms · step 4 of 5") follows the person anywhere while it runs; History › AI work shows the same card in full. No stacked overlays. **B — right drawer, minimisable to a chip:** one look everywhere; stacks on top of panels that launch runs (the finder is a panel), against TEAM-PATTERNS "no stacked overlays". **C — a page (today's AI work detail):** full detail; takes the person away from the work; the screen the founder found "looks stuck".

## 4. Business rules (each has a pass and a fail test, §7)
* BR-1 **One door:** forms enter only through the library (download from canada.ca or upload); a combination picks only from the library; "Attach a sample file" and the Sources-tab drop zone for forms are removed (guides/checklist pages stay sources) [F] P-103, P-102.
* BR-2 **Downloads only from canada.ca:** https, host `www.canada.ca` or `ircc.canada.ca` (visa-office instruction PDFs live there), the PDF link taken from the form's own page found through canada.ca's form list; a redirect to another host is refused; size ≤ 25 MB; PDF only; requests identify as the product (a browser-like agent was refused 26 Oct) [F] P-103.
* BR-3 **Identity is read from the file, never typed:** number, title, printed edition, published date, checksum (today's intake, no AI) [F] P-32, P-51, P-41.
* BR-4 **Duplicates:** same checksum → "Already in the library", nothing added; same number and printed edition, different file → held "Same edition, different file" [C2]; same number, newer edition → new-edition review (P-32, P-38); older → "Older edition: not used"; French version → held. Checked before download where the page link equals a stored source link, and always after.
* BR-5 **Held until approved:** a downloaded or uploaded file is unusable (combination, AI, impact) until approved; the approval records who, when, the canada.ca page and the checksum [F] P-57, P-80, P-103 [C1 → Q2].
* BR-6 **AI proposes with evidence:** each proposal carries the quoted line, the condition, for whom and the page; a quote not found on the page is dropped and listed; page text is data, never instructions [F] P-103, STANDARDS §3.8, §8.
* BR-7 **Rules first, AI on request:** scan + known list always run (US$0); AI runs only when chosen, after its cost is shown; with AI off or failing the finder still finishes [F] P-105 pattern, STANDARDS §4.
* BR-8 **Present / missing are calculated:** On this combination · In the library, not on it · Missing (proposed, not in the library) · Dismissed (with reason, shown under "Dismissed (2)"); counts say what they count [F] EXPERIENCE §2.5, §2.7.
* BR-9 **What IRCC accepts** per edition: PDF · Online · PDF or online, with basis "Sample" until the expert confirms; Online shows "needs the browser extension (later wave)"; the combination default (W1-08) must be within it [F] P-107 [M2].
* BR-10 **One run component, one state set** for every background run (finder, download, field mapping, sections, conditions, duplicate checks): Queued · Running (step n of m) · Stopping · Stopped · Failed (reason + next step) · Done · Done with problems [F] P-110.
* BR-11 **Cost first, logged:** a live AI run states "about US$x, at most US$y" and the Wave 1 budget left (US$40) before Start; refused if over; every run's cost goes to the spend ledger and AI work; replays and system steps say US$0 [F] P-110, STANDARDS §8.
* BR-12 **Stop really ends it:** Stop ends the run's process within 10 s (terminate, then force); no call starts after Stop; the cost of a call already sent is recorded; finished steps' results stay; a step past 2× its usual time says "Taking longer than usual"; a step past its limit (AI read 10 min, download 60 s, Sample) stops itself as Failed [F] P-110.
* BR-13 **Reset empties the library;** Load sample puts the 5 combinations' official PDFs in from the committed copies (Scaffolding, never shown as the product's download) [F] P-108, P-83.
* BR-14 **Server-side permission,** deny by default: author for finder, download, upload; approval per Q2; retire, never delete, a form in use [F] STANDARDS §6, P-52.

## 10. Questions for the founder (recommendation first)
1. **How should the form finder find a combination's forms?** **Recommended: Rules then AI** — free scan of the IRCC page and the known list first, AI (about US$0.10, Sample) only for conditions and exceptions, you select. Or a second person for every file, as today.
