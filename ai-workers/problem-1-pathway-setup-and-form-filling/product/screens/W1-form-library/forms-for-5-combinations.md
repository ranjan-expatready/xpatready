# Forms for the 5 wave-1 combinations + the LMIA example (W1-12) and structure names (W1-03)

Research on official canada.ca pages (IRCC, ESDC), read 26 Oct 2026; no PDFs downloaded. **Every row is Sample — the immigration expert confirms.** Legal points: Unconfirmed: no counsel.
Shorthand: **Page** = `https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/application/application-forms-guides/<slug>.html`; **PDF** = `https://www.canada.ca/content/dam/ircc/documents/pdf/english/kits/forms/<path>`. **Edition** = the "Last updated" IRCC shows on the form page (the PDF folder carries the same date as DD-MM-YYYY). Channels: **SA** = IRCC secure account, **APR** = Authorized Paid Representatives Portal, **IP** = IRCC Portal, **PRP** = Permanent Residence Portal (applicant or representative version), **LO** = ESDC LMIA Online.

### Representatives and a deterministic lookup
* [Accounts for representatives](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/corporate/partners-service-providers/authorized-paid-representatives-portal.html): **APR portal** = visitor visa, study and work permits, Express Entry, IEC, eTA; **PR portal (representative)** = non-EE PR incl. spousal/family sponsorship and non-EE PNP; **IRCC Portal for representatives** = TRVs and in-Canada refugee claims.
* PRP forms are a mix of digital forms and PDFs; forms needing third-party signatures (IMM 1344, IMM 5476) are signed and uploaded ([2021 notice](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/news/notices/new-rep-pr-portal.html)). The **new IRCC Portal** refuses anyone "using a representative" ([page](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/application/online-account.html)).
* **No machine-readable forms list found** (no CSV/JSON/open data). The [forms index](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/application/application-forms-guides.html) is an HTML table (number | title | date modified) and URLs follow fixed patterns, so a lookup by number is feasible by reading that table plus each form page — with exceptions to handle: guide 5256 still links an old IMM 5476 PDF path (`/content/dam/ircc/migration/...`), IMM 5475's PDF folder date ≠ its "Last updated", and "date modified" on the index is the page's date, not the edition.
