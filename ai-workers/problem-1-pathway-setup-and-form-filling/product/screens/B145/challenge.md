# B145 · C9.5 Adopt a published update on an open case: challenge

Fresh challenger (did not draft it), 7 Nov 2026, against `concept.md`, `design/draft-release-update.html`, `best-in-class-sources-2026-11-07.md` and the app on `origin/main` 1e1ca443e (studio unchanged on this branch). Team sources: `domain/requirements-2026-11-02/` (SA-1B-253/-254/-255/-257, AG-7-56). `ai-workers/tools/freshness.sh` cannot run here (no `code/` in the cloud copy): team-source freshness **Unverified**.

## Verdict
**Build after these changes.** The job, the preview-before-move, all-or-nothing refusal and the insert-only pin chain are right. One rule (drafts filled again at the move) breaks the team's spec and the move's atomicity. Several back-end readers and the four "effect" groups are wrong for the checkpoint's own release 2, and the pack would pass with nothing to test.

## Objections

### Blocker
**B1 · Drafts "fill again" at the move (BR-4, BR-6, Q2): this goes against SA-1B-254 rule 6 and P-65, and the move stops being all or nothing.** *Needed for the proof.*
* What breaks: SA-1B-254 rule 6 (SA-01B l.4554): "A move never rewrites a form by itself. Forms whose map or edition changed are listed; the officer presses Autofill as usual." P-65 (d) (`decisions/P-65-independent-review.md:22`) says "a changed fact **marks** dependent drafts stale". It does not say they re-fill. So the concept's claim that P-65 backs auto re-fill is a misreading. Mechanically, re-fill is asynchronous (`fill-case.ts:263-299` in-memory queue). The jobs run after the move commits, so a job that FAILS leaves the case moved with its drafts half-made. That breaks the "all or nothing" promised in §4b. The cap of 5 waiting copies per case (`fill-case.ts:49`, `:241`) means a family of four with 2+ changed forms each gets a 429 in the middle of the move. On the checkpoint, the changed IMM 5257 is Online in release 2, and `generate` refuses Online forms (`fill-case.ts:230`). A replaced family form has no draft to fill "again": the board lists the *new* form under "Fills again".
* Fix: at the move, mark drafts of changed forms "Made on release 1: fill again" (derived, see M4). The officer presses Fill, which already fills on the case's newest pin. The preview lists them under **To fill again** ("press Fill"). The toast says "3 forms to fill again", not "fill again". No job is started inside the move. Because Q2 departs from SA-01B, it is the founder's question (P-251, P-316), not the tech lead's.

### Major
**M1 · The pin chain breaks every case read unless `reach()` and the direct readers change. The concept does not plan this.** *Needed for the proof.*
* `runtime.ts:72` refuses any case with `pins.length !== 1`, so after the first move every case route answers 409 "pin does not match". `runtime.ts:68` includes pins with no `orderBy`, so `pins[0]` (read at `runtime.ts:76,140`, `case-prepare.ts:78`, `case-filings.ts:20`, `case-checklist.ts:59`, `assessments.ts:64,172,176`, `fill-case.ts:248`) is whichever row Postgres returns first. `where-used.ts:240-242` builds `Map(caseId → release)` from unordered pins, so the last row wins and a moved case can be counted on release 1.
* Fix: `reach()` includes pins `orderBy: { revision: "desc" }`, checks the newest pin (hash and combination) and every older revision's hash, and leaves `pins[0]` as "current". `where-used` keeps the highest revision per case. `fill-case.ts:275-281` stays as it is (it finds the copy's own release among all pins; correct). Test: a case with revisions 1 and 2 inserted in reverse order reads release 2, and where-used counts it once, on 2.

**M2 · Move and Fill race.** *Needed for the proof.* `generate` reads the pin through `officerCase` **before** taking the case lock (`fill-case.ts:221` vs `:240`). It then writes `configReleaseId: c.pins[0]` (`:248`). A move that commits in between leaves a new copy on release 1 after the move, unlisted and unmarked. Fix: the move takes the same `FOR UPDATE` on `runtime_case`. Under that lock it refuses on QUEUED/RUNNING copies (BR-7) and on a stale `from_revision`. `generate` re-reads the newest revision under its lock and answers 409 if it changed. Test: hold the move's transaction open, fire a Fill, and expect 409 or a copy on release 2, never a copy on release 1.

**M3 · The four effect groups do not fit the checkpoint's own release 2. They would lead an RCIC to file the wrong thing.** *Needed for the proof.*
* Release 2 (`checkpoint/130-release-two.ts:1-3, 27-33, 61`) moves IMM 5257 to **Online**, replaces the family information form, and makes IMM 5476 hand-signed and scanned. The board puts a validated PDF of a form now filed Online under "Stays as it is, marked out of date". An RCIC reads that as "still valid, just re-check", but `case-prepare.ts:295` stops looking at validated copies on Online lines, and the PDF is now a copy not for filing (P-145). The replaced form's draft or signed copy fits no group: its item goes inactive (`case-prepare.ts:162-163`) and disappears from Prepare without a word.
* Fix: the effect list gets **No longer required** (a form or document line the target drops; its copies, scans and uploads kept, listed) and **Filed another way now** (the channel or signing changed: names the new way, e.g. "now typed into the IRCC account from the answer sheet; the validated PDF is not filed"). A new form appears only under *What changes*, never under "fill again".

**M4 · Out-of-date words would report a release change as fact changes, with an Undo.** *Needed for the proof.* `case-prepare.ts:235-239` compares a copy's stored facts with the **current** release's coverage. After a move, a form whose map changed lists facts as "changed from no answer to …" (`out-of-date.ts:28-45`), and Undo is offered wherever answer history exists (`case-prepare.ts:328-337`). An RCIC could undo a true answer. `paperValidated` (`:211`) has the same flaw. Fix (no schema: `FormInstance.configReleaseId` exists, schema l.1556): when a copy's release ≠ the newest pin's, it is out of date because of **the release**. The words name the release and what it changed, the copy shows no fact lines and no Undo, and it never counts as current. Add `configReleaseId` to the selects at `case-prepare.ts:204` and `:217`.

**M5 · "Approval withdrawn" cannot come from the review fingerprint, and withdrawing passes wholesale breaks SA-1B-254 rule 5.** *Needed for the proof.*
* The review counts while `factsHash` (form|subject|facts, `readiness-inputs.ts:70,99`) is equal. A channel or signing change (both in release 2) leaves it equal, so the representative's review would survive a change to how the filing is filed. Changing the hash formula instead would reopen every stored review at deploy. Passes are keyed by item id plus part (`readiness.ts:120`; `ReadinessPass.keys`, schema l.2292). They already carry on unchanged items and lapse on changed ones, so "C7.6 passes given before the move" listed as withdrawn (BR-4, board) contradicts rule 5 ("Items with the same stable ID keep their approvals, waivers", l.4553).
* Fix (no schema): a review counts only if its hash is equal **and** either it was made after the newest pin's `pinnedAt` or the difference between revisions n-1 and n touches nothing on that filing (pure `release-update.ts`, computed on read). Passes are left to their keys, and that line is removed from "Approval withdrawn". C6.9 is already derived (`eligibility.ts:106`, `assessments.ts:172`).

**M6 · The BR-8 Ready-to-file warning would force a pass on every filing of every unmoved case.** *Needed for the proof.* `readiness.ts:179`: a filing is ready only with no open warning. So "Release 2 is out" on every filing makes the RCIC pass it with a reason even when the release touches none of that filing's items, and "Later" is no longer free. SA-1B-253 rule 11 (l.4524): "Only filings that use the changed item show the warning." Fix: the warning appears only on filings the difference touches, and names what it touches (one line per filing, passable). With that, Q3's recommendation stands.

**M7 · The pack would pass with nothing to test.** *Needed for the proof.* Release 2 has no document-line change (test 9 has nothing to remove) and no field-type change (BR-7's type refusal has no test). Validated IMM 5257 copies are "kept on the Mac" in cloud and CI (`150-filled-copies.ts`, QA-285), so test 7 may find no finished item. Release 2 itself can stay "waiting" (`130-release-two.ts`, `r2Why`). "Forms picked by state at run time" then picks nothing. Fix: each test asserts its group is non-empty and **fails, never skips**, including when release 2 is missing. The pack makes its own finished items through product routes (a signed scan via C8.1, a review via C7.6). The document-line removal and the type mismatch (a field's type can change in a draft, `studio.ts:645`) are made by the pack on its own later release through the author's routes, labelled Sample. If that is not done, the PR states BR-6 (documents) and BR-7 (type) as not proved.

**M8 · Q1 (move per case) goes against founder decision P-316 (2), so the founder decides, not the tech lead.** *Not built (per filing).* P-316 (2) "each filing is pinned to its release (SH4)". P-246 (2) "The case pin moves to the filing when ADOPT is built". AG-7-56 rule 7 (AG-07 l.2196). The concept's reasons (one questionnaire per case; SH4's contract not agreed, `BACKLOG.md:59`) are sound for the proof, but under AGENTS.md rule 2 this conflict goes to the founder. Name the consequence too: once one group is recorded, the case's other filings can never move. Fix: Q1 below; the refusal copy says so ("Client A's filing is recorded as submitted; this case's other filings stay on release 1").

## What is sound
Stays until moved; the list is computed without writing; pins stay insert-only with the trigger kept and the revision unique restored (the shape of the original `20261023220000_runtime_r1:139`); one reason, pre-filled; 409 on a stale revision; refused with the reason named and nothing changed. Answers carry for free: they are stored per person and field, never per release. FilingItem ids persist across releases, so item-keyed passes and channel choices carry. `FormInstance.configReleaseId` and `fill-case.ts:281` let old copies and late jobs keep reading release 1. "Recorded" comes from `FilingRecord` (no schema). The fees line is honest. No AI. Best-in-class is drawn from primary sources. Copy names the action.

## Build discoveries
*Builder, 7 Nov (B145 build).*
* **D1 · The filing's facts fingerprint depended on the order the answers were read in.** `case-prepare.ts` (C7.6 `factsHash`, its "waiting" part) listed each waiting fact's answers in the order `Runtime.state` read them, with no `orderBy`. On a fresh copy the pack saw the spouse's review stop counting with no fact changed (2 of 4 runs; `reviewed: [false, true]` in the C9.5-8 detail), so a review could be withdrawn for nothing, and "approval withdrawn" would have been wrong. Fix (this PR): those answers are sorted before hashing. Consequence: a stored review whose waiting facts had more than one answer reads as changed once after deploy (the representative reviews it again). Runs after the fix: 28/28 (lane copy), 28/28 and 29/29 (fresh copies).
* **D2 · Release 2 of the checkpoint touches every filing and changes no field map**, so "to fill again" and "an untouched filing" cannot be shown on 1 → 2 (as M7 foresaw). The pack proves both on its own later Sample release (IMM 5476's form fields filled differently, the marriage certificate line removed, the organisation's name a date): the young child's filing is untouched there.
* **D3 · The validated IMM 5257 of the checkpoint family case is "kept on the Mac" (QA-285)**: its file is not served in cloud copies, so no validated copy can be made on that case by product routes. The finished item for C9.5-7 is a signed scan (C8.1) made by the pack; the release-1 copy's checksum is checked unchanged.
* **D4 · A hidden answer would have refused a move.** The type check first read every answer; an answer the new release no longer asks (kept, never read) blocked the move with no question to change it on. Now only answers the new release asks of that person refuse (BR-7 wording unchanged).
* **D5 · Release words:** the case header showed the combination's version ("Release 2") while every release word elsewhere is `releaseLabel` (G13: "Release 6" for the same row). The header now shows `release.label`, so the notice, panel, header and Prepare agree. Proposed, applied because the screen would contradict itself otherwise; the tech lead may revert to the version number.
