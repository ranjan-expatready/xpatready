## 1. Job and journey
**Job.** *"A newer release of this combination is out: what would it change for my client, and should I move now?"* The case officer sees that a newer release exists, reads what it would change for this case (forms, documents, questions, eligibility, per person) and what it would do to work already done, then moves the case in one click with a reason, or carries on and decides later.
* **Who:** case staff of the case's firm (officer, representative). Client 403; configuration people 403; another firm 404.
* **When:** any time while preparing, before any filing is recorded as submitted. Journey: J7 (Visitor visa), after C9.1 released a new version (AG-7-56 rule 2, BR-21: open cases keep their release until a person moves them).
* **Not:** corrections reaching open filings without a click (SA-1B-255; held, SA1B-D100, P-316 (1)); moving back; moving a submitted filing.

## 3. Business rules
* **BR-1 Stays until moved** (AG-7-56 rule 2, BR-21): a newer release never changes an open case; the case reads its newest pin revision; earlier revisions stay (history, and the copies made on them).
* **BR-2 Target**: the newest released row of the case's own combination with a higher version (forward only, SA-1B-254 rule 8); none → "This case is on the newest release". A demo release marks itself as everywhere (G3).
* **BR-3 What changes, computed without writing** (SA-1B-254 rule 2): the case's people re-resolved against the target: forms added, removed, on a new edition or channel, map changed; document lines added, removed or changed; questions added or removed; eligibility rules changed (C6.9). Each line names the people it touches and old against new. Fees are not in a release yet (`samples/fee-table.json`, R2): the panel says so, never shows a guessed difference.
* **BR-4 Effect on work done, shown before the move** (the beat, §4b; challenge M3): each existing piece sorted into **To fill again** (a draft copy of a form whose map or edition changed: the officer presses Fill, SA-1B-254 rule 6), **Filed another way now** (the form's channel or signing changed: names the new way, e.g. "now typed into the IRCC account from the answer sheet; the validated PDF is not filed"), **No longer required** (a form or document line the target drops: its copies, scans and uploads kept and listed, SA1B-D15), **Approval withdrawn** (the representative's review of each filing the difference touches; a C6.9 result), **Stays as it is, marked out of date** (a validated, signed, scanned or entered item of a changed form still filed the same way), **No change** (work on items the release does not touch keeps its approval and its passes, SA-1B-254 rule 5; passes stay keyed to their items, `readiness.ts:120`). A new form or line appears only under *What changes*.
* **BR-5 One click, one reason** (AG-7-56 rule 4): "Move this case to release 2" with a reason pre-filled "Adopting the current release" (editable, required, 3-500 characters); one new pin revision; audited (who, when, reason, from, to). The request names the revision and release it read; a different one now → 409 "Read the list again" (no lost update).
* **BR-6 After the move**: questions, checklist, filings and forms read the newest pin revision (`reach()` and `where-used` order by revision; challenge M1); new questions land in Needs you as questions, never as a refusal (SA-1B-254 rule 7 end); answers carry by field (stored per person and field, never per release); a removed question keeps its answer (hidden, not deleted); a removed document line reads "No longer required" with its uploads (new reading code: `ChecklistLine`, `CaseDocument` persist); a move never rewrites a form (SA-1B-254 rule 6): a copy made on an earlier release is **out of date because of the release**, its words name the release and what it changed, with no fact lines and no Undo (challenge M4), and the officer's Fill makes the new copy on the new release ("drafts re-render" in the row); signed, validated, entered or filed items never change (P-65). The representative's review counts only if its facts hash is equal **and** it was made after the newest pin, or the difference touches nothing on that filing (computed, no schema; challenge M5).
* **BR-7 Refused, nothing changed, with the reason named** (SA-1B-254 rule 7): an answer that no longer fits its field's type in the target (names the answer and the person); a filing recorded as submitted ("Client A's filing is recorded as submitted; this case's filings stay on release 1", Q1); a fill job queued or running. Checked under the case lock (`runtime_case FOR UPDATE`), which Fill also takes; Fill re-reads the newest revision under its lock and answers 409 if it changed, so no copy is made on the old release after a move (challenge M2). The case keeps working on its release.
* **BR-8 Later is normal** (Renovate's approval flow): closing the panel stores nothing; the notice stays under the header; Ready to file warns **only on filings the difference touches**, naming what it touches ("Release 2 files IMM aaaa Online; this filing is on release 1"), passable with a reason (warn, never block, P-237; SA-1B-253 rule 11; challenge M6). The edition and fee "Adopt update" lines (`readiness.ts:151-161`) stay as they are unless the target release carries that edition.
* **BR-9 Who**: case staff of the firm (`case` or `represent` duty); client 403, configuration 403, another firm 404, no pin match 409 (as `reach()`).

## 5. Copy
Notice ⓘ: "A release is a frozen set of the combination's forms, documents and questions. A case keeps its release until someone moves it, so nothing changes under your client." · Group tips: "To fill again: this draft was made on release 1 and its form changed. Press Fill to make it on release 2; the old copy stays in the history." · "Filed another way now: release 2 files this form differently; the copy you have is kept but is not what you file." · "Approval withdrawn: the representative reviews this filing again, because what it files changed." · "Stays as it is: signed, validated or filed items are never changed; they are marked out of date with the reason." · Refusal: "Can't move yet: Client A's answer to 'Date of birth' does not fit release 2 (it expects a date). Nothing was changed." · "Can't move: Client A's filing is recorded as submitted on release 1." · Fees line: "Fees are not part of a release yet; they come from the fee table."
