## 1. Job and journey
**Job:** an immigration firm finds our prices, signs up in one short form, proves its email, starts a free trial with no card, completes its profile, and later pays, changes plan or cancels on Stripe's own pages; if it stops paying it can still pay and take its data out. **Personas:** the firm's Owner (signs up, profile, plan, cancel), Admin (card and overdue bill, SA-4-67), other staff (see the hold page only), our sales staff (send an invited link; their console is Not built here). **Journey (contracts §7 l.80):** Pricing (S1) → Sign up (S2) → Check your email (S3) → Confirm (S3b) → Stripe Checkout, nothing to pay (hand-off S4) → back: "Sign in to continue" → identity provider's sign-in and two-step enrolment (P-567; not drawn) → Starting your trial (S4) → Firm profile (S5) → Plan and billing (S6) → Overdue banner / Payment hold (S7). Side door: invited link → Invited sign-up (S8) → same from S4. **Cards:** SA-2-9, -22, -24, -25; SA-4-1, -5, -8, -20, -21, -25, -26, -28, -30, -31, -52, -54, -67, -79; AG-1-65.

## 3. Business rules (each a pass and a fail test)
* **BR-1** Pricing shows only active plans from `GET /api/public/plans`: name, seats, one monthly and one yearly CAD price (switch Monthly / Yearly), trial length, "No card needed"; each card's button "Start free trial" opens Sign up with that plan and period carried (SA-4-1; contracts l.76 "no card at trial start"; §7 G1 on the pricing table).
* **BR-2** Sign up asks: work email, password (the provider's rules shown as you type, §7 G2), firm name; country is shown fixed "Canada" with "can't be changed later" (SA-2-9; PF-3 immutable `country_code`); the carried plan shows with "Change"; no web address, no address, no card. Hidden honeypot, minimum fill time and rate limit: a trapped or too-fast submit gets the normal answer and nothing is stored (PF-5; contracts l.80).
* **BR-3** One tick "I agree to the Terms of Service, Privacy Notice and Data Processing Terms", each a link naming its version; unticked → "Please accept the terms to continue." on the field; on submit three acceptance rows: kind, version, time, who (PF-5 rev 8). Documents Sample, Unconfirmed: no counsel.
* **BR-4** Every accepted submit shows **Check your email** naming the address; known or unknown email, the screen is the same (PF-5); a second submit while a link is live sends no second email but the screen is identical; "Send it again" is rate limited (Sample: once a minute) (PF-5 test "one pending sign-up and one email").
* **BR-5** The email (Sample text, from us via `MailPort`) has a **Confirm my email** button and the 6-digit code; the button opens S3b which confirms by itself; the code can be typed on S3 (one input, `autocomplete="one-time-code"`); single use, 24 hours (contracts l.80); used or expired → "This link has expired or was already used." with "Send a new link" and "Already confirmed? Sign in" (PF-5: 404, one answer). No firm exists before confirmation (PF-5).
* **BR-6** Confirmation creates the firm (Setting up, `PROVISIONING`) and its Owner, then hands off to Stripe Checkout with the plan's trial and no card (PF-5). Our return page shows no firm data: "Your trial is being set up. Sign in to continue." The first sign-in enrols two-step on the provider's pages (P-567). Until Stripe's event lands the app shows **Starting your trial…** (our record, never the redirect: contracts §0.4); after 60 s (Sample) "This is taking longer than usual. Your sign-up is saved; refresh in a minute." Leaving Checkout or coming back later shows **One step left: start your free trial** (`POST /api/platform/billing/checkout`); signing up again with the same email resumes, never a second firm (SA-2-24; PF-5 "one firm and one Owner").
* **BR-7** Firm profile fields (contracts l.78): operating name, legal name, country (read-only), business address (street, city, province, postal code), owner contact (name, email, phone), billing email (optional). The contact email belongs to one firm: "This email is already used by another firm account." (SA-2-24). The address is marked "Needed before your first invoice: we use it to work out tax." and is never asked again by Checkout (§7 G8; SA-4-52). Legal fields (legal name, business address, billing email, owner contact) are Owner-only; an Admin edits the operating name; others read with "Only the Owner can change this." (AG-1-65; register §A "Firm profile"; SA-4-67; §7 G9).
* **BR-8** Plan and billing shows our record: plan, status in the requirement's words (Trial, Active, Payment overdue, Payment hold, Ended; contracts l.76), trial end or next billing date, "6 of 10 seats used" (PF-4b's seat line; active + suspended + pending invitations, contracts l.80), "Cancels on <date>" when set. Buttons (SA-4-28, -31, -67; PF-5): Owner **Manage plan and billing** (Stripe portal: change plan, cancel or undo, invoices, card); Admin **Update card or pay** (portal limited to card and paying). One primary.
* **BR-9** Returning from Stripe (portal or Checkout) shows "Checking for changes from Stripe…" until the record moves, then the new state; a status is never set from the return link (contracts §0.4; PF-5 "handlers converge out of order").
* **BR-10** Trial line on S6 and the app home for Owner/Admin: "Free trial: 9 days left (ends 28 Nov 2026). Add a card before then to keep access." (from the stored trial end; SA-4-30, -79). An app-wide banner and reminder emails wait on §7 G6.
* **BR-11** Payment overdue: Owner and Admin see one orange "needs you" banner on every staff page: "Payment didn't go through. Update your card by 30 Nov to keep access." Full access for 14 days (SA-4-20, -21; PF-5 day-0/7/12 notices).
* **BR-12** Payment hold: any staff page other than Plan and billing and the data download shows **Your firm's account is on hold**, with the reason (trial ended without a card · payment not received · subscription ended), what still works, and the deletion date when the API gives one; Owner/Admin primary **Add a card and pay**; others "Ask your firm's Owner or Admin to pay." Ends only on a paid invoice: "Checking payment…" then normal (SA-4-25, -26, -79; contracts l.77; PF-5 402 in the guard).
* **BR-13** Invited sign-up: the link opens a page with the email fixed (from the link), firm name, password and terms; confirming the email is not asked again (the link was emailed there); then BR-6 from Checkout on. Used, expired or unknown → one page "This sign-up link doesn't work. It may have been used or expired. Ask us for a new one." (PF-5: 404; SA-2-22 14 days).
* **BR-14** Buttons follow role and state (table §6); nothing for a module not built; Stripe-hosted actions open in the same tab with a return.

## 4b. Best in class (partly verified, 14 Nov 2026)
* **Stripe** (docs.stripe.com, read 14 Nov): the pricing table "takes customers directly to checkout. It doesn't currently support adding an intermediate step (for example, asking the customer to create an account…)" and allows a custom call-to-action on only one product (`/payments/checkout/pricing-table`, Limitations); Checkout trials without a card use `payment_method_collection=if_required` and `trial_settings.end_behavior.missing_payment_method` = cancel or pause, and a paused subscription resumes when a card is added in the portal (`/payments/checkout/free-trials`).
* **Clio** (clio.com/en-CA/pricing, page title "7-day free trial, no credit card", seen in search 14 Nov; page itself refused our reader): **sign-up fields Unverified**. **Docketwise** (docketwise.com/pricing, read 14 Nov): per-user monthly prices, a Monthly/Yearly switch, "Try for Free".
* We match: no card to start, short form, plan picked before sign-up. We go further: the address is asked once (BR-7), status always from our record, never the redirect (BR-9), the hold page still lets a firm pay and take its data (BR-12), the same answer for known emails (BR-4).

## 7. Questions and gaps (decided by Lead B, 14 Nov; folded into PF-5 rev 8.2)
