1 · The words: one set, each with one meaning

Every button, message, To review card and History line uses these words and no others. A greyed action always shows why in words beside it.

Delete

Ends an item that was never released and is used nowhere. A second person approves; it is gone then. The History line stays.

P-52 L1 · P-128 (3)

Remove from …

Takes a line out of one combination, section or questionnaire. The item stays in the library. Undo while the draft is open.

P-123 · P-116

Retire

Hides a released item from pickers, AI and new setups. Releases and open cases keep it. Reversible.

P-52 L2

Restore

Brings a retired item back to pickers and AI, by the same path: a second person approves.

P-52 L4

Withdraw

The author takes back their own waiting change before it is decided. Nothing changes.

built

Replace edition

A newer IRCC edition takes over through impact review. The earlier one is kept for releases and cases.

P-32 · P-123

Archive

Same job as Retire: one word only.

Deactivate · Disable

Same job as Retire.

Trash · Discard

Suggests a bin we do not have.

Remove (alone)

Today it means Delete on Program structure and To review.

C1 code vs P-128

"(L1)" "(L3)" "(L6)"

Rule codes in today's messages: plain words instead.

C2 code vs PRODUCT §4

Undo

On the toast, 10 s, after any reversible step. On a waiting change it is a Withdraw with no reason asked.

2 · The rule table: state × action

The state is calculated from where it is used, never set by hand. "Used" means a section placement, field map, condition, check, document line, form line, structure child or combination (P-52). "Current" means a draft or the latest release; earlier releases and open cases never stop an action, they are listed as "kept".

7 · Founder questions (recommendation first) and the Lead's calls

Q1 Delete your own never-approved draft item at once, with Undo?

Recommended: yes. Nothing reached a second person or firms; the History line stays (Linear, Contentful drafts). Anything approved or AI-made and accepted still needs a second person.

Or: keep a second person for every delete (P-52 L4, today).

Q2 When does a retire take effect?

Recommended: at approval for library items; with the release for a combination or a structure name firms see. The guard already proves no current setup uses it, and "next release" for an unused field confuses people (QA-52).

Or: every retire and restore waits for a release (today).

Q3 "Replace with…" for fields, sections and document types?

Recommended: keep it Not built for the proof. The block lists each use with a link; form editions already have Replace edition.

Or: build it now, picking a substitute that every draft use moves to in one step (Salesforce, Jira).
