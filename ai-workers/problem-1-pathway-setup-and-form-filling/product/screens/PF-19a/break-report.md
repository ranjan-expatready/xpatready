# PF-19a break report: messages schema and consent store (PR #1754, head `deb03f13f`)

Breaker pass, 14 Nov. Scope: the database rules in `20261114190000_pf19a_platform_messages/migration.sql` and the `ConsentPort` adapter and fake (`ports/consent.ts`, `consent-store.ts`, `messages-firm.ts`). There is no screen and no route, so the screen checks (keyboard, 1024 px, zoom, reload) do not apply.
Set-up: scratch databases `xr_studio_pf19a_break_1` and `_2` on :54329, with every migration from `deb03f13f` applied in order. They held two `platform_firm` rows and rows of every msg_ table. Each SQL attack ran in its own `BEGIN … ROLLBACK`. Adapter attacks ran through `ConsentStore(auditEvent, FakeClock)` over `@prisma/adapter-pg`, plus `fakeConsent`. Both databases have been dropped. Nothing touched :4400 or `xr_studio_demo`.
**Note:** while this pass ran, uncommitted edits appeared in this working tree: a new `msg_consent_withdrawal` table, a `jurisdiction` column, and changes to `ports/consent.ts` and `migration.sql`. Every result below is for `deb03f13f`. The new head needs its own break pass.

## What held
* **Cross-firm, all refused by the database:** firm A's consent naming firm B's wording (also with upper-case uuids), naming a wording of the wrong channel or purpose, or copying B's consent; A's outbox naming B's template version or consent; A's event on B's outbox; a template version whose firm differs from its template's (A on B's template, A on a platform template, NULL on A's template); moving an outbox or template to another firm or to `PLATFORM`; a template's channel. `ConsentRefused` from the port for a wording of another firm. `allows` for firm B never sees firm A's consent. Upper-case firm or subject ids give the same answers as lower case.
* **Write-once, all refused:** wording, template-version and event rows against `UPDATE … SET col=col`, `DELETE`, `ON CONFLICT DO UPDATE`, `MERGE … UPDATE/DELETE` and `TRUNCATE`, including `TRUNCATE … CASCADE` reached from `msg_firm`, `msg_outbox`, `msg_consent` and `platform_firm`. Consent `DELETE`, a primary-key change, `ON CONFLICT DO UPDATE` and any other column changed alongside the withdrawal. A withdrawal cleared, set again, set `col=col`, set before `given_at` or set without by/via. `platform_firm` delete gives RESTRICT.
* **Checks, all refused:** SERVICE on EMAIL; a copy naming itself; a wrong text hash; duplicate platform or firm template keys and versions; a template key in upper case or with a space; a negative send counter.
* **Gate:** a MARKETING send with only the WhatsApp opt-in gives `NO_CONSENT` on both WhatsApp and email; a WhatsApp SERVICE send with the opt-in gives `CONSENT`; a suppression stops TRANSACTIONAL.
* **Concurrency and audit:** 20 concurrent `publishWording` calls (mixed-case firm id) gave versions 1-20 with no gaps and no errors. 10 concurrent `suppress` calls on a firm's first write gave one `created:true` and nine `created:false`. Two concurrent `withdraw` calls gave 1 and 0. A forced audit failure inside a transaction left 0 rows. A scan of all 27 msg_ audit lines found no address, number or wording text in `summary`/`detail`.
* **Migration:** `prisma migrate diff` from a fresh database to the `deb03f13f` schema shows no msg_ difference.

## Not findings (outside the row's "What gets built")
`msg_suppression` has no write-once trigger: it can be updated or deleted, so a suppression could be lifted by SQL, and the row does not ask for a trigger. The requirements make hard bounces and complaints platform-wide (SA11-D5, SA05-Q10), but the table is per firm only; that is for the release holder or PF-19b. A NULL `must_keep` is accepted by SQL.
