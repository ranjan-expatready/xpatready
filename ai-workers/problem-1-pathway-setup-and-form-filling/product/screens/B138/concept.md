## 1. Job and journey
**Job.** *"My partner should answer their own questions, and what they tell the firm is theirs."* The principal (or the case officer) sends an adult on the case their own link; the adult proves the email is theirs with a code, reads who sees what, answers and confirms their own sections; from then on the principal sees how far that adult has got, never their answers.
* **Who:** the principal on their family link (the case's client, AG07-D22 Q43); case staff of the firm; the adult on their own link. A minor gets no link and no privacy (AG07-D23).
* **When:** while the client questionnaire is open (C6.2), before filing.
* **Today (main):** no per-adult link; the principal sees and edits every family member's client sections (`engine/runtime/case.ts:310`, `runtime.ts:344`); the `by_principal` words already promise "confirms on their own link" (`contracts/runtime.ts:102-107`).

## 3. Business rules
* **BR-1 Adults only** (AG07-D23): a person whose recorded date of birth makes them 18 or over today (`ageOn`; the age is **Sample; Unconfirmed: no counsel**: majority is 19 in some provinces), role spouse or partner, child or other; never the principal or the case subject. No date of birth → no Send, the screen says why. A minor: no Send, 409 in words.
* **BR-2 Privacy is stored once per adult** (the team's "Keep private from the principal", AG-7-107, Q71): it switches **on when the adult first enters the right code** (so a mistyped address never locks the principal out), or when staff tick it with a reason; it stays on after take back; switching it off is staff's untick (SA-01B l.5862), **not built in M3** (Q1). Every filter reads the stored setting, never age: a later date-of-birth edit changes nothing already private.
* **BR-3 Who sends where** (challenge B1): the principal may send or resend only while the adult has not accepted, or after acceptance only to the **kept** address (403 in words otherwise); a new address after acceptance is staff only; an address already held by another person on the case is refused (409). The officer's chip says what the code proves: "Answered on the link sent to p•••@example.com (address given by Client A)".
* **BR-4 Link and code** (OWASP, GOV.UK, the removed pairing): the link carries a 32-byte random token in the URL **fragment** (`/link#t=…`), exchanged by POST, cleared with `history.replaceState`, `Referrer-Policy: no-referrer`; opening the link sends nothing: the adult presses **Send me a code**; a 6-digit code to the address, stored hashed, valid 15 minutes, typed with spaces or dashes; 5 wrong codes void the link; tries limited per address and per server. A link lives 14 days (product choice, not an immigration value) and dies when taken back, superseded by a resend or re-addressed.
* **BR-5 The adult's session**: 8 hours, scoped to **that case and that person**, on `/api/link/*` only: their own person parts to answer; shared relationship and case answers read-only (SA-1B-269 rule 2); everything else refused. Coming back: the link again, a new code.
* **BR-6 Consent, then answers** (consent wording **Unconfirmed: no counsel**): before the first question the adult reads "Your answers go to the firm handling this application. The person who started it will see how far you have got, not your answers. The firm can change this." Answers saved on the link are the person's own (`own_link`, P-78); answers the principal gave earlier show "answered by Client A" with **Confirm** (the same value saved again on the link, with the answer's id: a new own answer) or Change.
* **BR-7 What the principal sees of a private adult** (AG-15-56): progress only, counted on the server ("4 of 7 parts done": a part is done when nothing required is missing or invalid; optional parts say so), no values, no follow-up questions, no documents; every write on that person 403 (answers, none-apply, rows, uploads). Staff always see and edit everything (SA-1B-269 rule 10).
* **BR-8 Take back** (principal or staff; staff give a reason): the link and its sessions die at once; privacy stays on; the principal keeps progress only and no edits (challenge M4, SA-01B l.5862); the ways forward are Resend (kept address) or staff.
* **BR-9 History**: send, resend, code sent, wrong code, lockout, consent, take back, tick, each with who and when, the masked address only; never the token or the code.
* **BR-10 Who**: the principal's actor for their own case; staff of the firm; configuration people 403; another firm 404; a link session only within its case and person; a dead real link says why without naming a person ("This link was taken back on 10 Nov. Ask whoever sent it for a new one."); an unknown link names no case or person.
