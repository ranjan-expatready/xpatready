## 1. Job and journey
**Job.** *"My partner should answer their own questions, and what they tell the firm is theirs."* The principal (or the case officer) sends an adult on the case their own link; the adult proves the email is theirs with a code, answers and confirms their own sections; from then on the principal sees how far that adult has got, never their answers; the link can be taken back or sent again.
* **Who:** the principal on their family link (the case's client, AG07-D22 Q43); case staff of the firm; the adult on their own link. A minor gets no link and no privacy (AG07-D23: both parents see a minor's answers).
* **When:** while the client questionnaire is open (C6.2), before filing.
* **Today (main):** no per-adult link; the principal sees and edits every family member's client sections (`engine/runtime/case.ts:310`, `runtime.ts:344`); the words for `by_principal` already promise "confirms on their own link" (`contracts/runtime.ts:102-107`), which does not exist.

## 3. Business rules
* **BR-1 Adults only** (AG07-D23): a person 18 or over on the case's date today (`ageOn`, `MINOR.underAge`), role spouse or partner, child, or other; never the principal, never the "case" subject. A minor: no Send, refused 409 in words; a child turning 18 becomes eligible on the day.
* **BR-2 Who sends**: the principal on their family link, or case staff of the firm (Q3). The email is typed once and kept on the person (`contactChannelsJson.email`), checked as an address.
* **BR-3 Link and code** (OWASP, GOV.UK): the link carries a 32-byte random token, stored hashed; opening it shows the masked address and sends a 6-digit code to it, stored hashed, valid 15 minutes, typed with spaces or dashes allowed; 5 wrong codes void the link (the removed pairing's lockout, `d57515064^:api/src/extension.ts:92-96`); tries are limited per address and per server. A link lives 14 days (product choice, not an immigration value), dies when taken back, superseded by a resend, or the email changes; a dead link says why ("This link was replaced by a newer one sent on 9 Nov").
* **BR-4 The adult's session**: a right code gives an 8-hour session scoped to **that case and that person**: their own person sections to answer, their shared relationship and case answers read-only (SA-1B-269 rule 2); every other person, every staff route and every other case refused (404 for another case, 403 for staff routes). Coming back later: the link again, a new code.
* **BR-5 Consent, then answers** (consent wording **Unconfirmed: no counsel**): before the first question the adult reads who sees what ("Your answers go to the firm handling this application. Client A will see how far you have got, not your answers.") and continues; answers saved on their own link count as their own (`confirmation()` → own, P-78); answers the principal gave for them earlier show "answered by Client A" with **Confirm** or change (P-78).
* **BR-6 Private from the principal** (AG-15-56, SA-1B-269 rule 2): from the adult's first code check, the principal's family link shows that adult's tab as **progress only** ("Partner is answering on their own link · 4 of 7 parts done"), no values, no edits (the API refuses a principal's write on that person, 403); the same filter applies to every client route that returns a person's facts or uploads. Staff always see and edit everything (SA-1B-269 rule 10). Privacy is one way in M3 (Q1).
* **BR-7 Take back** (principal or staff, reason for staff): the link and its sessions die at once; the principal answers that adult's **unanswered** questions again; answers the adult gave stay private (shown "Answered by Partner on their own link") and cannot be overwritten by the principal; staff can. **Resend**: a new link and code to the kept or a corrected address; the old link is superseded.
* **BR-8 History**: send, resend, open, code sent, wrong code, lockout, consent, take back, each with who and when (`Audit.log`); never the token or the code.
* **BR-9 Who**: the principal's actor for their own case only; staff of the firm; configuration people 403; another firm 404; a link session only within its case and person; an unknown token gives a plain page that names no case or person; a real but dead token says why (BR-3), naming nothing beyond the masked address it was sent to.

## 4. Layout
**Principal, family link (mobile first, 390 px):** each adult's tab head gets "Send to Partner…" (secondary) → a dialog (≤3 steps): their email · what happens ("Partner gets a link and a code by email. Once they open it, you will see how far they have got, not their answers.") · **Send to Partner**. After: tab shows "Sent to p•••@example.com on 9 Nov · Resend · Take back"; after the adult's code check, the tab becomes the progress card. **Adult, own link:** code page ("We sent a 6-digit code to p•••@example.com" · Resend code) → consent card → their sections one group at a time, as C6.2. **Officer, people list (1440 px):** per adult a link chip in words: *Answers by Client A* · *Link sent 9 Nov* · *With Partner since 9 Nov (private from Client A)* · *Taken back 10 Nov*; actions Send · Resend · Take back (reason). States: empty (no adult: no Send), loading, sent not opened, wrong code (attempts left), locked ("Too many wrong codes: ask Client A or the firm to send a new link"), expired or superseded (why), session ended, refused minor (no button; API 409), read-only staff without the duty.

## 4b. Best in class

| Who | Best at | Source |
|---|---|---|

| OWASP Forgot Password cheat sheet | random, long, stored hashed, single use, expiring, rate limited | `OWASP/CheatSheetSeries` |
| HMRC agent-client relationships (open source) | access starts only when the person accepts; checked on every read at the narrowest level | `hmrc/agent-client-relationships` README |
| The team's spec | one privacy per adult, none for a minor; principal sees progress only | AG-15-56, AG-07 C3.5, SA-1B-269 |
**Copied:** the token and code rules, the dead-link explanation, acceptance by the person. **Beaten:** the principal is told what they will and will not see before sending, and sees progress instead of a blank; the adult sees who can see what before answering; take back keeps the adult's answers private and theirs; every step is in the history. **Effort:** one dialog for the principal, a code and one consent card for the adult; nothing asked twice (answers already given by the principal are confirmed, not retyped).
