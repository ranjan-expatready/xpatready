## Verdict
**No Blocker. 2 Major, 4 Minor.** The main path is correct. The problems are at the edges: after the staff link has died (taken back, locked or expired), Client A's Resend sends a fresh link to B, and the history then names Client A as the one who gave B. There are also races with the code step.

## Major
**M1. After staff take back the link at B, or B locks after 5 wrong codes, Client A's one-press Resend sends a new live link to B.**
* Repro (take back): re-addressed. As `user-O`, `DELETE …/people/:S/link {"reason":"Wrong inbox, belongs to someone else"}` → 200. Client A's line now has `can.resend_kept:true` and `resend:{to_masked:"p•••", same_link:false}`. As `user-P`, `POST …/people/:S/link {}` → 201 `created:true`. B's inbox then has one link email, and `POST /api/link/open` with its token gives `state:"live"`. In `person_link`, the new row has `address=B, address_given_by=user-P, sent_by=user-P`.
* Repro (lock): re-addressed. On B's token, send a code, then enter 5 wrong codes → 410 locked. Client A's Resend → 201 `created:true`, a new live link at B.
* Expected (P-412 / F12 (b)): only staff decide where an accepted adult's link goes. Taking back or locking B is the firm's (or the lock's) signal that B should not hold a link. Client A's Resend should not bring B back. It also must not go to A (it does not: Client A sending to A after the take back → 403). With no working link, nothing should be sent without staff.
* Effect: whoever holds B (for example the wrong inbox the officer just withdrew) can enter a code and answer Partner's own private questions, after one press by Client A. The screen invites that press: the Resend button stays on Client A's line (`PL:108` `resend_kept`).
* Cause: `PL:125` reads the newest link's address, voided or not. `PV:62` allows `current` (the newest address) even when that link was taken back or locked. Before this commit, the same press went to A.

**M2. That new link is recorded as "address given by Client A", so the history and the officer's line say Client A chose the address staff set.**
* Repro: as in M1, or simply let the staff link expire (`expires_at` set in the past on the copy), then Client A's Resend → 201 `created:true`. The history line reads "Client A sent a link … to p•••@example.com (address given by Client A)" (take back, lock) or "Client A sent a new link … (address given by Client A)" (expired). The officer panel shows "Link sent 9 Nov to p•••@example.com (address given by Client A) · private from Client A", and `link.given_by` is "Client A".
* Expected: P-441 says the address is the one staff set. The record of who decided who receives an access link should name the officer. Staff cannot tell this send apart from Client A choosing an address, which Client A may not do.
* Cause: the new-link branch always writes `addressGivenBy: actor.id` (`PL:163`) and the summary "(address given by ${actor.name})" (`PL:167`).

## Minor
* **m1. A Resend at the moment Partner enters the right code at B leaves the link email in B's inbox after acceptance (break M4 comes back under a race).** Repro: re-addressed; send a code at B; fire `POST /api/link/code` (right code) and Client A's Resend together, 0-10 ms apart. In 6 of 11 trials the accepted B link still had 1 `demo_message` of kind link. The token in it equals the staff's token, `open` gives `live, accepted:true`, and `POST /api/link/code/send` with it → 200. Cause: the same-link branch (`PL:152-157`) reads `open` without locking the link row, and the right-code transaction (`PL:330`) does not take the participant lock, so the copy is inserted after the delete. One related refusal was seen: in 2 of 11 trials Client A got a 409 instead.
* **m2. Same race with the 5th wrong code: a dead token is emailed, and Client A is told it still works.** Repro: as m1, but with 4 wrong codes first and the 5th fired together with Resend. In 3 of 4 trials: Resend → 201 `created:false` ("…again. It still works."), the link is `locked`, and the copied email stays in B's inbox. Cause: the wrong-code update (`PL:304`) runs outside the participant lock that `send` takes.
* **m3. The same-link path has no idempotency and no cap: each press adds one more identical email.** Five presses gave 8 messages and 1 distinct token. The same `idempotencyKey` sent twice → 201/201, 2 more emails. 20 concurrent presses → 30 messages in B's inbox, 29 "resent" history lines. Before this commit a Resend replaced the previous email (the link died and its messages went). The `idempotencyKey` is ignored on this path because no `person_link` row carries the key (`PL:130` `replay` vs `PL:152-161`). The address belongs to the person staff chose, so this is a nuisance, not a leak.
* **m4. The toast is computed when the page renders, not from the server's answer.** Repro (keyboard only, 1024 px): Client A opens `/cases/:id/questionnaire` while B is live (the text says "Resend sends it there again"). Then the staff link expires, or the officer takes it back. Client A Tabs to Resend and presses Enter. The toast says "The firm's link was sent to Sample Partner at p•••@example.com again. It still works." In fact a new link was created (`address_given_by=user-P`, the old one voided). Cause: `WP:106` passes `success: resendOf(person).done`, a fixed string. `useAction` accepts `(r) => string`, so the toast could follow the response's `created`. Screenshots (scratchpad, not committed): `/tmp/claude-0/-home-user-xpatready/b4429ca6-0658-5424-8650-4d4fa19f4a5f/scratchpad/br/shots/client-toast-after-staffTakeBack-1024.png`, `…/shots/client-toast-after-expire-1024.png`.

Observation (not counted; the build may intend it): if the officer re-addresses back to A, the open staff link is at the kept address, so Client A's Resend takes the old path. It kills the staff's link at A and sends a new token to A (`resendsSameLink` is false when `open.address === kept`, `PV:69`). The recipient stays the same. Whether P-441's "does not end the staff's link" covers this case is for the founder.

## What held
* **The main P-441 path:**
  * Client A's Resend after the re-address sends the same token to B, nothing to A, and B stays `live`. `person_link` gets no new row. The history reads "sent the same link again … (address given by Case officer); it still works".
  * Client A typing A, A in upper case, A padded with spaces, or a third address C → 403 "The firm sent Sample Partner's link to p•••@example.com. Only the firm can change the address." Nothing reaches A or C.
  * Typing B in upper case or with spaces is normalised and sends the same link (201 `created:false`). A non-ASCII staff address (`PÄRTNER…`) typed in lower case by Client A matched. An empty address → 400 in words.
* **After B accepts:** `kept` becomes B. Client A's Resend sends a new link to B (old path) and A gets nothing. Client A typing A → 403 "already uses …".
* **Officer moves the link on (B, then C):** Client A typing B → 403 naming C. Resend sends the same link to C. B stays dead.
* **Staff paths unchanged:** staff Resend still makes a new link and kills the old one. Staff `resend.same_link` is always false, and the staff tip says the earlier link stops working.
* **Races with staff:** Client A's Resend at the same moment as a staff send to C gave 403 for Client A, 201 for staff, nothing at B and a live link at C (4 of 4). Resend together with a staff take back runs one after the other, since both take the participant lock (it then ends in M1 or a clean take back).
* **Roles:** Client B and the solo firm → 404. `user-A` and `user-C` → 403. No actor, or a wrong-case actor id (`user-p`), → 401; the persona alias `client` acts as Client A (201).
* **No address leak:** neither the client's nor the officer's privacy list contains a full address. `resend.to_masked` is masked, and so are the 403 words and the history lines.
* **Layout:** the "where" line shows at 1024 px and at 640 px (200 % of 1280) with no sideways scroll. Resend can be reached and pressed by keyboard.
