## Verdict: Ship after fixes (merge blocked until the Must and the Majors are fixed and rechecked)
The model is right and most of it works through the API: `check_forms_channels.ts` **66/66** rerun on the exact commit; a blank capture becomes a held online version, a second person approves it, 9 of 13 inputs map from the IMM 5257 PDF twin with no AI, channels and portal derive the filing method, the validator blocks review and release, option maps fill the PDF (marital status "married" → `01`; map without it → "by hand"), merges redirect and leave releases alone. The migration keeps today's data exactly. What fails are the edges a real author reaches quickly: dead ends with no API way out, lost updates, and unsafe merge and alias behaviour.

## Must
1. **An answer list on a field read by a PDF form field with no `options_resolved` blocks release with no way out.** `formOptions` reads only the inventory's `options_resolved` (`api/src/forms.ts:405-411`). IMM 5707's marital status fields keep their codes only in the `code_list:` transform text. So after `POST /library/fields/person.marital_status/answer-list`, `option-maps/draft` on IMM 5707 returns `[]`, and `PATCH …/option-maps` refuses the form's real codes ("\"5\" is not one of this form field's codes", `:394-398`). The validator then raises 5 errors, "no code for …" / "it has no option map yet" (`engine/channels.ts:58-62`). No endpoint unlinks a list (`:346-358`; `updateField` has no `answerListKey`). Result: `vv.single` and `wp.family` can never be released again. A person who sends `map: {}` also turns a fill that worked (`code_list`) into "by hand" (`api/src/fill.ts:55`). Fix: take the PDF codes from `code_list:` the way `p5` `codeFor` does, and add unlink.

## Major
2. **Leftovers cannot be resolved through the product, so an Online line never reaches release.** `acceptScreenMap` runs once (`forms.ts:250-251`; a second accept and a redraft both return 409). No other endpoint writes an online map row, and submit is refused ("4 online inputs are neither mapped…"). The suite reaches "validator pass" only by SQL (`tests/check_forms_channels.ts:172`). P-33 (scaffolding) and STANDARDS §4 "works by hand with AI off".
3. **A passed re-check date can never be cleared.** An identical re-capture ends `IN_LIBRARY` ("its re-check date can be confirmed", `forms.ts:126-129`) but `recheck_by` stays `2026-01-01`. No endpoint confirms it, so `channels.ts:52` blocks release until the portal itself changes. Proven on :4451.
4. **Concurrent screen adds are lost while the person is told "added".** `addScreens` reads, runs the reader (seconds), then writes the whole record and sets `status: "OPEN"` unconditionally (`forms.ts:80,106`); `finish` does not claim the row (`:116,143`). Measured: (a) two adds at once both answer "added" and 1 screen is stored; (b) an add during `finish` answers "added, OPEN", then `finish` writes HELD with 1 screen while `files` lists 2. STANDARDS §4 optimistic concurrency.
5. **Duplicate element ids are accepted, then the screen map can never be accepted.** The contract and reader allow the same input id on two screens (applicant and spouse screens; a re-added screen after a return). Finish and approve succeed (18 inputs), then accept fails on `@@unique([formEditionId, dataPath, version])` (`prisma/schema.prisma:334`) with the generic "Already in progress or already exists… Refresh", every time. Fingerprint, `difference` and twin matching are all keyed by id alone (`engine/capture.ts:60`).
6. **A returned capture cannot be fixed.** After "Returned: screen 1 looks edited", the capturer can only append (`forms.ts:80,101`): the corrected screen becomes screen 3 next to the bad screen 1. Re-finishing gives 20 questions with duplicate ids (then item 5 applies). No remove-screen or abandon exists, and a RETURNED capture shows no next step.
7. **Merges are not re-checked at approval.** `approveMerge` (`forms.ts:438-453`) does not verify that both fields are still active. Proven: X→Y approved after Y→Z leaves X redirecting to the retired Y, with mappings moved onto Y. X→Y and X→Z both approved makes X an alias of two fields (`fork_x v1 →fork_y`, `v2 →fork_z`). That breaks P-65 "one id survives".
8. **Learned aliases and the person on a screen give wrong-meaning proposals that accept-all takes.** Accepting a screen map learns raw labels as aliases with no one-label-one-field check (`forms.ts:265-269`, unlike `addAlias` `:321-322`). "Country or territory" became an alias of `person.country_of_birth`. An online-only form's "Current country of residence › Country or territory" is then proposed as `person.country_of_birth` (alias). Alias matches ignore the group (`engine/twin.ts:96`) and always use `form_subject` (`:97`). The PDF-twin step prefers `form_subject` (`:84-85`), so a "Your spouse or common-law partner" screen maps all 8 inputs to the applicant's fields. Accepted rows get `confidence: "exact"` (`forms.ts:259`).
9. **The agreed rule "each line's channel has a matching version" is built for Online lines only.** `channelProblems` skips other channels (`engine/channels.ts:47`). A PDF upload line for IMM 7200, which has only an online version (added through `POST /combinations/:id/lines`), gives just a warning, "No field map yet", so release passes with nothing to fill (challenge point 4, accepted by the Lead).
10. **"Finish" prints the review PDF inside the web request: 62 s, 71 s, 92 s and 114 s measured** (machine load 9–25; `print_pdf.mjs` alone takes 10 s, because the bundled browser is missing and it falls back to Chrome). `forms.ts:142`; STANDARDS §4 "never inside a web request". During that minute a second click or an add races (item 4).
11. **The test that says the review/release validator runs the channel rules cannot fail.** `tests/check_forms_channels.ts:176` passes on `JSON.stringify(review).includes("Field map")`, which matches an attachment's status text ("Field map applied to the draft…"). Every other channel test calls `/channels`, which calls `channelProblems` directly (`forms.ts:309`), so removing the hook at `engine/validate.ts:71` fails nothing. The behaviour is right (I saw the error in the combination's `draft.problems` and in submit), but it is untested. STANDARDS §7 [M]. Also untested: the filler's option-map branch (`fill.ts:44-55`, engine only), and refusal of ticked radios and checkboxes, filled text areas and dated values (I checked: all refused).
