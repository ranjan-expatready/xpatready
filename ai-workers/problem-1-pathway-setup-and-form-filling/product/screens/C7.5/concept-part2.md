Persona: case officer at an RCIC firm (the representative of record waives a CRITICAL). Journey: Prepare application → **Review** → Ready to file (C7.6). ## 3. Business rules
* **BR-1 Cost first (P-110, P-227).** Before anything runs the card shows **Saved · US$0.00** (or why none exists) and **Live · about US$1.06, at most US$4.80** for IMM 5257, under the US$5 job cap (P-232). Live is disabled with the words "a live review is not allowed without the founder's Go" while the switch is off. Only Saved and Live are offered.
* **BR-2 What the AI reads.** The form's official text (inventory) and its **review sheet**: for each box a copy would write, its words (the printed caption; the fact's meaning when the caption has none, "*", "DD"), fact and **printed value** (the filler's own value step, `engine/form-values.ts`: a date split over DD, MM, YYYY reads as its parts; a choice by its words, "Afghanistan" not "252"); never the representative's facts; never contact details (email, phone, fax: not what AG-11-69 cross-checks; fewer client data sent; the guard refuses them too). The same values give the same sheet.
* **BR-3 Replay key.** A Saved run is found by the form edition and the sheet's checksum (and the prompt), never the PDF checksum. Other values → "No saved review of this form edition with these values yet".
* **BR-4 Two rounds (P-121).** A first round and a blind checker; High when both raise the same fact with the same severity and the rules pass; otherwise **Low**. Every finding carries **both rounds' reasons**: what each said, that it raised nothing, or that its finding failed the rules and why. No third round. Low first (BIC-06).
* **BR-5 Never made up (AG-11-70).** A finding must name a fact on the sheet and quote words found in that fact's value; otherwise it is dropped and the run says why.
* **BR-6 Life of a finding** is part 1's: acknowledge, dismiss, waive (CRITICAL, representative of record only). A dismissed AI finding is not raised again (same person, form, fact, quote); a finding whose quoted words left the form is **resolved by the rules when the findings are read** (US$0), as a rules finding is.
* **BR-7 Warns, never blocks (P-71, P-168).** An open AI finding is a warning on the filing's readiness: decided here, or passed with a reason on Ready to file; never a hard stop.
* **BR-8 Stop (P-110).** A live run shows Stop (the page names the run id before it starts); the round running now ends, nothing after it is sent. One live run per case at a time; Live reviews one form at a time (two forms are over the job cap).
* **BR-10 Made-up runs never count.** A test's made-up saved run lives only in an isolated copy's own folder (`STUDIO_MADE_UP_REVIEW_RUNS=1`, `STUDIO_VAR` set), says so on every line, and pack step C7.5-6b fails on it; a record marked `test_fixture` is refused anywhere else.
* **BR-9 Stand-in SI-6** chip on the card and on replayed findings ("Replayed").

## 4. Layout (EXPERIENCE §3 "Source + proposals")
Path: Cases › reference › Review. **Run card** (top): Person (select, people on the case), Form (select, that person's PDF forms with a printed edition, IMM 5257 first); two rows, each a radio with its price first — *Saved US$0.00 · replays the checked run of <date>* / *Live about US$1.06, at most US$4.80 · needs the founder's Go* (disabled, the reason under it); SI-6 chip; the four steps listed; primary button "Run review (Saved, US$0.00)". While running: the steps tick in order with a live region; Stop on Live. After: one result line ("Saved: 3 new findings, 1 already on the case. US$0.00. Each finding warns; none blocks Ready to file.") and any dropped finding or box the AI could not judge, with its reason. Below, **Source + proposals**: left, **What the form says** (the sheet, box by box: what the rounds read); right, **Findings** grouped **per filing, then person** (filing label from Prepare), each row as part 1 plus, for AI rows: agreement pill (High / Low), both rounds' reasons, "The form says “<quote>”" and **Show on the form**, which highlights the quoted line in the source and the row itself. The case page keeps its Review panel, with "Open Review" in its header. States: loading, error with Retry, no saved run (Saved disabled with the reason), nothing filled (the reason, link to the Agent view), empty findings, made-up test runs (a banner on every line; test copies only).
