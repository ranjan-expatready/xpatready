## Is the weak-act rule honest?
Half of it is. *"Opening a case or sending a filing for review is not preparing it"* is true and removes a real falsehood. The second half — *"…unless nobody else worked on it, and then it names you"* — is a guess dressed as a fact: the same click is called preparing or not preparing depending on what other people did later, and the product then prints "Prepared by: Case officer (Sample)" for someone who typed nothing (B1) and refuses a review in the same breath (M1). The honest rule is the one the screen already has words for: weak acts may carry the **reviewer's own** "Self-reviewed" (they stand behind the filing, P-155 (1)), but must never name a second person; a case holding nobody's work says "nobody recorded".
