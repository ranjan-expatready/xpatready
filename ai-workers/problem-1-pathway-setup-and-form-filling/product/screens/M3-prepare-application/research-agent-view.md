## 1. How IRCC lets a paid representative work online
IRCC gives an authorized paid representative three accounts and says which applications go in each ([Sign in to an account for representatives](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/corporate/partners-service-providers/authorized-paid-representatives-portal.html), 2026-10-05):

| Account | Applications IRCC lists | How the forms are done | Who signs | What the representative gets back |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **APR Portal** ([user guide](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/corporate/partners-service-providers/authorized-paid-representatives-portal/user-guide.html), 2026-08-04) | visitor visa, study and work permit; Express Entry; IEC; eTA; IFHP | Visitor, study and work: questions answered in the portal, then the checklist's documents uploaded (validated PDF forms are among the uploads: see §2). Express Entry and IEC: profile sections ("Not started · In progress · Complete"), with no upload step | The representative: given and last names, a security question, then "Transmit" or "Transmit and Pay" | A status page with the client's UCI, the application number, the date received and the biometrics status; the "Transaction Approved" page is the fee receipt |
| **IRCC Portal for representatives** ([sign in](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/corporate/partners-service-providers/authorized-paid-representatives-portal/sign-in-ircc-portal-representative.html), 2025-07-07) | in-Canada refugee claims; temporary resident visas; emergency and special-measure TRVs | Online forms in place of the PDF: the IMM 5257 page says "You don't need this form if you're applying: in the IRCC Portal" ([IMM 5257](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/application/application-forms-guides/imm5257.html), 2026-10-06) | Not stated on the page | Not stated |
| **Representative PR Portal** ([how representatives submit PR online](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigration-citizenship-representative/pr-representatives-online.html), 2026-02-04) | permanent residence other than Express Entry (PNP, start-up visa, self-employed …) | Four *digital forms* (IMM 0008, IMM 5669, IMM 5406, IMM 5562 when it applies), filled from a Principal Applicant's Profile; every other form is a PDF completed as the program's guide says, then uploaded with the documents and the fee receipt | **The client** e-signs in the client portal (the consent and declaration page and the IMM 5669 web form); then the representative ticks the declaration. Submit turns blue only "once all signatures, declarations and validations are complete" | An incomplete application is returned and flagged in the dashboard; it can be submitted again |

Limits that apply to every account ([PR portal sign-in](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/corporate/partners-service-providers/authorized-paid-representatives-portal/sign-in-permanent-residence-portal-representatives.html), 2026-02-04; [IMM 5476](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/application/application-forms-guides/imm5476.html)):
* A paid representative cannot open a client's account, cannot e-sign for the client and cannot sign in with the client's username and password. The PR portal also blocks editing basic information inside the forms: it is changed in the profile.
* A representative may hold only one APR Portal account. Only the representative can link an existing application to that account, and the details must match the form exactly.
* The Use of a Representative form (IMM 5476) is uploaded with the online application. A sponsorship needs one for the sponsor and one for the principal applicant, both under "Additional application forms". After submission it goes through IRCC's web form. Each application has one representative.
* The APR Portal's "case ID" is the representative's own reference: IRCC does not use it. **This is where our filing reference belongs.**

## 2. How IRCC lets a representative work with PDFs
From the Help Centre ([2D barcode forms](https://ircc.canada.ca/english/helpcentre/answer.asp?qnum=1523&top=4), 2026-09-28) and the form pages:
* Some forms have a Validate button at the top and bottom. "You must always click the Validate button", online and on paper. When nothing is missing, a barcode page appears at the end; when something is missing, the fields are outlined in red. Acrobat Reader 10 or later is needed (IMM 5409's page gives the same steps).
* **Uploaded online:** do not print the form and do not use the signature panel. Save the validated file and upload it to the account; after the last upload, the name and security answer act as the electronic signature.
* **On paper:** print the whole form with the barcode page; the barcodes must be clear (laser printer, white bond paper); sign and date in pen; put the barcode page on top of the package (IMM 1295: "page 5 of 5"). A guide warns that a package with missing or unreadable barcodes is returned unprocessed.
* Some forms produce no barcodes at all ([Guide 5798](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/application/application-forms-guides/guide-5798-application-permanent-residence-caring-people-high-medical-needs-class.html)): "validated" must be recorded per edition, not assumed.
* When an online application is used, the portal gives a personalised document checklist ([work permit outside Canada: forms and documents](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/work-canada/permit-outside/forms-documents.html), saved copy in `official-sources/ircc/web-pages/`). That list can differ from our released lines.

**What it means for us.** P-46 holds: IRCC's Validate runs only in Adobe. Our "Check this form" is our own engine and must never say "Validated". The validated copy comes back by upload and we read it back. A PDF headed for an online account is never printed or signed with the signature panel; a paper one always is, in pen, with the barcode page on top. **The account decides more than the form does.** The same IMM 5257 is a PDF in the IRCC secure account or on paper, and is not needed at all in the IRCC Portal; IMM 0008 is a digital form in the PR portal and a PDF on paper.

## 3. Employer facts on a worker's application
A worker usually needs one employer number: the LMIA number on the employer's ESDC decision letter (the employer gives the worker a copy), or, for an LMIA-exempt job, an **offer of employment number**, an "A" followed by seven digits, which the employer gets after submitting the offer and paying the compliance fee in the Employer Portal ([Guide 5487](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/application/application-forms-guides/guide-5487-applying-work-permit-outside-canada.html); [Help Centre 164](https://ircc.canada.ca/english/helpcentre/answer.asp?qnum=164)). An open work permit needs no employer documents. This supports P-106: the employer case is the single home of the employer's name, address, LMIA or offer number and the LMIA letter, and the worker's case reads them from it.

## 4. The agent view — what it must do (design rules for the concept)
**AV-1 Filing route first, then a channel per form (P-107, refined).** The officer first picks the **route** for the filing: APR Portal · IRCC Portal · PR Portal · Paper (the combination proposes one; the library says which routes the application allows). The route then sets each form's default channel: digital form in the portal (**Online**) or **PDF** (uploaded, or printed for paper). The officer may change a form's channel only where the library's PDF / Online / both allows it; any other choice is refused with the reason ("IRCC accepts this form as a PDF only"). An Online form shows "Fill in the portal by hand from the answer sheet; the browser extension comes in a later wave" (P-102). Forms the route does not need show as "Not needed on the IRCC Portal" instead of disappearing. → Q1.
**AV-2 One item per form × person**, each in the state its channel needs:
* *PDF, online account:* Fill (confirmed facts only, P-72) → Check (our engine) → Download → Validate in Adobe → Upload the validated copy → Read back → Uploaded to IRCC (a tick).
* *PDF, paper:* the same until Read back, then Print with the barcode page → Signed in pen → Scan received.
* *Online:* an answer sheet in portal order (answer, Copy, source) → "Entered in the portal" per screen, freezing the answers it covers (challenge M6).
* *Documents:* Requested → Received → Uploaded; IMM 5476 always appears, one per sponsor and principal applicant where IRCC asks for it.
**AV-3 Signatures follow the account and are never ours.** PR Portal: one step "Client e-signed in the client portal", ticked by the officer once the portal shows it; the product never asks for or stores the client's portal credentials. APR Portal: "Representative declaration signed" at submission. Paper: pen signatures per form and person (who signs, from Screen 03 S2's `signedBy`, challenge M4).
**AV-4 Linked employer case (P-106).** On a worker's case, "Relies on employer case …" lets the officer pick a case under Corporate. The employer's facts (name, address, LMIA number or offer of employment number) and documents (LMIA letter, offer of employment) show on the worker's case read-only, marked "From employer case E-…", with a link to open it. A change on the employer case marks the worker's items stale (the same rule as BR-7) and is never copied silently. Removing the link asks for a reason. Sample values only.
**AV-5 Confirm section by section.** The facts sections of the agent view (Personal details, Family, Employer, Travel, Background …) are each one card: facts with their source (client, officer, employer case, document), "Confirm section", and the items it unblocks ("Confirming Employer fills 3 items"). Background and declaration questions stay the client's (P-97): the officer sees them but cannot confirm them for the client.
**AV-6 Record the filing in IRCC's own words.** Route; our filing reference (typed as the APR "case ID"); submitted on; per person, the application number and UCI as shown on IRCC's status page; the fee receipt (the "Transaction Approved" page) as a document; status Submitted · Returned incomplete (PR Portal) · Resubmitted. Recording makes the filing read-only except "Correct a number", which keeps the history (challenge m14). A filing is case × combination on the pin, with its persons (challenge M1).
**AV-7 Readiness.** Blocks: a form-check error, a missing or unconfirmed fact, a changed fact on an entered or uploaded item, and a missing required signature step. Warns only: AI review findings (P-71) and missing documents (challenge M5, recommended warn), because IRCC's checklist can differ from ours.

## 5. Layout options
All three use the case's "Prepare application" tab, which absorbs today's Forms panel (challenge M7), and right-hand panels, never modals.

**Option A — Filing workspace (Workspace pattern, EXPERIENCE §3).** Header: the five-part trail, a route chip (APR Portal ▾), readiness ("7 of 12 ready · next: Confirm Employer"), one primary action. Left (320 px): items grouped *Confirm facts · Online forms · PDF forms · Signatures · Documents*, each with a person chip and a state. Right: the chosen item (answer sheet, PDF steps, section card, signature step); a channel switch on a form row, inside what the library allows. Footer action: Record the filing.
*For:* an existing pattern; one screen for every route; grouping by channel follows IRCC's own split (digital forms vs uploads). *Against:* a long list on a family PR filing (about 20 rows); the order of work is implicit.

**Option B — Five fixed steps (a stepper over the same items).** Route & channels → Confirm facts → Prepare forms → Signatures → Submit & record, mirroring the portal's flow (APR guide: client details → checklist → review → upload → declaration → pay). One step is open at a time; the others collapse to a summary.
*For:* it reads like the portal the officer knows; it suits a new officer. *Against:* EXPERIENCE §3 limits wizards to three steps and to *creating* things; real work is not linear (a fact changes after upload; a client signs late), so steps would reopen all the time.

**Option C — Facts first.** Left: the facts sections (AV-5), each with its confirmation state; right: for the open section, every form, screen and field that uses it, by channel. The forms and filing record sit on a second tab.
*For:* the strongest proof of "answers once" and of the employer link. *Against:* a new pattern; channel work (validate, upload, sign) is split from the facts that feed it; two places to find a form.

**Recommendation: Option A**, with B's five steps as a **progress strip** in the header that filters the list (click "Signatures" to see only signature items) rather than as a wizard, and C's "this section fills N items" inside each section card. It uses a pattern we already have, keeps every state in one list for any route, and still shows the officer the order of work. The board in `design/draft-prepare-application.html` already has A's shape; the concept revises it for the route chip (AV-1), the Signatures group (AV-3), the employer facts (AV-4) and the filing record (AV-6).

## 6. Questions (one at a time, recommendation first)
* **Q1 (founder) — Where does the choice sit?** Recommended: **route per filing, channel per form inside it** (AV-1), because IRCC's account decides whether a form is a digital form, an upload or not needed at all (IMM 5257 vs IRCC Portal; IMM 0008 in the PR portal). Alternative: a channel per form only, as P-107 reads today, which lets an officer pick combinations no IRCC account accepts. "Both" stays a library value (what IRCC accepts), not something the officer files twice.
* **Q2 (immigration expert) — Route per application:** confirm the route list for the founder's 5 Retail combinations and the Corporate Sample, and which of their forms each route turns into digital forms. The canada.ca pages above are the starting point; they are Sample until the expert confirms.
* **Q3 (Lead) — Where the employer case's facts live:** the employer case is an ordinary case under the Corporate pathway type with its own sections (recommended), not a client-record master; this settles challenge M1's "filing" shape for linked cases.
