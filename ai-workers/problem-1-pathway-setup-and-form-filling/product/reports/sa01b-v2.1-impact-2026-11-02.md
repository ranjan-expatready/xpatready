# SA-01B: what changed from v1.5 (18 Oct) to v2.1 (27 Oct), and what it means for the Pathway Configurator POC

Read-only analysis, 2 Nov 2026. Sources read on Drive: v2.0 SESSION-STATUS (26 Oct), v2.0 KNOCK-ONS (26 Oct, all of it), SA-01B v2.1 (document control, the full version history v1.6 to v2.1, Part A, every card heading, the key cards' outcome, rules and non-goals lines, the Part C business-rule headings), and the Drive copy of REDESIGN-DECISIONS (compared line by line with the repository's 18 Oct copy). Ours: `product/DECISIONS.md` (P-229 back to P-46, searched by topic), `product/qa/CAPABILITIES.md`, and the studio schema for pins and filings.

**Limit:** Drive's text export of v2.1 stops at about 1.0 of its 1.2 MB, in Part C2. I did not see Part C3 to C7 (data, permissions, scale, built today, journey tests) or Part D (decision appendix, "where did it go", open questions D5). The version-history rows and the decision record cover what Part D summarises, so the findings below stand. Refreshing the snapshot (last section) closes the gap.

## Verdict

v2.1 is not an update to the document our POC was built from. It is a rewrite. v2.0 (26 Oct) recast SA-01B as 185 build cards in eleven chapters, 112 of them new. It rests on one model, "one fact, used everywhere": canonical fields with a scope (person, filing, household, organisation), a resolver per person or organisation on each filing, three sources per value, a pin per filing, two kinds of publish (correction or rule change), and a learning loop. v2.1 itself (27 Oct) is one small change: only emails a person writes are copied. Most of the new model **confirms what we already built or decided**: shared fields as the root, documents first, AI drafts with a person checking, a second person releasing, sources cited, online-only forms getting an answer sheet, AI never filling at fill time, and a blank never defaulted. Five product owner rulings **contradict founder decisions** and need the founder's answer (AGENTS rule 2):
- PQR answers no longer pre-fill the SQR.
- There is no assessment on the case.
- Only a form's own Validate may block.
- A publish needs an answer-key test and a source entry.
- PQR, cut-off and pin granularity (one PQR per country; pins per filing).

About 15 capability rows need adding or rewording, chiefly answer-key tests, corrections versus rule changes, application fixes, the pin per filing, review items and the document master. One new dependency stands out. The team has made switching on AI drafting in Release 1 wait for "the proof-of-concept counts". That is exactly the evidence our POC produces. The repository's requirement snapshot (19 Oct, v1.5) is stale and should be refreshed now.

## Conflicts for the founder (one at a time, each with a recommendation)

1. **PQR answers pre-filling the SQR.** Ours: P-77(1), C6.8 (not built, after M3): PQR answers appear in the SQR filled and marked "please confirm". Team (G3, SA-1B-202): only personal details carry from prospect to client; the SQR asks everything else fresh; PQR pre-fill is on the Release 2 wish-list. Their reason is that PQR answers are often what-ifs. **Recommendation:** adopt the team's rule for Release 1. Carry the personal details only, and keep "please confirm" on them. It costs nothing (C6.8 is not built), removes the conflict, and P-76's PQR ⊂ SQR still holds.
2. **An assessment on the case.** Ours: P-77(2), P-129, P-164, C6.9 (Partial, M3 gate): a staff eligibility page in the case. Team (G3b, G11, SA-1B-202/-204): the assessment is a sales tool in the prospect module only; "the case shows no assessment"; on Express Entry and provincial filings only, a staff-only "Calculate score" from confirmed facts. **Recommendation:** keep our page, renamed and scoped to match G11 ("case check from confirmed facts", staff only, never the sales assessment). Move the PQR-based view to the prospect side when the PQR is built. Tell the team that our page proves the gates engine they will reuse.
3. **What may block.** Ours: P-168, keeping P-71 and P-151(1). Ready to file is refused for an out-of-date signed item or a missing signature, a barcode form not validated, an edition or fee past IRCC's accepted-until date, no representative review, and (P-71) missing or conflicting facts on the form. Team (BR-4, G31, G20(a), SA-1B-29): within case and form work only a form's own Validate stops staff; everything else is CRITICAL at submission, passable with a reason. The founder chose P-168 knowing about AG-07 BR-26, but v2.1 restates the opposite for SA-01B. **Recommendation:** keep P-168. Each block is a fact IRCC returns a file for, or a legal duty of the representative. Send the team the evidence per block as a challenge. Do not change the code.
4. **Gates on our own publish.** Team: no answer-key test, no publish (G17); no source-register entry, no publish (G20(c)). Ours: P-170 allows "Sample" values, and C5.1 has no test gate. These gates sit on ExpatReady's own publish, not on firm work, so they do not contradict P-168. They would, however, stop our Sample content from releasing. **Recommendation:** adopt both gates in the POC with a "Sample" pass. A Sample value counts as the team's "None published – set by ExpatReady", and a combination's answer-key test can be a made-up family whose expected values are marked Sample until the expert signs. That keeps the demo moving and builds the gate the team needs.
5. **Free hand authoring, and PQR granularity.** (a) Team (SA-1B-292): a Content publisher can write or correct any item by hand at any time. Ours: P-158, no free manual add (an item comes from a source; people confirm, rename and merge). **Recommendation:** keep P-158 for creating items, which the team does not forbid (it only wants hand-made items to take the same path), and confirm that hand *correction* of any item is allowed, which it is today. (b) Team (SA-1B-201): one PQR per country. Ours: P-132, per combination, with the country PQR as a view, and the Lead's reply to the team still owed. **Recommendation:** send that reply now. The team's per-country PQR can be our assembled view, so the two can coexist.

Two further divergences need no founder ruling unless they want one. P-46 (Validate in Adobe, safety first) against the team's in-app Validate on trial-passed editions: keep P-46 for the POC. Fees: our family-cap rules (P-171) against the team's "no automatic family cap": keep ours, since it is sourced.
