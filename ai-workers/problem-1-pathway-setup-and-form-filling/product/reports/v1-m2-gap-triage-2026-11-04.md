They are about **lists** (addresses, jobs, travel: asked once, with dates and the form's overflow rule), **composite fields** (phone, address), **document dates** (a passport valid long enough), **named document alternatives**, and a **completeness view** that would have caught the gaps in my walk. They need one founder decision on Release 1 scope, then M3. Change watch needs a founder ruling (it conflicts with P-65). The rest can go to M3 or later with little filing risk. The 3 built items need capability lines only (records, no build).

**Knock-on:** M2 has 60 capability rows, and the target is 58–59 (P-309). Adding 6 rows makes 66. Each new row needs the founder's OK, as with P-228 (5) for G1–G4. Recommendation: add them as M2 rows, but keep "60 of 60 confirmed" as the stated gate and report the 6 separately. That way the target does not move silently.
