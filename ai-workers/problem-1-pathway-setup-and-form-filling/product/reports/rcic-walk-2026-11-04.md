**Asked by:** the founder, 3 Nov about 23:40 IST, through the tech lead: be sure, from a licensed consultant's (RCIC's) point of view, that the tool can file a real application, and check that nothing is built on our own assumptions.
**Done by:** builder-3, read-only on the app. Walk from 23:19 to 23:35 IST on 3 Nov, on a fresh isolated copy of `origin/main` at `48954b537` (port 4480, database `xr_studio_rcic`; never :4400). Browser at 1440 px. Live AI off, **US$0**: no model call; the AI results used are saved replays.

## 1. Verdict in plain words

**No: an RCIC cannot file this family's visitor visa with the tool today.** The parts that are built are mostly right: one application per person, the family's fees, the biometrics exemption for the child, a parent signing for the child, the Family Information form only for the adults, and IMM 5257 filled from confirmed answers and read back. Three things stop real use:

1. **No path in the product from setup to a case.** After "Reset to the six", Visitor visa has 0 of 5 setup steps done and 13 problems that block its release [03][06]. "Load the configured checkpoint" stops at 394 problems, opens no family case, and says IMM 5257 waits for a paid AI run [07]. A case cannot be opened because "Nothing is released yet" [08]. To keep walking, I built the release with the acceptance tests' own setup scripts (`tests/runtime/setup_visitor_visa.ts`, then `acceptance/lib/setup_vv_m3.ts`, which passed 44 of 46 lines). Those scripts call the same routes the screens call, but no person can do these steps on the screens at US$0 today.
2. **"Ready" does not mean complete.** Every form said ready, yet each person's IMM 5257 still had 13 or 14 fields that IRCC's form marks mandatory left empty [19][21]. Examples: "Do you have a national identity document?", the US green card question, the person or organisation the family will visit and their address in Canada, and the employment history. IRCC's Validate button would refuse these forms, and an incomplete application is returned.
3. **Documents IRCC asks for are missing, and the "Prepare application" screen is not built.** The release asks for only 8 document lines [10]. It has no proof of status in the country of residence, purpose of travel or letter of invitation, marriage certificate, US visa copy or translations. Fees, the family's filings, the checklist per person, signing and "Ready to file" exist in the server, but no screen shows them (the web app never calls those routes; §2.6).

**What the consultant would still do by hand, even on this release:** complete the setup and release (or ask us to run the scripts); answer the IMM 5257 questions the questionnaire did not ask; write 5 fields per IMM 5257 by hand in Adobe and run Validate there [20]; type the Family Information form into the portal (online fill is a later wave [09]); print, sign and scan IMM 5476; collect and check every document the release does not list; work out and pay the fees in the portal (CAD 470 for this family; the tool computes it, but only in the server); upload everything one file per field; and watch for the biometrics letter.

**Built on an assumption (no citation and no Sample label): none found** among the values the walk showed. The labelling mechanism works (compliance trace, Sample labels, and the release check "Every published number has its answer").
**What it does not catch:** missing requirements. The trace checks each rule we *wrote*; nothing compares a release with IRCC's whole checklist. That is how 5 document lines, 4 conditional forms and the IMM 5257 mandatory fields slipped through (§3). Recommendation: a coverage check per combination. Every line of IRCC's checklist (APPLY, IMM 5484, the form's mandatory marks) becomes a row that is mapped to a release line, marked "not for this release" with a reason, or blocks the release.

## 6. Method and limits
* The case walk used a release built by the acceptance setup scripts, not by people on the screens, because the screens cannot release Visitor visa at US$0 today (§2.2–2.4). Questions not marked required were left unanswered on purpose, so the walk shows what a consultant gets when they answer exactly what the tool asks.
* Answers are Sample values ("SAMPLE", fixed dates). The 5 hand-written fields come partly from those values ("SAMPLE" is not in a country list); with real values that number may fall.
* Not done: Adobe Validate (a person's step on a computer, IRCC-P3-06), signing, uploading, and the eligibility screen. No submission is possible in a prototype.
* IRCC's personalized online checklist is generated inside an account, so the comparison rests on APPLY, Guide 5256 (a paper guide) and IMM 5484. The sources disagree on a few points (IMM 5645 or IMM 5707; whether a letter of invitation and proof of funds are required or recommended; translations). Those points are marked for the founder and the expert, not decided here.
