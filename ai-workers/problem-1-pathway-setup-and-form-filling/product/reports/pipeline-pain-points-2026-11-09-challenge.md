# Challenge: delivery pipeline pain points (9 Nov 2026)

Independent, read-only challenge of `pipeline-pain-points-2026-11-09.md`, run 18:15 to 18:40 IST on 9 Nov. The study's numbers were re-run with fresh GitHub REST pulls (1,431 PRs, open PRs, `ci.yml` runs for 1, 4, 7, 8 and 9 Nov, five PR timelines, `origin/main`), its own cached timelines read with a separate parser, and every Mac transcript re-counted with a separate script. Times in IST.

**Short verdict.** The headline totals hold: M3 is 17 of 29 Works, token cost is three quarters re-reading, and batch runs tripled. But three of the five proposed changes are **already in place**, and two numbers move a lot once the study's own definitions are fixed:
* The study counts **112 qa status PRs** (branches `claude/qa-*`) as builder PRs. Without them, a row needed a median of **6.5 builder PRs**, not 12. The builder cycle did not slow steadily: it **jumped on 4 Nov** (1.65 h to 4.9 h) and has stayed at 3.6 to 5.7 h since.
* The "idle standby" sessions behind T2 are **acting Leads**: standbys that were told to take over (Leads 36 to 40) and then did the Lead's work. T2's "54 to 58% of spend is idle" is wrong.
* The four "PRs with no decision" are **not idle**. Each has 16 to 35 commits, 3 to 6 merges of `main` and pushes on 9 Nov. They are large PRs (1,200 to 3,100 lines) going round a conflict, re-review and dequeue loop.

## What the study missed that the data shows is bigger

1. **Throughput fell by about four fifths while the cycle time only tripled.** Builder merges per day (no qa PRs): 80, 97, 95 on 1 to 3 Nov; 20 to 36 a day on 5 to 9 Nov. That fall is mostly **capacity**: account switches on 5, 6 and 9 Nov, the 4 Nov 13-hour cloud outage, the 93% weekly stop at 15:44 IST on 9 Nov, and fewer lanes after the lean switch. No pipeline change fixes that. Plan capacity is the first-order limit on M3.
2. **PR size.** Median lines per merge on `main` roughly tripled between 3 to 5 Nov and 7 to 9 Nov, and the study's own table shows time doubling per size band. The four stuck PRs are all over 1,200 lines. Small PRs cut items 2, 4 and part of 3 at once, with no gate removed.
3. **The merge-of-`main` loop is one mechanism, not three items.** Big PR → conflict on a hot file → merge `main` → label guard removes the Approve → new Opus review → new ~50 min run → queue → `main` moved → again. Items 2, 4, 5 and T3 are this loop seen from four sides.
4. **Two copies on one branch** (#1262: "two copies merged `main` at the same time", then one stood down). Duplicate work that also produced extra merges.
5. **The batch slowed partly because gates were added on purpose** (P-416 on 8 Nov, B122 on 6 Nov). That is quality bought with time, and should be judged as such, not as waste.
