# Decisions P-79, P-80, P-82, P-83 (moved word for word from DECISIONS.md, 30 Oct)

| # | Date | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| P-83 | 24 Oct | **Official blank PDFs on GitHub.** The official blank IRCC/ESDC PDFs listed in `official-sources/*/manifest.json` are committed so cloud sessions and CI have them (canada.ca refuses cloud requests, and IRCC replaces files at the same link). The push guard admits a PDF only under `official-sources/` with a manifest checksum; filled PDFs and every other PDF stay excluded. Amends P-81/P-82. *Founder, 24 Oct.* |
| P-82 | 24 Oct | **GitHub is the single source of truth.** The Mac and cloud sessions are ordinary clones, worked by pull at start, push at end; `sources/`, `code/`, `archive/`, every PDF except the manifest official blanks (P-83), databases, `.tools/`, logs and secrets stay on the Mac only, untracked and gitignored; the filtered mirror is retired; old commit IDs are translated by `history/COMMIT-MAP-2026-10-24.txt`. Packages come from the committed studio lockfile (`npm ci`); the push guard runs in `.githooks/pre-push` and CI. Amends P-81 and AGENTS.md rule 1. *Founder, 24 Oct.* |
| P-80 | 23 Oct | **A held PDF is approved in the product by a second person.** The uploader records the IRCC page the file came from; a different person compares the file with that page and approves or rejects it with a reason on the To review page (P-79), server-enforced; on approval the file's checksum joins the approved source list with who, when and the page, and the file can then be used (library, combination, edition impact, AI). Same pattern as portal captures (P-66). Amends P-57: approval no longer needs a manifest row added outside the product. Answers Screen 02 draft Q2. *Founder, 23 Oct (R-90).* |
| P-79 | 23 Oct | **One "To review" page, first under Configure.** Everything waiting for a person or a second person is listed in one place: AI proposals (field maps, sections, document lines, rules), new IRCC editions with their impact, portal captures, online screen maps, new shared fields, merges, held PDFs, and combinations waiting for release (a row linking to the combination's Review and release tab, where the approval itself happens, P-76). Filters Needs you · Waiting for others · Running · All, by kind and by search; each row shows what and why, status, AI cost and one next step for the viewer's role; the maker sees their item but cannot approve it (server-enforced). History › AI work stays the log of AI runs. Amends the menu of P-04 (PRODUCT §3). Answers Screen 02 draft Q1. *Founder, 23 Oct (R-89).* |
