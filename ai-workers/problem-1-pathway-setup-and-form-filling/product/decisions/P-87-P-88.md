# Decisions P-87, P-88 (moved word for word from DECISIONS.md, 30 Oct)

| # | Date | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| P-88 | 25 Oct | **The shared app stays on the founder's Mac.** The team sees the running app on :4400 while the Mac is on, or by screen share; no hosted copy and no Mac setup script for now. A team member's clone stays in sync through GitHub (P-82, P-87); a Linux clone can run the app with `cloud-setup.sh`. *Founder, 25 Oct: "Keep it on your Mac" (asked: where should team members see and run the app; scenario: a team member checks Screen 01 at 10 am while the Mac is asleep; recommendation was a shared URL plus local run).* |
| P-87 | 25 Oct | **Every change reaches `main` by PR; the Lead merges everything.** Nobody pushes to `main`: the Mac Lead, cloud Leads, the scheduled Lead (P-84), lanes and team members work on their own branches. A Lead works on `lead/*` (Mac) or its `claude/*` session branch (cloud, marked as the Lead copy by a local `.claude/lane`), merges checked lane branches into it, and opens one PR to `main`; any PR (a team member's too) merges once CI (guard, checks, suites) is green on its head and every review-bot comment is answered, and the Lead merges it itself, app code included, with no person's approval required (the standing merge OK of 23 Oct extends to PRs). Lanes and qa sync `origin/main`, so the Lead hands a lane back and sends `/qa after-merge` only once the PR carrying the note or merge is merged. `main` should be locked by a GitHub branch rule (PR required, those checks required, no force-push), but a private repository on GitHub's free plan cannot have one (GitHub Pro or Team needed; checked 25 Oct: `main` unprotected); until then the pre-push hook refuses pushes to `main` in every copy where hooks are set. The one-Lead check (`lane.sh leads`) also lists Lead commits on other branches not yet in `main`. Amends P-82 and P-84. *Founder, 25 Oct: "PR; Lead merges everything" (asked: how should every change reach main, for everyone; scenario: a scheduled Lead's 3 am slice and a team member's 9 am laptop fix; recommendation was the same with a person's OK on app code).* |
