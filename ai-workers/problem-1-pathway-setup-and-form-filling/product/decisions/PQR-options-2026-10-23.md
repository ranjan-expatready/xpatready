# PQR (pre-qualification) — industry research and options for the founder · 23 Oct 2026 (open, R-84)

**Why this page:** the team's SA-01B (SA-1B-77 to 89; redesign decisions 8, 9 and the prospect flow) and our P-47 disagree on how the PQR works. The founder asked for an evaluation grounded in industry practice before deciding. Their starting view: a question can belong to a kind of combination, there can also be a general PQR, and PQRs may be embedded in marketing campaigns or on the website. Research by an agent with web sources; legal and privacy points are **Unconfirmed: no counsel**.

## What the industry does
| Tool | General or per programme | Embeddable | Who sees the result | Answers carried into the case |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| IRCC "Come to Canada" | One general questionnaire across all programmes | Public website | The person, as "for reference only, no decision is made" | Yes: a reference code moves answers into the Express Entry profile, where the person reviews and completes them |
| INSZoom (Mitratech) | Short questionnaires per immigration type (family, employment), for first contact | Link for the firm's website | Staff, on the intake sheet | A "convert prospect to client" step; field carry-over not documented |
| Lawmatics | One master intake form branching by practice area; disqualification field; Source and Campaign fields | External and internal versions | Staff | Standard and custom fields save to the contact and matter |
| Clio Grow | Intake templates; completion sets matter type and pipeline stage | Public link | Staff | A matter is created |
| Maple CRM (Canadian immigration) | Assessment forms per programme with points calculators | Not stated | Staff rank by score; a score report "can be shared with the client" | Not stated |
| Docketwise | Embeddable lead form and chatbot; one smart client questionnaire | Yes | Staff | Leads flow in; no eligibility result |
| RCIC firm "free assessment" pages | Mostly one general form | Yes | Staff: a licensed consultant replies in 24–48 h; next step a paid consultation | Not visible |
**Pattern:** one general entry form that branches, with per-programme detail later or staff-side; results staff-side in practice tools (prospect-facing results come from government tools, always "reference only"); answers carried forward only with the person's confirmation.

## Rules that matter (Unconfirmed: no counsel)
* CICC Code of Professional Conduct (SOR/2022-128): marketing must not guarantee success or mislead (s.44(2)); a written consultation agreement before an initial consultation (s.23(1)); "Looks eligible" shown to a prospect risks reading as a promise.
* PIPEDA consent: state at collection that answers may open and pre-fill a case; express consent for sensitive information; a retention rule for prospects who do not convert. CASL: a separate unticked box for marketing email on campaign forms.

## Options
| | A · Country blocks only (SA-01B as written) | B · Per combination only (P-47 as written) | C · General PQR built from combination flags, with entry points (recommended by the research) |
|---|---|---|---|
| How it works | One PQR per country: a common block plus a block per programme of interest that unfolds from intent and status | Each combination flags part of its SQR; the prospect answers that one PQR | One question bank. Each combination flags its pre-qualification fields; a programme block is assembled from its combinations' flags; the general PQR unfolds blocks. Firms publish entry points (website or campaign link) preset to a programme or a combination, which skip what they already know and record Source and Campaign |
| Prospect sees | Questions, then "we'll be in touch" | A verdict ("Looks eligible") and a next step | Short, relevant questions, then a next step worded by the firm, no verdict |
| Result | Staff only | Prospect and staff | Staff by default |
| Into the case | Personal details; the assessment is a read-only record | Everything | Personal details automatically; other answers pre-fill the SQR marked "please confirm"; the frozen assessment record |
| Precedent | IRCC tool, Lawmatics, firm pages | INSZoom, Maple CRM | IRCC + reference code, Lawmatics branching with campaign fields, progressive profiling |
| Fit | Full SA-01B fit; P-47 drops per-combination and the verdict | Conflicts with SA-1B-77–79 and redesign 8, 9 | Keeps SA-1B-77–84 and redesign 8, 9b; changes redesign 9 from read-only to pre-fill-to-confirm (that decision says it can be added later); P-47 renderer needs only a block-set mode and a next-step end screen |
| Risks | A campaign for one programme still starts general; answers retyped in the SQR | Most prospects do not know their combination; duplicate questions; s.44(2) guarantee risk | Entry points are firm-side scope SA-01B does not specify; pre-fill-to-confirm needs care; consent wording at collection |

## Sub-questions after the model is chosen
1. Should a prospect ever see a verdict (research: no in Release 1, next-step message only)?
2. Do PQR answers pre-fill the SQR "to confirm", or stay a read-only record?
3. Entry points preset to a programme or combination: Release 1, or later?
