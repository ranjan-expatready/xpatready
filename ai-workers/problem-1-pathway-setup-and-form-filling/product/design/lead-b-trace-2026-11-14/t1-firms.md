## 2. Mandatory in the requirement for the parts we keep, missing from our design

1. **One country per firm, set at creation, never changed (SA-2-9 l.435, AG-1-26 l.611, BR-7).** Fix: add `country_code` (not null, immutable by a trigger) to `platform_firm` in contracts §1.1 tables, and an immutability test in row PF-3. Judgment: a thin month-one value is a single Sample "CA".
2. **Owner contact and business address at creation (SA-2-1 l.420, SA-2-25 l.463).** Required fields: owner name, email, phone, language, address (country, province, city, postal code). Fix: add `owner_contact` and `address` columns to `platform_firm` in §1.1 and make the sign-up body in §1.1 API take them. GST/HST number is asked later (trial end), so defer (see §3).
3. **Operating name and legal name (SA-2-1; AG-1-4 l.554).** Fix: split `name` into `operating_name` and `legal_name` in §1.1 tables.
4. **Contact email unique across firms; match on a firm still setting up resumes it (SA-2-24 l.462, BR-3, BR-4).** Fix: add to §1.1 Rules and a pass/fail test line in PF-5 (one firm per contact email; resume, not duplicate; sign-up idempotent).
5. **Audit of every status change: actor or system, prior, new, reason, UTC time; operator changes need a reason; illegal moves refused with a clear message (SA-2-5 l.428, BR-5, BR-16; AG-1-3).** Fix: add an `audit_log` (or `platform_firm_status_change`) table to §1.1 and a test line in PF-5. Our `module_event` is for the case side and refuses update/delete; it can double as the audit trail only if it carries actor and reason. Say which in §1.1.
6. **Seat limit counts active users and pending invitations; server-side at invite and reactivation; deactivated users free their seat (AG-1-13 l.564).** We keep `seats` on plan and subscription but no rule. Fix: add one line to §1.2 Rules (seat check at invite and reactivate) and a test in PF-4/PF-6.
7. **Billing email optional and a separate recipient for billing notices (SA §3.8 l.377-, SA2-D23).** Fix: add nullable `billing_email` to `platform_firm`; recipient rule can wait (see §3).
8. **Firm status gates access as the requirement defines it: PAYMENT_HOLD only payment screens and data download (SA-2-6 l.429).** Fix: either adopt (see §4-3) or the founder decides; in §1.1 Rules.
9. **Every firm-admin mutation audited (AG-1-3, AG-1-46 l.653).** Same fix as item 5, extend to staff, roles, licence and profile edits in §1.2 Rules.
10. **Licence edits audited (who, whose profile, before and after; viewing not logged) and licence PII never in logs, emails or notifications (AG §3.5 l.431-, AG-1-31).** Fix: add one Rules line to §1.2 and a "licence number never in a log or event payload" test to PF-4. Our §0.5 already says payloads carry no secrets; make licence numbers explicit.
11. **Deactivated person's licence never stamped on new documents (AG-1-51 l.575, AG1-D8).** Our deactivate endpoint exists; the rule belongs to the retainer module. Fix: note in §2.3 retainers (FO-5) that the lead representative must be an active licensed person. No change in PF-3/PF-5.
12. **Support access ("log in as") with read-only default, reason, 60-minute limit, visible to firm owner (SA §3.7, SA-2-16 to SA-2-19, SA-2-41).** Mandatory for the requirement; our design has "our support staff only" for verify, but no support-access model. Fix: only if we keep a staff-into-firm path; otherwise mark deferred in §1.1 (see §3). Judgment: month one needs no way into a firm; say so.

## 4. Conflicts between requirement and our design

1. **Open public sign-up.** Ours: `POST /api/public/firms`, public door (C l.28, §0.6 l.18; Rows PF-5). Requirement: "sign-up by private link only in Release 1", open page deferred (SA l.149 deferred table; SA2-D2 l.48; SA-2-22 l.460). Needs the founder? No: the requirement settles it. Judgment: a thin option is that our endpoint accepts only a valid single-use link token (created by our support staff) or a Super Admin creation call; keep the open form behind a flag, off. If product wants open sign-up early, that is a founder decision against the team's deferral.
2. **Licence check by our staff before ACTIVE (D-4; C l.26, l.30, l.109; Rows PF-5).** Requirement: no human verification gate; set-up complete by system moves PROVISIONING to ACTIVE or TRIAL (SA §6 l.508-511); licence is entered by the person, a soft format check only, "No licence check ever blocks a permission" (AG §3.5 l.431-; AG-1-34 l.622; AG-1-58 l.645). The only licence-linked stop: a new retainer needs an active licensed person (AG-1-51 l.575, AG-1-38 l.629). Needs the founder? Yes, because no official source or best practice settles it; D-4 is already a founder decision, now with a conflict against the team's requirement. Judgment: if kept, call it Expat Ready's own "firm verification", not a licence check, keep the setting default off to match the requirement, and do not make it a status the requirement lacks.
3. **Suspended behaviour.** Ours: SUSPENDED = "sign-in works, read-only, export works" (C l.30). Requirement: PAYMENT_HOLD = staff reach only payment screens and data download; all else refused (SA-2-6 l.429; SA §3.4 l.352-353); clients view and download only (SA-2-35 l.473-). Needs the founder? No, the requirement settles it; adopt "payment and download only" (only the Stripe portal and export routes open) when the status is PAYMENT_HOLD. Judgment: cheaper to build than a general read-only mode.
4. **CLOSED vs requirement's exit path.** Ours: CLOSED reached by us with a data download (C l.24, l.27, l.29). Requirement: voluntary exit is cancellation to period end, then PAYMENT_HOLD, then DELETED after 90 days (SA-2-7, SA-2-21 l.457, SA §3.9); "close" exists only for a security-locked account, Platform Owner only, with a reason (SA-2-34 l.472). Needs the founder? No. Fix: month one = `CLOSED` renamed `SECURITY_LOCK` + a Platform-Owner-only close, or keep CLOSED as a Sample end state that maps to DELETED later; either way no hard delete (BR-13).
5. **Admin may edit the firm profile.** Ours: `GET|PATCH /api/platform/firm` for owner and admin (C l.28). Requirement: editing the legal firm profile is Owner-only; Admin boundary list of six Owner-only actions (AG-1-65 l.591). Needs the founder? No. Fix: owner-only for legal fields; admin may edit non-legal fields (branding, defaults).
6. **Licence on the role grant; "REPRESENTATIVE" as a role.** Ours: licence body/category/number live on `platform_role_grant` of a representative; check constraint (C l.34; Rows PF-3). Requirement: licence belongs to the person's own profile, one record per person (unique per user), representative type + licensing body + number; no representative role in the six starter roles (AG-1-17 l.583; AG-1-30 l.618; AG §3.5 l.431-; AG-1-58). Needs the founder? No. Fix: a `platform_licence` table (firm_id, staff_id unique, representative_type, licensing_body, number) and a derived "licensed user"; keep REPRESENTATIVE only as our duty mapping to `represent` (C l.37), not a stored role. Check constraint then moves to "a lead representative must hold an active licence".
7. **Two-step sign-in required for all staff.** Ours: required for every staff member, "Decided by Lead B (industry standard)" (C l.32, l.114). Requirement: optional, off by default; the Owner may switch on a firm-wide requirement; enforced at login (AG-1-40 l.639; AG §3.7 l.483-). The built default in the team's code is "mandatory", which the requirement orders reversed (AG l.489). Needs the founder? Judgment: low; our default is stronger and standard practice for a new build (regulated, client data), so deviate and log it: keep required, label as a deliberate deviation from AG-1-40, and offer the Owner no switch. If the founder prefers parity with the team's product, use their wording.
8. **Status vocabulary and state machine.** Ours C l.26 / Rows PF-5 versus SA-2-4 l.427 and §6 (l.506-). Differences: no TRIAL; PAST_DUE as a state (requirement: indicator on TRIAL/ACTIVE); no SECURITY_LOCK; no ABANDONED; PENDING_VERIFICATION replaces PROVISIONING; "resume" on a firm still setting up. Needs the founder? No. See §5 for the mapping; the §6 transition table is the one to copy.
