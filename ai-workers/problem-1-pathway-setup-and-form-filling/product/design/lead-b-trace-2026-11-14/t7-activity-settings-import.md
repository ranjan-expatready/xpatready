### What PF-10 must follow (AG-18 v1.9, summary only)
- One import engine for the platform; leads CSV is an adapter on it (AG-18 §3.0, §3.10, decision AG18-D2, l.264-282, 402-407).
- Nothing is written before a mandatory dry run showing create, merge, skip, error per row with reasons, and the duplicate warnings; the run cannot be stopped midway; undo is one click for the whole batch (BR-1, BR-28, AG18-D4; l.303-319, 386-393, 748-780).
- Duplicate checks warn and never block; defaults: exact email match "Use existing"; a merge never overwrites a non-empty value; same file twice caught before mapping (AG-18-94, -98, §3.3).
- Undo window default 14 days; undo removes only what the batch created, restores merged records, refuses records a person has worked on (§3.8, BR-12).
- Imported people, leads and cases start "Not started" (quiet for client-facing automation) until the firm confirms "Start client automations" after a preview of what would be sent; internal actions still run; no consent is invented (BR-19, BR-18, §3.7).
- Imported values carry "imported from <source>, <date>, by <user>" and stay unverified (BR-5, BR-6); every batch is firm-scoped and attested by the Owner when it holds personal data (BR-16, BR-26).
- Permissions: `agent/import:run`, `:undo`, `:view-report` (Owner, Admin) (§10). Release 1 is people, clients, cases, key dates, documents, notes, opening balances; invoices history is Release 2 (AG18-D3).

## 4. Conflicts

1. **Two-step sign-in.** Ours: required for every staff member (C line 112, ROWS PF-4, Lead B decision "industry standard, P-486"). Team: one firm switch, default OFF; staff may opt in; the earlier firm levels were removed (AG-16-24 l.899, AG-16 §3.6 l.625; AG-16-23 withdrawn). The team's requirement is the first source of truth and was an explicit product-owner decision. Our rule is stricter and costs a switch-free build. Founder needed: yes, one yes/no (keep required for month one as a deliberate deviation, or follow the switch default OFF). Sample/Unconfirmed: no counsel for any legal angle.
2. **Trust account.** Ours: trust money by card waits for counsel; trust lines recorded as offline (C D-3, line 107). Team: a second trust payment account connects the same hosted way after a one-time Owner question (AG-16-14, -77, -78). Not truly opposed: we defer a decided feature, and the deferral rests on a legal point marked Unconfirmed: no counsel. Founder needed: already open as D-3; add the AG-16 card ids to that decision.
3. **Timeline as write or read.** Ours (ROWS FO-2) says each change writes a timeline entry. Team: the timeline writes nothing, read-time projection (AG-13 BR-16 l.615, AG-13-30). Founder not needed; wording fix in ROWS FO-2 and C line 52.
4. **Who owns audit and the timeline.** Requirements make both shared subsystems (AG-13 §1 "Every other module composes AG-13", l.248-250). C places them inside our fenced folders and C line 8 says nothing about an audit table. Lead 37 holds the shared records; evidence is C line 7 (fences) against AG-13 §3.0. Founder not needed; Lead 37 and Lead B settle it, then record it.
5. **Audit failure behaviour.** Ours: the event is written in the same database transaction (C line 18); SA-11-7 and BR-5 say a failed audit write must not roll back the user's action but be retried and alarmed. Different design, both defensible; our approach is stricter for events. Founder not needed; record the choice.
6. **Everyone sees all as a boolean.** Requirement uses per-role keys (AG-13-67, AG-2 l.3387). Our boolean is a thin stand-in; the default is the same. Founder not needed.
7. **Outside items not found in the team's text.** Cognito and BoldSign are not named anywhere in AG-13, AG-16, AG-18 or SA-11 (searched, no hit). They are our own picks (P-553); the team's identity and e-sign behaviour are in AG-01 and AG-05, outside this trace. Not a conflict by itself.
