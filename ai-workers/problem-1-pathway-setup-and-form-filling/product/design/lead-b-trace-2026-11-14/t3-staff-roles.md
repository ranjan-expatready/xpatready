## 4. Conflicts

1. **Roles: our four versus the requirement's six.** Evidence: S6 SA-6-31 (L665), AG-1-17 (A1 L583) and SA-6-27 (L661) name six roles and say there is no licensed-representative role; S6 L428 and A1 L146 say licensed is a person who entered a licence on their own profile. Our REPRESENTATIVE role and its licence-bearing grant do not exist there. Settled by the requirement's own decisions (SA6-D2, SA6-D9): no founder needed. Proposal for Lead B: seed the requirement's role names (Owner, Admin, Case Manager, Case Officer; the other two come later); our REPRESENTATIVE becomes Case Manager (the holder of sign-off keys); licence moves to the person (section 2). Open for Lead B: our ADMIN versus OWNER, both mapping to duty `firm_admin`, matches the requirement only if Admin lacks the six Owner-only actions.
2. **Permission keys instead of role checks.** Evidence: S6 BR-1, BR-9, SA-6-14 (L644), SA-6-15, AG-1-19 and the matrix in S6 section 10b (L1043 onward), Appendix C (L1723). Fact: the requirement checks only keys; roles only bundle keys; the guard is fail-closed. Judgment: yes, use keys. Month one needs few: `agent/staff:manage` (invite, edit, deactivate; Owner and Admin, S6 L1063), `agent/role:assign` (S6 L1058), `agent/security-policy:manage` (S6 L1060), `agent/firm-credential:manage` (S6 L1053), `agent/settings:list`, and the prospect keys the front office uses (for example `agent/prospect:edit`, `:create`, `:assign`, `:convert`, `:lose`, `:log-call`, `:show`, `:view.own`, `:view.all`; S6 L1065-1088). Cost: a seed table `role -> keys` copied from S6 section 10b, and each route declares a key. This also makes FO-2's "a case officer sees Unassigned and own items" a key test (`view.own` versus `view.all`, BR-17) rather than a role test. No founder needed: the requirement already decides it. Note: S6 marks several prospect keys "NEW"/"exists unverified" in their code status column, so key names there are the target, not the team's code.
3. **Two-step sign-in: required (ours) versus optional (requirement).** Evidence: A1 AG-1-40 (L639), AG01-3/3a (L176): optional, off by default; the firm Owner (or Admin with `agent/security-policy:manage`) may switch on "Require two-step sign-in for all staff", and the built default being mandatory was to be changed. Mandatory without a switch applies only to ExpatReady's own staff (S7 BR-2, L601). SA3-D18 (A1 L165) says requiring it for every staff member is part of the security floor on every plan. No official source I read settles the default. Founder needed: yes, one question (the requirement records it as a founder-level product decision). Lead B recommendation to put: keep mandatory for firm staff in month one (it is stricter, simpler, and Cognito supports it per pool), but store it as the per-firm switch `mandatory` (default true for new firms) so the requirement's switch stays possible. Until answered, build the switch, default on.
4. **Invitation lifetime: 7 days (ours) versus 72 hours (requirement).** Evidence: A1 Open question 7 (L1142: built default 72 hours, "keep 72 hours and state it"); S7 L377 (72 hours, deploy configuration). Judgment: low stakes; adopt 72 hours as a setting with resend (A1 AG-1-12) so an expired link costs one click. No founder needed unless Lead B wants 7 days; then record it as a stated deviation.
5. **Platform support role and our verify route.** Evidence: S7 Appendix A Firms rows (L869-880): Support has read-only list, health and support sessions; creating firms, resending owner invitation and stalled set-ups belong to Platform Owner and Admin. Nothing in SA-7 gives any role a licence verification. Judgment: PF-5's "our support staff only" is not a role the requirement gives that duty to. Fix: name the duty (`system/firm:...` key chosen by Lead B and flagged as new) and give it to Platform Owner and Admin, or confirm with the requirement owner that Support should hold it. Founder or the requirement owner decides; I found no source settling which platform role should hold it.
6. **Licence check before a firm goes active (D-4).** Evidence: A1 AG1-D19 (L146): ExpatReady "never checks, tracks, reminds about, warns about or acts on" licence validity and never verifies it against any register; "We are not monitoring RCIC licences" (product owner, 5 Oct, A1 L142). Our D-4 and PF-5 add a human licence check by our staff before ACTIVE. Fact: the requirement's decision was about monitoring and permissions; a one-time onboarding check by hand is not named either way. Founder needed: yes, already D-4; add that AG1-D19 points the other way, so the founder decides knowingly. Non-blocking for staff roles (PF-5 only).
7. **Last-owner status code and wording.** Evidence: SA-6-44, AG-1-22 say 403 and a fixed message; ours 409. Best practice does not settle it (both are used); the requirement is the first source. Follow the requirement; no founder.
8. **Admin and Owner billing split (for PF-5, noted only).** Evidence: S6 L424 and Appendix C (L1735 area): Admin may update the card and pay an overdue bill; upgrades, billing details, downgrade and cancellation are Owner-only; Admin, Owner and Billing / Finance view subscription invoices. PF-5 makes checkout and portal "owner only". Judgment: the portal route (card update) should also allow Admin. No founder needed.
