## What this means for the software (plain words, Unconfirmed: no counsel)

- Build the retainer warning list from CODE s. 24(3)(a)-(z): 26 content items, plus the s. 24(2) steps (confirm client details, send a draft first, check for an earlier representative). Show each as a checklist line with the section number.
- A separate, simpler consultation agreement (s. 23, five items) is needed before the first consultation, even if free. Offer it as a second template.
- Block "ready to sign" until the draft has been sent to the client (s. 24(2)(b)); record the date it was sent. Keep the signed copy and record that the client received one (s. 24(4)).
- Store the signed agreement and every amendment; an amendment must be agreed in writing (s. 24(5)). Extra fees or disbursements over the estimate need the client's written agreement (s. 31(3)), so build that approval as a step.
- Money in advance (any "advance payment") must go to a client account at a College-approved bank. The software should flag any advance payment that is not tied to a client account deposit, issue a receipt showing the amount (and, per the Guide, date and services), and keep a separate deposit/withdrawal ledger per client.
- Withdrawals from trust: require an invoice first (services or disbursements already done), then the withdrawal within 30 days of the invoice. Issue a receipt naming the invoice for every payment. Guide-only items (client approval of the invoice, 14-business-day deposit) should be shown as "College guidance", not as law.
- On file closure, prompt the 30-day list: return documents, accounting of funds, final invoice, refund of trust money above the invoice.
- Keep fee, refund policy, and trust-error reporting fields. A trust error must be reported to the College within 30 days (s. 39(f)).
- Do not claim a reconciliation frequency, a record-retention period, or electronic-payment/card-processor rules. They were not confirmed from an official page. Ask the immigration expert or the College for By-law 2021-2 and the Client Account Regulation before building those parts.
- Re-read the Code at each release: the page is "current to 2026-10-19", and the College's 2026 regulations (SOR/2026-68) are new; the Interpretation Guide dates from 2022.
