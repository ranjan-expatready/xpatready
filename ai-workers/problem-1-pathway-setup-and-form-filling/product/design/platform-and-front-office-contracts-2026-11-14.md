## 0. Ground rules for both areas

1. **Fenced folders, new files only.** API: `prototype/studio/api/src/platform/` and `api/src/front-office/`, each registered by its own file in `api/src/features/` (the existing seam: no edit to `main.ts`). Web: `web/src/modules/platform/` and `web/src/modules/front-office/`, registered through the web seam of PF-1 (no further edit to `App.tsx`). Schema: `platform.prisma` and `front-office.prisma` in the folder the database-file split (DB-SPLIT, Lead 37) creates; one file per area, owned by its module; two module schema rows may run at once when each touches only its own area file and migration folder, and the second to merge re-stamps its migration and shows `migrate diff` empty (P-559 (2)). Tests: `tests/check_pf*_…` and `tests/check_fo*_…`. Nothing in existing case, forms or runtime code without Lead 37's OK; the one-line hooks this needs are listed in section 5.
2. **Every table carries `firm_id`** (uuid, not null, indexed first) except the plan catalogue and the event log's delivery columns. Every read and write goes through one helper, `firmScope(firmId)`, and every row ships a cross-firm test (firm A cannot read, list, change or guess firm B's rows: 404, never 403, so ids leak nothing). The database-enforced walls (row rules) are Lead 37's design; these tables are written so those rules can be added without a data change.
3. **Outside services sit behind our own ports**, one interface per service with two adapters: a `fake` adapter (in-process, deterministic, used by every CI check, no network) and the real adapter in the service's test mode. Live keys never reach CI or a lane; a real-adapter check runs only on the Mac or the test environment with the founder's test-mode keys (P-558 (5): he opens the accounts).
   * `IdentityPort` → Amazon Cognito (Canadian region) · `local` stand-in until the account exists.
   * `BillingPort` → Stripe Billing (firm subscriptions to us).
   * `ClientPaymentsPort` → Stripe Connect (client payments into the **firm's own** Stripe account; we never hold client money).
   * `ESignPort` → BoldSign sandbox (Canadian region).
   * `DocumentRenderPort` → docxtemplater (in-process; no service).
   * `CalendarInvitePort` → calendar invite files (`.ics`) by email; live Google/Outlook sync later.
   * `MailPort` → the existing demo inbox in the reference app; Amazon SES Canada in the test environment.
4. **Webhooks** (Stripe, BoldSign) land on `/api/hooks/<service>`: signature checked first (refused 400 without it), stored once in `webhook_receipt` by the service's own event id (a repeat is answered 200 and ignored), then handled; handling never calls the service back inside the request.
5. **Events to the case side** go through one transactional outbox, `module_event` (written in the same database transaction as the change that causes it). Shape: `{ id (uuid), firm_id, type, version, occurred_at, subject_id, payload (json), delivered_at }`. The case side reads with `GET /api/platform/events?after=<id>` (service-to-service) or an in-process subscriber; delivery is at least once, consumers are idempotent by `id`. Payloads carry ids and the facts the consumer needs, never secrets or card data.
6. **Public doors** (firm sign-up, enquiry form, booking page, signing return, payment return) are marked open on the API guard (`OpenRead`/an explicit allow-list line, P-348 (4)), rate limited per IP and per firm, and carry no firm data beyond what the visitor typed. They are added to the outside security test's scope.
7. **Words** (PRODUCT.md style, and AG-02's where it names a thing): firm, staff member, owner, representative (licensed), case officer, lead, prospect, stage, Engage, Take it, Unassigned leads, consult, retainer agreement, signing, invoice, payment. "Client" once the prospect is won.

## 1. Platform modules

### 1.1 Firms and plans (`platform/firms`)
* **Job:** a firm signs up, picks a plan, starts a subscription, keeps its profile (name, office, licence numbers of its representatives, branding), and is suspended for non-payment or closed with a data download.
* **Tables:** `platform_firm` (id, key = today's `firmKey`, **the existing Sample firms keep their keys `sample-firm` and `sample-solo`** so cases and platform share one firm identity (P-559 (4)), name, status `PENDING_VERIFICATION | ACTIVE | PAST_DUE | SUSPENDED | CLOSED`, office, branding, created_at) · `platform_plan` (catalogue, no firm_id: code, name, seats, monthly and yearly Sample prices, Stripe price ids, active) · `platform_subscription` (firm_id, plan, Stripe customer and subscription ids, status, current period end, seats) · `platform_firm_verification` (firm_id, licence body and number given, checked_by, checked_at, outcome) · `webhook_receipt` (service, event id, received_at, handled_at, outcome) · `module_event` (section 0.5).
* **API:** `POST /api/public/firms` (sign-up: firm name, owner's email; creates the firm `PENDING_VERIFICATION` and the owner's invitation) · `GET /api/public/plans` · `POST /api/platform/billing/checkout` (owner: plan → Stripe Checkout URL) · `POST /api/platform/billing/portal` (owner: Stripe customer portal URL) · `GET|PATCH /api/platform/firm` (owner, admin) · `POST /api/hooks/stripe` · `POST /api/platform/firms/:id/verify` (our support staff only: the licence check).
* **Events out:** `firm.created`, `firm.activated`, `firm.subscription_changed` (plan, status), `firm.suspended`, `firm.closed`.
* **Reads from others:** nothing from the case side.
* **Rules:** a firm is usable for cases only when `ACTIVE` (subscription active or trialing **and** the licence check passed: the check is recommended, founder decision D-4); `PAST_DUE` keeps working with a banner for a grace period (Sample: 14 days), then `SUSPENDED` (sign-in works, read-only, export works).

### 1.2 Staff, sign-in and roles (`platform/identity`)
* **Job:** staff sign in (two-step sign-in required for every staff member), are invited, given roles, deactivated with instant lock-out; a firm can never lose its last owner.
* **Tables:** `platform_staff` (firm_id, id, email, names, identity subject (Cognito `sub` or local id), `actor_id` (the existing simulated id, e.g. `user-R`, for the seeded Sample staff; unique when present: the identity join's bridge), status `INVITED | ACTIVE | DEACTIVATED`, last_sign_in_at) · `platform_role_grant` (firm_id, staff_id, role `OWNER | ADMIN | REPRESENTATIVE | CASE_OFFICER`, licence body/category/number for a representative, granted_by, granted_at, ended_at) · `platform_invitation` (firm_id, email, roles, token hash, expires_at, accepted_at) · `platform_session_revocation` (firm_id, staff_id, revoked_at: tokens issued before it are refused).
* **API:** `GET /api/platform/me` → `{ staffId, firmId, firmKey, roles, duties }` · `POST /api/platform/staff/invitations` · `POST /api/public/invitations/:token/accept` · `PATCH /api/platform/staff/:id/roles` · `POST /api/platform/staff/:id/deactivate` · local stand-in only: `POST /api/platform/session/local` (refused unless `PLATFORM_IDENTITY=local`, and never in the test environment).
* **Guard:** one Nest guard reads the bearer token (Cognito access token, verified against the pool's keys; or the local stand-in's signed token), loads the staff member and their grants, refuses a deactivated member or a revoked session at once, and puts `{ staffId, firmId, roles }` on the request. Every platform and front-office route declares the roles it allows.
* **Roles to today's duties:** `REPRESENTATIVE` → `represent`, `CASE_OFFICER` → `case`, `OWNER`/`ADMIN` → `firm_admin` (the `Duty` words of `engine/runtime/team.ts`). This is what lets `firms.ts` later read firms from the database in the same shape (Lead 37's join, section 3).
* **Events out:** `staff.joined`, `staff.roles_changed`, `staff.deactivated`.
* **Rules:** last-owner guard (removing or deactivating the only active owner is refused 409); a representative needs a licence body and number; invitations expire (Sample: 7 days) and are single use.

### 1.3 Walking skeleton (`platform/skeleton`)
* **Job:** the app builds into one container image, starts against PostgreSQL, migrates, answers `/api/health`, and is deployed by CI to a test environment on every merge to `main` that touches the app; a smoke journey (health → local or Cognito sign-in → `GET /api/platform/me`) proves each deploy.
* **Parts:** `Dockerfile` and a compose file for a local test environment (no account needed) · a path-filtered CI job that builds the image and runs the smoke journey against compose · later, the Canadian cloud deploy (infrastructure as code; tool chosen in PF-2's technology gate) once the founder's account exists.
* **No events, no tables.**

## 2. Front-office modules (enquiry to payment)

### 2.1 Leads, prospects and the pipeline (`front-office/sales`)
**Follows the team's requirement AG-02 (Leads, prospects and sales, v2.6, 28 Oct; name-free copy in `domain/requirements-2026-11-02/`), thin for month one (P-553 (2)).** Its model, kept: a **lead** is neutral (a person who showed interest: source, owner or none, "Interested in", state `OPEN | ENGAGED | CLOSED`; no stage, no type, no value; BR-22). **Engage** is one staff click that makes a **prospect** on the pipeline, with a stage, an owner and an optional value. A prospect is **WON** once, by an executed retainer (FO-6), "Signed offline" or a person's "Convert to client"; front office never opens a case by itself (AG-02 A1 (5)).
* **Job:** every door (month one: the firm's web form and "Add a lead" by hand) runs one arrival path that writes a receipt, the person, the lead and the consent at once and flags a possible match; a lead waits in **Unassigned leads** until someone clicks **Take it** or **Assign to…**; staff log calls and notes, close a lead with a reason, or **Engage** it; prospects move on the board with a cause on every move; lost needs a reason.
* **Tables:** `fo_person` (firm_id, names, emails, phones (E.164), language, time zone; the AG-02 person record for month one; the shared person record is Lead 37's join) · `fo_arrival_receipt` (firm_id, door `WEB_FORM | MANUAL`, submission id (unique per form: a repeat post is one lead), received_at, raw fields, ip hash, page and campaign fields) · `fo_lead` (firm_id, number `L-nnnnn` per firm, person_id, source, interested_in, owner_staff_id (empty = Unassigned), state, close reason and note, first response at/by/kind, possible_match_person_id) · `fo_prospect` (firm_id, number `P-nnnnn`, lead_id, person_id, type `INDIVIDUAL` (Corporate later), stage_id, owner_staff_id, value, close date, status by stage kind, won_via `E_SIGNED | SIGNED_OFFLINE | CONVERTED`, lost reason and note) · `fo_stage` (firm_id, pipeline `INDIVIDUAL`, key, label, order, kind `ACTIVE | WON | LOST | NURTURE`; seeded with AG-02's eight: Engaged, Qualified, Consult, Fees discussed, Retainer sent, Won – client, Lost, Nurture) · `fo_reason` (firm_id, kind `LEAD_CLOSE | PROSPECT_LOST`, label, active; Sample lists) · `fo_call` (firm_id, person_id, lead_id or prospect_id, direction `IN | OUT`, outcome (AG-2-37a's seven: Connected, No answer, Left voicemail, Busy, Wrong number, Callback requested, Not in service), seconds, notes, started_at, by) · `fo_note` (firm_id, person_id, context, text, by, at) · `fo_stage_move` (firm_id, prospect_id, from, to, cause `PERSON | BOOKING | RETAINER_SENT | RETAINER_EXECUTED | SIGNED_OFFLINE | CONVERT`, by, at) · `fo_consent` (firm_id, person_id, purpose, wording version, given_at, withdrawn_at, evidence) · `fo_web_form` (firm_id, name, token, allowed sites, consent wording, thank-you text, on/off; fields fixed in month one: names, email, phone, "Interested in", message, consent tick).
* **API:** `POST /api/public/forms/:token/submissions` (open door: form token, allowed site, honeypot, rate limit, size limits; never says whether the person is known) · `GET /api/front-office/leads?view=unassigned|mine|all` · `POST /api/front-office/leads` (by hand: checks for a known person before saving) · `GET /api/front-office/leads/:id` · `POST …/leads/:id/take|assign|close|reopen|engage` · `GET /api/front-office/prospects?view=board|list&owner=` · `GET /api/front-office/prospects/:id` · `POST …/prospects/:id/move|lose|convert` · `POST /api/front-office/people/:id/calls|notes` · `GET /api/front-office/people/:id/timeline` · `GET|PUT /api/front-office/web-form` · `GET /api/front-office/stages`, `GET|PUT /api/front-office/reasons` (owner, admin).
* **Rules (AG-02 cards named):** a repeat enquiry is a new lead, even while one is open; a possible match (same email or same normalised phone in the firm) is flagged, never linked or merged by itself (AG-2-84); the first logged call (any outcome, either direction) is the lead's first response (AG-2-37a); a call never moves a stage; the system never moves a prospect backward by itself, and every move names its cause (AG-2-26); lost needs a reason (AG-2-44); Engage makes the clicker the lead's owner when it has none (AG-2-75); in a one-user firm, Take it and Assign are hidden and the one user owns every lead (X-4).
* **Visibility (month one):** owners and admins see all; others see Unassigned leads, their own leads and prospects, and all when the firm's "everyone sees all" is on (Sample default on). AG-02's sharing setting is "Anyone takes it" only in month one; round robin later.
* **Events out:** `lead.created`, `lead.taken`, `lead.closed`, `lead.engaged` (= prospect created), `prospect.stage_moved` (with cause), `prospect.lost`, `prospect.won` (§2.6).
* **Later (not month one, AG-02 cards kept for then):** Corporate Engage and the Corporate pipeline, Link/Merge/Combine, Nurture with a date, round robin, follow-up runs, saved views and bulk actions, the form builder with several forms and languages, Meta/Zapier/WhatsApp/CSV doors, reports.

### 2.2 Consultations and booking (`front-office/booking`)
* **Job:** the firm sets consultation types (length, video/phone/in person, who can take it) and staff hours; a person (from a link in the reply email) or staff books a slot; nobody is ever double booked; both sides get a calendar invite; reschedule and cancel by link; the outcome is recorded (attended, no-show, "prepare retainer").
* **Tables:** `fo_consultation_type` (firm_id, name, minutes, mode, staff_ids, buffer minutes, active) · `fo_availability` (firm_id, staff_id, weekday, start, end, time zone) · `fo_time_off` (firm_id, staff_id, from, to) · `fo_booking` (firm_id, person_id, prospect_id (when open), staff_id, type_id, starts_at, ends_at (UTC), booker's time zone, status `BOOKED | RESCHEDULED | CANCELLED | ATTENDED | NO_SHOW`, manage token hash, outcome note) with a database exclusion constraint so one staff member's `BOOKED` ranges never overlap.
* **API:** `GET /api/public/firms/:firmKey/slots?type=&from=&to=&tz=` · `POST /api/public/firms/:firmKey/bookings` · `POST /api/public/bookings/:token/reschedule|cancel` · `GET /api/front-office/bookings?from=&to=&staff=` · `PATCH /api/front-office/bookings/:id` (outcome) · `GET|PUT /api/front-office/availability` · `GET|PUT /api/front-office/consultation-types`.
* **Events out:** `booking.made`, `booking.changed`, `booking.outcome` (with `prepare_retainer: true|false`). A booking moves the person's only open prospect forward to Consult with the cause shown, never backward (AG-2-26); a booking by a new person runs the arrival path (door `BOOKING`, AG-2-10).
* **Reads from others:** staff and roles (1.2); the person, lead and prospect (2.1).

### 2.3 Retainer agreements (`front-office/retainers`)
* **Job:** from the prospect and the consult, staff produce the firm's retainer agreement from its template (fees, services, the program, the representative, the client's details filled in), see a completeness warning for every required item missing, keep versions and addenda, and send it for signing.
* **Tables:** `fo_retainer_template` (firm_id, name, version, the `.docx` file (stored), the list of placeholders, active) · `fo_retainer` (firm_id, prospect_id, template version, representative staff_id, fee lines (amount, currency CAD, tax, trust or operating tag), status `DRAFT | SENT | SIGNED | VOID`, document file, document sha256, version, supersedes_id) · `fo_required_item` (catalogue, no firm_id: the items the regulator requires in a written retainer, each with its official source, page, quote and date).
* **Required items:** taken from the regulator's own published code and guidance (P-170: cited with page, quote and date) by the builder of FO-5; until counsel reviews the starter template, its wording is **Sample** and labelled so on screen.
* **API:** `GET|POST /api/front-office/retainer-templates` (owner, admin; `.docx` upload, placeholders read and checked) · `POST /api/front-office/prospects/:id/retainers` (draft from template) · `GET /api/front-office/retainers/:id` (with the completeness list) · `POST /api/front-office/retainers/:id/send` (→ 2.4) · `POST /api/front-office/retainers/:id/void`.
* **Events out:** `retainer.drafted`, `retainer.sent` (moves the prospect forward to Retainer sent, cause shown), `retainer.voided`.

### 2.4 Signing (`front-office/signing`)
* **Job:** the retainer goes to the person for e-signature (and the representative's counter-signature) through BoldSign's sandbox; single-use link and a one-time code by email; when all have signed, the sealed copy and the signing evidence are stored with us and the retainer is `SIGNED`.
* **Tables:** `fo_signature_request` (firm_id, retainer_id, provider `BOLDSIGN | FAKE`, provider document id, signers (role, email, signed_at), status `SENT | VIEWED | SIGNED | DECLINED | EXPIRED`, sealed file, evidence file, sha256 of each).
* **API:** created by `retainers/:id/send`; `GET /api/front-office/signature-requests/:id`; `POST /api/front-office/signature-requests/:id/remind|cancel`; `POST /api/hooks/boldsign`.
* **Events out:** `retainer.executed` when every signer has signed (retainer id, prospect id, representative staff id, fee lines, sealed file sha256); it makes the prospect WON with `won_via E_SIGNED` (§2.6).

### 2.5 Invoices and client payment (`front-office/payments`)
* **Job:** the firm connects its own Stripe account (Stripe-hosted onboarding); an invoice is raised from the retainer's fee lines; the client pays by a Stripe-hosted payment page into the firm's account; receipts go out; refunds and cancellations are recorded. Money tagged **trust** is not taken by card until counsel answers (founder decision D-3); such lines are recorded as paid offline by the firm.
* **Tables:** `fo_payment_account` (firm_id, Stripe connected account id, charges enabled, payouts enabled, updated_at) · `fo_invoice` (firm_id, person_id, prospect_id, retainer_id, number (per firm, per year), lines, tax, total, currency CAD, status `DRAFT | OPEN | PAID | VOID | REFUNDED`, due date) · `fo_payment` (firm_id, invoice_id, method `CARD | OFFLINE`, Stripe payment intent id, amount, received_at, recorded_by for offline, refund of).
* **API:** `POST /api/front-office/payment-account/onboarding` (owner: Stripe onboarding link) · `GET /api/front-office/payment-account` · `POST /api/front-office/retainers/:id/invoice` · `POST /api/front-office/invoices/:id/pay-link` · `POST /api/front-office/invoices/:id/offline-payment` · `POST /api/front-office/invoices/:id/void` · `POST /api/hooks/stripe` (shared with 1.1, routed by account).
* **Events out:** `invoice.issued`, `payment.received`, `payment.refunded`.

### 2.6 The prospect is won: hand-over to the case side
* A prospect becomes **WON once**, by `retainer.executed` (`won_via E_SIGNED`), a person's **Signed offline** (with a reason) or a person's **Convert to client** (confirm: "Retainer not signed. Work can start. Invoices will warn you, and the case will show a warning."). Front office then writes one **`prospect.won`** event.
* **Payload (P-559 (4)):** `{ firm_id, firm_key, prospect_id, lead_id, won_via, retainer_id (when signed), representative_staff_id, representative_actor_id, owner_staff_id, interested_in, answers: { "person.given_names", "person.family_name", "person.email", "person.phone_number" (country code and number, the shape BK-23 writes), "person.current_residence_country" }, consent_ids, sealed_retainer_sha256 }`. The person's facts travel as **canonical answer keys** so the case pre-fills them; the FO-8 builder checks each key against the field library on `main` before building (today `main` uses `person.email` in most places and `person.email_address` once: the library's key wins). `firm_key` is the same key cases carry (`Case.firmKey`, e.g. `sample-firm`); `representative_actor_id` is the staff member's existing simulated id while the identity join is open (e.g. `user-R`).
* **The case side (Lead 37) consumes it:** makes the client once (client number, file owner = the prospect's owner, AG-02 Ch 10), and shows the person who won it **"Open a case?"** with everything pre-filled; **a person decides** (AG-02 never opens a case by itself; the case opens on that click, AG-07). The combination is chosen by the representative in the case's first step, unless `interested_in` names a released combination, which is then offered first. The case side writes **`client.created`** `{ firm_id, prospect_id, client_id, client_number }` and, on the click, **`case.opened`** `{ firm_id, prospect_id, case_id, case_number }`; front office shows both on the person's timeline. Nothing the person typed is asked again.
* **Payment is not a condition of WON** (AG-02: money is AG-06's edge). An unpaid first invoice shows on the client and the case as a warning, never a block (EXPERIENCE §2.9).
