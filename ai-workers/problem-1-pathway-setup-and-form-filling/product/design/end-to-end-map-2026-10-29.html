One picture of the whole loop, without the browser extension (P-101/P-102: parked, back last for online fill only). A combination's forms are found by AI and fetched from canada.ca or uploaded by a person; AI turns them into proposed master data; people curate and release; a case answers every fact once — documents before questions (P-130); the engine fills every channel the filing uses. Where AI stands today: two AI jobs are proven — the form finder (which forms, which channel, IRCC's quote) and form-field mapping — both replayed at US$0. The next and biggest one, "Draft questions and documents" (one 7-step run producing every remaining master as proposals), is agreed and its step runner is on the forms branch; it runs live once on Visitor visa (about US$50, P-131) and is saved after. Still by rule, AI never creates: sources (the system fetches; a person uploads), immigration values (the expert), approvals and releases (two people), case facts and form values (client + officer), and keys (generated by the system).
The one rule that makes it safe: AI output exists only as a proposal beside its source passage, runs only on a person's click with its price stated (P-138), and every proposal gets a draft round + an independent checker round + the rules check — bulk-accept for High only (P-121). The engine writes to the draft library only what a person accepted; new masters pass a second person; releases publish them. So the shared library — the canonical master data every combination reuses — stays deduplicated, auditable and reversible, and Load sample replays the same proposals through the same path at US$0.
