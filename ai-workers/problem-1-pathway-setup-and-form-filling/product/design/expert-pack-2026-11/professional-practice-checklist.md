# Professional-practice checklist — CICC Code and IRCC representative rules, mapped to the app

**Not legal advice. Every legal reading here is Unconfirmed: no counsel.** The expert confirms how practice works; counsel confirms what the law requires (AIW-D64). How to mark each row: `README.md` in this folder.

The steps a licensed consultant takes on a file, from the College of Immigration and Citizenship Consultants' Code of Professional Conduct and IRCC's rules for representatives, and where the reference app supports each step today. "The app" is this reference build (`prototype/studio/`, `main` `8fc65d0b9`); the team's product builds the business screens (retainer, billing, CRM) from its own requirements, named where they cover a step.

Paid representatives are "consultants who are members in good standing of the College of Immigration and Citizenship Consultants (CICC); lawyers and paralegals who are members in good standing of a Canadian provincial or territorial law society, and notaries who are members in good standing of the Chambre des notaires du Québec" | The firm's representative carries a licence body and number (C6.11); one representative of record per case; IMM 5476 fills the membership ID from it | **No check that the licence is valid or in good standing** (the product never checks a licence: `ai-workers/requirements/counsel-pack.md` row 1). ## Gaps, ranked by impact (for the tech lead; not settled here)

1. **IMM 5476 on a superseded edition** (P2): blocks any real use of the representative form.
2. **No identity verification and no "other representative" check** (P3, P4), and the team's AG-05 non-goal reads against the Code: a founder decision (AGENTS rule 2), with counsel.
3. **No licence-standing check** (P1): an application with a paid representative not in good standing is returned.
4. **No agreement on record before work starts** (P5): the team's retainer covers it; the case should link to it.
5. **Records retention and client-file export** (P12), **status and copy to the client** (P14): the expert names what practice requires.
