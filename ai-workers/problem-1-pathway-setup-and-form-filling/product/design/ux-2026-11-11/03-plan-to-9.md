# UX plan: from 6.1 to "best for our purpose" (step 3 of A19 (k))

**From:** the UX planner, 11 Nov 2026, read-only (P-455 (5)). **For:** the tech lead, who turns rows into BACKLOG rows and lanes. **Baseline:** journey **6.1**, product 6.4, 68 screens inventoried (`02-baseline-score.md`, `inventory/INDEX.md`). **Style decided by the founder (P-473, 11 Nov), §2a.** **Stop line** (`01-rubric.md` §4): journey ≥ 8.5, no journey dimension below 8, D2-D5 and D9 at 9 or more, and the Bake's two walks passed with no help.

## 1. The shape of the plan in one paragraph

The baseline says the product is accessible and honest but **heavy**: too much on one page, too many pages for the client, numbers that disagree, and next steps that wait on someone else. So the order is: **(0) a tool that keeps the inventory true; (1) six shared pieces every screen reuses** (tokens, page header, next-step panel, error pattern, help pattern, a count that means one thing), most of them riding on Bake rows already planned; **(2) the two journeys that carry the weight**, the client's questionnaire and the case page, cut and ordered; **(3) set-up and polish.** Each wave ends with a re-score round by a fresh reader. 27 rows, one PR each (UX-0 and UX-13 are several), about **38-46 builder sessions**: **9 rows in or beside the Bake**, the rest a counted **M4 Track U** for the Lead's next milestone plan (P-450).

## 2. Rules every row follows

* **One row = one PR**, branch from `origin/main`, under ~800 lines, the existing layers (STANDARDS §2); the screens lane only touches `web/src/`, forms and runtime rows say which files they own.
* **Reuse first:** a row that needs a piece from wave 1 waits for it; no screen builds its own header, next step, error or help.
* **"Done when" has two parts, always:** (a) **the measure** from the crawl (UX-0's `uxCheck`), counted on the named screens and on the baseline's own data; (b) **a Playwright test at 1440 and 375 px with axe** (`@axe-core` 0 serious/critical) that fails on the old page and passes on the new. Plus the row's behaviour tests as STANDARDS §7 asks.
* **No immigration value is invented**: a country list, a reason or a wording that is a value comes from IRCC with page, quote and date or is labelled Sample (P-170).
* **Nothing below goes against a decision.** Where a row meets one, it says so: P-296 and P-299 (no bulk accept of new fields), P-240 (Sample marked), P-235 (PQR carry-over), P-447 (no new capabilities in the Bake).

## 3. The rows

**Lane:** S = screens (`web/src`), F = forms (samples, form setup, wording content), R = runtime (`engine/`, `api/src`), P = pipeline (tools, CI), Q = qa. **Where:** **Bake** = inside an existing Bake row or its reserve (no new count); **Bake+** = small, on the journey, proposed into the Bake's cap-walk set (P-447 (3)); **M4** = M4 Track U. **Expected** = the journey dimension(s) it moves, from → to, on the named screens.
