# D8: how a family files a Study permit (outside Canada), and its fees (Lead 20, series 2; 11 Nov 2026; P-486)

Ruled by the Lead under P-486 from official pages, read 11 Nov 2026. Quotes checked on the live pages by the Lead (fees page fetched directly; Guide 5269 through the fetch tool).

| Point | Official answer, plain words | Source · quote · date |
|---|---|---|
| One application or several? | Each family member files **their own application**; they may be submitted together, with one payment receipt for the total. | Guide 5269 (page details 2026-10-06), https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/application/application-forms-guides/guide-5269-applying-study-permit-outside-canada.html · "Family members must complete their own application forms." · read 11 Nov 2026 |
| A child who will study | Gets **their own study permit**, applied for with the parent's. | Guide 5269 · "You must apply for their study permit at the same time as your own." · read 11 Nov 2026 |
| Study permit fee | CAD 150 per person. | IRCC fee list, https://ircc.canada.ca/english/information/fees/fees.asp · "Study permit (including extensions) – per person 150.00" · read 11 Nov 2026 |
| Work permit (spouse, when eligible for an open work permit) | CAD 155 plus the open work permit holder fee CAD 100. | Fee list · "Work permit (including extensions) – per person 155.00"; "Open work permit holder 100.00" · read 11 Nov 2026 |
| Biometrics | CAD 85 per person, with a family maximum when applying at the same time. | Fee list · "Biometrics – per individual 85.00" · read 11 Nov 2026 |
| Visitor visa (a family member who neither studies nor works) | The visitor visa fee per person (the same fee for single or multiple entry). | Fee list · "The same fee applies to both types of visas." · read 11 Nov 2026 |

**Ruling (P-486):** the seeded `child: on_principal` (a child charged the study-permit fee on the principal's application, even when not studying) has no official basis. Each person's fee follows **the document that person applies for**: study permit (a child who studies), work permit + open work permit holder fee (an eligible spouse), visitor visa or visitor record (anyone else), plus biometrics per person up to the family maximum.
