One fact, entered once, fills every form.

The Pathway Configurator reference build turns an immigration pathway into data. A content publisher sets a pathway up once. After that, every case is asked each fact once, and its government forms are filled, read back and checked.

Configured, not coded

Forms, questions, documents and rules of a pathway are set in screens and frozen into a release that a second person approves. 93 of 112 capabilities Works.

Asked once

One answer per person per fact, never overwritten. It fills every box mapped to that fact, on every form, for every pathway.

Fills real IRCC PDFs, and proves it

Writes IRCC's own PDF forms (the XFA kind), reads every box back and checks the signatures. The team's build refuses XFA forms (28 Oct capture).

AI drafts, people decide

AI maps form boxes and drafts questions; a person approves each one. Every paid run shows its price first. Product AI spend so far: US$82.65 over 136 calls.

Built by an AI delivery line

791 merged pull requests in about three weeks. Only an independent QA lane may mark a capability Works, after its own re-run.

How configurable, flexible and scalable is it?

Independent audit of the code, 10 Nov 2026. Score out of 10: filled = today, dashed = target after M4. Evidence in appendix E.

Configurable
6 → 8

Held back by: seven rule tables (fees, box gates, roles and more) live in files, outside the screens and the release.

Lift: move them into library screens, frozen in the release.

Flexible
5 → 8

Held back by: the case side has Visitor-visa-only guards and a fixed family shape.

Lift: five general mechanisms, then delete the guards.

Scalable
5 → 8

Held back by: one PDF process per form, an in-memory queue, local disk, no load test.

Lift: durable job queue, object storage, a cache, a measured run at 1,000 cases.

Acid test: a full Study permit case tomorrow needs 7 screen steps by a content publisher plus 5 to 8 repository edits by an engineer. The target is zero edits.

Not yet proven

  • Content is Sample. No immigration expert has confirmed it; no answer key is signed.
  • Adobe Validate has not passed on a complete form filled by the app (capability C7.2 is Partial).
  • Only Visitor visa runs a full case. On other pathways fees, checklists and linked applications still need code.
  • No real users, hosted environment or load test. Tenancy, sign-in and hosting are the team platform's.
  • Legal points are "Unconfirmed: no counsel", including whether IRCC's terms allow a portal extension.

M4 · Track AAny pathway, zero code

  • Five general mechanisms on the case side: an application filed together with its own fees; an extra filing that depends on an answer; a checklist line that can be waived with a reason; one that never can; an answer sheet for forms IRCC takes only online.
  • Seven rule files move into screens and into the release.
  • Proof: a second pathway family runs a full case with no engineer.

M4 · Track BChrome extension for the IRCC portal

  • Six rows: read a screen's structure and match it with AI; detect a changed screen; fill with the representative's check per screen; a navigation helper that never holds a password; a vision fallback with masking; screen maps handed over for four forms.
  • Before any real use: a counsel check on IRCC's terms.
