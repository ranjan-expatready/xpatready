## 2. How it was run

1. A fresh isolated copy (`isolated.sh ev_bk13 4460`), started from this branch's files.
2. Demo › Reset, then Demo › Load the configured checkpoint (`POST /api/demo/load {sample: configured}`, ~2 min): it releases the Study permit with the other five (row 10).
3. `POST /api/demo/sample-case {combinationId: sp.outside}` (BK-12): the Study permit's own Sample case, SAMPLE-SP-STUDENT-1, answered from `samples/sample-student-answers.json`, then a copy of every form it files.
4. As the representative: `GET /api/runtime/cases/:id/forms`, `/prepare`, `/filings`, `/checklist`; as the case officer, `/questionnaire?mode=agent`.
5. As the case officer, the IMM 5707 parents answered and confirmed through the product's own routes (`PUT …/answers/:subject/parent_1/:fact`, `POST …/sections/:section/confirm` with the relative's subject id), then IMM 5707 generated again.
6. Run three times: on `main`'s data before any change (baseline), after the fee, document and answer data, and a final check with every data change.

Script: `bash dryrun.sh <out> 4460 xr_studio_ev_bk13` (kept in the session's scratchpad; the steps are 1-4 above).
