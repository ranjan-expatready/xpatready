## 1. In one paragraph

The Bake makes what already works feel like one product and stop depending on Visitor-visa-only code. On the screen side (capped at 24 builder sessions, P-447): one frame around every screen with a way back from everywhere, one sign-in where you pick your role with hand-off buttons where the work changes hands, a visible arrow on every fold-out section, a start page with three starting points, and the open Major findings that sit on the main journey. On the engine side (~16 sessions, P-448): the remaining Visitor-visa-only code becomes general rules read from data, so a new pathway is content, not code. **We know it is done when two tests pass:** (a) one person walks the whole journey (set up, release, family case, client answers, representative review, forms filled) with no new login, no dead end and no help, first qa's fresh reader, then the co-founder cold, with 0 open Majors on the journey; (b) Study permit reaches filled, checked forms with data and release changes only and no change to engine or screen code (P-449). It is an internal, production-grade tool the team reuses for its SaaS product, not a SaaS launch: no sign-up, no multi-tenant work (P-450, P-335, P-357). No new capabilities (P-447 (4)).
