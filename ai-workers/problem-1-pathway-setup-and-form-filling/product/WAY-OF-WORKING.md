# How we work (lean model, P-224, from 1 Nov night)

The one page a builder reads; it replaces the swarm (P-220), the team board and the where-checks-run rows (P-181…P-215). Why (1 Nov): 81–85% of PR time was queue wait, 37% of batches failed, 39% of commits were records.

## Roles
| Role | Who | Owns |
|---|---|---|
| **Product owner** | the founder | direction, product decisions, money |
| **Tech lead** (no app code) | one Lead chat on the Mac (`/lead`) | shapes rows ahead, answers questions in batches, reads diffs touching migrations, money or permissions, updates records daily, talks to the founder |
| **Builders** | cloud sessions the tech lead starts, one fresh per row (`/next <area>`); Mac only for standbys he names while the Mac has room (P-344 (3)) | one row each, end to end |
| **Reviewer** (never builds) | fresh agent `screen-reviewer`; plus `screen-breaker` for screens, money, permissions, schema | Approve or Block, once per PR |
| **qa** (never fixes) | one session (`/qa <mode>`) | tests `main` like a person; `qa/BUGS.md`, `qa/CAPABILITIES.md` |
| **Evidence** | Mac session, when needed | official forms, Adobe checks, `sources/`, `code/` |
| **Strategy desk** (never builds, never records) | one Fable session at medium effort (P-511, P-514, P-521); opened and reopened by the Lead with the `strategy-desk` skill | strategy, integration plans, guides, contract drafts, plain-words answers; read-only; files privately in its notes folder; talks to the founder and to the current Lead directly |
| **Integration builder** (P-521) | one cloud session the Lead starts, under the Lead, on the founder's private synced copy only (P-520); a fresh one per row | the hands-on integration work: the nightly sync, their checks green, capability by capability to the desk's contract; never our records; its rules travel in its brief and in a notes file inside the founder's folder of the copy |
| **Outside check** | Devin, weekly | report-only findings into `inbox/` |

The Mac runs the tech lead, the :4400 demo, qa and at most two builders with test copies (standbys the tech lead names, P-344 (3), P-347), plus evidence work on request. No fixed builder count: the tech lead adds builders while batch failures stay under 10% and approved-to-merged under 1 hour, and removes them when either breaks. Areas (screens · forms and AI · runtime · evidence) are file ownership; row owners are at the top of `BACKLOG.md`. Ports: tech lead 4420–4429 · runtime 4430–4439 · screens 4440–4449 · forms 4450–4459 · qa 4470–4479. The demo is :4400 and is never used for tests.

## The loop, one row at a time
1. **Ready.** The row has its acceptance test, its text ("Done when") and every founder decision it needs answered; no build on an open question. A new screen part needs a board and the founder's early look, or the tech lead's approval after an independent challenger when there is no board (P-223 (4); skill **screen-concept**).
2. **Build.** Own worktree, own branch from `origin/main`, own files. Read → plan in the PR body → implement. Never build on an unmerged PR; split anything bigger than a row (unfinished work merges switched off).
3. **Check.** Locally `bash P/tools/run-suites.sh --changed origin/main..HEAD` exits 0 (judge by the exit code, not the output). The PR is a draft until the reviewer approves: each step is pushed to it (P-344) and a draft push runs only the guard; the PR's own GitHub check runs its affected suites once, when it is marked ready (P-371).
4. **Review.** The reviewer agent once, at the end, on the exact commit; the breaker too for screens, money, permissions, schema. Fix every Blocker and Major with a fail test; fix the Minors or list them in the PR.
5. **Merge.** Add the label `review: approved` once the reviewer approves the current commit: Mergify queues it and tests it with the latest main before merging. Merge `origin/main` in only when GitHub shows a conflict (P-270), then re-run the touched suites and push. Any later push needs a new review and the label again: the label guard (`.github/workflows/label-guard.yml`, founder 6 Nov) removes `review: approved` on a push, and the moment the label is added (F4), unless a PR comment line `Reviewer: Approve <sha>` names the new head, or the push is a pure merge of main touching none of the PR's files (P-318 (1)). Records-only PRs (only records under `product/`: .md, images, .txt, .html; `tools/records-only.sh`, the one definition) are labelled `records` by CI; labelled `review: approved` too, they go through Mergify's `records` queue, never a product batch (P-371, B119 #1147; code goes through the one `product` queue since D1, 8 Nov; never merged by hand while a batch is checking, P-306 (3)).
6. **Done.** The row's acceptance test is green on `main`, the demo shows it, and qa has passed it: no row counts as Works without qa (quality is never traded for speed).
   **qa per merge batch (P-277, amends P-250):** qa scores `main` after each merge batch, row by row; a Blocker or Major found there becomes a fix row that jumps the queue. qa still scores a PR's own head before merge only when it changes money (paid runs, ledgers), the schema or a migration, permissions, or adds a new screen. **Before P-277 (kept for those PRs):** qa scores a PR as soon as it carries `review: approved`, on the PR's own head commit while it waits in the queue, and comments its verdict on the PR. A Blocker or Major found there → qa comments, and the tech lead removes the label (the builder fixes it before merge). Minors become follow-up rows. On a paid-result PR qa also adds the label after the reviewer's Approve and its own Saved check on the same head (P-275). qa's scores go into one qa records PR, labelled like any other; it goes through Mergify's `records` queue, never a product batch (P-371, B119). qa re-checks on `main` only what the merge could change (the row's acceptance test after merge).

**No idle builder (P-259):** a builder holds a queue of at least two ready rows; after labelling a PR it starts its next row at once, without waiting for the tech lead, and comments "queue empty" on its last PR when it has none. **WIP (P-267):** right after labelling, a builder builds its next row on its own copy, pushes each step to a draft PR (P-344; a draft runs only the guard) and marks it ready only once its last PR has merged; meanwhile it also fixes its red checks and review findings. Open Majors in an area come before its new rows (P-228). **Finish before starting (P-231):** order by nearness to Works (qa only → one saved AI run → small gaps → new); no new capability while its area has a workable half-done one, unless it is on the strategic path; at most one PR in build and one in review per builder.

## Every PR
* **Title** names the capability IDs it moves (`forms: C11.15 — …`); the first line says "migration" or names a changed shared contract.
* **Body:** what and why, the plan, checks and results, before/after screenshots for anything visible, "On the demo:" (page at :4400 and what to try), the cost (US$0 unless approved), context share, "Questions" with your recommendation.
* Adds `qa/built/<ID>.md` per capability it finishes (one line: the PR and what a person can now do; P-221).
* **Builders never edit** `STATE.md`, `DECISIONS.md` or `qa/CAPABILITIES.md`; status goes in the PR body; the tech lead updates the records daily.
* Every business rule has a passing and a failing test; check labels name the capability IDs they prove (`C1.6: …`); `node P/tools/graph.mjs --check` passes; explicit `any` must not rise (`count-any.mjs`).
* A wait a test relies on fails loudly when it times out (no `.catch(() => undefined)` after a wait).
* Screens use skill **screen-build**. Minor bugs go in one PR per area per day.

## Shared files
Schema: one migration in flight at a time, read by the tech lead; two when both only add new tables or columns to different tables (P-228). One owner per table. `contracts/`: owned by the area that created each (`contracts/workspace.ts`: the forms-in row owner). `api/src/studio.ts`: additive only. Changes to any of them are named in the PR's first line.

## Where checks run
Every PR and every merge-queue batch runs its affected suites on 4 GitHub Linux machines at once (free: the repository is public); the Mac runner path is gone (D1, P-416, 8 Nov; off since 4 Nov). Each run tests the PR merged onto `main` as it is when the job starts (`tools/pr-base.sh`, D6), so a re-run after a fix on `main` tests that fix. The nightly run (GitHub, 20:23 UTC = 01:53 IST) runs every suite, and every capability qa marks Works runs its acceptance pack on its scoring data, pooled on up to 5 machines (D5). Gate B (part 1) runs the packs of the capabilities a PR names; job "packs" runs the packs the change touches (a changed pack's row if built, and every Works row whose pack reads a changed file), pooled on up to 5 machines, each on its scoring data (D2). A Partial row reached only by reading is listed and runs when the PR names it, with its green steps. Each merge-queue batch re-runs every Works row's pack, all milestones, pooled on up to 5 machines (D3, D4). Mergify checks up to 3 batches at once (up to 6 PRs each, 5-minute fill), in one `product` queue and the `records` queue.

**The flow (P-371, 6 Nov; measured 4-5 Nov: half the PR-check machine hours were thrown away, 181 runs cancelled by a newer push):** a push to a draft PR runs only the guard (push guard: excluded paths, PDFs, secrets, names; about a minute). The full check runs once, when the PR is marked ready; a ready head's check is never cancelled (a newer push waits for it). A label never starts a check. Its parts never stop each other, so **a red part is re-run alone**: on the run's page "Re-run failed jobs", or `gh run rerun <run-id> --failed` (the tech lead; a cloud lane's GitHub access cannot re-run, 403 on 6 Nov, so it asks in the PR); only when its log shows no code cause, never to roll the dice on a real failure, and only on the PR's newest run (an older one would replace a newer run still waiting). **Memory** (`tools/ci-memory.mjs`): a later run of the same PR on the same base skips the suites that passed at an earlier head and whose inputs the new changes do not touch (select-suites.mjs's rules; a shared file, the lockfile or ci.yml runs everything; the smoke set always runs again; once any app file changed, only suites that start no server, browser or database are remembered), shown as "remembered" in the table; a second "ready" or a reopen on a head already green on that base, with the same title and body, reports that verdict; a red head is always tested again. The merge queue runs its full set and the nightly run every suite on `main`, as before; a red night shows in the nightly run's summary and in `daily-numbers.sh`, and qa writes the `qa/BUGS.md` row (or a quarantine, P-205). Lanes never wait and watch CI.

**A test failing without a code cause is quarantined at once** in `tools/run-suites.sh` (`QUARANTINE`) with its evidence and fix row; nightly still runs it (P-205).

## Questions and stuck
Never wait for a human: put the question in the PR under "Questions" with your recommendation and carry on with what is not blocked; stuck for 10 minutes, ask rather than retry blindly. The tech lead answers in batches, product questions going to the founder one at a time. Disagree with evidence in the PR or in `screens/<item>/challenge.md`; never deviate silently.

## Spend tokens like money (founder, 1 Nov)
Every turn re-reads a session's whole context, so long, often-woken sessions are the biggest cost (1 Nov: the Lead at ~700k tokens woke every 4 minutes). **Sessions are short:** a builder session ends when its row merges (never left idle to be re-woken); the tech lead hands over at the first check after 200k tokens beyond its own start read (P-446; was P-314's 400k) with `STATE.md` as the resume point. **No polling:** the tech lead watches with event monitors and one 20-minute check, never minute-level sweeps. One reviewer run per PR at the end; read only the line ranges you need; run only your files' suites; helpers only for real parallel work; cheaper models only for searches where the model cannot change the result (P-303); one PR comment per change of state. **Models: quality first (P-303, ends P-278's pilot):** the reviewer, the breaker, qa's scoring and every judgment (rulings, verification, re-scoring) run on the best model (Opus, the default); Sonnet only for mechanical work where the model cannot change the result (finding files, running suites and reading exit codes, collecting logs). Never a cheaper model to save cost if it could lower quality. One row per builder session; after a push the reviewer re-reviews only the diff since its last approved commit; the breaker runs only for money, schema, permissions and new screens.

## How the tech lead handles a founder ask (founder, 31 Oct)
(1) **Restate** the ask and the decision it serves; if unclear, one question at a time, with a recommendation. (2) **Evidence first:** all our history, not one sample; outside claims from primary sources; evidence marked apart from estimate. (3) **Challenge:** say no, name knock-on effects on other screens, areas or rows, or "no clean solution as asked". (4) **Recommend** with trade-offs, in plain words. (5) **Act** on agreement for anything bigger than a small fix; record it in `DECISIONS.md` or the backlog.

**Delivery order (P-348):** by a person's journey, thin first; a row counts when a person can do it by screens and the next step can use its output (gate A). `RULES.md` has the current order.

Principles: don't follow blindly; evidence, not vanity numbers; simple, no quality cut; feature by feature, in users' order; small justified spend (about US$5–10 a month).

## Night playbook and the 3 Nov rules (founder, P-291; evidence: `reports/build-review-2026-11-03.html`)
* **Plain words:** name every capability by what it does, code after ("Bulk accept of AI suggestions (C3.2)"); say capability, never "row"; a technical word gets its functional meaning next to it.
* **Works means confirmed:** a capability is Works only when its acceptance pack is green on main **and** a session that did not score it re-ran it. Report "scored n, confirmed m". When a decision changes behaviour, its pack changes in the same PR.
* **Builders:** 6 at a time for the main goal; add one only while approval-to-merge stays under 1 hour and batch failures under 10%. Read-only testers do not count. Each brief names the builder's next two capabilities in its area, so a lane keeps going after its PR is labelled without a new session.
* **Start sessions only in a founder-typed turn**, all of them at once, before the founder leaves; a timed check or a message cannot start sessions (it can only leave a card).
* **Every 30-minute check:** run `tools/flow-watch.sh` and act on every RED; read every open PR's new comments and answer "Question for the tech lead" on the PR; a lane with no push in 90 minutes gets "continue" plus its next step, and if it still does not move by the next check, its capability goes to another idle lane with a stand-down message to the first; records PRs go through Mergify's `records` queue (a labelled one waiting over an hour: read Mergify's summary on it); report the weekly plan %.
* **Tokens:** the Lead hands over 200k beyond its start read (P-446; was P-314's 400k) (the Lead's re-read context was 29% of local cost); Sonnet only where the model cannot change the result (P-303); stop the timed check when nothing is in flight.
* **Tests:** each change runs the full suite once, in the merge queue; the PR itself runs only the suites its change can affect. Change the number of parallel parts only on measured evidence (start-up per part, machine wait, flaky failures), with pros and cons stated; never add capacity because it is available.
* **Times in IST (P-302):** every time the founder reads (messages, reports, pages, delivery plans, estimates, token resets) is in IST (UTC+5:30), labelled "IST"; new records lines use IST too; earlier UTC lines stay as written.
* **Daily measures (the tech lead; once a day and when asked):** rows reaching Works · median approved-to-merged time · batch failure rate (target under 10%) · bugs found after merge · AI spend · share of commits that are records · and P-371's flow numbers from `python3 -I P/tools/flow-measure.py --since <UTC time>` (PR-check hours thrown away under 20%, records under 20% of merges, approved-to-merged under 1 hour).
* **Reporting to the founder (P-292):** every 30-minute check posts one or two lines only when something changed or needs the founder; every 2 hours while the founder is present, and once when the founder returns, a fixed one-screen report: (1) capabilities scored vs confirmed and which changed, (2) pipeline: merges, approval-to-merge, failed batches, test minutes, builders working, anything stuck, (3) quality: new bugs by severity, anything red on main, (4) cost: weekly plan %, paid AI, (5) going well / not well, at most 2 each with evidence, (6) needs from the founder, at most 3, each with a recommendation, (7) next 2 hours. Once a day the build review page is refreshed. Numbers come from tools (`tools/flow-watch.sh`, `tools/progress.sh`, GitHub), never from a builder's words; project data only, never personal or user data.
* **Notes-only changes (P-292):** a PR that changes only notes (`*.md` records, reports) runs only `check-docs.sh` and `decision-impact.sh`, not the app suites; labelled `review: approved`, it goes through Mergify's `records` queue (P-371, B119; never by hand while a batch is checking, P-306 (3)); bundle notes into as few PRs as possible.
* **Waste removed, quality bar unchanged (P-293):** after the approval label, push only a real fix (review notes go in before the label; a fix is re-tested as usual) · builders post questions as PR comments (the guard allows comments; only the tech lead merges) · at most two review rounds (the review, then one diff-only check); after that the tech lead decides between another round and a fresh reviewer, never a pass without Approve · lanes put their notes in their code change, the tech lead merges at most one notes PR an hour. Every change still needs green tests, the reviewer's Approve and the independent re-run before it counts as Works; if fixes after merge or failed batches rise, the change is undone.
* **Leads:** a new series per restart ("Lead 1 · 3 Nov", …); two idle standbys started in the first founder-typed turn; the founder talks only to the current Lead, which relays every lane's questions.

## Product rules (still in force)
* `main` always works: a red check on `main`, a failed demo update or a qa Blocker is fixed before anything else.
* **Reuse, never rebuild:** filling and barcodes (`prototype/p5/fill.py` via `studio/api/src/fill.ts`; `prototype/evidence-lane/E1/tools/`); research on filling, reading or AI drafting starts from `E1/*/STATUS.md`, `prototype/p1`–`p5`, `prototype/expected/source-text/`, `research_notes/`, `reports/`.
* Capability or scaffolding for every element (P-33); the scope test for every finding (P-26); no control cut (P-60). Re-check an agreed but unbuilt screen against `main` before building it (R-109).
* **Demo story** (P-93, `DEMO.md`): a feature is done only when it shows in the story.
* D17 and D20 need an intended user: the scores are never self-awarded.
* **Disk:** below 10 GB free, remove finished copies whose work is pushed (`tools/disk.sh`); never the main copy, the demo copy, `.tools`, `sources/` or `code/`.
* Decisions go in `DECISIONS.md`, one line each, appended, never rewritten. **Process frozen until 10 Nov 22:30 IST** (P-312): a change before then needs measured evidence and the founder's OK; after that, at most one a week. Instruction files stay short (skills and agents under 100 lines, 10 KB).

## Daily measures (tech lead)
Rows reaching Works · median approved-to-merged time · batch failure rate (target under 10%) · bugs found after merge · AI spend. Rising bugs after merge send the cause back. Demos and briefs on demand; unasked only for a decision, risk or spend.

## Guards (unchanged)
* **Push guard** (`.githooks/pre-push`, CI): never commit `sources/`, `code/`, `archive/`, PDFs, databases, logs or secrets.
* **Merge guard:** no merge without green CI and the reviewer's Approve on the current commit.
* **Live AI is off on every copy.** Never set `*_LIVE=approved` by hand (only through `run-suites.sh` or a test's usage line, which pair it with the fake launcher, P-192); never click a priced button. A paid run needs the founder's Go with the cost stated first; every call goes through the spend ledger.
* **Official sources:** immigration values cite page, quote and date (P-170), or come from the immigration expert; anything else is labelled Sample with a row in `ASSUMPTIONS.md` the same day. Legal points: "Unconfirmed: no counsel".
* **No personal names** in files; roles only.
* `sources/`, `code/` and `archive/` are read-only references.
* **Never force-push** or rewrite history; commit and push only on your own branch.
* **New technology** only through `STANDARDS.md` §1 with the founder's OK, recorded in `ENVIRONMENT.md`.

## Tech lead housekeeping (moved from the lead skill word for word, 6 Nov, to keep the skill under 10 KB, P-203)
* Founder questions (founder, 7 Nov, P-390): one list, STATE "For the founder" (question, recommendation, what it blocks, since). Only product direction goes there (P-384); the Lead decides the rest. While he is away the Lead goes ahead on its recommendation when the step is reversible, says so on the row, and parks only what is irreversible or shapes the product. At his next typed turn, first thing: ask the oldest open row with AskUserQuestion, one at a time, then mark it answered with his words and the DECISIONS id.
* Screens (founder, 7 Nov, P-392): each new screen or visible behaviour goes on "For the founder" when it lands (screenshots, best-in-class study, the Lead's view); it does not block builders; changes become follow-up rows. Before labelling a PR with a new screen, the Lead checks its concept has the best-in-class section (screen-concept step 4b: named products, open-source projects and published practice, from primary sources, with what each does best); none → the builder adds it first. Lead numbers keep counting and never reset; the session id is the unique internal id.
* Lead branch (founder, 7 Nov, P-388): each day the Lead starts a fresh `lead/<today>` branch from `origin/main` and opens its records PR from it; never merge `main` into an old lead branch (6-7 Nov: that merge waited ~7.4 h on one approval prompt while the founder slept). Stall guard: while the founder is away, no call that can wait on a prompt; when `lead-watch.sh` says the previous check was missed, tell the founder at once. Watchdog (P-389): the first standby Lead keeps one recurring job, exactly `run lead-heartbeat; notify the founder if stale`, every 15 min (`tools/lead-heartbeat.sh`: exit 1 → push notification to the founder + message to the current Lead; 0, 2, 3 → nothing). On take-over it deletes that job before creating its check; the next first standby starts one.
* Sidebar: nested sessions move only with their parent and cannot be moved alone. A finished session (PR merged, nothing unpushed) is archived directly by the Lead (founder, 2 Nov: "whatever it can archive, it can archive"). Simplest rule (founder, 3 Nov): when every lane under a parent session (an old Lead) has merged or been replaced, the Lead tells the founder **in chat**, in one line, "Archive <parent title> now"; archiving the parent archives its finished nested sessions with it. Until then, nothing is archived and the founder reads no file for it.
* Disk (founder, 2 Nov): after archiving, remove that session's worktrees (`git worktree list`, including scratchpad `wt-*` copies); first commit any uncommitted record (reviews) to a records branch and check the head is on origin; never remove `demo-main` or a running session's copy; `git worktree prune`; report free disk.
