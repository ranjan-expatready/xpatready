## 2. The layered model (how SA-01B works)

```
SA-01B CONFIG (ExpatReady)
 Row = Pathway › Program › Category › Sub-category   (allowed locations per row)
 ├─ Base forms        (always for this row; may carry "only if", e.g. outside Canada)
 ├─ Base documents    (always for this row; may carry "only if")
 └─ AI reads every form + IRCC checklist + guide → fields, sections, rules, validations,
    and triggers "this answer adds that document / form"; each field: Client or Case officer
          ↓
CASE
 Officer sets each person's location at opening → client answers client fields only,
 officer fills officer fields on the full screen
          ↓
DOCUMENT CHECKLIST (per person, grows live as answers arrive)
   Layer 1  base documents of the row
 + Layer 2  documents triggered by answers (married → marriage certificate; outside → GIC)
 + Layer 3  extra documents the officer requests on this case
 + optional: the client adds a document of their own (officer sorts it)
FORMS follow the same layers (base forms + forms triggered by answers, e.g. IMM 5409)
```

- One "only if" mechanism for sections, documents, forms and steps; location is one condition among others (marital status, age, country lived in).
- Conditions are written once in the library and reused by every row.
- Everything is per person; a document serving several people (marriage certificate) is uploaded once.
- Building the list is automatic; accepting each upload is the officer's (accept, send back, or "not applicable" with a reason — Decision 7).

## 3. End-to-end flow

1. **Pack (ExpatReady):** rows list sections, documents, forms and steps, each with "only if"; AI drafts, people review, expert confirms, second person publishes.
2. **Prospect:** small lead form → PQR → Assess (scoring engine).
3. **Case opens** (client signs): programme chosen, start at any stage; people added; each person's location set.
4. **Each person gets** their questions, documents, forms and steps, each with its reason.
5. **Collect:** family link with tabs ("Send to…"); officer fills officer fields; answers change → lists update; uploads kept.
6. **Officer confirms** all data on one screen → Autofill → checks and fixes on the form → Validate in ExpatReady (barcodes).
7. **Approve, sign, submit:** warnings shown, the representative decides; only a failed form validation stops.
8. **After submission:** government steps and deadlines → "Start next stage" for multi-stage programmes.
