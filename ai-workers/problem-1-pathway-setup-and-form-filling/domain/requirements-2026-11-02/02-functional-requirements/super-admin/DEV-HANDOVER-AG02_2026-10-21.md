## 3. The main changes, in one page

- A **lead** (anyone who showed interest) and a **prospect** (a sale, made by one click, Engage) are separate.
- A person becomes a **client** in one step, three ways: e-signature complete, "Signed offline", or "Convert to client". Undo is allowed while the client has nothing yet.
- **Duplicates** are never merged by the system. A banner shows; staff click Link, Keep separate or Merge.
- **Sharing new leads** is one setting: Anyone takes it, Round robin, or Always one person. Bookings with several hosts have their own pick rule on the appointment type (First free person or Round robin, AG-4-12) *(changed 22 Oct — final check)*.
- **Quiet by default:** the reply reminder is off unless the firm turns it on; "going cold" is a mark, never a notice.
- **"Message many"** replaces campaigns: email or WhatsApp, only to people who agreed on that channel. A paid extra.
- **Labels** on the person; **"Interested in"** can include the firm's own services.
- **Facebook lead ads** through an ExpatReady app on Zapier. Native Meta is Release 2.
- **"Who is this" panel** on every email and WhatsApp conversation (AG-10).
- **New product rules:** X-4 "Grows with the firm" (team features appear only when a second person, office or channel exists) and X-5 "The firm sets the timings that shape its work" (one Timings screen in Settings → Advanced).
- **Release 2:** SMS, Instagram and Messenger, per-programme pipelines, forecasts and leaderboards, native Meta Lead Ads.
