## Found while building and testing (all fixed)

1. "Trials disagree" first fired on 260 items because each trial names its own fields; now limited to outcome, person, row and kind of transform (40 items). A chosen proposal keeps this library's field, or brings that trial's field definition with it.
2. Two draft entries could share an id (the requirements and checks drafts both declared four calculations); items now get unique ids, and a repeat is flagged as a possible duplicate.
3. Refusal order: the author's approval now names the D254 reason first.
4. Browser run 1: the result message was overwritten after submit and publish, and the user selector caused a sideways scroll at 375 px. Run 2: three small display flaws (the chosen proposal not shown, blank options, a lingering message). Runs 1 and 2 are kept in `p3/evidence/run-1-before-fixes/` and `run-2/`. Run 3 is the evidence above.
5. The browser pane in the app was hidden and did not repaint after scrolling, so screenshots were taken with Playwright and Chrome instead. Also, a server started by the pane's launcher hung before listening (a likely macOS folder-permission prompt); it was started from the shell instead.
