# Module card: api-platform (P-615)
Firms, staff and sign-in, settings, secrets, jobs, logs, complaints and message consent. Read this before editing these files; update it in the same PR when an entry point, invariant, table or test below changes (or say `Card unchanged: <reason>` in the PR body).

**Purpose.** `firm-scope.ts` the one firm filter; `identity/` tokens, staff and the guard (Cognito or local); `settings.ts` and `config/` typed firm settings and app configuration; `secret-box/` AES-256-GCM secrets; `jobs/` the one job runner (pg-boss); `observability/` the one logger, redaction, metrics, alerts; `web-address/` each firm's web address; `calendar/` the firm's calendar: zone helpers, the business-day test, open days and `countBack` for reminders, `readFirmCalendar` (CS-DL1: a government or legal date is never moved; a reminder counted back lands on the last open day on or before the computed day, never after the date; AG-7-94); `complaints/` (its acknowledge-by period, over `calendar/`); `messages/consent/` the consent store; `audit/` (`audit/firm-read/` the firm's own activity log and its CSV export, AUD-4; `audit/csv.ts` the one audit CSV writer); `firms/` self-serve sign-up to a no-card trial (PF-5); `terms/` pending terms and their acceptances (PF-5t); `keep-period/` the keep-period table's read and the firm's own number for closed client files, a reminder with a warning below the College's 6 years, never a lock (AUD-5; `ports/keep-period.ts` holds the table, the port and its fake); `billing/` BillingPort's Stripe adapter (`ports/billing.ts` holds the port and its fake), Stripe's webhook and the moves it makes (`webhook.ts`), the billing routes (`account.ts`) and the payment hold (`routes.ts`, PF-5 part 2); `support/` support tickets a firm's staff raise to us (PF-18b); `settings/offices/` a firm's offices, each with its own time zone, hours and closed days (PF-18a; OfficeClockPort's real adapter `OfficeClock`, `ports/offices.ts` holds the port and its fake); `files/file-type.ts` the one file-type and size check every upload door calls (PF-F4: `detect`, `checkDeclared`, `checkFile`; pure code): an upload's type comes from its bytes, never its name or claim.
**Owner area.** platform module rows PF-* (Unconfirmed: no lane map in code).
**Entry points.** `/api/platform/complaints` (PF-21w; PF-21v adds `GET handlers`); `/api/platform`: `GET me`, `POST session/local`, `POST staff/invitations`, `POST staff/invitations/batch` (PF-4c: 1-20 rows, flags per row, one seat check), `PATCH staff/:id/roles`, `POST staff/:id/deactivate`, `PUT staff/:id/credential`; PF-4b: `GET staff`, `GET staff/:id`, `GET licences`, `GET|PUT me/account`, `GET settings/two-step`, `POST session/callback`, `POST session/sign-out`, `POST staff/:id/sign-out-everywhere`, `POST staff/:id/reset-two-step`, `/api/public/invitations/:token` (+ `/accept`), `requireStepUp` (401), `MAIL_PORT`, `IDENTITY_ADMIN_PORT`; PF-6r: `GET roles` (the six starter roles, each key with class and scope); PF-19b: `MESSAGE_PORT`, `MESSAGES_MAIL` (MailPort over MessagePort: booking mail; since PF-19r also `MAIL_PORT`'s staff invitation and two-step reset notice, and the complaint reminder; `MessagesMail.sendIn` writes in the caller's transaction), `GET /api/demo/platform-inbox` (demo only, with attachments); `firmScope(firmId)`, `PlatformJobs` (`jobs.handle`), `CONSENT_PORT`, the guard of `/api/platform/*` and `/api/front-office/*`; AUD-5: `GET keep-periods`, `PUT keep-periods/closed-client-files`, `KeepPeriods` (KeepPeriodPort); `/api/public` (PF-5, open doors): `GET plans`, `POST signup`, `POST signup/verify`; PF-18a: `GET|POST /api/platform/offices`, `PATCH /api/platform/offices/:id`, `OFFICE_CLOCK` (OfficeClockPort), `zoneForProvince(code)`; PF-5t: `GET me/terms`, `POST me/terms/accept`, `GET terms/acceptances`, `TERMS_GATE` (the guard's 428 TERMS_PENDING); PF-18b: `/api/platform/support` `POST tickets` (multipart), `GET tickets`, `GET tickets/:id`, `POST tickets/:id/messages`, `POST tickets/:id/files/:n/link`, `GET files/:token`, and `SupportService.replyAsSupport(tx, …)` (no route: PF-17's console).
**Invariants (set by).**
- Every read and write of a platform or front-office table goes through `firmScope(firmId)`; another firm's row is "not found" (PF-3, contracts §0.2; P-559).
- The database walls it (PF-12): the API connects as `xr_app` (owns no table; it refuses to start otherwise), each query runs as its caller's firm (`firm-scope.ts`: the guard's firm, a door's resolved firm, a job's firm, a case opening's own platform firm (CS-K1 part 2); none reads nothing); crossing firms only through `asBypass(use)` for a use on `BYPASS_USES`, each use logged. Narrow definer lookups outside it (owned by `xr_wall_reader`, search_path pinned; EXECUTE for `xr_app`, and PF-12b part 2's also for `xr_staff_bypass`, which already reads every firm, for suite and tool processes): `platform_firm_calendar(firm key)` (CS-DL1, until CS-W3 part 1) returns a firm's three calendar settings only, for the case stage views; PF-12b part 2's seven own-firm door lookups return the firm id only (`platform_firm_of_key`, `platform_staff_firm`, `platform_invitation_firm`, `fo_sales_web_form_firm`, `fo_booking_token_firm`, `platform_web_address_firm`) or yes/no (`platform_staff_email_taken`, accept's one-account-one-firm check), and the door then runs as that firm: sign-in, the guard, invitations, the web form, the booking page and link, and the host routing are not bypass uses. Every bypass use writes `platform.bypass` to the touched firm's trail, or is refused (PF-12b: `touchFirm(tx, firmId)` in that firm's transaction; a line that cannot be written rolls the work back, 500 in plain words or a failed job; each entry's `audit` says `per-firm` or `none: <why>`; one bypass use at a time, except the one named pair: `platform.account.mail` inside `platform.firms.signup`, by its `inside` field). Every new `platform_*`, `fo_*`, `money_*`, `msg_*`, `auto_*`, `plat_complaint*` table ships its forced policy in its own migration.
- Sized for about 100 firms in one process (P-562); two-step sign-in is required for every staff member (P-567).
- The audit trail is append-only, enforced at the database since AUD-1a (UPDATE, TRUNCATE and any DELETE outside the demo reset's Sample lines refused); demo reset deletes only demo lines, never a platform line (P-571 (1)).
- Every line through AuditPort (`audit/audit-event.ts`) carries its firm, actor type and the request's or job's correlation id, read by `audit/context.ts` from PF-23's request scope; a line for a firm other than the request's or job's is refused, and a failed line raises one `audit_write_failed` alert and rolls the change back (AUD-1b, P-607 (3)).
- When role change, invitation accept or firm signup gives a role that brings money keys, it writes one `platform.staff.money_keys_granted` line through `identity/money-line.ts` only, in the caller's transaction (PF-6b ruling 2 (b), PF-6m, PF-6m2). An invitation is accepted only while its inviter is active and may still give every role invited; otherwise 410 and `platform.invitation.refused_inviter` (PF-6m2).
- Personal data never reaches a system log: redaction at the logger (PF-23, NFR5-14).
- A job's key is its pg-boss id: one key never queues a second job (PF-14).
- The consent store is the only writer of the `msg_` tables, inside the caller's transaction (PF-19a, P-584).
- Values for experts to set are labelled Sample, never guessed (P-170).
- One office → the firm's own zone, hours and closed days; office rows exist only once a second office is added, and that add writes the first office from the firm's settings; an office is never removed here (PF-18a, AG-1-74, AG-16-68).
- A sign-up's password goes to the identity provider only: never in a table, log, audit line or answer; the sign-up door answers the same whether or not an email is known (PF-5, P-566).
- A person with a current terms version to accept (privacy notice: each person; terms of service and data processing: an Owner for the firm) gets 428 TERMS_PENDING on every guarded route but `GET me`, the two `me/terms` routes and sign-out (PF-5t; wording Sample, Unconfirmed: no counsel).
- A support message is insert-only and a ticket is never deleted (the database refuses both); its file keys lie in its own ticket's folder; the ticket's words never reach an audit line, an event or the support email (PF-18b, AG-16-63). `SupportService.replyAsSupport` reads a ticket by id without `firmScope`: our team answers every firm (the system plane); under the walls it finds nothing unless PF-17 runs it on a named `asBypass` use.
- Firms' billing goes through BillingPort only; CI and lanes use its fake or recorded Stripe answers, never a live call (PF-5, contracts §0.3); a held firm's (PAYMENT_HOLD) signed-in staff get 402 everywhere but `@HoldOpen` routes, `me`, the two `me/terms` routes and the session routes (so pending terms are accepted, then billing paid); a Stripe event is stored once by its id and handled by reading Stripe's current subscription, so order does not matter; the hold ends only on a paid invoice (PF-5 part 2).
- `PLATFORM_ENVIRONMENT` is required; no default: unset or blank stops the start-up, naming it (PF-24w, rev 16.5). Every setting, sign-in's and the Sample seed's included, is read by the one loader `config/settings.ts` once per process; `identityConfig` and `sample-seed.ts` read no variable themselves.
